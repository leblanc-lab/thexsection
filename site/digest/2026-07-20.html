What the data teaches without being told

A modest-volume day (27 papers) with no collaboration headline, but the center of gravity has shifted from reconstructing physics with ML to learning it — three generative/graph models today extract Standard Model structure, tagger knowledge, and robust tracking straight from data or across experiments. The other real weight is cosmological: Khoury et al.’s dark-axion/dark-baryon model (arXiv:2607.16191) fits DESI DR2 phantom-crossing with Δχ²=−14.48, and two non-Gaussianity papers push the excursion-set and bispectrum machinery into strongly non-Gaussian tails. Underneath, a steady stream of novel dark-matter detection proposals and scotogenic neutrino model-building. A tool-and-idea day rather than a measurement one.

Themes

Machine learning that discovers rather than reconstructs

The month’s ML thread — trigger GNNs, transformer calorimetry, LQCDMaster — takes a conceptual step today. Kato et al.’s ShellFlow arXiv:2607.16144 is the standout: a Riemannian flow-matching model trained on ~10⁹ real ATLAS 13 TeV open-data events, given only the on-shell condition and the invariant-mass formula, that recovers the J/ψ, Υ, Z, W and top masses at their PDG values and the leptonic Weinberg angle — none of it a training target. Gao et al.’s JetCoRD arXiv:2607.15531 distills ATLAS GN2 and CMS ParT into a single 82k-parameter student that beats both teachers at high-purity working points, notably by building in teacher unreliability. And Reuter et al. arXiv:2607.15361 show a Belle II GNN track finder treats detector ageing as a domain shift — halving the efficiency loss of baseline tracking after retraining.

Novel dark-matter detection targets

A cluster of “new target class” proposals. Zhang et al. arXiv:2607.15338 propose metallic carbon nanotubes as a 1D Luttinger-liquid plasmon target reaching the keV freeze-in benchmark, with sidereal-day modulation from the aligned geometry. Li et al. arXiv:2607.15612 push chip-scale Rydberg-atom superheterodyne detection of >10 GHz hidden photons 3–4 orders below current limits. And Dev et al. arXiv:2607.15368 turn the collimated muon beam at neutrino facilities into a bremsstrahlung source for HNLs beyond meson-decay kinematic reach at SBND/DUNE. The diversifying-search mood the memory has tracked, now on the detection side.

Dark energy and non-Gaussian cosmology

Khoury et al. arXiv:2607.16191 give the day’s most consequential cosmology entry — a concrete dark-sector realization of apparent phantom crossing that improves on ΛCDM by Δχ²=−14.48, with an EDE-like injection near equality that is, honestly, too small to move H₀. Natural sequel to the H₀ World Cup’s early-dark-energy verdict. On non-Gaussianity, Yang et al. arXiv:2607.15337 forecast folded PNG from the galaxy bispectrum, and Teuscher et al. arXiv:2607.16152 apply large-deviation theory to halo/void abundances beyond perturbative moment expansion. Cembranos et al. arXiv:2607.15354 raise Starobinsky’s n_s toward ACT-preferred values via a tunnelling exit — the latest in the running effort to rescue Starobinsky from ACT data.

Scotogenic neutrino masses

Two minimal-model entries: Rizzo arXiv:2607.15965 generates ~0.05 eV Dirac masses at one loop from E₆-like portal matter, and Peinado et al. arXiv:2607.16049 find D₄ the smallest flavor symmetry unifying seesaw-scotogenic masses with a 50–200 GeV dark-matter candidate.

Worth a look

  • arXiv:2607.16144 — ShellFlow learns W, top, Z masses and the Weinberg angle from raw ATLAS open data with only two physics priors; a genuinely surprising demonstration.
  • arXiv:2607.16191 — Khoury et al.’s dark-axion/dark-baryon model fits DESI phantom crossing at Δχ²=−14.48; the concrete particle realization the DESI-anomaly literature needed.
  • arXiv:2607.15338 — Carbon nanotubes as a 1D plasmon target reaching the keV freeze-in benchmark, with a built-in directional handle.
  • arXiv:2607.15392 — Solar-system p-nuclide abundances (Mo, Ru, ⁹²Nb) as a fossil signature of fast collective neutrino oscillations in supernovae; an unexpected observational handle on an elusive phenomenon.
  • arXiv:2607.15531 — First cross-experiment tagger distillation in HEP, with a reliability signal that lets the 82k-param student beat its ATLAS and CMS teachers.
