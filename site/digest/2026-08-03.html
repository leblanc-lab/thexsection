Dark sectors crowd the board

The day’s volume is dark matter — three separate dark-photon production stories, a monopole-DM construction, two new detection concepts, and an axion-WIMP dark-energy model — but the sharpest single result is a measurement: BESIII observes a charged charmoniumlike \(X(3790)^-\) near the \(D^0D^-\) threshold at 5.3σ (arXiv:2607.29138), \(J^P=1^-\), extending the collaboration’s exotic-hadron run. Underneath, quantum-information language shows up twice as a genuine observable — in DIS and in top tomography — which is the one thread that carries forward from the recent \(t\bar t\) entanglement work.

Quantum information as a hadron and collider probe

This is the collider-QI program from the citation radar moving from demonstration to measurement.

Deep dives

arXiv:2607.29138 — Observation of a Charged Charmoniumlike Structure in \(e^+e^-\to D^0D^-\pi^++c.c.\) (BESIII Collaboration)

BESIII performs a partial-wave analysis of \(e^+e^-\to D^0D^-\pi^+\) on 1667 pb\(^{-1}\) collected at \(\sqrt{s}=4682\) MeV, using combined single- and double-tag reconstruction. A near-threshold enhancement in the \(D^0D^-\) system, modeled with a Flatté lineshape, requires \(J^P=1^-\) and yields mass \((3794.7\pm6.0\pm8.7)\) MeV/\(c^2\) and \(D^0D^-\) coupling \((1.6\pm0.2\pm0.2)\) GeV. The raw fit significance is \(7.7\sigma\); after 320 pseudo-experiments account for the look-elsewhere effect and systematics are folded in, it settles at \(5.3\sigma\) — enough to claim observation of this new charged charmoniumlike state, \(X(3790)^-\). Cross-checks (alternative \(J^P<4\), added PHSP or non-resonant amplitudes) leave the signal at \(\geq8\sigma\) when nuisance components are included. The analysis simultaneously updates the excited charmed mesons, with new \(D_2^*(2460)\), \(D_1^*(2600)\) and \(D_1^*(2760)\) parameters.

Why it matters: this is the first isovector state seen coupling to a \((D\bar D)^\pm\) pair — a channel previously argued unlikely, since spin-parity conservation forbids the \(D\bar D\pi\) vertex under pure pion-exchange binding, yet phenomenology and lattice predict a scalar \(D\bar D\) bound state. A charged \(1^-\) structure right at threshold extends the \(Z_c\)/\(X(3872)\) molecular narrative into a new final state and constrains heavy-quark-symmetry partner counting. Caveats: the fit fraction is small (\(1.3\pm0.3\%\)), systematics on mass are large (\(\pm8.7\) MeV), and the Flatté parameterization plus fixed \(D_3^*(2750)\) and \(\bar D^{*0}\)-width inputs carry model dependence; the state’s nature (molecule vs. threshold cusp) is not resolved here.

arXiv:2607.28722 — Gravity Probe-DM: The Gravitational Laboratory for Dark Matter (Yu-Dai Tsai et al.)

Tsai and Foote propose a purely gravitational Solar-System dark-matter search exploiting the irreducible downstream wake the Sun’s gravity carves into any incident unbound DM flow. Two precision-ranged spacecraft act as a heliocentric gradiometer: a uniform acceleration cancels, but the wake tidal tensor projected along the baseline does not. For a LISA-like \(2.5\times10^6\) km baseline crossing a 0.1 AU coherent width at 30 km/s, a one-percent density excess gives a raw uniform-core range response of only 0.63 pm over the 5.77 d crossing. Because the free response grows as \(T^2\) while the projected tide stays coherent, an axial “wake-skimming” arc lifts this to 2.5 nm over one year; an optimistic low-lag dark-disk benchmark (20% of local density, 30% focused contrast) reaches ~15 nm. REBOUND/REBOUNDx integrations validate the linear-in-density scaling and the \(\dot\omega\propto M_{\rm od}\) precession law.

Why it matters: existing planetary/asteroid-ephemeris bounds only limit smooth, near-spherical DM around the Sun; this concept instead targets an anisotropic, directional structure tied to the Galactic flow, giving a distinctive turn-on/turn-off tidal waveform and, for wave DM, de Broglie-scale interference. A detection would be the first Solar-System gravitational DM map and would calibrate the local phase-space model underlying direct detection. Caveats are substantial and stated plainly: nanometre signals sit beneath solar/planetary/asteroid tides, radiation pressure and thermal recoil; the \(T^2\) enhancement requires a passive one-year dwell that is unavailable near 1 AU without going to several AU; and every number is a pre-fit response scale, not a demonstrated sensitivity. LISA Pathfinder archival data is proposed only as a methodological null test.

arXiv:2607.28761 — Quantum Tomography of Top Quarks as a Probe of Charge-Parity Violation (Eren Erdogan et al.)

Erdogan et al. compute the first fully renormalized one-loop Higgs-induced correction to the \(t\bar t\) production spin density matrix from a CP-mixed top-Yukawa coupling \(\mathcal{L}=-(m_t/v)h\,\bar t(a_t+ib_t\gamma_5)t\), predicting its imprint on all fifteen Fano coefficients. The couplings enter at \(\mathcal{O}(y_t^2/16\pi^2)\); CP-odd effects are linear in \(a_tb_t\) and populate specifically the antisymmetric correlation \(C_{nr}^-\) (dispersive) and the polarization difference \(P_k-\bar P_k\) (absorptive), none of which shift the cross section. On-shell renormalization forces imaginary top field-renormalization constants \(\propto a_tb_t\) — the first complete renormalized CP-odd density matrix. Confronting CMS’s full 15-parameter tomography with its covariance, they find at \(a_t=1\) the bound \(-1.40<b_t<1.23\) (95% CL, inclusive Fano fit), tightening to \(-1.01<b_t<1.26\) when spin and \(m_{t\bar t}\)-shape information are combined — comparable to ATLAS’s \(|b_t|\approx1.1\) from tree-level \(t\bar tH/tH\).

Why it matters: this converts the collider quantum-information program from demonstrating entanglement/magic to a genuine precision measurement, using the reconstructed quantum state itself as the observable. The method is model-agnostic — any operator shifting the density matrix can be confronted with the same tomography without regenerating events. Caveats: the SM sits within 68% CL, so this is a constraint not a hint; the bound assumes all other new physics decouples from the loop; and the existing binning was optimized for quantum-property studies, so finer \(m_{t\bar t}\) bins near threshold — where Higgs corrections peak — would sharpen sensitivity. Two mirror minima under \((a_t,b_t)\to(-a_t,-b_t)\) remain unresolved.
