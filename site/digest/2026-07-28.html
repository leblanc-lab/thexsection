Flavor precision, detectors gone solar

The day’s weight sits on flavor. BESIII posts its best-precision measurement of \(D\to\pi\ell^+\nu\) across all four charge/lepton modes (arXiv:2607.23945, arXiv:2607.23949), pinning \(f_+^{D\to\pi}(0)|V_{cd}|\) to sub-percent and testing \(e/\mu\) universality; and Allwicher et al. arXiv:2607.24494 give the NA62 golden-mode measurement its next interpretive turn, translating \(K^+\to\pi^+\nu\bar\nu\) into constraints on \(B\to K\nu\bar\nu\) and \(B_s\to\mu\mu\) — the cross-mode phenomenology the memory has tracked since the kaon result anchored the week. CMS adds a substantial off-shell Higgs analysis (arXiv:2607.23352). The other genuinely coherent cluster is quieter and newer: direct-detection experiments repurposed as solar-neutrino observatories.

Themes

Flavor precision, kaons to charm

Allwicher et al. arXiv:2607.24494 is the direct continuation of the NA62 thread that has run all month (Blanke et al. on KOTO II room, then the lattice \(R_{e/\mu}\) benchmark): under different flavor assumptions the \(K^+\) result now feeds \(B\to K\nu\bar\nu\), \(B_s\to\mu\mu\), and forecasts for \(K_L\to\pi^0\nu\bar\nu\). Independently, BESIII’s \(D\to\pi\ell\nu\) (arXiv:2607.23945) delivers \(|V_{cd}|\) and the form factor at record precision plus first forward-backward asymmetries, with a null scalar-current search — clean, and SM-consistent. Two rungs of the CKM ladder tightened on the same day.

Direct detection as a solar-neutrino observatory

A real coalescence. Amaral et al. release SNuDD arXiv:2607.22817, an open code for solar-neutrino rates at xenon detectors, and set NSI limits from LZ/XENONnT/PandaX-4T already approaching dedicated-experiment reach. Beccaria et al. arXiv:2607.24584 use the newly observed CEνNS signal in those same detectors to bound invisible \(\nu_2\) decay, competitive with SNO. Han et al. arXiv:2607.24421 supply the sub-percent flux framework (Earth-matter MSW, time-varying baseline) such measurements now need. The neutrino floor has become a physics program.

Hubble tension and where the neutrino mass hides

Roy Choudhury arXiv:2607.24443 pushes a 1.1%-precision distance-ladder \(H_0=73.3\), a \(6.85\sigma\) tension that the corrected Pantheon+/DES-DOVEKIE reanalysis does not soften — the counterpoint to last week’s “walls against tension-solvers.” Qu et al. arXiv:2607.24742 make the complementary move: deconstructing which probes drive the \(\sum m_\nu<0.056\) eV bound and building a late-Universe-free route that relaxes it to \(<0.41\) eV, trading statistical power for dark-energy independence and landing near Project 8’s target.

Deep dives

arXiv:2607.23945 — Precision measurements of \(D^0\to\pi^-\ell^+\nu_\ell\) and \(D^+\to\pi^0\ell^+\nu_\ell\) (\(\ell=e,\mu\)) (BESIII Collaboration et al.)

Using 20.3 fb⁻¹ at \(\sqrt{s}=3.773\) GeV and the double-tag technique, BESIII measures all four \(D\to\pi\ell\nu\) modes to record precision. The branching fractions land at \((2.950\pm0.017\pm0.017)\times10^{-3}\), \((2.817\pm0.037\pm0.019)\times10^{-3}\), \((3.622\pm0.034\pm0.018)\times10^{-3}\), and \((3.507\pm0.043\pm0.026)\times10^{-3}\) for the \(\pi^-e\), \(\pi^-\mu\), \(\pi^0e\), \(\pi^0\mu\) channels respectively — a factor 2–3 improvement over the previous BESIII results, which are superseded. A simultaneous fit of partial rates in ten \(q^2\) bins yields \(f_+^{D\to\pi}(0)|V_{cd}|=0.1425\pm0.0005\pm0.0003\) (sub-percent), giving \(|V_{cd}|=0.2262\pm0.0008\pm0.0005\pm0.0018_{\rm LQCD}\) or \(f_+^{D\to\pi}(0)=0.6339\pm0.0024\pm0.0014\) depending on which external input is assumed. First forward-backward asymmetries are extracted, and a first scalar-current search gives \(\mathrm{Re}(C_S^\mu)=0.022\pm0.023\pm0.003\), consistent with zero.

Why it matters: this tightens one rung of the CKM ladder (\(c\to d\)) on the same day the digest highlights kaon-to-\(B\) propagation, and the null scalar current directly constrains the two-Higgs-doublet/leptoquark scenarios invoked for the \(b\to c\) LFU anomalies — here in a clean, SM-consistent charm transition. LFU is tested via \(\mu/e\) rate ratios across \(q^2\). Caveat: the \(|V_{cd}|\) extraction is now dominated by the lattice form-factor uncertainty (\(\pm0.0018_{\rm LQCD}\) versus \(\pm0.0008\) statistical), so further gains require theory, not more data.

arXiv:2607.23352 — Off-shell Higgs boson measurements: Yukawa couplings, self-coupling, compositeness, and width (CMS Collaboration)

(Abstract only.) CMS analyzes off-shell Higgs production in the four-lepton channel with 138 fb⁻¹ at 13 TeV, combining \(H\to ZZ\) and \(H\to WW\) to extract \(\Gamma_H=5.1^{+2.0}_{-1.8}\) MeV and exclude the no-off-shell hypothesis at \(>5\sigma\). The analysis delivers several firsts: a direct test of composite-Higgs models with \(\Lambda_H>870\) GeV at 95% CL, the first off-shell constraint on the Higgs self-coupling, and — combined with on-shell data — the tightest light-quark Yukawa limits to date while relaxing the usual bound on the \(HVV\) coupling for more model-independent width results.

Why it matters: the off-shell region breaks the coupling-vs-width degeneracy that plagues on-shell rate measurements, and folding in BSM loop content and modified vector couplings makes the \(\Gamma_H\) determination more robust than earlier off-shell extractions. The compositeness and self-coupling handles are novel uses of the same dataset. Caveat: no methodological detail is available beyond the abstract, so assumptions behind the model-independence claims cannot be assessed here.

arXiv:2607.24494 — Implications of \(K\to\pi\nu\bar\nu\) for new physics in \(B\) decays (Lukas Allwicher et al.)

Allwicher and Bordone propagate the new NA62 \(\mathcal{B}(K^+\to\pi^+\nu\bar\nu)=9.6^{+1.9}_{-1.8}\times10^{-11}\) (sub-20% precision) into two BSM frameworks. In the modified-\(Z\)-coupling scenario, under Minimal Flavor Violation \(B_s\to\mu\mu\) dominates the constraints, while under Partial Compositeness the kaon mode becomes the sharpest handle on right-handed couplings \(\delta g_R\) — even resolving a two-fold solution that \(B\to K\nu\bar\nu\) then breaks. Both give effective NP scales \(\Lambda^Z_{\rm eff}\gtrsim8\) TeV, tree-level accessible at HL-LHC. In the alternative \(U(2)^5\) semileptonic fit with third-generation dominance (\(\Lambda_{\rm eff}\gtrsim1.8\) TeV), the more precise NA62 value now admits an \(\epsilon\sim0\) / \(C_{\ell q}^-\sim0\) SM-like solution at 68%, though in mild tension with \(R_{D^{(*)}}\) and \(B\to K^{(*)}\nu\bar\nu\); the preferred large-BSM branch predicts \(\mathcal{B}(K_L\to\pi^0\nu\bar\nu)/\mathcal{B}(K^+\to\pi^+\nu\bar\nu)\sim1.5\) (normalized to SM), within the Grossman–Nir bound.

Why it matters: this is exactly the cross-mode phenomenology the digest has tracked since the NA62 result — one kaon branching ratio now feeds correlated predictions for \(B\to K^{(*)}\nu\bar\nu\), \(B_s\to\mu\mu\), and future \(K_L\to\pi^0\nu\bar\nu\) (KOTO II). The framework makes \(K_L\) a genuine prediction, testable by combining projected NA62 (15%), KOTO-II (25%), and Belle II (8%) precision. Caveat: the persistent \(B^+\to K^+\nu\bar\nu\) central value sits above all predictions, and the two green fit lobes remain equally allowed at current precision — conclusions hinge on which branch nature picks.
