Friday, December 8, 1995

arXiv:hep-ph/9512260 §

Precision Measurements at a Muon Collider

S. Dawson

Watchlist hit (muon collider) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing.

We discuss the potential for making precision measurements of \(M_W\) and \(M_T\) at a muon collider and the motivations for each measurement. A comparison is made with the precision measurements expected at other facilities. The measurement of the top quark decay width is also discussed.

Friday, August 4, 1995

arXiv:hep-ph/9508230 §

Physics with Like-Sign Muon Beams

Frank Cuypers et al.

Watchlist hit (muon collider) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing.

We point out that both the specific lepton number content and the high energies potentially attainable with muon-muon colliders make it advisable to consider the technical feasibility of including an option of like-sign incoming beams in the studies towards a proposal to build a muon-muon collider with center-of-mass energies in the TeV region. This capability will add some unique physics capabilities to the project. Special attention will have to be paid to polarization retention for the muons.

Tuesday, August 1, 1995

arXiv:hep-ph/9508209 §

The ttbar threshold at a muon collider

M. S. Berger

Watchlist hit (muon collider) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing.

The beam energy spread is a major issue in future attempts to study the \(t\overline{t}\) threshold at \(e^+e^-\) colliders. Muon colliders are expected to naturally have narrow band beams making them an ideal place to study the excitation curve. We present the parameter determinations that are possible from measuring the total cross section near threshold at a \(μ^+μ^-\) collider.

Wednesday, April 19, 1995

arXiv:hep-ph/9504330 §

s-Channel Higgs Boson Production at a Muon-Muon Collider

V. Barger et al.

Watchlist hit (muon collider) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing.

High luminosity muon-muon colliders would provide a powerful new probe of Higgs boson physics through \(s\)-channel resonance production. We discuss the prospects for detection of Higgs bosons and precision measurements of their masses and widths at such a machine.
