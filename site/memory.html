This week

The Cross Section: Rolling Synthesis, week ending 2026-10-21

Overview. The week’s center of gravity was the field checking its inputs and backgrounds before arguing about new physics. The LZ 248 keV event moved through three stages. Model-building gave way to halo assumptions, then to the Galactic Center excess, and finally, on 10-21, to an audit of LZ’s own electron-recoil background. SU(3)\(_F\) breaking became a pressure point across \(B\), \(\Xi_b\) and charm physics. Several anomalies moved toward the SM once their inputs were sharpened, including the \(t\bar t\) threshold, gallium and the \(C_9\)-via-\(|V_{cb}|\) route. Lattice HVP results kept accumulating on one side of the \(g-2\) gap. Precision theory had a strong week: two independent \(g^6\) hot-QCD pressures matched the lattice, and a policy paper formalized error budgets beyond scale variation.

Running themes

LZ 248 keV event: heating up, and changing character. The event drew one paper on 10-15 (Deka’s exothermic freeze-in, arXiv:2610.18974) and none on 10-19. Over the week the argument shifted: - Halo tail. Graham et al. (arXiv:2610.21041) showed that the thermal Higgsino survives the solar-neutrino bound only with a different high-velocity halo tail. They also proposed non-xenon tests. - Shared origin with the GCE, disputed. Abazajian et al. (arXiv:2610.23713) found the 20 GeV halo excess is foreground-dominated and argued against a common origin with LZ. Kim et al. (arXiv:2610.25423) built exactly that common origin anyway. - Background audit. Bandyopadhyay et al. (arXiv:2610.27105) put the event’s significance itself in question. LZ’s published calibration does not constrain the ER response tail. Tails consistent with that calibration give an ER contribution comparable to the entire quoted background in the comparison region.

SU(3)\(_F\) breaking: heating up. Bhattacharya et al. (arXiv:2610.20611) found a >5σ failure of the SU(3)\(_F\)-limit SM in \(\bar B\to DP\). Absorbing it requires about 60% breaking once CP observables are included, up from about 20% with branching ratios alone. This extends August’s \(B\to PP\) puzzle to the charm side. SU(3) breaking also dominates the normalization error in LHCb’s first \(\Xi_b^-\to\Xi^-\mu^+\mu^-\) observation (arXiv:2610.22452). The theory responses so far are a first-order U-spin sum-rule program (arXiv:2610.24975) and flavor-selective production to separate the two \(D_0^*(2300)\) poles (arXiv:2610.26175).

HVP lattice vs data-driven: steady, consolidating on the lattice side. - ETMC (arXiv:2610.22224) reached about 1% and widened the gap to pre-CMD3 data-driven values. - A QCD sum-rule result (arXiv:2610.20945) also lands on the lattice side. - New tools target the gap: the \(a_e\)–\(a_\mu\) correlation (arXiv:2610.26130) and the missing isospin-breaking \(\rho\pi\pi\) correction for the τ route (arXiv:2610.28506).

Anomalies dissolving into inputs: steady. - The gallium anomaly vanishes when the Sun is used as a calibrated source (arXiv:2610.22219). - A neutrino-anomaly review (arXiv:2610.23474) finds the tensions weakened, with no uncontested explanation. - The Galactic Center excess narrows to a τ-rich corridor once 30 dwarf spheroidals are imposed (arXiv:2610.26648). - NA61/SHINE’s kaon excess is the counter-current (see Open questions).

Error budgets and precision QCD: steady. Canonero et al. (arXiv:2610.25091) turned the cold-QED N³LO lesson into a prescription: supplement 7-point scale variations with known missing higher-order terms. N³LO building blocks kept arriving (arXiv:2610.20820, arXiv:2610.22533).

Turning points

  • \(t\bar t\) threshold. Ball et al. (arXiv:2610.21479) propagated PDF, \(m_t\) and \(\alpha_s\) correlations through a global PDF analysis. They find nothing at 8 TeV and just over 2σ at 13 TeV, against CMS’s 6.1σ, with a strong Δ–\(m_t\) correlation. Any future threshold or toponium claim should be quoted jointly with \(m_t\).
  • \(C_9\) escape route closed. Crivellin et al. (arXiv:2610.22490) gave a no-go for faking the lepton-universal \(b\to s\ell\ell\) deficit through NP in \(|V_{cb}|\) and hence \(V_{ts}\).
  • \(\mathcal O(g^6)\) hot Yang-Mills pressure completed. Two separate calculations (arXiv:2610.23675, arXiv:2610.23676) close a gap open for over two decades and agree with the lattice. This is a direct pass of dimensional-reduction EFT against data.
  • Weak magnetism in the free neutron. Camarasa-Domene et al. (arXiv:2610.26792) separate \(g_M\) from \(\lambda\) and exclude zero at 5.1σ, consistent with CVC. The significance is robust to the neutron-lifetime tensions.
  • LZ significance on the table. arXiv:2610.27105 (above) changes the default frame for all 248 keV papers.

Open questions

  • LZ ER tail. Will LZ or others perform the calibrations listed in arXiv:2610.27105? Do model-builders begin propagating the tail uncertainty?
  • SU(3)\(_F\). Is ~60% breaking a failure of the amplitude parametrization or of the symmetry expansion itself? Watch for responses to arXiv:2610.20611.
  • \(|V_{cb}|\). The inclusive/exclusive split remains unresolved. Marzocca’s DIS route (arXiv:2610.18961) projects about 0.8% at LHeC and 0.7% at FCC-eh, with orthogonal systematics.
  • NA61/SHINE kaon excess. The collaboration reports a charged-over-neutral kaon excess in Ar+Sc that known mechanisms do not explain (arXiv:2610.23498). Uncertainties are sizeable, so confirmation from other experiments is the thing to watch.
  • GCE corridor. Only τ-rich channels and light-mediator \(4b\) cascades survive arXiv:2610.26648. Watch whether shared-origin models like arXiv:2610.25423 move into that corridor.
  • Birefringence sources. New non-axion and axion–monopole sources of the 3.5σ post-dust signal appeared (arXiv:2610.27082, arXiv:2610.27059), joining Kasai’s lepton-asymmetry route. The stiff-pseudoscalar window is closable by astrophysical axion searches.
  • Neutrino mass ordering. Gluza et al. (arXiv:2610.27270) find most mixing schemes fit IO better. This sits awkwardly with the replacement, on 10-21, of Jin et al.’s 3.3σ normal-ordering result.
  • Smaller watch items:
  • IceCube’s 1.9σ linear Lorentz-invariance-violation preference (arXiv:2610.25214).
  • The compact \(X_b\) partner predicted ~60 MeV below \(B\bar B^*\) (arXiv:2610.19066).
  • The unexplained millikelvin SiPM double pulse (arXiv:2610.20132).

The month's trajectories.

This month

The Cross Section: Rolling Synthesis, month ending 2026-09-30

September belonged to a single LZ nuclear recoil: one 248 keV event at 2.6σ (arXiv:2609.02823). The theory response ran a full arc. It started with a consensus model, saw that model excluded, scattered into evasion strategies and then revived at high volume. Solar capture became the constraint every model is built around. Several other trajectories gained ground. Precision QCD became a center of gravity from mid-month. Flavor physics flipped from an LFU anomaly to a \(|V_{cb}|\) puzzle. Spectroscopy had its strongest run of the season. The axion program widened from lattice work into detection and cosmology. ML tooling moved steadily up the stack, from agents to inference. The overall mood was consolidation: anomalies were mostly being settled by bounds rather than amplified.

Running themes

LZ inelastic dark matter (heating → fading → revived; always shaped by solar capture). - W36, consensus: Groups converged on a ~1.1 TeV endothermic higgsino with ~300–370 keV splitting (arXiv:2609.01504, arXiv:2609.01583). - Exclusion: Pospelov’s solar-capture + IceCube bound excluded that reading (arXiv:2609.02775). Bose pushed the splitting bound to ≳557 keV (arXiv:2609.07807). - W37, evasion: The thermal higgsino survives only at 10⁵–10⁶ GeV (Langhoff arXiv:2609.09385). The exothermic reframing gave the main escape route (Dent arXiv:2609.04673). - W38, cooling: The thread thinned to cosmological survival strategies, including An’s endo/exo selection (arXiv:2609.17412). - W39, revival: Output rose to 5–7 papers a day, each built to dodge solar capture (arXiv:2609.21823). Examples include confining sectors and Murayama’s 11–20 TeV inert doublet (arXiv:2609.28819). - Lesson repeated all month: The event’s kinematics pin down mass and splitting, and astrophysics then decides which models survive.

Precision QCD and nucleon structure (heating since W38). - Four-loop non-singlet splitting functions (arXiv:2609.17267). - First GPD review in two decades (arXiv:2609.19544) and lattice GPDs near \(x=\pm\xi\) (arXiv:2609.20545). - W39 added three-loop massive form factors (arXiv:2609.22034) and a four-loop massive cusp anomalous dimension (arXiv:2609.23247). - Much of this work is aimed at the EIC and FCC.

Flavor: from LFU tension to \(|V_{cb}|\) puzzle (pivoting). - The exact-\(m_c\) NNLO calculation of B→Xsγ made the radiative sector fully consistent with the SM (arXiv:2608.29863). - Belle II’s \(R(D^*)=0.242\) (arXiv:2609.21947) then eased the semileptonic side. - The tension migrated to inclusive vs exclusive \(|V_{cb}|\) (arXiv:2609.28752 vs arXiv:2608.24332). The inclusive fit quality is poor.

Hadron spectroscopy (heating). - W38: f0(980) was triangulated as a scale-dependent state from three independent methods. - W39: LHCb observed \(\Omega_{cc}^+\) at 8.7σ (arXiv:2609.21921) and a light exotic beauty-strange state at >7σ (arXiv:2609.25178). BESIII observed \(\eta(2600)\) (arXiv:2609.25158).

Axions and strong CP (steady-to-heating, widening). - Lattice: a second \(\bar g_0\) determination (arXiv:2609.04129). - Detection: high-temperature-superconductor and higher-order-mode haloscopes (arXiv:2609.08144, arXiv:2609.09717). - Cosmology: JWST limits exclude 0.5–2.5 eV QCD-axion dark matter (arXiv:2609.10674). - Late-month benchmark: lattice \(\mathcal{C}_{\rm QCD}=1.965(35)\) (arXiv:2609.22698).

ML and statistics tooling (steady, climbing the stack). - Agentic standards came first: HEPToolBench (arXiv:2608.28232) and versioned scientific operations (arXiv:2609.00107). - Autodiff reached the inference layer: IRIS-HEP blueprint (arXiv:2609.19992). - SBI was then applied to a live physics question (arXiv:2609.26965).

Turning points

  • Pospelov’s solar-capture exclusion (arXiv:2609.02775), generalized by Nguyen (arXiv:2609.11833). This is the constraint every LZ model must now beat.
  • Neutrino-scattering explanations of LZ ruled out (Chattaraj arXiv:2609.10504).
  • Belle II \(R(D^{(*)})\) (arXiv:2609.21947): the first major result of the season to weaken the LFU tension.
  • Atomki reopened. Cosmic muons reproduce the 17 MeV opening-angle peaks (arXiv:2609.18383).
  • Sterile-neutrino preference down to 2.7σ in a joint MiniBooNE+MicroBooNE fit, with the two experiments in internal tension (arXiv:2609.26965).
  • Direct Higgs width \(\Gamma_H<307\) MeV from ATLAS (arXiv:2609.19948).

Open questions

  • Which LZ reading survives? Two decisive tests are pending:
  • an LZ 271–800 keV sideband search (arXiv:2609.26698);
  • argon’s 336 keV line at DEAP-3600 (arXiv:2609.15782).

Annual modulation (McCabe arXiv:2609.04181) is also untested. Heavy multiplets may require FCC-hh or a muon collider (arXiv:2609.26870). - \(|V_{cb}|\): Is missing theory behind the poor inclusive fit? Watch for new correlated moment measurements. - Beauty-strange mass deficit (arXiv:2609.25178): watch for interpretation papers and partner states. - 17 MeV boson: does the beam-off data survive the cosmic-muon reinterpretation (arXiv:2609.18383)? - Collider quantum information: entanglement is being pitched as a BSM probe (arXiv:2609.02691). Against this, standard tomography may fail at NLO (arXiv:2609.25217). - Smaller threads to watch: - toponium vs scalar (arXiv:2609.11641); - \(C_9\) via \(Z'\) (arXiv:2609.15026); - the spread in event-shape \(\alpha_s\) (arXiv:2609.03002); - BBN vs supercooled transitions, new late in the month (arXiv:2609.28348).

The Cross Section — Seasonal Synthesis, Term Ending 2026-08-31

Overview. The term closed with flavor physics as its backbone, a maturing collider quantum-information program, and a tooling revolution in the plumbing. Data-driven flavor ran a full arc — model-building numerology, a mid-August B-physics anomaly flare, then consolidation where nulls firmed but lepton-flavor-universality and charm-CPV tensions refused to die. Two structural shifts reshaped how the field works: collider quantum information graduated from entanglement demonstrations into a CP and “quantum magic” discovery program, and an autodiff/GPU wave went from background hum to the default inference paradigm. Sharpest single results were CMS’s toponium confirmation and a first-principles lattice strong-CP arc. EFT/SMEFT hardened into the standard collider language throughout, and small-system QGP paid off in Run 3 oxygen data.

Running themes

Flavor and CP — the spine, now bifurcated (live). The trajectory ran numerology → data eruption → consolidation. Early CKM-texture/baryogenesis model-building (Bigaran et al., arXiv:2608.05269) gave way to a hard data flare: model-independent \(C_9\) at 4.3–4.8σ (arXiv:2608.12215), the \(B\to PP\) SU(3)\(_F\)-breaking puzzle (arXiv:2608.11298), and six CP phenomena in \(B^+\to K^+\pi^+\pi^-\) (arXiv:2608.12614). Exclusive channels then consolidated toward the SM (\(\phi_s\) arXiv:2608.14119), except exclusive \(|V_{cb}|\) (arXiv:2608.24332), which leaves \(R(D^{(*)})\) with little overlap with data. Net: nulls firm, LFU and charm CPV tensions live.

Collider quantum information — heating, evolving. From deployable toolkit to discovery program: complete \(t\bar t\) density matrices (arXiv:2607.28761), POVM tomography under missing neutrinos (arXiv:2607.28346), tau polarimetry (arXiv:2608.10961), CP-dipole observables to 200 TeV (arXiv:2608.12463), and “quantum magic” in EW scattering (arXiv:2608.19095). Reached nucleon structure via Compton scattering (arXiv:2608.05330).

Autodiff/GPU tooling — now dominant. The autodiff-everything pattern spans event generation, likelihood fitting (RABBIT arXiv:2608.26376), amplitude analysis (CTPWA arXiv:2608.25963), photon transport, cosmology, and two differentiable oscillation engines (MANGO arXiv:2608.13429, Newtrinos.jl). Gradient-based inference is the default bet.

EFT/SMEFT — steady, hardening. First global top-FCNC fit over all 24 operators (arXiv:2608.19547), NLL EWPO across 210 coefficients (arXiv:2608.10064), FCC-ee projections. Diboson/top searches now report EFT combinations by default.

Small-system QGP — paid off. Run 3 oxygen data landed (\(\Upsilon\) suppression arXiv:2608.00182, charm \(v_2\) splitting), culminating in CMS’s baryon-to-meson enhancement in O–O (arXiv:2608.18256) — deconfinement in a genuinely small system.

Turning points

  • CMS single-lepton toponium confirmation (arXiv:2608.19546) — 6.1σ, moving the \(t\bar t\)-threshold excess to two channels; interpretation contested (NRQCD pseudoscalar vs 2HDM CP-odd Higgs).
  • The mid-August B-anomaly flare — unanticipated, the term’s most argue-worthy data.
  • Strong-CP made concrete — nEDM (arXiv:2608.22587) plus lattice \(\tilde g_{\pi NN}\) (arXiv:2608.23685) put strong CP on first-principles footing.
  • Higgs self-coupling matured — quartic entered phenomenology (arXiv:2608.16998); \(\kappa_3\) bound recalibrated 6.1→4.9 (arXiv:2608.25705).
  • ATLAS Run 2 Higgs combination (arXiv:2608.07332) — now theory-limited, closing the incremental-update era.

Open questions

  • *R(D)/LFU tension (arXiv:2608.24332) — persists against a consolidating null landscape; the flavor follow-up to watch.
  • Do the \(C_9\)/\(B\to PP\) anomalies survive? Two sectors, same direction.
  • Toponium vs elementary scalar — bound state or top-philic CP-odd Higgs?
  • CMB birefringence at 4.8σ (3.5σ post-dust, arXiv:2608.06480) — axion Chern-Simons if real.
  • Charm CPV — SM still short (arXiv:2608.21212).
  • Graviton quantumness — three independent no-gos (arXiv:2607.27313, arXiv:2608.08803, arXiv:2608.17015); the wall holds.
  • Unclosed LHC excesses — 13.6-GeV diphoton, 146-GeV \(e\mu\); plus a look-elsewhere reckoning (arXiv:2608.19764, arXiv:2608.24872).

Recurring references
