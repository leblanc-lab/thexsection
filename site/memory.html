The Cross Section — Week Ending 2026-07-23

This week ran on method more than discovery: the field spent it building tools, sharpening systematics, and squeezing precision out of hard channels. Two experimental turning points bookended it — NA62’s golden-mode kaon measurement (mid-week) and a pair of polarization results from ATLAS and BESIII (Thursday) — while the connective tissue was a steady diet of dark-matter detection proposals, first-order-transition/GW modeling, and a maturing machine-learning thread that has shifted from reconstructing physics to learning it. Detector hardware reasserted itself twice, and the UHE-neutrino anomaly finally got a number.

Running themes

ML that discovers, not just reconstructs — heating. The month’s ML thread took a conceptual leap. After LQCDMaster’s agentic lattice workflows (arXiv:2607.15001, Jul 17), ShellFlow (arXiv:2607.16144, Jul 20) recovered W/Z/top masses and the Weinberg angle from raw ATLAS open data with only two physics priors; JetCoRD (arXiv:2607.15531) achieved the first cross-experiment tagger distillation; and SymbolFit (arXiv:2607.19750, Jul 23) rediscovered published dijet fit functions via symbolic regression. The trajectory is toward models that extract structure autonomously.

Dark-matter detection diversification — steady, now with limits attached. A month-long “new target class” mood matured from proposals into bounds. Early-week exclusions (DM–neutrino scattering, arXiv:2607.15020; N-naturalness tower, arXiv:2607.14243) gave way to novel targets (carbon-nanotube plasmons arXiv:2607.15338; Rydberg-atom hidden-photon detection arXiv:2607.15368) and finally to QUALIPHIDE’s 13 meV MKID array (arXiv:2607.19319, Jul 22) setting the strongest hidden-photon limits in its band. The Earth-as-transducer review (arXiv:2607.16342) anchors the ultralight end.

Phase transitions & GW backgrounds — steady, systematics-dominated. The recurring message: GW “signals” hinge on poorly controlled non-equilibrium inputs. Wall-friction thermal-mass treatment (arXiv:2607.14867), cosmic-string reach degraded 10⁵× by foregrounds (arXiv:2607.15219), the fluid-ansatz-vs-WallGo discrepancy (arXiv:2607.18207), and the daisy-resummation verdict for supercooled transitions (arXiv:2607.18233) all sharpen the microphysics-to-spectrum map rather than deliver signals. Late week added model-building (domain walls, stasis) atop the systematics drumbeat.

Hadron spectroscopy & QCD structure — steady. Heavy-flavor spectroscopy consolidated around LHCb’s recent Σ_c states (HQET sum rules, arXiv:2607.17923), the Tcc pole re-examined with proper left-hand-cut treatment (arXiv:2607.19009), and glueball claims: N_f=1 lattice mixing (arXiv:2607.17294, Jul 21) and BESIII’s X(2370)-as-lightest-0⁻⁺-glueball argument (arXiv:2607.20366, Jul 23). The EIC-facing structure program (proton gluon-cloud shape, arXiv:2607.14955) ran strong early, fading by week’s end to lattice gravitational form factors (arXiv:2607.20337).

Quantum information at colliders — steady. Now ordinary vocabulary: three collider entanglement papers Jul 17 (arXiv:2607.15153, arXiv:2607.14558), and DIS entanglement entropy against H1 data (arXiv:2607.18464, Jul 22).

Turning points

  • NA62’s K⁺→π⁺νν̄ at 20% precision, SM-consistent (arXiv:2607.16413, Jul 21) — immediately spawned the Blanke et al. phenomenology sequel (arXiv:2607.19055) mapping what room remains for KOTO II’s K_L→π⁰νν̄ (tenfold enhancement still allowed with new phases + right-handed current).
  • COMPASS/JPAC first high-energy observation of an exotic Reggeon (arXiv:2607.17850, Jul 21), >5σ, tied to π₁(1600) — a production-side foothold for the spin-exotic program.
  • UHE-neutrino anomaly quantified (arXiv:2607.19487, Jul 23) — joint ANITA-IV/KM3NeT/IceCube fit puts diffuse-flux tension at 7.5σ, rare transients at 5.9σ, hardening the KM3-230213A puzzle.
  • ATLAS longitudinal-Z in WZjj-EW at 4.0σ (arXiv:2607.20246, Jul 23) — direct sequel to the July 15 ZZ polarization milestone; the EWSB polarization program is now a genuine thread.
  • BESIII’s secondary-scattering polarimetry (arXiv:2607.19927 / arXiv:2607.19948, Jul 23) — turns a general spectrometer into a large-acceptance polarimeter; method with broad reuse value.

Open questions

  • UHE-neutrino tension — 7.5σ for diffuse flux, unresolved by any standard population; watch for exotic-source or systematics follow-ups.
  • KOTO II room — how much K_L→π⁰νν̄ enhancement survives the SM-consistent K⁺ result.
  • Phase-transition systematics — daisy-resummation error swamps LISA reconstruction for supercooled models; can the universe even be matter-dominated post-transition (arXiv:2607.19469 says not immediately)?
  • Glueball identity — is X(2370) truly the lightest 0⁻⁺ glueball? Cross-check against the N_f=1 mixing result.
  • Tcc pole character — pion-exchange treatment turns the pole complex; predicted to move toward the real axis at physical mₚᵢ.
  • Neutrino energy reconstruction — interaction-model-dependent calorimetric bias (arXiv:2607.20419) adds to the ongoing generator-spread anxiety.

Rolling Synthesis — Week Ending 2026-07-17

After a fortnight dominated by theory and precision-machinery, the week closed with experiment and hardware quietly reasserting themselves. No single collaboration bombshell landed, but several trajectories sharpened: the dark sector visibly pivoted from model-building to constraining; quantum information matured from novelty into a working collider vocabulary; real-time reconstruction crossed into production; and cosmology got its first refereed tournament of Hubble-tension fixes. The connective tissue all week was instrumentalization — neutrinos, spin states, gravitational-wave polarizations, and open data all treated as tools rather than objects.

Running themes

  • Dark sector: from building to bounding (turning). Early-week diversification (majorons, quark pellets, heavy charged relics, Dark Phoenix, reheating-set relics) gave way by week’s end to a constraining mood — DM–neutrino scattering, boosted-DM attenuation, PBH minispikes. The candidate zoo is still widening, but the exclusionary papers now outnumber the inventive ones.
  • Axions, fanning out (heating). The strongest sustained thread. Every mass window and philosophy appeared: merged-photon LHC bounds, gluon-only invisible QCD axions, a charm-coupled GeV axion tying isospin and quality problems together, ultralight coherence probes, and — mid-week — axions/inflatons asked to carry cosmological loads (dark energy, leptogenesis). Nobody is converging.
  • Quantum information at colliders (heating, consolidating). Flagged as emerging July 15, consolidated by July 17. Spin states, entanglement, concurrence, CHSH now appear as ordinary observables in ττγ, Z′-mediated tt̄, and ALP decays. arXiv:2607.12015 (Cheng et al., FSR decoheres or enhances entanglement) recurred across both days.
  • Flavor precision (steady, shifting focus). Moved from the b→sℓℓ / C₉ long-distance debate (July 13) toward CKM structure and QED systematics (July 16): a b-column rescaling blind spot where ε_K leads, and QED-corrected B→τν.
  • Instrumentation / real-time reconstruction (heating). Bookended the week: Belle II’s GNN calorimeter trigger commissioned (July 13), LHCb’s parameterised Kalman filter in production on 500 GPUs plus a dense ITk/irradiation hardware cluster (July 17). The ML- and firmware-trigger wave is reaching production.
  • AI as collaborator (steady undercurrent). Progression from LLM-auditing and ML-verification (VERaiPHY) toward agentic systems handed the keyboard (LQCDMaster, arXiv:2607.15001).

Turning points

  • H₀ World Cup (arXiv:2607.13282, arXiv:2607.13283): fourteen tension solutions, one framework — early dark energy and early modified gravity win, varying electron mass intermediate, late-time and enhanced-radiation scenarios fail. The reference bake-off going forward; Paper II’s robustness gauntlet is what model-builders will cite.
  • N-naturalness benchmark killed (arXiv:2607.14243): first experimental search for the N-naturalness neutrino tower excludes the N=10⁴ GUT-unification benchmark for Majorana neutrinos — the scenario’s tidy selling point gone.
  • Strong CP, computed and questioned (July 14): a high-precision lattice neutron-EDM coefficient (arXiv:2607.10606) landed the same morning as Williams’ argument (arXiv:2607.10272) that the problem is contingent on optional global topology — sharpening tools and questioning premises at once.
  • C₉ pulled back toward SM (arXiv:2607.09458, Bordone et al.): data-driven dispersive B→K*μμ reading finds ≤2σ tension once long-distance effects are data-constrained — continuing Ciuchini et al.’s July 3 hadronic-effects argument.
  • AGN-spike bounds deflated (arXiv:2607.08904, Farzan): neutrino-DM attenuation limits shown to hinge on mediator mass, landing the same day as a paper deriving flavor signals from the same physics.
  • Strongest DM–neutrino scattering bound (arXiv:2607.15020, Mosbech et al.): hydro-simulated Lyman-α data excludes earlier hints.
  • LISA cosmic-string realism check (arXiv:2607.15219): astrophysical foregrounds degrade tension reach by ~10⁵ in Gμ.

Open questions to watch

  • Belle II B⁺→K⁺νν excess: LNV SMEFT can’t explain it without fine-tuning unless a light right-handed neutrino is added (arXiv:2607.13142) — watch for follow-ups.
  • HVP / muon g−2: still unsettled; a Dyson-Schwinger determination (arXiv:2607.12918, 710.0×10⁻¹⁰) now agrees with lattice — is the continuum consensus firming?
  • b→sℓℓ: is the residual C₉ pull genuinely hadronic, or does a small short-distance shift survive?
  • Strong CP: is it even a problem? Williams’ contingency claim awaits response.
  • Neutron-star hyperon puzzle: whether neutrino-induced hyperon FSI at SBND/DUNE can survive YN systematics (Nowak trilogy).
  • T2K/Hyper-K systematics: no current generator describes carbon–oxygen kinematic-imbalance data across phase space.
  • Extra GW polarizations: a fresh modified-gravity coalescence (Weyl, GQFT, defect networks) betting on polarimetry — watch whether it persists.
  • KM3NeT 220 PeV event: GUT-scale relic-decay interpretation (arXiv:2607.10760).

week

2026-W28

This week ending July 9, 2026, was defined by a transition from speculative modeling toward foundational infrastructure and joint, high-precision frameworks. Prompted by sobering reminders of the ultra-long timelines of next-generation facilities (arXiv:2607.08131), the community focused on rebuilding its primary tools from first principles. This shift was evident across all scales: from raw calorimeter cell-level machine learning and joint PDF+SMEFT fits at the High-Luminosity LHC, to the rigorous dissection of degeneracies in the PMNS neutrino mixing matrix and the deployment of new three-dimensional cosmic backgrounds.

Running themes

  • Machine Learning Transitions from Calibration to Raw Reconstruction: A major paradigm shift is underway. Within days of ATLAS presenting the performance of its transformer-based GN2 flavor-tagger (arXiv:2607.05322), they deployed and calibrated it on Run 3 data (arXiv:2607.06199). This transformer wave immediately spread to electromagnetic calorimetry, where researchers bypassed high-level shower shapes to process raw cell-level signals (arXiv:2607.08175), achieving direct invariant mass regression for collimated diphotons.
  • The Breakdown of Isolated SMEFT and QCD Factorization: It is no longer viable to treat standard inputs and new physics independently. The field is realizing that PDF profiling can accidentally “absorb” high-mass SMEFT signals, highlighting the urgent need for joint fits (arXiv:2607.07791). Similarly, sub-percent precision demands that standard QCD factorization be replaced by joint QCD+QED frameworks utilizing Lepton Distribution Functions (arXiv:2607.07811).
  • Neutrino Degeneracies and CP Mimics: As JUNO, DUNE, and Hyper-Kamiokande approach operation, the community is deeply concerned with “fake” signals. Non-unitary PMNS mixing (arXiv:2607.06108) or Lorentz-invariance violation (arXiv:2607.06513) can mimic or degrade CP asymmetries. Breaking these degeneracies requires exact analytical resummation schemes (arXiv:2607.07213) and combined multi-detector fits.
  • Evasion Strategies for Cornered WIMPs and Axions: Under pressure from LZ direct-detection limits, which have severely cornered traditional natural higgsino dark matter (arXiv:2607.05648), theorists are pivoting to unified discrete symmetries to naturally solve the MSSM \(\mu\) problem while yielding viable DFSZ axions (arXiv:2607.07921).

Turning points

  • First Direct Bounds on Triple Higgs Coupling Modifier Space: The CMS Collaboration’s search for nonresonant \(HHH \to 6b\) (arXiv:2607.05145) directly excluded regions of the \((\kappa_3, \kappa_4)\) coupling space for the first time, replacing theoretical perturbative unitarity bounds with experimental data.
  • Resolution of the \(b\)-Baryon Lifetime Tension: Calculations of the NNLO QCD corrections to \(b\)-quark decay within the Heavy Quark Expansion (arXiv:2607.06538) dramatically reduced theoretical uncertainties, resolving a decades-long tension between predicted and measured \(b\)-baryon lifetime ratios.
  • Neutron-Capture Calorimetry: The demonstration that gadolinium-loaded liquid scintillators can capture delayed neutrons from hadronic showers (arXiv:2607.08587) cut energy resolution from 21.8% to 13.3%, offering a hardware solution to the problem of invisible hadronic energy fluctuations.

Open questions

  • The Fermi-LAT 20 GeV Galactic Halo Excess vs. Dwarfs: A rigorous pixel-level likelihood analysis with full PSF forward folding (arXiv:2607.08552) has confirmed the 20 GeV excess in 15 years of Fermi-LAT data. However, its WIMP interpretation remains in \(4\text{--}5\times\) tension with dwarf spheroidal limits. Will low-velocity-enhanced (Sommerfeld or Breit-Wigner) annihilation models be sufficient to reconcile this gap?
  • The Relic Neutrino Wind Exposure Penalty: Theoretical calculations have shown that observing the dipolar anisotropy of the Cosmic Neutrino Background via tritium \(\beta\)-decay requires a massive \(10^5\) exposure penalty relative to observing the isotropic flux, with Majorana signals highly suppressed (arXiv:2607.05221). Can any planned tritium experiment scale to meet this threshold?

Recurring references

  • arXiv:2607.05322 & arXiv:2607.06199 — The development, deployment, and calibration of the transformer-based GN2 flavor-tagging algorithm at ATLAS.
  • arXiv:2607.05648 & arXiv:2607.07921 — Baer et al.’s analysis of LZ limits cornering light higgsinos, and their subsequent resolution utilizing discrete \(Z_n^R\) symmetries to generate an accidental Peccei-Quinn symmetry.
  • arXiv:2607.06108 — The benchmark paper detailing how PMNS non-unitarity mimics CP violation and requires joint DUNE + Hyper-K analyses to resolve.
