The Cross Section — Rolling Synthesis, week ending 2026-08-21

Overview. The week opened on flavor and heavy ions and closed on a collider bang. Its defining event is CMS’s 6.1σ confirmation of a \(t\bar t\)-threshold excess in the single-lepton channel (arXiv:2608.19546), giving the toponium/near-threshold story two independent channels. Underneath the day-to-day, three methodological currents kept resurfacing: EFT hardening into the default collider language, a new family of jet-free precision observables built from conserved currents, and the collider quantum-information program pushing past entanglement. Flavor spent the week consolidating rather than breaking, and a season-long ML-methodology reckoning kept accreting community entries.

Running themes

Toponium / \(t\bar t\)-threshold excess — heating, now the marquee thread. CMS’s single-lepton confirmation (arXiv:2608.19546), using relative velocity plus spin/parity angular observables, cross-checks the earlier dilepton excess as a color-singlet pseudoscalar. Interpretation is already contested: Matsuoka (arXiv:2608.19651) reads it as a top-philic Type-III 2HDM CP-odd Higgs and finds no viable baryogenesis branch. Two readings, one bump.

EFT as default language — steady, hardening. The 08-20 CMS aTGC merged-jet bounds (arXiv:2608.18245) and the 08-21 first global top-FCNC fit over all 24 SMEFT operators (arXiv:2608.19547) mark diboson and top searches converging on EFT combinations. Tooling followed: basis-independent two-loop four-fermion running (arXiv:2608.19316).

Jet-free observables from conserved currents — emerging, worth naming. Zhang et al.’s one-point charge correlator, a jet-free reformulation of the EW forward-backward asymmetry from DELPHI LEP-1 open data (arXiv:2608.14779, arXiv:2608.15357), set the template; projected energy correlators against \(Z/\gamma\) (arXiv:2608.14793) and Ke et al.’s Gell-Mann–Nishijima jet-flow relation (arXiv:2608.19708) share the logic — precision from currents, not jet algorithms. The archival-data route is itself part of the point.

Collider quantum information — steady, moving beyond entanglement. Entanglement entries (gluon-pair Bell states arXiv:2608.17685; Bethe–Heitler GHZ/W states arXiv:2608.18030) gave way to “quantum magic” in EW scattering, with the weak mixing angle again near a magic-minimizing value (arXiv:2608.19095), and tripartite entanglement selecting gauge/diffeo-invariant theories (arXiv:2608.18732).

Flavor — consolidating, not tensioning. After a mid-week \(C_9\) flare, LHCb’s SM-consistent \(\phi_s\) (arXiv:2608.14119), the completing leading-power factorization piece for \(b\to s\ell\ell\) (arXiv:2608.14341), and a kaon global fit (arXiv:2608.14474) all pointed toward control. Separately, flavor-mixing numerology (Koide from topological condensation arXiv:2608.19277; TBM-to-Cabibbo deformation arXiv:2608.19691) returned — parametrization staking out falsifiable numbers.

Turning points

  • CMS single-lepton toponium confirmation (arXiv:2608.19546) — moved the threshold excess from single-channel curiosity to two-channel signal.
  • CMS baryon-to-meson enhancement in O–O (arXiv:2608.18256) — the season’s small-system-flow program paid off with a deconfinement signature in a genuinely small system.
  • Quartic Higgs self-coupling enters phenomenology — first HL-LHC four-Higgs (8b) study bounding \(c_3,d_4\) (arXiv:2608.16998), territory single/double-Higgs rates can’t reach.
  • Third graviton-quantumness no-go (arXiv:2608.17015, after Bao et al. and Miyauchi et al.) — the wall against detectable graviton squeezing keeps holding.

Open questions

  • Toponium vs elementary scalar: is the \(t\bar t\)-threshold enhancement NRQCD pseudoscalar bound state or a 2HDM CP-odd Higgs? Watch further channels.
  • Two unclosed LHC excesses: the \(3.5\sigma\) CMS 13.6-GeV diphoton (ALP fit, arXiv:2608.18506) and the 146-GeV \(e\mu\) excess (arXiv:2608.18738) — both have concrete follow-up strategies proposed.
  • Gravitational parity violation claimed from HERA Compton-polarimeter spectra (arXiv:2608.14703) — extraordinary; awaiting scrutiny.
  • Hadronic-asymmetry tensions: does the LEP one-point charge correlator (arXiv:2608.15357) offer a new handle?
  • Absolute neutrino mass in oscillations (arXiv:2608.13736) — surprising NLO claim; JUNO sensitivity to a novel mass combination.
  • Trials-factor accounting for BSM resonance searches (arXiv:2608.19764) — a caution flag over how excesses get counted.

Recurring references

2026-W33

The flavor front dominated the week, swinging from neutrino mixing numerology early on to a data-side eruption of \(B\)-physics anomalies and CP-violation discoveries by week’s end. Two precision benchmarks anchored the collider side (ATLAS’s full Run 2 Higgs combination, FCC-ee projections), while the collider-QI/tomography program matured from entanglement toward CP observables. Neutrino systematics visibly began tightening, and a provocative \(4.8\sigma\) CMB-birefringence hint opened the week as the field’s most argue-worthy unresolved number.

Running themes

Flavor and B anomalies — heating up sharply. The week opened on neutrino/flavor model-building: Barger’s \(\pi/12\) model with a falsifiable \(\delta=272^\circ\) (arXiv:2608.08394) and his broader commensurate quark-lepton mixing (arXiv:2608.10312), plus seesaw texture constructions. Then the weight swung decisively to data: LHCb’s model-independent \(C_9\) from transversity amplitudes at 4.3–4.8σ (arXiv:2608.12215) and Bouchard et al.’s \(B\to PP\) fit demanding ~1000% SU(3)\(_F\) breaking (arXiv:2608.11298) — two independent anomalies in the same direction. This culminated 08-14 in LHCb’s three-paper \(B^+\to K^+\pi^+\pi^-\) harvest (arXiv:2608.12612, arXiv:2608.12613, arXiv:2608.12614), establishing six CP-violation phenomena at once, including first evidence in wave interference and a spin-3 resonance.

Neutrino systematics and tooling — steady, consolidating. The DUNE cross-section warning (Puhan et al., arXiv:2608.08609: model choice shifts CP/octant sensitivity 25–30%) found a partial answer days later in NOvA’s most-precise \(\bar\nu_\mu\)–H CCQE cross section (arXiv:2608.12293). Two differentiable oscillation engines appeared the same day — MANGO (arXiv:2608.13429) and Newtrinos.jl (arXiv:2608.12940) — betting on gradient-based inference as the new default.

Collider-QI tomography — evolving from entanglement to CP. The season-long quantum-information thread became a deployable toolkit: TauPolaris’s normalizing-flow tau polarimetry with \(4.3\sigma\) entanglement discrimination in \(H\to\tau\tau\) (arXiv:2608.10961), then Perelstein et al. turning top-pair spin tomography into optimal CP-dipole observables reaching 40–200 TeV (arXiv:2608.12463).

SMEFT/precision benchmarks — steady. ATLAS’s full Run 2 Higgs combination (arXiv:2608.07332, now signal-theory-limited) set a reference; Haisch et al.’s NLL SMEFT EWPO across all 210 coefficients (arXiv:2608.10064) and FCC-ee reaching 4 MeV Higgs-mass precision (arXiv:2608.12071) extended the thread toward future \(e^+e^-\) global fits.

Dark matter — model-building pile-up plus steady hardware. Weak-scale DM revisited economically late in the week (singlet-doublet across all production mechanisms, arXiv:2608.12480); axion/haloscope hardware and detection-methods clusters ran throughout without a single decisive result.

Turning points

  • ATLAS Higgs combination (arXiv:2608.07332) closed the incremental \(HH\)/\(tH\) updates the memory tracked and shifted the leading uncertainty to theory.
  • The flavor axis flipped from parametrization/numerology (Barger) to hard data-side tension (08-13 to 08-14). Nothing in recent memory anticipated an anomaly flare-up of this size.
  • NOvA’s cross section began pinning down the very inputs behind the DUNE systematics warning.
  • The quantum-GW no-go converged: Miyauchi et al. (arXiv:2608.08803) extended Bao et al. (arXiv:2607.27313) from haloscopes to gravity — the second no-go in two weeks, closing on a wall from multiple directions.

Open questions

  • Will the \(C_9\) (arXiv:2608.12215) and \(B\to PP\) (arXiv:2608.11298) anomalies survive scrutiny? Two independent sectors, same direction — the follow-up story to watch.
  • CMB birefringence at \(4.8\sigma\) (3.5σ post-dust, arXiv:2608.06480) — does it survive systematics? Points at an axion-like Chern-Simons coupling if real.
  • Barger’s falsifiable \(\delta_{\rm CP}\) and \(\varepsilon_2\) predictions await Hyper-K/DUNE resolution.
  • Nowak et al.’s predicted narrow \(1^+\) beauty-strange state at \(5747\pm2\) MeV (arXiv:2608.07268) — a sharp spectroscopy follow-up.
  • ALICE’s ultra-central \(v_3/v_2\) puzzle (arXiv:2608.11199) remains unresolved in the small-system/transport program.
  • Muon EDM limit (\(|d_\mu|<1.1\times10^{-19}\,e\,\)cm, arXiv:2608.11124) — a clean null, benchmark for future improvement.

Overview. A theory-weighted week whose spine was flavor and CP structure: a late-July CKM-texture wave matured into a full baryogenesis cluster, culminating in a provocative claim that CKM phases alone might suffice. The collider quantum-information program kept metastasizing into new final states, reaching all the way down to low-energy Compton scattering. Running themes

Flavor/CP structure → baryogenesis (heating up). The strongest through-line. The CKM-texture wave (Singh’s Jordan-algebra skeleton arXiv:2608.01445, SU(15) composites arXiv:2608.00166, radiative-mass models arXiv:2607.29331) fed into a three-paper baryogenesis cluster on the final day: Bigaran et al.’s MFV-leptoquark claim of baryogenesis from CKM phases alone (arXiv:2608.05269), axion-driven spontaneous leptogenesis (arXiv:2608.05279), and dim-8 CP operators in sphalerogenesis (arXiv:2608.05950). Watch whether the CKM claim survives scrutiny.

Collider quantum information (heating up / spreading). Now a fully established program, migrating final state by final state: from Erdogan et al.’s complete one-loop \(t\bar t\) density matrix as a top-Yukawa CP probe (arXiv:2607.28761) and DIS tensor-charge access (arXiv:2607.28724), to a POVM proof of what’s recoverable under missing neutrinos (arXiv:2607.28346), to BESIII hyperon pairs (arXiv:2608.03131), and finally to photon-nucleon entanglement in Compton scattering with a no-go theorem and neutron-polarizability control (arXiv:2608.05330). The program has now reached low-energy nucleon structure.

Small-system QGP / Run 3 oxygen data (heating up). The freshest experimental thread. LHCb’s ~2× \(\Upsilon(2S)\) suppression in OO (arXiv:2608.00182), ALICE’s charm baryon-meson \(v_2\) splitting (arXiv:2608.02161) and strangeness enhancement (arXiv:2608.02874), and ATLAS’s first forward-rapidity jet suppression — stronger than central (arXiv:2608.03261). ALICE’s three-proton femtoscopy as an effective 3→3 scattering experiment (arXiv:2608.05708) extends the small-system toolkit.

Muon collider as neutrino factory (new). Two coincident re-evaluations (Burk et al. arXiv:2608.02714; Choi et al.’s MINT MC arXiv:2608.02718) find forward neutrino fluxes ~2 orders of magnitude above prior estimates at 10 TeV — an accidental neutrino-physics case for an energy-frontier machine. Persisted into 06 in a minor key (top-partner study arXiv:2608.04399).

Hadron spectroscopy (steady, high volume). The reliable numerical bulk. Anchored by real data — BESIII’s charged \(X(3790)^-\) at 5.3σ (arXiv:2607.29138) — and organized by Brambilla et al.’s BOEFT survey of near-threshold quarkoniumlike states (arXiv:2608.04105), likely to become a reference multiplet assignment.

Turning points

  • Ghosh et al.’s doubly-charged-Higgs recast (arXiv:2608.03988) — argues the ATLAS four-lepton efficiency exceeds a strict bound, shifting the mass limit down ~100 GeV. Cite the original number with care.
  • Material-agnostic DM-electron ceiling (arXiv:2608.05282) — a first-principles upper bound on scattering rates for the entire direct-detection program.
  • DUNE’s first Jarlskog-invariant sensitivity (arXiv:2608.04059) — parametrization-independent leptonic CP, plus a sharp θ23-octant gain under a reactor θ13 prior.
  • CMS semivisible-jet / \(Z'\to t\bar t\) limits (arXiv:2608.05323, arXiv:2608.01251) — SM-consistent, tightening BSM mediator space.

  • How small can QGP be? Sequential melting and flow now appearing in OO; awaiting more Run 3 OO analyses.
  • The doubly-charged-Higgs mass limit — is the ATLAS efficiency bound (arXiv:2608.03988) accepted?
  • Hubble tension — neutrino distance ladder (arXiv:2608.00923) offers a propagation-systematic-free rung (huge error bars); Bianchi-anisotropy route ruled out (arXiv:2607.29197). New probes, no resolution.

Overview. Running themes

The structure track is newer and speculative: a geometric CKM reparameterization (arXiv:2607.26681) followed a day later by Arkani-Hamed et al.’s \(\pi/8\) Yukawa textures (arXiv:2607.27315) — two swings at the same empirical pattern, the latter with falsifiable unitarity-triangle predictions.

Building past LQCDMaster (July 17), the week added amplitude-as-program search (805× speedup, arXiv:2607.21629), SMEFT-Pheno-Agent’s full MadGraph→Delphes chain (arXiv:2607.22331), agentic SFitter recasting (arXiv:2607.22813), and CLVisc Agent for viscous-hydro heavy-ion runs (arXiv:2607.27822). CMS set a new mass benchmark, \(m_H=125.13\pm0.15\) GeV (arXiv:2607.28396).

Turning points

  • \(B\to VV\) pushes charmless SU(3)\(_F\) past \(7\sigma\) (arXiv:2607.27202) — a hadronic-amplitude anomaly with deliberately minimal theory input, a distinct corner from the FCNC modes the week circled.
  • The GW-quantumness conversation flipped in one day. Blas et al.’s coherent-GW\(\to\gamma\gamma\) decay claim (arXiv:2607.24930) was answered by Bao et al.’s general no-go (arXiv:2607.27313), closing off “quantized gravity from GWs” proposals.
  • IceCube’s 5.7σ Galactic-plane neutrino emission (arXiv:2607.25966) — the constructive counterpart to the ongoing UHE ANITA/KM3NeT-vs-IceCube tension.

  • The Hubble tension stayed hard: Roy Choudhury’s \(6.85\sigma\) (\(H_0=73.3\)) survived the corrected Pantheon+/DES reanalysis (arXiv:2607.24443).

  • Small-scale-structure DM: a ~2.4σ dwarf central-density diversity tension with CDM (arXiv:2607.27316).
