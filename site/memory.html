The Cross Section — Rolling Synthesis, week ending 2026-08-11

A theory-and-methods-heavy week bookended by two precision benchmarks and threaded throughout by the neutrino sector. The field kept circling three durable programs — collider/quantum-information entanglement, the “can we detect quantized gravity” no-go, and the origin of the baryon asymmetry — while opening a genuinely new thread: the muon collider reconsidered as an accidental neutrino factory. Underneath, axions maintained their steady uncoordinated spread, heavy-ion physics pressed the “how small can QGP be” question, and hadron spectroscopy supplied the daily numerical ballast. Two reference documents (the ATLAS Run 2 Higgs combination, a 4.8σ cosmic-birefringence hint) landed mid-week and will anchor future citations.

Running themes

  • Collider quantum information — heating, still spreading. The entanglement/tomography program kept reaching new final states: from \(t\bar t\) (Erdogan, earlier) to hyperons at BESIII (arXiv:2608.03131), to invisible-final-state spin tomography via a POVM argument (arXiv:2607.28346), and — its most novel turn — to photon-nucleon entanglement in Compton scattering, with neutron polarizabilities controlling whether entanglement exists (arXiv:2608.05330). The program is migrating from energy frontier to low-energy nucleon structure.

  • Quantum nature of gravity/GW — converging on a wall. A second no-go in two weeks: Miyauchi et al. (arXiv:2608.08803) extended the coarse-grained-mode obstruction of Bao et al. (arXiv:2607.27313) from haloscopes to gravitational waves, finding a detector-side squeezing loophole that graviton coupling throttles. Multiple independent arguments now point at the same limit.

  • Neutrino sector — dominant, methods-forward. Repeated emphasis that systematics rival oscillation physics: DUNE’s Bayesian reframing and first Jarlskog-invariant sensitivity (arXiv:2608.04059), detector-motion spectroscopy (arXiv:2608.04091), and the warning that cross-section model choice shifts DUNE CP/octant sensitivity 25–30% (arXiv:2608.08609). Flavor model-building stayed active into the week’s end.

  • Baryogenesis/leptogenesis — a live cluster. Three mechanisms circled the same target on 08-07; the provocation is Bigaran et al.’s claim that CKM CP violation alone, via an MFV leptoquark, can generate the asymmetry (arXiv:2608.05269). Continued into 08-10 (Fodroci et al., arXiv:2608.06983).

  • Axions — steady, diffuse. Every day carried several: lattice histories, thermal/astrophysical bounds (\(g_{ae}<3.8\times10^{-14}\)), PQ-scale/seesaw unification (arXiv:2608.05150), a real photonic-bandgap haloscope (arXiv:2608.07718), and small-instanton breaking of the \(m_a\)–\(g_{a\gamma\gamma}\) relation (arXiv:2608.08602).

Turning points

  • ATLAS full Run 2 Higgs combination (arXiv:2608.07332) — the new reference for couplings, \(\kappa_\lambda=1.3^{+3.1}_{-1.6}\), \(\Gamma_H=3.6\) MeV, and a first running \(b\)-mass extraction. Notably now signal-theory-limited; closes the incremental \(HH\)/\(tH\) updates.
  • Cosmic birefringence at 4.8σ (3.5σ post-dust) from joint ACT DR6 + Planck DR4 (arXiv:2608.06480) — the week’s most argue-worthy hint, pointing at an axion-like Chern-Simons coupling if it survives.
  • de Vries et al. one-loop SMEFT RG for \(0\nu\beta\beta\) (arXiv:2608.07657) — operator mixing now yields leading bounds on many dim-7 operators, beating meson decays.
  • ATLAS first forward-rapidity jet \(R_{AA}\) (arXiv:2608.03261) — stronger suppression than central; a new handle on energy-loss geometry.
  • Ghosh et al. recast (arXiv:2608.03988) — shifts the ATLAS doubly-charged-Higgs mass limit down ~100 GeV; check before quoting.

Open questions

  • Does the cosmic-birefringence signal survive systematics, and is the Chern-Simons/axion reading forced?
  • Can SM CKM CP violation alone drive baryogenesis? Bigaran et al.’s counterexample needs scrutiny.
  • Falsifiable predictions to watch: Barger’s \(\pi/12\) model pins \(\delta=272^\circ\pm2^\circ\) with an opposite-sign \(\varepsilon_2\), decidable by Hyper-K at 3σ (arXiv:2608.08394); Nowak et al. predict a narrow \(1^+\) beauty-strange state at \(5747\pm2\) MeV (arXiv:2608.07268).
  • How small can QGP be? OO strangeness enhancement (arXiv:2608.02874) extends the small-system question opened by LHCb \(\Upsilon\) and ALICE charm \(v_2\).
  • Is the quantum-GW/graviton “wall” truly firm, or does the detector-squeezing loophole leave room?
  • The muon-collider neutrino flux: two groups now agree it’s ~2 orders above prior estimates — does the neutrino-physics case reshape a machine sold as energy-frontier?

Recurring references

  • arXiv:2607.27313 — Bao et al., graviton-detection no-go (anchor for the GW-quantization thread).
  • arXiv:2607.28346 — Ai et al., POVM spin tomography with missing neutrinos.
  • arXiv:2608.02718 — Choi et al., MINT muon-collider forward-neutrino simulation.
  • arXiv:2608.05269 — Bigaran et al., CKM/MFV baryogenesis counterexample.
  • arXiv:2608.07332 — ATLAS Run 2 Higgs combination.
  • arXiv:2608.06480 — Eskilt, ACT+Planck cosmic birefringence.

(Background hum, not yet trajectories: a running ML/AI-for-physics thread — SMEFT LLM agents, the archived HEP-ML Living Guide arXiv:2608.09531 — and a phase-transition/GW-spectrum thread on resummation-scheme dependence.)

Rolling Synthesis — Week Ending 2026-08-07

Overview. A theory-weighted week whose spine was flavor and CP structure: a late-July CKM-texture wave matured into a full baryogenesis cluster, culminating in a provocative claim that CKM phases alone might suffice. The collider quantum-information program kept metastasizing into new final states, reaching all the way down to low-energy Compton scattering. Run 3 oxygen-collision data began landing, pushing the “how small can QGP be” question onto real measurements. Two coincident papers opened a new thread — the muon collider as an accidental neutrino factory — and hadron spectroscopy remained the steady numerical ballast underneath everything. Automation/LLM tooling and phase-transition GW work ticked along as background hums.

Running themes

Flavor/CP structure → baryogenesis (heating up). The strongest through-line. The CKM-texture wave (Singh’s Jordan-algebra skeleton arXiv:2608.01445, SU(15) composites arXiv:2608.00166, radiative-mass models arXiv:2607.29331) fed into a three-paper baryogenesis cluster on the final day: Bigaran et al.’s MFV-leptoquark claim of baryogenesis from CKM phases alone (arXiv:2608.05269), axion-driven spontaneous leptogenesis (arXiv:2608.05279), and dim-8 CP operators in sphalerogenesis (arXiv:2608.05950). Watch whether the CKM claim survives scrutiny.

Collider quantum information (heating up / spreading). Now a fully established program, migrating final state by final state: from Erdogan et al.’s complete one-loop \(t\bar t\) density matrix as a top-Yukawa CP probe (arXiv:2607.28761) and DIS tensor-charge access (arXiv:2607.28724), to a POVM proof of what’s recoverable under missing neutrinos (arXiv:2607.28346), to BESIII hyperon pairs (arXiv:2608.03131), and finally to photon-nucleon entanglement in Compton scattering with a no-go theorem and neutron-polarizability control (arXiv:2608.05330). The program has now reached low-energy nucleon structure.

Small-system QGP / Run 3 oxygen data (heating up). The freshest experimental thread. LHCb’s ~2× \(\Upsilon(2S)\) suppression in OO (arXiv:2608.00182), ALICE’s charm baryon-meson \(v_2\) splitting (arXiv:2608.02161) and strangeness enhancement (arXiv:2608.02874), and ATLAS’s first forward-rapidity jet suppression — stronger than central (arXiv:2608.03261). ALICE’s three-proton femtoscopy as an effective 3→3 scattering experiment (arXiv:2608.05708) extends the small-system toolkit.

Muon collider as neutrino factory (new). Two coincident re-evaluations (Burk et al. arXiv:2608.02714; Choi et al.’s MINT MC arXiv:2608.02718) find forward neutrino fluxes ~2 orders of magnitude above prior estimates at 10 TeV — an accidental neutrino-physics case for an energy-frontier machine. Persisted into 06 in a minor key (top-partner study arXiv:2608.04399).

Hadron spectroscopy (steady, high volume). The reliable numerical bulk. Anchored by real data — BESIII’s charged \(X(3790)^-\) at 5.3σ (arXiv:2607.29138) — and organized by Brambilla et al.’s BOEFT survey of near-threshold quarkoniumlike states (arXiv:2608.04105), likely to become a reference multiplet assignment.

Turning points

  • BESIII’s \(X(3790)^-\) (arXiv:2607.29138) — a clean 5.3σ charged charmoniumlike observation extending the exotic-hadron run.
  • Ghosh et al.’s doubly-charged-Higgs recast (arXiv:2608.03988) — argues the ATLAS four-lepton efficiency exceeds a strict bound, shifting the mass limit down ~100 GeV. Cite the original number with care.
  • Material-agnostic DM-electron ceiling (arXiv:2608.05282) — a first-principles upper bound on scattering rates for the entire direct-detection program.
  • DUNE’s first Jarlskog-invariant sensitivity (arXiv:2608.04059) — parametrization-independent leptonic CP, plus a sharp θ23-octant gain under a reactor θ13 prior.
  • CMS semivisible-jet / \(Z'\to t\bar t\) limits (arXiv:2608.05323, arXiv:2608.01251) — SM-consistent, tightening BSM mediator space.

Open questions

  • Can CKM CP violation alone drive baryogenesis? The Bigaran et al. counterexample (arXiv:2608.05269) directly contradicts conventional wisdom — the week’s most argue-worthy claim.
  • How small can QGP be? Sequential melting and flow now appearing in OO; awaiting more Run 3 OO analyses.
  • The doubly-charged-Higgs mass limit — is the ATLAS efficiency bound (arXiv:2608.03988) accepted?
  • Hubble tension — neutrino distance ladder (arXiv:2608.00923) offers a propagation-systematic-free rung (huge error bars); Bianchi-anisotropy route ruled out (arXiv:2607.29197). New probes, no resolution.
  • Phase-transition GW predictions remain scheme-sensitive (nucleation prefactor arXiv:2608.00169; resummation arXiv:2608.04102; wall radiation arXiv:2608.02729).

Recurring references

  • arXiv:2607.28761 — complete \(t\bar t\) density matrix / top-Yukawa CP; QI-program anchor.
  • arXiv:2607.28346 — POVM spin tomography under missing neutrinos.

The Cross Section — Weekly Synthesis, week ending 2026-07-31

Overview. The week’s spine was flavor precision tightening from several directions at once — lattice, charm/kaon measurements, and a hadronic-amplitude anomaly that grew teeth — while a parallel automation wave saw LLM agents move from checking work to orchestrating full pipelines across pheno, fits, and now nuclear theory. Cosmology kept its month-long defect/phase-transition GW drumbeat going, direct-detection experiments consolidated into a genuine solar-neutrino program, and IceCube delivered a Galactic milestone. Two conversations turned sharply: a top-down attempt to explain CKM structure, and a fast theoretical rebuttal to the “quantized gravity from GWs” idea.

Running themes

Flavor precision — heating up, on two tracks. The measurement track tightened the CKM ladder: the first lattice QCD+QED SD correction to \(R_{e/\mu}\) for \(\pi/K\) (arXiv:2607.22358, the SM input PIONEER/NA62 will quote), BESIII’s record \(D\to\pi\ell\nu\) / \(|V_{cd}|\) (arXiv:2607.23945), the NA62 golden mode propagated into \(b\to s\) new physics (arXiv:2607.24494), and a first \(B^0\to K^0_S\tau^+\tau^-\) limit (arXiv:2607.25607). The structure track is newer and speculative: a geometric CKM reparameterization (arXiv:2607.26681) followed a day later by Arkani-Hamed et al.’s \(\pi/8\) Yukawa textures (arXiv:2607.27315) — two swings at the same empirical pattern, the latter with falsifiable unitarity-triangle predictions.

Agents at the console — heating up, spreading. Building past LQCDMaster (July 17), the week added amplitude-as-program search (805× speedup, arXiv:2607.21629), SMEFT-Pheno-Agent’s full MadGraph→Delphes chain (arXiv:2607.22331), agentic SFitter recasting (arXiv:2607.22813), and CLVisc Agent for viscous-hydro heavy-ion runs (arXiv:2607.27822). Reproducibility/audit manifests are the explicit selling point.

Defect & phase-transition GW — steady drumbeat. Global \(\mathbb{Z}_N\) strings (arXiv:2607.21701), global-monopole annihilation (arXiv:2607.22481), CosmoLattice 2.0 (arXiv:2607.24978), GUT-scale biased walls/monopoles (arXiv:2607.27158), and repeated domain-wall spectrum work (arXiv:2607.25073, arXiv:2607.28214) — the tooling is maturing and IR artifacts are being cleaned up.

Direct detection as neutrino observatory — new coalescence. SNuDD (arXiv:2607.22817), invisible-\(\nu_2\)-decay bounds from CEνNS rivaling SNO (arXiv:2607.24584), and a sub-percent solar-flux framework (arXiv:2607.24421). The neutrino floor is now a physics program.

Collider EW/Higgs precision — steady. EFT/aQGC is the default recast language (νSMEFT arXiv:2607.21711, ALP pairs arXiv:2607.21712, ATLAS EW \(\gamma\gamma jj\) at 6.2σ arXiv:2607.25869). Boosted di-\(\tau\) reconstruction, debuting in the July 27 boosted \(H\to\tau\tau\) evidence (arXiv:2607.22193), became standard kit across ATLAS’s July 31 \(\tau\) slate. CMS set a new mass benchmark, \(m_H=125.13\pm0.15\) GeV (arXiv:2607.28396).

Turning points

  • \(B\to VV\) pushes charmless SU(3)\(_F\) past \(7\sigma\) (arXiv:2607.27202) — a hadronic-amplitude anomaly with deliberately minimal theory input, a distinct corner from the FCNC modes the week circled.
  • The GW-quantumness conversation flipped in one day. Blas et al.’s coherent-GW\(\to\gamma\gamma\) decay claim (arXiv:2607.24930) was answered by Bao et al.’s general no-go (arXiv:2607.27313), closing off “quantized gravity from GWs” proposals.
  • IceCube’s 5.7σ Galactic-plane neutrino emission (arXiv:2607.25966) — the constructive counterpart to the ongoing UHE ANITA/KM3NeT-vs-IceCube tension.

Open questions

  • Does ~30% SU(3)-breaking rescue the \(B\to VV\) \(7\sigma\) discrepancy? (flagged by the authors themselves.)
  • The Hubble tension stayed hard: Roy Choudhury’s \(6.85\sigma\) (\(H_0=73.3\)) survived the corrected Pantheon+/DES reanalysis (arXiv:2607.24443).
  • Auger quantified the muon puzzle into a demanded 2.8–4.6× hadronic-energy amplification (arXiv:2607.22323) — watch for hadronic-model responses.
  • Will the \(\pi/8\) / geometric CKM patterns yield testable deviations or fade as numerology?
  • Small-scale-structure DM: a ~2.4σ dwarf central-density diversity tension with CDM (arXiv:2607.27316).

Running themes

  • First-order transitions & GW systematics — steady, now a live methods debate. July 17’s wall-friction thread persisted: Dorsch et al.   • Dark-matter detection diversification — heating, now with real limits. Running themes

  • Machine Learning Transitions from Calibration to Raw Reconstruction: A major paradigm shift is underway. Within days of ATLAS presenting the performance of its transformer-based GN2 flavor-tagger (arXiv:2607.05322), they deployed and calibrated it on Run 3 data (arXiv:2607.06199). This transformer wave immediately spread to electromagnetic calorimetry, where researchers bypassed high-level shower shapes to process raw cell-level signals (arXiv:2607.08175), achieving direct invariant mass regression for collimated diphotons.
  • The Breakdown of Isolated SMEFT and QCD Factorization: It is no longer viable to treat standard inputs and new physics independently. The field is realizing that PDF profiling can accidentally “absorb” high-mass SMEFT signals, highlighting the urgent need for joint fits (arXiv:2607.07791). Similarly, sub-percent precision demands that standard QCD factorization be replaced by joint QCD+QED frameworks utilizing Lepton Distribution Functions (arXiv:2607.07811).
  • Neutrino Degeneracies and CP Mimics: As JUNO, DUNE, and Hyper-Kamiokande approach operation, the community is deeply concerned with “fake” signals. Non-unitary PMNS mixing (arXiv:2607.06108) or Lorentz-invariance violation (arXiv:2607.06513) can mimic or degrade CP asymmetries. Breaking these degeneracies requires exact analytical resummation schemes (arXiv:2607.07213) and combined multi-detector fits.
  • Evasion Strategies for Cornered WIMPs and Axions: Under pressure from LZ direct-detection limits, which have severely cornered traditional natural higgsino dark matter (arXiv:2607.05648), theorists are pivoting to unified discrete symmetries to naturally solve the MSSM \(\mu\) problem while yielding viable DFSZ axions (arXiv:2607.07921).

Turning points

  • First Direct Bounds on Triple Higgs Coupling Modifier Space: The CMS Collaboration’s search for nonresonant \(HHH \to 6b\) (arXiv:2607.05145) directly excluded regions of the \((\kappa_3, \kappa_4)\) coupling space for the first time, replacing theoretical perturbative unitarity bounds with experimental data.
  • Resolution of the \(b\)-Baryon Lifetime Tension: Calculations of the NNLO QCD corrections to \(b\)-quark decay within the Heavy Quark Expansion (arXiv:2607.06538) dramatically reduced theoretical uncertainties, resolving a decades-long tension between predicted and measured \(b\)-baryon lifetime ratios.
  • Neutron-Capture Calorimetry: The demonstration that gadolinium-loaded liquid scintillators can capture delayed neutrons from hadronic showers (arXiv:2607.08587) cut energy resolution from 21.8% to 13.3%, offering a hardware solution to the problem of invisible hadronic energy fluctuations.

Open questions

  • The Fermi-LAT 20 GeV Galactic Halo Excess vs. Dwarfs: A rigorous pixel-level likelihood analysis with full PSF forward folding (arXiv:2607.08552) has confirmed the 20 GeV excess in 15 years of Fermi-LAT data. Can any planned tritium experiment scale to meet this threshold?
