## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2610.07145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07145) — The Back-to-Back Plateau of the Energy-Energy Correlator from Weak to Strong Coupling (Hao Chen et al.)

**Relevance.** This is only loosely a substructure paper. It studies the back-to-back (Sudakov) endpoint of the full-event EEC in 𝒩=4 SYM, not the collinear region inside jets. QCD jet measurements appear only as motivation. The overlap with the topic is energy-correlator technology.

**What they did.** Chen, Moult, Wang and Zhu resum both leading-power (LP, y⁻¹) and next-to-leading-power (NLP, y⁰) back-to-back logarithms through N³LL, including 1/N_c² terms. The method is the large-spin and twist-conformal-block pipeline of Chen–Zhou–Zhu, in five steps:
- twist-2 anomalous dimensions γ₁…γ₄ and OPE-coefficient corrections r₁…r₃ are put into a finite-difference large-spin basis;
- these are lifted to the twist-conformal-block basis;
- crossing of Φ(u,v) is used, which avoids needing twist-4 data;
- a Mellin/light transform is applied;
- the coefficients are matched to fixed-order data.

**Results.**
- **NLP towers and checks.** They give closed forms for the generic NLP coefficients C_k^NLP(n). Only C₂^NLP(1) and C₄^NLP(2) need fixed-order input; the remaining expansion terms agree with the known fixed-order expansion through three loops.
- **Structure of the NLP function.** The resummed NLP function G contains an erf(√a L_y/√2) tower. This term survives as y→0, giving a finite NLP shift to the plateau beyond the essential-singular LP term P_LP ~ a^{-1/2}e^{1/(2a)}.
- **Interpolation.** A two-parameter interpolation (p=2.34, q=1.81) is built to match the weak-coupling LP asymptotics and the strong-coupling result 1+1/a. It is fit to planar bootstrap points.

**Caveats.**
- The residual endpoint term H₀(a) is still undetermined beyond these logarithms.
- The perturbative description visibly degrades by a ~ 1/4, and the string expansion only reaches down to a ~ 0.5. The window in between is covered only by the fit.
- The interpolation is phenomenological, by the authors' own description.
- Transfer to QCD (running coupling, hadronization) is posed as an open question, not addressed.
