## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2610.00514](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00514) — Unified description of longitudinal and transverse parton dynamics in cold nuclear matter (Weiyao Ke)

**Relevance to this topic:** This paper is only peripherally about jets. It concerns parton propagation in cold nuclear matter for SIDIS and Drell–Yan. The link to substructure is the final section on the one-point energy correlator (OPEC) in DIS.

**What they did.** Ke combines two strands of SCET_G work into one TMD-level matching coefficient, Eq. (1):
- **Longitudinal part.** Impact-parameter-dependent medium-induced evolution M^med, building on Ke–Vitev's RG analysis of collinear SIDIS. The coupled quark/gluon equations (2)–(3) are in the dilute limit υ ≃ α_s ρ_G L²/(4E) ≪ 1. They run over ξ² ≲ μ² ≲ min{2E/L, μ_b²}. Finite b therefore truncates the coherent-radiation window, so energy loss depends on the transverse resolution.
- **Transverse part.** Broadening through the exponentiated Glauber cross section Σ_i(b,Y).
- **Fix to the collision kernel.** The kernel is BFKL-evolved in rapidity, but leading-order BFKL "produces excessively strong radiative broadening." It is tamed with a Kwieciński-type kinematical constraint (following Deak et al.) and a Kovchegov–Weigert-style running coupling.

**Results.** One parameter set (ρ_G = 0.5 fm⁻³, ξ² = 0.12 GeV²) reproduces the main trends of HERMES Xe/d multiplicity ratios and E866 Fe/Be and W/Be Drell–Yan ratios. The paper also gives EIC e+Pb/e+d predictions at √s = 60 GeV, Q² = 20 GeV², x_B = 0.1 and 0.4. Attenuation is stronger at x_B = 0.4, because the rest-frame parton energy E ≃ Q²/(2m_N x_B) is lower.

**The jet-substructure-relevant point.** Energy weighting kills the leading final-state energy-loss enhancement via the momentum sum rule. For the OPEC at √s = 141 GeV and 10 < Q < 15 GeV:
- the dynamical cold-nuclear-matter correction is below 1% in the back-to-back region;
- the roughly 30% nuclear modification comes from the nPDFs.

So for nuclear energy correlators, the final-state medium effect largely cancels at this order. This makes the OPEC an nPDF probe rather than a medium probe, and is worth knowing before interpreting eA energy-correlator data as jet quenching.

**Caveats.**
- Proceedings-length text; data agreement is described only as "main trends," with no fit quality quoted.
- Vacuum nonperturbative TMD input is used, and in-nucleus hadronization is explicitly excluded.
- The υ ≪ 1 limit is stretched at low-E HERMES and EIC kinematics.

## Muon collider

### [arXiv:2610.00723](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00723) — Neutrino trident process at a muon collider (Reinaldo Francener et al.)

**Setup.** The authors estimate trident rates in a forward neutrino detector ("MuColν"):
- **Detector:** 10 kg of tungsten (R = 10 cm, L = 1.65 cm), sited 500 m downstream of the IP.
- **Beam:** 5 TeV μ beams, with a tree-level decay spectrum.
- **Flux:** N_ν = 5×10¹⁷ ν_μ per year plus an equal number of ν̄_e. This assumes about 10% acceptance of decays along the 500 m straight section.
- **Cross section:** the Altmannshofer et al. full 2→4 generator (no equivalent-photon approximation), adapted to W with a Woods–Saxon form factor. Coherent and incoherent contributions are both included.

**Rates (before cuts, per year).**

| Final state | Events |
|---|---|
| e⁺e⁻ | ~1.0×10⁴ |
| μ⁺μ⁻ | 5.85×10³ |
| τ⁺τ⁻ | ~260 |
| e±μ∓ | 2.3×10⁴ |

Mixed-flavour channels are enhanced because only the W diagram contributes, so there is no destructive W/Z interference. Coherent scattering dominates (Z² scaling).

**After cuts.** Applying the FASER-style μμ cuts of Altmannshofer et al. [28] leaves about 3500 μμ events per year. The survival fractions are about 64% (coherent), 19% (incoherent p) and 48% (incoherent n).

**BSM reach (Δg_V, Δg_A).**
- The 95% CL contours greatly tighten the CCFR ring, whether statistics-only or with 5% systematics.
- For L_μ−L_τ Z′, the projections open new parameter space near m_Z′ ≈ 4 GeV and above the electroweak scale.
- A 10-ton detector is claimed to reach g′ ~ 10⁻⁴ for m_Z′ ≳ 10⁻² GeV.

**What this adds to the muon collider topic.** It contributes another entry to the emerging forward-ν physics case, alongside Marzocca et al., Adhikary et al., and Kling et al. Its distinctive argument is that the clean, calculable flux makes a percent-level trident measurement plausible.

**Caveats.**
- **Backgrounds are not studied for this environment.** Background rejection is borrowed from the LHC analysis; muon-collider beam-induced and halo backgrounds at the forward site are not discussed.
- **Flux treatment is idealized.** The flux uncertainty is only tree level, and the beam divergence (0.6 mrad) enters just through the acceptance estimate.
- **Statistical framing is inconsistent.** The Fig. 4 caption mixes "95% CL" with "±1σ."
- **The 10-ton projection** is statistics-only.
