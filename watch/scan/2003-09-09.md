## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ex/0309033](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0309033) — b-production at the Tevatron (Eric Kajfasz)

_Watchlist hit (b-jet) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The phenomenology of b-production and some of the previous Run I measurements of CDF and D0 are briefly reviewed. A new analysis by CDF of Run I b-bbar angular correlations and a new measurement by D0 of Run II b-jet cross-section, are presented.
