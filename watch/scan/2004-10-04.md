## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0410055](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0410055) — Resummed event shapes at hadron colliders (Giulia Zanderighi)

_Watchlist hit (event shapes) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present recently defined jet-observables for hadron-hadron dijet production, which are designed to reconcile the seemingly conflicting theoretical requirement of globalness, which makes it possible to resum them (automatically) at NLL accuracy and the limited experimental reach of detectors, so that they are measurable at the Tevatron and at the LHC.
