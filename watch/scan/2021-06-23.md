## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2106.13235](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.13235) — Fragmentation of jets containing a prompt J$/ψ$ meson in PbPb and pp collisions at $\sqrt{s_\mathrm{NN}} =$ 5.02 TeV (CMS Collaboration)

_Watchlist hit (jet fragmentation) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Jets containing a prompt J$/ψ$ meson are studied in lead-lead collisions at a nucleon-nucleon center-of-mass energy of 5.02 TeV, using the CMS detector at the LHC. Jets are selected to be in the transverse momentum range of 30 $\lt$ $p_\mathrm{T}$ $\lt$ 40 GeV. The J$/ψ$ yield in these jets is evaluated as a function of the jet fragmentation variable $z$, the ratio of the J$/ψ$ $p_\mathrm{T} $ to the jet $p_\mathrm{T}$. The nuclear modification factor, $R_\mathrm{AA}$, is then derived by comparing the yield in lead-lead collisions to the corresponding expectation based on proton-proton data, at the same nucleon-nucleon center-of-mass energy. The suppression of the J$/ψ$ yield shows a dependence on $z$, indicating that the interaction of the J$/ψ$ with the quark-gluon plasma formed in heavy ion collisions depends on the fragmentation that gives rise to the J$/ψ$ meson.
