## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0211361](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0211361) — DGLAP evolution extends the triple pole pomeron fit (G. Soyez)

_Watchlist hit (Gregory Soyez) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We show that the triple pole pomeron model \cite{CMS} provides an initial condition for a DGLAP evolution \cite{DGLAP} that produces a fit to high $Q^2$ experimental DIS data. We obtain good $χ^2$ for initial scales down to 3 GeV$^2$. Values of the initial scale smaller than 1.45 GeV$^2$ are ruled out at the 90% confidence level.
