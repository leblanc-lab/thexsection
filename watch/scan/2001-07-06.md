## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0107063](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0107063) — The $\pt$ spectrum of heavy quarks in photoproduction (S. Frixione et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Matteo Cacciari) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We illustrate a formalism that allows to match the next-to-leading order cross section for the photoproduction of heavy quarks to the cross section obtained by resumming logarithms of $\pt/m$ to the next-to-leading accuracy, thus giving a sensible prediction for any value of $\pt$. We present a comparison between our predictions and H1 and ZEUS data.
