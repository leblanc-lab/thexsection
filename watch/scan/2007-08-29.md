### [arXiv:0708.3994](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.3994) — BFKL at NNLO (S. Marzani et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Simone Marzani) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present a recent determination of an approximate expression for the O(alpha_s^3) contribution chi_2 to the kernel of the BFKL equation. This includes all collinear and anticollinear singular contributions and is derived using duality relations between the GLAP and BFKL kernels.
