## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0610246](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0610246) — |V_us| from Strange Hadronic Tau Data (Elvira Gamiz et al.)

_Watchlist hit (hadronic tau) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We report on recent work to determine the CKM matrix element |V_{us}| using strange hadronic τdecay data. We use the recent OPAL update of the strange spectral function, while on the theory side we update the dimension-two perturbative contribution including the recently calculated α_s^3 terms. Our result |V_{us}|=0.2220 \pm 0.0033 is already competitive with the standard extraction from K_{e3} decays and other new proposals to determine |V_{us}|. The actual uncertainty on |V_{us}| from τdata is dominated largely by experiment and will eventually be much reduced by B-factories and future τ-charm factory data, providing one of the most accurate determinations of this Standard Model parameter.

### [arXiv:hep-th/0610218](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0610218) — Holography with Schroedinger Potentials (Brian Batell et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Andrew Larkoski) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We examine the analogue one-dimensional quantum mechanics problem associated with bulk scalars and fermions in a slice of AdS_5. The ``Schroedinger'' potential can take on different qualitative shapes depending on the values of the mass parameters in the bulk theory. Several interesting correlations between the shape of the Schroedinger potential and the holographic theory exist. We show that the quantum mechanical picture is a useful guide to the holographic theory by examining applications from phenomenology.
