## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1307.0954](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.0954) — Charm production in association with W and Z at the LHC (Eleni Vryonidou)

_Watchlist hit (quark jets) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The production of charm quark jets in association with electroweak gauge bosons at the LHC can be used as a tool to constrain quark parton distribution functions. Motivated by recent measurements at the LHC, we calculate cross sections for W/Z +c, comparing these to W/Z+jet, for various PDF sets. The cross-section differences can be understood in terms of the different underlying PDFs, with the strange quark distribution being particularly important for W +c production. We discuss appropriately defined ratios and comment on how the measurements at the LHC can be used to extract information on the strange and charm content of the proton at high scales.

### [arXiv:1307.1060](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.1060) — The Vincia Parton Shower (Walter T. Giele et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Andrew Larkoski, Peter Skands) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We summarize recent developments in the VINCIA parton shower. After a brief review of the basics of the formalism, the extension of VINCIA to hadron collisions is sketched. We then turn to improvements of the efficiency of tree-level matching by making the shower history unique and by incorporating identified helicities. We conclude with an overview of matching to one-loop matrix elements.
