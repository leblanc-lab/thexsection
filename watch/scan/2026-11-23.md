## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:2611.26718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26718) — Dijet production in $e^+e^-$ collisions at NNLO+PS accuracy (Florian K\"onig et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Giulia Zanderighi) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present the first complete Monte Carlo event generator with next-to-next-to-leading order QCD accuracy for lepton collisions. The approach is based on a new formulation of the MiNNLO formalism and is applied to dijet production. Starting from the resummation of the three-jet resolution variable $y_3$, we present the corresponding MiNNLO master formula and implement it within the Powheg framework. We discuss phenomenological results relevant for LEP and future electron-positron colliders.

### [arXiv:2611.27681](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27681) — Zeroing in on BSM at Colliders (Cari Cesarotti et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Cari Cesarotti) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Destructive interference between light and heavy dynamics can produce a zero in the amplitude which, for strongly coupled UV completions, lies parametrically below the heavy threshold. We identify under which assumptions this parametrically light zero exists, and when it admits an effective field theory description. We study how absorptive effects and helicity structure affect the existence of the zero and turn it into a dip. When the dip is kinematically accessible, we show how its position, depth, and curvature constrain the UV dynamics. Collider examples illustrate the importance of interference in indirect searches and the information available when the zero is kinematically accessible.
