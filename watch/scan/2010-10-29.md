## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1010.6203](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.6203) — A measurement of the ratio of inclusive cross sections $σ(p\bar{p}\rightarrow Z+b{\rm\, jet})/ σ(p\bar{p}\rightarrow Z+{\rm jet})$ at $\sqrt{s}=1.96$ TeV (D0 Collaboration et al.)

_Watchlist hit (quark jet) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The ratio of the cross section for $p\bar{p}$ interactions producing a $Z$ boson and at least one $b$ quark jet to the inclusive $Z+{\rm jet}$ cross section is measured using $4.2\ {\rm fb}^{-1}$ of $p\bar{p}$ collisions collected with the \dzero\ detector at the Fermilab Tevatron collider at $\sqrt{s}=1.96$ TeV. The $Z\rightarrow\ell^+\ell^-$ candidate events with at least one $b$ jet are discriminated from $Z+$ charm and light jet(s) events by a novel technique that exploits the properties of the tracks associated to the jet. The measured ratio is $0.0193\pm0.0027$ for events having a jet with transverse momentum $\pt > 20 \GeV$ and pseudorapidity $|η| \leq 2.5$, which is the most precise to date and is consistent with theoretical predictions.
