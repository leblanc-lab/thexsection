## Muon collider

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9801410](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9801410) — Chargino mass determination at a muon collider (V. Barger et al.)

_Watchlist hit (muon collider) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We analyze the prospects at a muon collider for measuring chargino masses in the $μ^+μ^-\to \tildeχ^+\tildeχ^-$ process in the threshold region. We find that for the lighter chargino of a mass $100-200$ GeV, a measurement better than $50-300$ MeV should be possible with 50 fb$^{-1}$ integrated luminosity. The accuracy obtained here is better than with other techniques or at other facilities. The muon sneutrino mass, which enters through the $\tildeν_μ$ exchange diagram, can also be simultaneously measured to a few GeV if it is not too heavy.
