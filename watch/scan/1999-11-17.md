## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ex/9911023](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/9911023) — Fragmentation of CDF jets: perturbative or non-perturbative? (Alexei Safonov)

_Watchlist hit (jet fragmentation) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Presented are the most recent jet fragmentation results from CDF: inclusive distributions of charged particle momenta and their kT in jets; average track multiplicities, as well as angular distributions of multiplicity flow, for a wide range of jet energies with ET from 40 to 300 GeV. The results are compared with Monte-Carlo and, when possible, analytical calculations performed in resummed perturbative QCD approximations (MLLA).

## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9911391](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9911391) — Power corrections to the differential Drell-Yan cross section (Mrinal Dasgupta)

_Watchlist hit (Mrinal Dasgupta) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We estimate the power corrections (infrared renormalon contributions) to the coefficient functions for the differential Drell-Yan cross-section $d^2σ/dQ^2dy$, where $Q^2$ is the mass squared and $y$ the rapidity of the produced lepton pair. We employ the dispersive method based on the analysis of one-loop Feynman graphs containing a massive gluon.
