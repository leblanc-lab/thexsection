## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0412326](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0412326) — Issues in the Extraction of m_s and V_us from Hadronic Tau Decay Data (K. Maltman)

_Watchlist hit (hadronic tau) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Various complications encountered in the process of attempting to extract the basic Standard Model parameters, m_s and V_us, from hadronic tau decay data are discussed. Preliminary results for V_us, based on sum rules displaying good convergence and stability, are also presented.
