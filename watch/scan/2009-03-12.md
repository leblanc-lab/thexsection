## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:0903.2150](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.2150) — Some remarks on dipole showers and the DGLAP equation (Peter Skands et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Peter Skands) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

It has been argued recently that parton showers based on colour dipoles conflict with collinear factorization and do not lead to the correct DGLAP equation. We show that this conclusion is based on an inappropriate assumption, namely the choice of the gluon energy as evolution variable. We further show numerically that Monte Carlo programs based on dipole showers with "infrared sensible" evolution variables reproduce the DGLAP equation both in asymptotic form as well as in comparison to the leading behaviour of second-order QCD matrix elements.

### [arXiv:0903.2187](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.2187) — On the universality of hadronisation corrections to QCD jets (Mrinal Dasgupta et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Mrinal Dasgupta) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We improve previously derived analytical estimates of hadronisation corrections to QCD jets at hadron colliders, firmly establishing at the two-loop level the link to the well-known power corrections to LEP event-shape variables. The results of this paper apply to jets defined in the kt and anti-kt algorithms but the general framework presented here holds also for other algorithms for which calculations are in progress.
