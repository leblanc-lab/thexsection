### [arXiv:2611.07011](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07011) — Constraining the spectral gap of 2d CFTs just above c=1 (Nathan Benjamin et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Jesse Thaler) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We numerically study the space of compact unitary two-dimensional conformal field theories (2d CFTs) with central charge $c>1$ using the modular bootstrap. We focus on bounding the spectral gap $\Delta_{\rm gap}$ of such theories, namely the smallest scaling dimension among the non-vacuum primary operators. We use both a primal method that directly searches for consistent spectra and a dual method that yields rigorous exclusions. A key feature of our analysis is requiring the degeneracies of primary operators to be positive integers. At $c$ very near $1$, the integrality constraint greatly reduces the allowed spectral gap in both methods. For example, at $c-1 = 3\times 10^{-3}$ integrality strengthens the rigorous bound from $\Delta_{\rm gap} \lesssim 0.5$ to $\Delta_{\rm gap} < 0.041$. Our results suggest that the spectral gap might vanish as $c$ approaches $1$ from above.

### [arXiv:2611.07366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07366) — A framework for matrix-element-corrected QED antenna showers in hadron decays (F. Abudin\'en et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Peter Skands) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present a general framework for the description of QED parton showers in hadron decays. The formalism is constructed in terms of coherent QED antennae with full dependence on particle masses and extends beyond the YFS approach by systematically incorporating spin-dependent (quasi)collinear single-unresolved limits to all orders. In addition, we incorporate corrections derived from tree-level matrix elements up to $\mathcal{O}(\alpha^2)$ and describe a generic procedure to obtain such matrix elements using FeynRules and MadGraph. We provide an explicit implementation of the method for decays involving pseudoscalar mesons and for decays of vectors and pseudoscalars into fermion-antifermion pairs, within the combined Pythia+Vincia parton-shower Monte Carlo framework, and compare the resulting QED emission spectra to those generated with Photos and Sherpa+Photons++ through the EvtGen generator.

### [arXiv:2611.08388](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08388) — Top-Quark Pair Production and Decay in Extended Higgs Sectors (Andrea Banfi et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Andrea Banfi) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Extensions of the Higgs sector in the Standard Model address outstanding questions like the first-order electroweak phase transition and also present a rich collider phenomenology. The two-Higgs-doublet model is a minimal extension of the Standard Model with an extra complex $SU(2)_L$ doublet, and consists of an extra scalar, a pseudoscalar and a pair of charged Higgs bosons along with a Standard-Model-like Higgs boson. We present a modified version of the hvq library of the POWHEG-BOX-V2 event generator interfaced with the OpenLoops 2 amplitude generator to obtain predictions for top-quark pair production at NLO accuracy, including top-quark decays with full spin correlations in a framework that extends the SM Higgs sector with an extra heavy scalar and a pseudoscalar. The interference effects between the scalar and pseudoscalar signals, and the Standard Model QCD background are implemented. We include parton showers and hadronisation, and take detector effects into consideration. The status of our work and plans to extend it to future collider projects are discussed.
