## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:2003.07868](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.07868) — Reinterpretation of LHC Results for New Physics: Status and Recommendations after Run 2 (Waleed Abdallah et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Jesse Thaler, Cari Cesarotti, Juan Rojo, Michael Krämer) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We report on the status of efforts to improve the reinterpretation of searches and measurements at the LHC in terms of models for new physics, in the context of the LHC Reinterpretation Forum. We detail current experimental offerings in direct searches for new particles, measurements, technical implementations and Open Data, and provide a set of recommendations for further improving the presentation of LHC results in order to better enable reinterpretation in the future. We also provide a brief description of existing software reinterpretation frameworks and recent global analyses of new physics that make use of the current data.
