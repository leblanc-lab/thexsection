## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0210445](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0210445) — Leptonic Probes of the Standard Model (A. Pich)

_Watchlist hit (hadronic tau) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Precise measurements of the lepton properties provide stringent tests of the Standard Model structure and accurate determinations of its parameters. We overview the present status of a few selected topics: lepton universality, QCD tests and the determination of alpha_s and m_s from hadronic tau decays, the anomalous lepton magnetic moments and neutrino oscillations.
