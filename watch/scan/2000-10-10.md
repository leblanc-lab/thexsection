## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0010089](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0010089) — The V-A sum rules and the Operator Product Expansion in complex q^2-plane from tau-decay data (B. L. Ioffe et al.)

_Watchlist hit (hadronic tau) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The operator product expansion (OPE) for the difference of vector and axial current correlators is analyzed for complex values of momentum q^2. The vector and axial spectral functions, taken from hadronic tau-decay data, are treated with the help of Borel, Gaussian and spectral moments sum rules. The range of applicability, advantages and disadvantages of each type are discussed. The general features of OPE are confirmed by the data. The vacuum expectation values of dimension 6 and 8 operators are found to be O_6=-(6.8\pm 2.1)*10^{-3} GeV^6, O_8=(7\pm 4)*10^{-3} GeV^8.

## Muon collider

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0010092](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0010092) — Quest for the dynamics of $ν_{mu} \to ν_{tau}$ conversion (A. M. Gago et al.)

_Watchlist hit (muon collider) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We perform a quantitative analysis of the capability of K2K, MINOS, OPERA and a neutrino factory in a muon collider to discriminate the standard mass induced vacuum oscillation from the pure decoherence solution to the atmospheric neutrino problem and thereby contribute to unravel the dynamics that governs the observed $ν_mu$ disappearence.
