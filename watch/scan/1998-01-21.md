## Muon collider

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9801385](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9801385) — Technicolor and the First Muon Collider (Kenneth Lane)

_Watchlist hit (muon collider) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The motivations for studying dynamical scenarios of electroweak and flavor symmetry breaking are reviewed and the latest ideas, especially topcolor-assisted technicolor, are summarized. Technicolor's observable low-energy signatures are discussed. The superb energy resolution of the First Muon Collider may make it possible to resolve the extraordinarily narrow technihadrons that occur in such models--$π_T^0$, $ρ_T^0$, $ω_T$--and produce them at very large rates compared to other colliders.
