## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1412.3953](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.3953) — Leptons and QCD (Antonio Pich)

_Watchlist hit (hadronic tau) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Three important QCD-related aspects of the $τ$ and $μ$ dynamics are reviewed: the determination of the strong coupling from the hadronic tau decay width, leading to the updated value $α_s(m_τ^2) = 0.331 \pm 0.013$; the measurement of $|V_{us}|$ through the Cabibbo-suppressed decays of the $τ$, and the Standard Model prediction for the muon anomalous magnetic moment.
