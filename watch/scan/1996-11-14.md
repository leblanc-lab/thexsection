## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9611324](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9611324) — Associated J/psi + gamma Photoproduction as a Probe of the Colour-Octet Mechanism (Matteo Cacciari et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Matteo Cacciari, Michael Krämer) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The associated production of a J/psi and a photon in photon-hadron collisions is considered, and shown to be a good probe of the presence of colour-octet mediated channels in quarkonia production, as predicted by the factorization approach within NRQCD. Total and differential cross sections for photoproduction at fixed target experiments and at HERA are presented. Associated J/psi + gamma production at hadron colliders is briefly discussed.
