## Muon collider

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9704215](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9704215) — Strong WW Scattering Physics: A Comparative Study for the LHC, NLC and a Muon Collider (Tao Han)

_Watchlist hit (muon collider) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We discuss the model independent parameterization for a strongly interacting electroweak sector. Phenomenological studies are made to probe such a sector for future colliders such as the LHC, $e^+e^-$ Linear collider and a muon collider.
