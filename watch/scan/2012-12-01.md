## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1212.0091](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.0091) — The strong coupling from tau decays without prejudice (Diogo Boito et al.)

_Watchlist hit (hadronic tau) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We review our recent determination of the strong coupling α_s from the OPAL data for non-strange hadronic tau decays. We find that α_s(m^2_τ) =0.325+-0.018 using fixed-order perturbation theory, and α_s(m^2_τ)=0.347+-0.025 using contour-improved perturbation theory. At present, these values supersede any earlier determinations of the strong coupling from hadronic tau decays, including those from ALEPH data.
