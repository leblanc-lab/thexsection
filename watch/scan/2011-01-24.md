## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1101.4471](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.4471) — Peripherical processes 2 $\to$ 3 and 2 $\to$ 4 in QED and QCD in $p(\bar p)p$ high energy collisions (A. I. Ahmadov et al.)

_Watchlist hit (gluon jet) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Differential cross section of processes with high energy $p(\bar p)p$ collisions in frames of QED: creation of scalar, pseudoscalar and lepton pair - are considered in Weizsäcker - Williams approximation. In frames of QCD processes with conversion of initial proton (antiproton) to fermionic jets accompanied with one gluon jet as well as the state of two gluons and quark-antiquark pair (with out rapidity gap) are considered in frames of effective Reggion action of theory of Lipatov. Process of creation of a Higgs boson accompanied with two fermionic jets is considered. The azimuthal correlation in process of two gluon jet separated by rapidity gap is investigated. Effects of gluon reggeization are taken into account. Some distributions are illustrated by numerical calculations.
