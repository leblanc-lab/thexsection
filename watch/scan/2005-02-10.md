## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0502094](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0502094) — On Differences Between SCET and QCDF for B->pi pi Decays (Christian W. Bauer et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Iain Stewart) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We give a detailed description of the differences between the factorization and results derived from SCET and QCDF for decays B -> M1 M2. This serves as a reply to a comment about our work "B-> M1 M2: Factorization, charming penguins, strong phases, and polarization" [1] made by the authors in [2]. We disagree with their criticisms.
