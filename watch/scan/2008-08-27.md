## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:0808.3672](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.3672) — How to calculate colourful cross sections efficiently (Tanju Gleisberg et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Stefan Höche) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Different methods for the calculation of cross sections with many QCD particles are compared. To this end, CSW vertex rules, Berends-Giele recursion and Feynman-diagram based techniques are implemented as well as various methods for the treatment of colours and phase space integration. We find that typically there is only a small window of jet multiplicities, where the CSW technique has efficiencies comparable or better than both of the other two methods.

### [arXiv:0808.3674](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.3674) — Comix, a new matrix element generator (Tanju Gleisberg et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Stefan Höche) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present a new tree-level matrix element generator, based on the colour dressed Berends-Giele recursive relations. We discuss two new algorithms for phase space integration, dedicated to be used with large multiplicities and colour sampling.
