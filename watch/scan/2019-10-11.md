## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1910.05334](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.05334) — Identifying hadronic charmonium decays in hadron colliders (Nicolo de Groot et al.)

_Watchlist hit (quark and gluon jets, gluon jets) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Identification of charmonium states at hadron colliders has mostly been limited to leptonic decays of the J/ψ. In this paper we present and algorithm to identify hadronic decays of charmonium states (J/ψ, ψ(2S), χ_{c0,1,2}) which make up the large majority of all decays. The algorithm is able to identify hadronic $J/ψ$ decays with an efficiency of 36\% while suppressing a background of quark and gluon jets by a factor 100.
