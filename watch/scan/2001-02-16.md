### [arXiv:hep-ph/0102210](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0102210) — Search for Gluonic Mesons in Gluon Jets (Peter Minkowski et al.)

_Watchlist hit (quark jets, gluon jets) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._
