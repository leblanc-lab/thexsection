## Muon collider

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9801275](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9801275) — Top Quark Pair Production at Higher Energies at the First Muon Collider (A. H. Hoang)

_Watchlist hit (muon collider) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

For a proper theoretical description of the top quark pair production cross section at the First Muon Collider in the kinematic regime away from the threshold the complete mass and energy dependence has to be taken into account. Two-loop calculations for the photon mediated total cross section are reviewed.
