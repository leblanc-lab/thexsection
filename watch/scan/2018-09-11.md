## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1809.03982](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.03982) — SM+Top at the LHC (Thomas G. McCarthy)

_Watchlist hit (large-radius jets, boosted objects) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

These proceedings highlight a selection of recent results by the ATLAS, CMS and LHCb collaborations. The majority of the featured analyses make use of the large set of $\sqrt{s}=13$ TeV proton-proton collision data collected during the successful second run of the LHC. A particular focus is placed on analyses of Standard Model processes involving either hadronic jets or W/Z bosons. Searches and cross-section measurements involving top quark signatures are also given prominence, as are those targeting highly boosted objects such as W/Z and H bosons, and which feature the use of large-radius jets and substructure techniques.

## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:1809.04192](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.04192) — Hadronic Final States in DIS at NNLO QCD with Parton Showers (Stefan Höche et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Stefan Höche) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present a parton-shower matched NNLO QCD calculation for hadronic final state production in Deep Inelastic Scattering. The computation is based on the UNLOPS method and is implemented in the publicly available event generation framework SHERPA. Results are compared to measurements performed by the H1 collaboration.
