### [arXiv:0706.2130](https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.2130) — Progress on neural parton distributions (NNPDF Collaboration et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Juan Rojo) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We give a status report on the determination of a set of parton distributions based on neural networks. In particular, we summarize the determination of the nonsinglet quark distribution up to NNLO, we compare it with results obtained using other approaches, and we discuss its use for a determination of $α_s$.
