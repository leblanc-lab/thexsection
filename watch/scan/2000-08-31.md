## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0008332](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0008332) — Estimation of Collision Impact Parameter (M. Savina et al.)

_Watchlist hit (energy flow) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We demonstrate that the nuclear collision geometry (i.e. impact parameter) can be determined with 1.5 fm accuracy in an event-by-event analysis by measuring the transverse energy flow in the pseudorapidity region $3 \le |η| \le 5$ with a minimal dependence on collision dynamics details at the LHC energy scale. Using the HIJING model we have illustrated our calculation by a simulation of events of nucleus-nucleus interactions at the c.m.s energy from 1 up to 5.5 TeV per nucleon and various type of nuclei.
