## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0407225](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0407225) — A model for next-to-leading order threshold resummed form factors (Ugo Aglietti et al.)

_Watchlist hit (gluon jets) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present a model for next-to-leading order resummed threshold form factors based on a time-like coupling recently introduced in the framework of small x physics. Improved expressions for the form factors in N-space are obtained which are not plagued by Landau-pole singularities, as the included absorptive effects -- usually neglected -- act as regulators. The physical reason is that, because of faster decay of gluon jets, there is not enough resolution time to observe the Landau pole. Our form factors reduce to the standard ones when the absorptive parts related to the coupling are neglected. The inverse transform from N-space to x-space can be done directly without any prescription and we obtain analytical expressions for the form factors, which are well defined in all x-space.

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0407233](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0407233) — Nonperturbative solution of Yukawa theory and gauge theories (J. R. Hiller)

_Watchlist hit (energy correlator) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Recent progress in the nonperturbative solution of (3+1)-dimensional Yukawa theory and quantum electrodynamics (QED) and (1+1)-dimensional super Yang-Mills (SYM) theory will be summarized. The work on Yukawa theory has been extended to include two-boson contributions to the dressed fermion state and has inspired similar work on QED, where Feynman gauge has been found surprisingly convenient. In both cases, the theories are regulated in the ultraviolet by the inclusion of Pauli-Villars particles. For SYM theory, new high-resolution calculations of spectra have been used to obtain thermodynamic functions and improved results for a stress-energy correlator.
