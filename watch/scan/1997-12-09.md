## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ex/9712011](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/9712011) — Soft and Diffractive Phenomena (R. Eichler)

_Watchlist hit (quark and gluon jets, gluon jets) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._
