## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2010.14197](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.14197) — Production of massless charm jets in $pp$ collisions at next-to-leading order of QCD in comparison with CMS data (Gustav Kramer)

_Keyword hit (c-jet) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present predictions for the inclusive production of charm jets in proton-proton collisions at 2.76 and 5.02 TeV. The charm-quark is considered massless. In this scheme we find that the ratio of the next-to-leading order to the leading order cross section (K factor) is almost equal to one depending essentially on the choice of the renormalization and factorization scale. Adding non-pertubative corrections obtained from Pythia Monte Carlo calculations lead to reasonable agreement with experimental c-jet cross sections obtained by the CMS collaboration.
