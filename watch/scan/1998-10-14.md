## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ex/9810031](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/9810031) — Search for a Technicolor omega_T Particle in Events with a Photon and a b-quark Jet at CDF (The CDF Collaboration et al.)

_Watchlist hit (quark jet) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

If the Technicolor omega_T particle exists, a likely decay mode is omega_T -> gamma pi_T, followed by pi_T -> bb-bar, yielding the signature gamma bb-bar. We have searched 85 pb^-1 of data collected by the CDF experiment at the Fermilab Tevatron for events with a photon and two jets, where one of the jets must contain a secondary vertex implying the presence of a b quark. We find no excess of events above standard model expectations. We express the result of an exclusion region in the M_omega_T - M_pi_T mass plane.
