## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1011.3872](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.3872) — SCET approach to top quark decay (Xiaohui Liu)

_Watchlist hit (jet function) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

In this work we study QCD corrections to the top quark doubly decay rate with a detected $B$ hadron containing a $b$ quark. We focus on the regime among which the emitted $W$ boson nearly carries its maxim energy. The tool that we use here is the soft-collinear effective theory (SCET). The factorization theorem based on SCET indicates a novel fragmenting jet function. We calculate this function to next-to-leading order in $α_s$. Large logarithms due to several well separated scale are summed up using the renormalization group equation (RGE). Finally we reach an analytic formula for the distribution which could easily be generalized to other heavy hadron decay.
