## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ex/0408066](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0408066) — Search for D0-D0bar Mixing Using Semileptonic Decay Modes (B. Aubert et al.)

_Watchlist hit (flavor tagging) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Based on an 87-fb$^{-1}$ dataset collected by the Babar detector at the PEP-II asymmetric-energy $B$-Factory, a search for $D^{0}$--$\bar{D}^{0}$ mixing has been made using the semileptonic decay modes $D^{*+} \to π^{+} D^{0}, D^{0} \to K^{(*)}eν$ (+c.c.). The use of these modes allows unambiguous flavor tagging and a combined fit of the $D^{0}$ decay time and $D^{*+}$--$D^{0}$ mass difference ($ΔM$) distributions. The high-statistics sample of unmixed semileptonic $D^{0}$ decays is used to model the $ΔM$ distribution and time-dependence of mixed events directly from the data. Neural networks are used to select events and reconstruct the $D^{0}$. A result consistent with no charm mixing has been obtained, $R_{\rm{mix}}=0.0023 \pm 0.0012 \pm 0.0004$. This corresponds to an upper limit of $R_{\rm{mix}}<0.0042$ (90% CL).
