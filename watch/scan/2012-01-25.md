## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:1201.5288](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.5288) — NLO vector boson production with light jets (Z. Bern et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Stefan Höche) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

In this contribution we present recent progress in the computation of next-to-leading order (NLO) QCD corrections for the production of an electroweak vector boson in association with jets at hadron colliders. We focus on results obtained using the virtual matrix element library BLACKHAT in conjunction with SHERPA, focusing on results relevant to understanding the background to top production.

## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1201.5292](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.5292) — B-tagging in CMS (Cristina Ferro)

_Watchlist hit (b-tagging, quark jet) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The identification of b jets is a crucial issue to study and characterize various channels like top quark events and many new physics scenarios. Different b-tagging techniques are defined in CMS which benefit from the long life time, high mass and large momentum fraction of the b-hadron produced in b-quark jet. Effcient algorithms have been developed based on the measure of b-hadron secondary vertex or on tracks with a large impact parameter. Data collected in pp collisions at 7TeV in 2011 are used to estimate both the b-tagging effciency and the mistag rate from light flavor jets.
