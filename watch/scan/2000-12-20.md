## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ex/0012044](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0012044) — A Measurement of the QCD Colour Factors using Event Shape Distributions at sqrt(s)=14 GeV to 189 GeV (S. Kluth et al.)

_Watchlist hit (event shape) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Measurements of the QCD colour factors C_A and C_F and of the number of active quark flavours n_f in the process e+e- -> hadrons at high energy are presented. They are based on fits of O(alpha_S**2)+NLLA QCD calculations to distributions of the event shape observables 1-T, C, B_T and B_W measured at centre-of-mass energies from 14 GeV to 189 GeV. Hadronisation effects are approximated with power correction calculations which also depend on the QCD gauge structure. In this approach potential biases from hadronisation models are reduced. Our results for individually measured quantities are n_f = 5.64 +- 1.35, C_A = 2.88 +- 0.27 and C_F = 1.45 +- 0.27 in good agreement with QCD based on the SU(3) symmetry group where n_f=5 for the energies considered here, C_A=3 and C_F=4/3. From simultaneous fits of C_A and C_F we find C_A = 2.84 +- 0.24 and C_F = 1.29 +- 0.18, which is also in good agreement with the QCD expectation.

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0012261](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0012261) — Phenomenology of multiple parton radiation in semi-inclusive deep-inelastic scattering (P. M. Nadolsky et al.)

_Watchlist hit (energy flow) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

In the current region of semi-inclusive deep-inelastic scattering, most events are accompanied by intensive radiation of soft and collinear partons which cannot be reliably described at any fixed order of perturbative QCD. In this paper, a resummation formalism that describes such multiple parton radiation is compared to the HERA data on semi-inclusive DIS, including the distributions of energy flow and charged particle multiplicity. We show that the resummation of multiple parton radiation improves the agreement between the theory and the data. We make some suggestions on further experimental study of multiple parton radiation at HERA.

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0012262](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0012262) — Azimuthal asymmetries at HERA: theoretical aspects (P. M. Nadolsky et al.)

_Watchlist hit (energy flow) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We comment on theoretical aspects of the measurement of azimuthal asymmetries in semi-inclusive charged particle production, made recently by the ZEUS Collaboration at HERA. By taking the ratio of the two measured asymmetries, we find good agreement between the perturbative QCD prediction and the experimental data. To separate the perturbative and nonperturbative contributions to the asymmetries, we suggest that the azimuthal asymmetries of the transverse energy flow be measured as a function of a variable $q_T$ related to the pseudorapidity of the energy flow.
