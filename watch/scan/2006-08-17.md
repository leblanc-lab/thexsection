### [arXiv:hep-ph/0608194](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0608194) — Next-to-Leading order Higgs + 2 jet production via gluon fusion (John M. Campbell et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Giulia Zanderighi) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._
