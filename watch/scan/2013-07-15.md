## Muon collider

### [arXiv:1307.4037](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.4037) — KK MC 4.22: CEEX EW Corrections for f\bar{f}\rightarrow f'\bar{f'} at LHC and Muon Colliders (S. Jadach et al.)

_Watchlist hit (muon collider) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present the upgrade of the coherent exclusive (CEEX) exponentiation realization of the Yennie-Frautschi-Suura (YFS) theory used in our Monte Carlo ({\cal KK} MC) to the processes f\bar{f}\rightarrow f'\bar{f'}, f=μ,τ,q,ν_\ell, f'=e,μ,τ,q,ν_\ell, q=u,d,s,c,b,t, \ell=e,μ,τwith f\ne f', with an eye toward the precision physics of the LHC and possible high energy muon colliders. We give a brief summary of the CEEX theory in comparison to the older (EEX) exclusive exponentiation theory and illustrate theoretical results relevant to the LHC and possible muon collider physics programs.
