## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ex/0010009](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0010009) — Jet Substructure at HERA (Claudia Glasman)

_Watchlist hit (jet substructure, jet shapes, subjet) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Measurements of jet shapes and subjet multiplicities in photoproduction performed by ZEUS are presented and compared to leading-logarithm parton-shower Monte Carlo models. The predicted differences on the size of gluon- and quark-initiated jets are used to select samples to study the dynamics of the subprocesses.
