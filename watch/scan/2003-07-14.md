## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0307180](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0307180) — Measurement of the MSSM Higgs-bosons production in gamma gamma -> A,H -> b bbar at the Photon Collider at TESLA (P. Niezurawski et al.)

_Watchlist hit (b-tagging) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Results from a realistic simulation of the heavy MSSM Higgs-bosons A and H production, gamma gamma -> A,H -> b bbar, at the Photon Collider at TESLA are reported. In the scenario where a light SM-like Higgs boson h exists, we study (following M. Muehlleitner et al.) Higgs bosons A and H for masses M_A=200,250,300,350 GeV and tan(beta)=7. This scenario corresponds to parameters region not accessible at LHC or at the first stage of the e^+ e^- collider. NLO estimation of background, analysis of overlaying events, realistic b-tagging and corrections for escaping neutrinos were performed. The statistical precision of the cross-section measurement is estimated to be 8-20%.

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0307183](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0307183) — Light Higgs-boson production at the Photon Collider at TESLA with an improved background analysis (P. Niezurawski et al.)

_Watchlist hit (b-tagging) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Measurement of the Gamma(h -> gamma gamma)Br(h -> b bbar) at the Photon Collider at TESLA is studied for the Standard Model Higgs boson with mass of 120 to 160 GeV. The NLO estimation of background, analysis of overlaying events, realistic b-tagging and corrections for escaping neutrinos were performed. We find that for M_h = 120-160 GeV the Gamma(h -> gamma gamma)Br(h -> b bbar) can be measured with a statistical accuracy of 2-7% after one year of the Photon Collider running.
