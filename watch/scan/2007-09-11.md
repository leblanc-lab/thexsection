## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:0709.1608](https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.1608) — First results on e^+e^- -> 3 jets at NNLO (A. Gehrmann-De Ridder et al.)

_Watchlist hit (event shapes) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Precision studies of QCD at $e^+e^-$ colliders are based on measurements of event shapes and jet rates. To match the high experimental accuracy, theoretical predictions to next-to-next-to-leading order (NNLO) in QCD are needed for a reliable interpretation of the data. We report the first calculation of NNLO corrections (${\cal O}(α_s^3)$) to three-jet production and related event shapes, and discuss their phenomenological impact.
