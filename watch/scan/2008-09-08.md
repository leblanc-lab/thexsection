## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:0809.1093](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.1093) — Reparameterization Invariant Collinear Operators (Claudio Marcantonini et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Iain Stewart) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

In constructing collinear operators, which describe the production of energetic jets or energetic hadrons, important constraints are provided by reparameterization invariance (RPI). RPI encodes Lorentz invariance in a power expansion about a collinear direction, and connects the Wilson coefficients of operators at different orders in this expansion to all orders in alphas. We construct reparameterization invariant collinear objects. The expansion of operators built from these objects provides an efficient way of deriving RPI relations and finding a minimal basis of operators, particularly when one has an observable with multiple collinear directions and/or soft particles. Complete basis of operators are constructed for pure glue currents at twist-4, and for operators involving multiple collinear directions, including those appearing in e+e- -> 3 jets, and for pp-> 2 jets initiated via gluon-fusion.

## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:0809.1407](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.1407) — W/Z + Light Flavor Jets and W/Z + Heavy Flavor Jets at the Tevatron (Christopher Neu)

_Watchlist hit (b-jets) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Collider signatures containing bosons and jets are particularly interesting. Recent theoretical effort has been devoted to determining predictions of W/Z + multiple parton production; the high statistics sample of W/Z + jets events collected at the Tevatron is a valuable testbed for probing the validity of these calculations. The final state containing a Z or W boson and one or more b-jets is a promising Higgs search channel at the Tevatron and could be a window to new physics at the LHC. These searches benefit from a deep understanding of the production of W/Z + heavy flavor jets which constitutes a significant background to the more exotic sources of this signature. Herein the latest Tevatron results on these production mechanisms are reviewed with an emphasis on comparison of data results to the latest theoretical models.
