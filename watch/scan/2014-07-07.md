### [arXiv:1407.1621](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.1621) — High multiplicity processes with BlackHat and Sherpa (Jeppe R. Andersen et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Stefan Höche) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

In this contribution, we present an intermediate storage format for next-to-leading order (NLO) events and explain the advantages of presenting a NLO calculation in this format. We also present some recent applications, including the calculation of PDF uncertainties and the combination of different multiplicity samples for the prediction of gap fractions in inclusive dijet events.

## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1407.1796](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.1796) — Flavour tagged time dependent angular analysis of the $B_s \rightarrow J/ψφ$ decay and extraction of $ΔΓ$ and the weak phase $φ_s$ in ATLAS (ATLAS Collaboration)

_Watchlist hit (flavour tagging) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

A measurement of the $B_s \rightarrow J/ψφ$ decay parameters, updated to include flavour tagging is reported using $4.9 fb^{-1}$ of integrated luminosity collected by the ATLAS detector from $\sqrt{s}= 7$TeV $pp$ collisions recorded in 2011 at the LHC. The values measured for the physical parameters are: \begin{eqnarray*} φ_s & = & 0.12 \pm 0.25(stat.)\pm 0.05(syst.)rad \\ ΔΓ& = & 0.053 \pm 0.021(stat.) 0.010(syst.)ps^{-1} \\ Γ_s & = & 0.677 \pm 0.007 (stat.)\pm 0.004 (syst.)ps^{-1}\\ |A_{\parallel}(0)|^2 & = & 0.220 \pm 0.008 (stat.)\pm 0.009(syst.)\\ |A_{0}(0)|^2 & = & 0.529 \pm 0.006 (stat.)\pm 0.012(syst.)\\ δ_\perp & = & 3.89 \pm 0.47(stat.) \pm 0.11(syst.)rad \end{eqnarray*} where the parameter $ΔΓ$ is constrained to be positive. The $S$-wave contribution was measured and found to be compatible with zero. Results for $φ_s$ and $ΔΓ$ are also presented as $68\%$ and $95\%$ likelihood contours, which show agreement with the Standard Model expectations.
