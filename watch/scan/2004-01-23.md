## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ex/0401036](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0401036) — Dijet Mass Resolution at the LHC (Dan Green)

_Watchlist hit (jet mass) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

At the LHC the resolution of dijet mass is compromised by the pile up of energy from overlapping minimum bias events. Using anxillary measurements of the jet mass and the energy in distant cones, the resolution can be improved, as illustrated by a study of Z' of 120 GeV mass with the LHC operating at design luminosity.

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0401177](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0401177) — Small-$Q^2$ extension of DGLAP-constrained Regge residues (G. Soyez)

_Watchlist hit (Gregory Soyez) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

In a previous paper, we have shown that it was possible to use the DGLAP evolution equatio n to constrain the high-$Q^2$ ($Q^2 \ge 10$ GeV$^2$) behaviour of the residues of a high-e nergy Regge model, and we applied the developed method to the triple-pole pomeron model. W e show here that one can obtain a description of the low-$Q^2$ $γ^{(*)} p$ data match ing the high-$Q^2$ results at $Q^2 = 10$ GeV$^2$.
