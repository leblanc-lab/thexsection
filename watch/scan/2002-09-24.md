## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0209281](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0209281) — Consequences of t-channel unitarity for gamma(*)p and gamma(*) gamma(*) amplitudes (J. R. Cudell et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Gregory Soyez) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We show that t-channel unitarity constraints make it possible to obtain the photon-photon cross sections from the photon-proton and proton-proton cross sections, for Q2<150 GeV2. In order to do so, one must postulate the existence of double-pole or triple-pole singularities in the complex j plane.
