## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0111256](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0111256) — The hadronic tau decay of a heavy charged Higgs in models with singlet neutrino in large extra dimensions (Ketevi A. Assamagan et al.)

_Watchlist hit (hadronic tau) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We study the LHC sensitivity to the charged Higgs discovery in the channel H^- -> tau_L^- nu in models with a singlet neutrino in large extra dimensions. The observation of such a signal would provide a distinctive evidence for these models since in the standard two Higgs doublet model type II, H^- -> tau_L^- nu is completely suppressed.
