## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1101.2662](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.2662) — Seeing jets in heavy-ion collisions (Gregory Soyez)

_Watchlist hit (jet algorithms) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

In these proceedings, we briefly review how jets can be reconstructed in heavy-ion collisions. The main point we address is the subtraction of the large contamination from the underlying event background. We first present the main ingredients needed to define the jets and perform the background subtraction and then discuss the efficiency of the subtraction for different jet algorithms and background-estimation methods.

### [arXiv:1101.2665](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.2665) — A simple description of jet cross-section ratios (Gregory Soyez)

_Watchlist hit (jet algorithm, jet radius) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We compute the ratio of the inclusive jet cross-sections obtained with the same jet algorithm at two different values of the jet radius. We perform a computation of that observable at NLO (O(alphas^2)) in perturbative QCD and compute non-perturbative corrections from soft-gluon emission. We discuss predictions for RHIC and the LHC.
