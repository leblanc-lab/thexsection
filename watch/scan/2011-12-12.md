## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1112.2567](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.2567) — New approach to identifying boosted hadronically-decaying particle using jet substructure in its center-of-mass frame (Chunhui Chen)

_Watchlist hit (jet substructure) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

In this paper we introduce a new approach to study jet substructure in the center-of-mass frame of the jet. We demonstrate that it can be used to discriminate the boosted heavy particles from the QCD jets and the method is complimentary to the existing other jet substructure algorithms. Applications to searches for hadronically decaying W/Z+jets and heavy resonance that decays to a WW final state are also discussed.
