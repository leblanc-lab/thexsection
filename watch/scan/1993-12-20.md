## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9312314](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9312314) — Single-Lepton Top Signals with a b-Tag at the Tevatron (V. Barger et al.)

_Watchlist hit (b-tagging) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Events with a leptonic W-decay plus jets should contain top-quark signals, but a QCD W+jets background must be separated. We compare transverse W-momentum, jet multiplicity, and b-tagging separation criteria, and find that the main background after tagging comes from mistagging. We illustrate how to extract the mass m_t via event reconstructions and how to confirm signal purity by lepton angular distributions.
