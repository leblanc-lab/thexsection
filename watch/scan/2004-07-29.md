## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0407346](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0407346) — Inclusive cross-sections for jet production in nucleus-nucleus collisions in perturbative QCD (M. A. Braun)

_Watchlist hit (gluon jet) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Inclusive cross-sections for gluon jet production are studied numerically in the perturbative QCD pomeron model for central collisions of identical nuclei at high energies. Two forms for the inclusive cross-sections, with and without emission from the triple pomeron vertex, are compared. The difference was found to reduce to a numerical factor 0.8$÷$ 09 for momenta $k$ below the saturation momentum $Q_s$. Above $Q_s$ no difference was found at all. The gluon spectrum was found to be $\sim A$ for $k<Q_s$ and $\sim A^{1.1}$ for $k>Q_s$. At large $k$ the spectrum falls as $1/k^{2.7÷3.3}$ flattening with energy. The multiplicities are proportional to $A$ with a good precision. Their values are high amd grow rapidly with energy.
