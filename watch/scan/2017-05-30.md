## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1705.10651](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.10651) — Measurement of $\mathcal{R}(D^*)$ with Three-Prong $τ$ Decays at LHCb (Federico Betti)

_Watchlist hit (three-prong) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The observable $\mathcal{R} ( D^{(*)} ) = \mathcal{B}\left( B^{0}\to D^{(*)-} τ^{+} ν_τ \right) / \mathcal{B}\left( B^{0}\to D^{(*)-} μ^{+} ν_μ \right)$ is a probe for Lepton Universality violation, so it is sensitive to New Physics processes. The current combination of the measurements of $\mathcal{R} ( D^{(*)} )$ differs from Standard Model predictions with a $4σ$ significance. A measurement of $\mathcal{R} ( D^* )$ using three-prong $τ$ decays is currently ongoing at LHCb. The statistical precision of this analysis is 6.7%, i.e. the smallest statistical uncertainty for a single measurement of this observable. Therefore this measurement will be important to confirm or disprove the current discrepancy from the theoretical expectations.
