## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2011.10736](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.10736) — Maximum performance of strange-jet tagging at hadron colliders (Johannes Erdmann et al.)

_Keyword hit (jet tagging) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The maximum achievable performance of strange-jet tagging at hadron colliders and the loss in performance in different detector designs is estimated based on simulated truth jets from strange-quark and down-quark hadronisation. Both jet types are classified with a recurrent neural network using long short-term memory units, at first using all available truth particles and then applying selections to study the impacts of ideal tracking detectors, Cherenkov detectors, and calorimeters. Additionally, a manual reconstruction of strange hadron decays such as $K_S\rightarrow π^+ π^-$ from charged tracks is considered.
