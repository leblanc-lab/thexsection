## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9711364](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9711364) — CP Violation from Charged Higgs Exchange in Hadronic Tau Decays with Unpolarized Beams (J. H. Kuehn et al.)

_Watchlist hit (hadronic tau) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

CP violating signals in semileptonic $τ$ decays induced by an exotic scalar exchange are studied in a completely model-independent way. These can be observed in decays of unpolarized single $τ$'s even if their rest frame cannot be reconstructed. No beam polarization is required. The importance of the two-meson channel, in particular the $Kπ$ final state is emphasized.
