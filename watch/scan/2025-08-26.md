### [arXiv:2508.19190](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19190) — Hits to Higgs: Hit-Level Higgs Classification from Raw LHC Detector Data Using Higgsformer (Sascha Caron et al.)

_Keyword hit (particle transformer) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present Higgsformer, a transformer-based architecture that classifies Higgs events at the Large Hadron Collider directly from raw inner tracker hits, bypassing the traditional reconstruction chain of intermediate physics objects. As a benchmark, we focus on distinguishing $t\bar{t}H$ from $t\bar{t}$ events with $H \to b\bar{b}$, a particularly challenging task due to their similar final state topologies. Our pipeline begins with event generation in Pythia8, fast simulation with ACTS/Fatras, and classification directly from raw detector hits. We show for the first time that a transformer model originally developed for inner tracker hit-to-track assignment can be retrained to classify Higgs signal events directly from raw hits. For comparison, we reconstruct the same events with Delphes and train a Particle Transformer as an object-based classifier. We evaluate both approaches under varying dataset sizes and pileup levels. Despite relying exclusively on inner tracker hits, our large Higgsformer achieves an AUC of $0.855$, matching the performance of the traditional reconstruction pipeline at a $b$-tagging efficiency of $\approx 40\%$ under the same detector constraints.

### [arXiv:2508.19226](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19226) — The Quark Jet Function for $k_T$-like Variables in NNLO QCD (Luca Buonocore et al.)

_Keyword hit (energy flow, jet function, quark jet) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The precise description of jet processes requires observables capable of efficiently capturing the dynamics of the energy flow in hadronic final states. We consider a class of tranverse-momentum like resolution variables that smoothly describe the $n+1$ to $n$ jet transition in multi-jet processes. We discuss a general method for the computation of the corresponding quark jet function at next-to-next-to-leading order in perturbative QCD. Rapidity divergences are regulated by using a time-like auxiliary vector. We present explicit results for a variant of $y_{23}$ in the $E$-scheme and in the WTA scheme.

### [arXiv:2508.19404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19404) — Heavy Quark Pair Energy Correlators: From Profiling Partonic Splittings to Probing Heavy-Flavor Fragmentation (João Barata et al.)

_Keyword hit (energy correlators) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We introduce heavy-flavor energy correlators, $\langle Ψ|\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{H}}(\vec{n}_1) \mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{H}}(\vec{n}_2)|Ψ\rangle$, as a powerful observable for profiling partonic splittings and characterizing heavy-flavor fragmentation. We present its collinear factorization, perform resummation, and demonstrate the angular distribution's sensitivity to both the heavy-quark pair splitting and their subsequent fragmentation. We then apply the heavy-flavor EEC to probe medium-induced effects, revealing its sensitivity to medium modifications to heavy-quark pair splitting functions and to the medium's spatial structure.
