## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ex/0605123](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0605123) — Effective Charges in Practice (Klaus Hamacher)

_Watchlist hit (event shapes) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Experimental results on event shapes obtained within (or related to) the method of Effective Charges are discussed in view of measurements of the strong coupling, alpha_s, the beta-function and non-perturbative contributions to event shapes. The data strongly advocate to use of the ECH scheme instead of the conventional MSbar scheme.
