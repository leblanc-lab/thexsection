## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:0711.3272](https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.3272) — Unparticles in Gluodynamics (A. T. Alan)

_Watchlist hit (gluon jets) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The virtual effects of unparticle physics on the two-gluon jets production are explored for CERN LHC. It is found that depending on the scale dimension, unparticles can give rise to substantial enhancements in the transverse momentum and pseudo-rapidity distributions of hadronic cross sections, up to a factor of four-orders of magnitude.
