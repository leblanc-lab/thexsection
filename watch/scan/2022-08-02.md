## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2208.01389](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.01389) — Measurement of charged-particle jet properties in p--Pb collisions at $\sqrt{s_{\rm NN}} = 5.02$ TeV with ALICE (Prottoy Das)

_Watchlist hit (jet fragmentation) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present the measurement of charged-particle jet properties in minimum bias p--Pb collisions at $\sqrt{s_{\rm NN}}$ = 5.02 TeV in ALICE. Jets are reconstructed from charged particles at midrapidity using the anti-$k_{\rm T}$ jet finding algorithm with jet resolution parameter $R$ = 0.4. The mean charged particle multiplicity and jet fragmentation function for leading charged-particle jets in the $p_{\rm T}$ interval 10 $< p_{\rm T, jet}^{\rm ch} <$ 100 GeV/$c$ are measured and compared with theoretical model predictions.
