## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:1410.6664](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.6664) — Progress in Double Parton Scattering Studies (Sunil Bansal et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Wouter Waalewijn, Andrzej Siodmok) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

An overview of theoretical and experimental progress in double parton scattering (DPS) is presented. The theoretical topics cover factorization in DPS, models for double parton distributions and DPS in charm production and nuclear collisions. On the experimental side, CMS results for dijet and double J/ψ production, in light of DPS, as well as first results for the 4-jet channel are presented. ALICE reports on a study of open charm and J/ψ multiplicity dependence.

### [arXiv:1410.6808](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.6808) — Neutral Naturalness from the Orbifold Higgs (Nathaniel Craig et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Nathaniel Craig) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present a general class of natural theories in which the Higgs is a pseudo-goldstone boson in an orbifolded gauge theory. The symmetry protecting the Higgs at low energies is an accidental global symmetry of the quadratic action, rather than a full continuous symmetry. The lightest degrees of freedom protecting the weak scale carry no Standard Model (SM) quantum numbers and interact with visible matter principally through the Higgs portal. We find that the twin Higgs is the simplest example of an orbifold Higgs. This opens the door to the systematic study of "neutral naturalness": natural theories with SM-neutral states that are as yet untested by the LHC.
