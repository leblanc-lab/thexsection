## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1202.4213](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.4213) — Dijet signals of the Little Higgs model with T-parity (Debajyoti Choudhury et al.)

_Watchlist hit (flavor tagging) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The Littest Higgs model with T-parity (LHT), apart from offering a viable solution to the naturalness problem of the Standard Model, also predicts a set of new fermions as well as a candidate for dark matter. We explore the possibility of discovering the heavy T-odd quark Q_H at the LHC in a final state comprising two hard jets with a large missing transverse momentum. Also discussed is the role of heavy flavor tagging.
