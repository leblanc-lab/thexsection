## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1107.1808](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.1808) — Application of Jet Trimming in Boosted Higgs Search (Wenhan Zhu)

_Watchlist hit (jet trimming, boosted Higgs) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present the study of the $WH$ and $ZH$ search with the Higgs Boson decayed to $b\bar{b}$ at the Large Hadron Collider. The Higgs Boson and the Vector Boson are required to be boosted, and the Higgs Boson is reconstructed with Jet Trimming Technique. The statistical significance for 30$fb^{-1}$ data is 4.5 $σ$, which is comparable to the previous result.
