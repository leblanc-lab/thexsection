## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:2409.03824](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.03824) — Supergroup Symmetries and the Hierarchy Problem (Nathaniel Craig et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Nathaniel Craig) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We show that the mass of a scalar field transforming in the fundamental of an $SU(N|N+1)$ supergroup internal symmetry is protected against one-loop corrections from quartic, gauge, and yukawa interactions. Although the negative-norm states responsible for this protection are likely fatal to a unitary interpretation of the theory, such ultraviolet insensitivity may open a new avenue to understanding the lightness of the Higgs.
