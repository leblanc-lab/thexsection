## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0407187](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0407187) — Rise and Fall of the Bottom Quark Production Excess (Matteo Cacciari)

_Watchlist hit (Matteo Cacciari) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We review the history of comparisons between bottom production measurements and QCD predictions. We challenge the existence of a `significant discrepancy', and argue that standard approaches to QCD calculations do a good job in describing the experimental findings.

## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0407193](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0407193) — Central inclusive dijets production by double pomeron exchange. Comparison with the CDF results (Adam Bzdak)

_Watchlist hit (gluon jets) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Using the Landshoff-Nachtmann model of the pomeron, rapidity and transverse momentum distributions of gluon jets produced by double pomeron exchange in pp collisions are calculated. The comparison with the CDF Run I results on the central inclusive dijets production cross sections is performed. We find the model to give correct order of magnitude for the measured cross sections.
