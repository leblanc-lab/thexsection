## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1605.04138](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.04138) — Constraints on interacting dark energy models from Planck 2015 and redshift-space distortion data (André A. Costa et al.)

_Watchlist hit (energy flow) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We investigate phenomenological interactions between dark matter and dark energy and constrain these models by employing the most recent cosmological data including the cosmic microwave background radiation anisotropies from Planck 2015, Type Ia supernovae, baryon acoustic oscillations, the Hubble constant and redshift-space distortions. We find that the interaction in the dark sector parameterized as an energy transfer from dark matter to dark energy is strongly suppressed by the whole updated cosmological data. On the other hand, an interaction between dark sectors with the energy flow from dark energy to dark matter is proved in better agreement with the available cosmological observations. This coupling between dark sectors is needed to alleviate the coincidence problem.

### [arXiv:1605.04280](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.04280) — Boosted-bottom jet tagging and BSM searches (Zack Sullivan)

_Watchlist hit (jet substructure, jet tagging) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

I present a new scheme for tagging boosted heavy flavor jets called "$μ_x$ tagging" and its application to TeV-scale physics beyond the Standard Model. Using muons from B hadron decay to define a particular combination "x" of angular information, and jet substructure variables, we identify a clean ($ε_{\mathrm{fake}}/ε_b\sim 1/100$) good efficiency ($ε_b = 14\%$) tag. I demonstrate the usefulness of this new scheme by showing the reach for discovery of leptophobic $Z^\prime\to b\bar b$ and $tH^\pm\to t t b$.
