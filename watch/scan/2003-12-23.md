## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0312331](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0312331) — Effective theory approach to unstable particle production (M. Beneke et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Giulia Zanderighi) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Using the hierarchy of scales between the mass, $M$, and the width, $Γ$, of a heavy, unstable particle we construct an effective theory that allows calculations for resonant processes to be systematically expanded in powers of the coupling $α$ and $Γ/M$. We illustrate the method by computing the next-to-leading order line shape of a scalar resonance in an abelian gauge-Yukawa model.
