## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ex/0106036](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0106036) — Jets and Event Shape Studies in ep-collisions at HERA (Roman Poeschl)

_Watchlist hit (event shape) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The results of complementary tests of perturbative QCD in DIS at large centre-of-mass energies at HERA are presented. The analysis of event shape variables allows for the investigation of approaches to understand aspects of non-perturbative QCD such as the formation of the hadronic final state. The strong coupling constant, $α_s$, is extracted from both inclusive jet cross sections and dijet rates and is found to be competitive with the world average.
