### [arXiv:2610.07145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07145) — The Back-to-Back Plateau of the Energy-Energy Correlator from Weak to Strong Coupling (Hao Chen et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Ian Moult) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The back-to-back limit of the energy-energy correlator (EEC) exhibits a finite plateau, capturing the evolution from perturbative soft-collinear radiation at weak coupling to string dynamics at strong coupling. At weak coupling, we resum its leading and next-to-leading-power logarithms through N$^3$LL in $\mathcal N=4$ SYM, including non-planar contributions. Combining this weak-coupling endpoint with bootstrap and strong-coupling data, we construct an interpolation for the plateau height from weak to strong coupling. The calculation uses a human-in-the-loop agentic workflow.

### [arXiv:2610.07150](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07150) — Monopole Catalysis from Standard Model Anomalies (Nathaniel Craig et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Nathaniel Craig) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We study the generalized symmetries associated with Standard Model flavor and their fate in the presence of magnetic monopoles. This illuminates a striking phenomenological consequence: even when baryon and lepton number are classically conserved, the electroweak anomaly induces $\Delta B = \Delta L \in 3 \mathbb{Z}$ in monopole-fermion scattering for any monopole activating the anomaly. We demonstrate this mechanism in Pati-Salam theory and trinification.
