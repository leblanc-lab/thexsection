## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2509.02669](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02669) — Flow between extremal one-point energy correlators in QCD (Marc Riembau et al.)

_Keyword hit (energy correlators) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The energy density generated by a vector current is characterized by a single parameter $a_{\mathcal{E}}$ bounded by unitarity to $-1/2 \leq a_{\mathcal{E}} \leq 1$, with extremal values saturated by free theories of different matter content. Through confinement, QCD transmutes fermionic matter into scalars, revealing a nontrivial flow between extremal correlators. We reconstruct this flow using perturbative QCD and chiral perturbation theory. The observable is accessible with currently available experimental data.

### [arXiv:2509.02790](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02790) — Perturbative QCD (Gudrun Heinrich et al.)

_Keyword hit (event shape) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We give an introduction to perturbative Quantum Chromodynamics, focusing on a pedagogical description of concepts and methods to calculate cross sections measured at high energy colliders. After introducing basic concepts that allow for a perturbative expansion, such as factorisation and asymptotic freedom, we introduce loop integrals and the treatment of ultraviolet and infrared divergences in QCD. The definition of jets and event shape observables is also discussed. Finally, we give a brief overview of the current state of the art.
