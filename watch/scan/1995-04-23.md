## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9504366](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9504366) — Quark and gluon jet spectra in $γγ$ collisions (M. Dubinin)

_Watchlist hit (gluon jet) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We consider three jet production at high $p_T$ in the $γγ\to q \bar q g$ process and calculate quark and gluon jet spectra. The possibility of quark-gluon jet separation is discussed and compared with the $e^+ e^- \to q \bar q g$ case.
