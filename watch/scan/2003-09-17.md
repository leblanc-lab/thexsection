## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0309193](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0309193) — Jet calculus problems of the perturbative quantum chromodynamics (I. M. Dremin)

_Watchlist hit (quark and gluon jets, gluon jets) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The perturbative quantum chromodynamics (pQCD) has been extremely successful in the prediction and description of main properties of quark and gluon jets. There are, however, some problems of the jet calculus with the higher order corrections of the modified perturbative expansion which should be resolved to get more precise statements. Some of them are discussed here.

## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0309204](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0309204) — Higgs Radiation off Bottom Quarks at the Tevatron and the LHC (Stefan Dittmaier et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Michael Krämer) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Higgs-boson production in association with bottom quarks, ppbar/pp -> b bbar H + X, is one of the most important discovery channels for supersymmetric Higgs particles at the Tevatron and the LHC. We have calculated the next-to-leading order QCD corrections to the parton processes qqbar,gg -> bbbar H and present results for total cross sections and for distributions in the transverse momenta of the bottom quarks. The QCD corrections reduce the renormalization and factorization scale dependence and thus stabilize the theoretical predictions, especially when the Higgs boson is produced in association with high-pT bottom quarks. The next-to-leading order predictions for the total cross section are in reasonable numerical agreement with calculations based on bottom-quark fusion bbbar ->H.
