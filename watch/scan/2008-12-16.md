## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:0812.3114](https://arxiv.org/abs/0812.3114) — Estimate of the systematic error in tau decay due to duality violations (Oscar Cata et al.)

_Watchlist hit (hadronic tau) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

With the help of a physically motivated ansatz, which is fitted to the data, we estimate the size of possible duality violations in hadronic tau decay. The result is that the uncertainty associated with these violations could impact the alpha_s determination from the total decay width at a level which we estimate to be δalpha_s(m_tau) \sim 0.003-0.010. Consequently, it cannot be neglected.
