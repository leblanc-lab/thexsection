## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9409456](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9409456) — Physics at Low x (J. Kwieciński)

_Watchlist hit (energy flow) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The QCD expectations concerning the deep inelastic lepton - hadron scattering at low $x$ and their phenomenological implications for HERA are summarised. Theoretical predictions for the structure function $F_2(x,Q^2)$ based on the leading log$1/x$ resummation are presented and compared with the results obtained from the Altarelli-Parisi equations. Theoretical predictions are confronted with the recent data from HERA. The role of studying the final states in deep inelastic scattering for revealing the dynamics at low $x$ is emphasised and some dedicated measurements like deep inelastic plus jet events, transverse energy flow and dijet production in deep inelastic scattering are discussed.
