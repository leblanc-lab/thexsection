## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0408044](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0408044) — V_us and m_s from hadronic tau decays (E. Gamiz et al.)

_Watchlist hit (hadronic tau) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Recent experimental results on hadronic tau decays into strange particles by the OPAL collaboration are employed to determine V_us and m_s from moments of the invariant mass distribution. Our results are V_us = 0.2208+-0.0034 and m_s(2 GeV) = 81+-22 MeV. The error on V_us is dominated by experiment, and should be improvable in the future. Nevertheless, already now our result is competitive to the standard extraction of V_us from K_e3 decays, and it is compatible with unitarity.
