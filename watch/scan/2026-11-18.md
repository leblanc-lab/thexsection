### [arXiv:2611.21763](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21763) — A new angle on coherence-violating logarithms in color-singlet processes (Mrinal Dasgupta et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Mrinal Dasgupta) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We introduce a new formulation for the resummation of coherence-violating logarithms for the transverse energy flow in rapidity gaps in the Drell-Yan process. Our formalism allows us to resum coherence-violating logarithms in the gap cross section in the phenomenologically important regime where the gap size is comparable to the logarithm of the transverse energy. In the formal small gap limit, our result reduces to the known state-of-the-art resummation of coherence-violating logarithms for the Drell-Yan process. However, we identify sizable new corrections even for moderate gap widths that significantly modify the phenomenological picture for LHC applications. This work will form an important element of future extensions to a range of global and non-global observables.
