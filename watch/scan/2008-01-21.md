## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:0801.3278](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.3278) — General Gauge Mediation (Patrick Meade et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Patrick Meade, David Shih) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We give a general definition of gauge mediated supersymmetry breaking which encompasses all the known gauge mediation models. In particular, it includes both models with messengers as well as direct mediation models. A formalism for computing the soft terms in the generic model is presented. Such a formalism is necessary in strongly-coupled direct mediation models where perturbation theory cannot be used. It allows us to identify features of the entire class of gauge mediation models and to distinguish them from specific signatures of various subclasses.

## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:0801.3281](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.3281) — Discriminating Supersymmetry and Black Holes at the Large Hadron Collider (Arunava Roy et al.)

_Watchlist hit (event shape) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We show how to differentiate the minimal supersymmetric extension of the standard model from black hole events at the Large Hadron Collider. Black holes are simulated with the CATFISH generator. Supersymmetry simulations use a combination of PYTHIA and ISAJET. Our study, based on event shape variables, visible and missing momenta, and analysis of dilepton events, demonstrates that supersymmetry and black hole events at the LHC can be easily discriminated.
