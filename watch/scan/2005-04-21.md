## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0504186](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0504186) — In Search of Lonely Top Quarks at the Tevatron (Matthew T. Bowen et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Matthew Strassler) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Single top-quark production, via weak-interaction processes, is an important test of the standard model, potentially sensitive to new physics. However, this measurement is much more challenging at the Tevatron than originally expected. We reexamine this process and suggest new methods, using shape variables, that can supplement the methods that have been discussed previously.

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0504192](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0504192) — Crossing Heavy-Flavour Thresholds in Fragmentation Functions (Matteo Cacciari et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Matteo Cacciari) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

In analogy with parton distribution functions, also parton fragmentation functions obey matching conditions when crossing heavy-flavour thresholds. We compute these matching conditions at next-to-leading order in the strong coupling constant alpha_s in the MSbar scheme. Our results can be used for the dynamical generation of the heavy-flavour component in next-to-leading order fits to light-hadrons fragmentation functions. Furthermore, when computing perturbatively the charm fragmentation function from first principles and evolving it to higher scales, our matching conditions should be used for consistency when crossing the bottom threshold.
