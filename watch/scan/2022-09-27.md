### [arXiv:2209.13128](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.13128) — Report of the Topical Group on Physics Beyond the Standard Model at Energy Frontier for Snowmass 2021 (Tulika Bose et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Nathaniel Craig, Cari Cesarotti, Mike Williams, Nhan Tran, Philip Harris, Timothy Cohen) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

This is the Snowmass2021 Energy Frontier (EF) Beyond the Standard Model (BSM) report. It combines the EF topical group reports of EF08 (Model-specific explorations), EF09 (More general explorations), and EF10 (Dark Matter at Colliders). The report includes a general introduction to BSM motivations and the comparative prospects for proposed future experiments for a broad range of potential BSM models and signatures, including compositeness, SUSY, leptoquarks, more general new bosons and fermions, long-lived particles, dark matter, charged-lepton flavor violation, and anomaly detection.

_Watchlist hit (Gian Michele Innocenti) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._
