## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9311308](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9311308) — Hadronic Event Shapes in $B$ Decay (Michael Luke et al.)

_Watchlist hit (event shapes) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We compute the differential momentum correlation function for hadrons produced in the decay of $B$-mesons. This measure of hadronic event shapes tests the free $b$-quark decay picture for nonleptonic and semileptonic decays of $B$-mesons. Our results can also be applied to $B_s$ and $Λ_b$ decay.
