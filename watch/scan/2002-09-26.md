## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ex/0209073](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0209073) — Jet measurements at DO using a KT algorithm (V. Daniel Elvira)

_Watchlist hit (subjet, jet algorithm, quark and gluon jets, quark jets, gluon jets) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

DO has implemented and calibrated a KT jet algorithm for the first time in a ppbar collider. We present two results based on 1992-1996 data which were recently published: the subjet multiplicity in quark and gluon jets and the central inclusive jet cross section. The measured ratio between subjet multiplicities in gluon and quark jets is consistent with theoretical predictions and previous experimental values. NLO pQCD predictions of the KT inclusive jet cross section agree with the DO measurement, although marginally in the low pT range. We also present a preliminary measurement of thrust cross sections, which indicates the need to include higher than alpha_s^3 terms and resumation in the theoretical calculations.

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0209313](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0209313) — Recent progress in QCD at low energies (B. L. Ioffe)

_Watchlist hit (hadronic tau) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The recent results in QCD at low energies are reported. The theoretical analysis of hadronic tau-decay is performed in complex q^2-plane. The terms of perturbation theory (PT) up to alpha^3_s are accounted, the terms of operator product expansion (OPE) -- up do dimension 8. The momenta of charmed quark vector current polarization operator are analyzed in 3 loops with account of dimension 4 and 6 OPE terms. The main conclusion is that in the range of low-energy phenomena under consideration PT and OPE are in good agreement with experiment starting from Q^2=-q^2 ~ 1 GeV^2. The values of alpha_s(m^2_tau), alpha_s<qq>^2, <(alpha_s/pi)G^2> and m_c(m_c)were found.
