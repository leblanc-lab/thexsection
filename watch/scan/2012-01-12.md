## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1201.2688](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.2688) — Angular Scaling in Jets (Martin Jankowiak et al.)

_Watchlist hit (jet shape, infrared and collinear safe) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We introduce a jet shape observable defined for an ensemble of jets in terms of two-particle angular correlations and a resolution parameter R. This quantity is infrared and collinear safe and can be interpreted as a scaling exponent for the angular distribution of mass inside the jet. For small R it is close to the value 2 as a consequence of the approximately scale invariant QCD dynamics. For large R it is sensitive to non-perturbative effects. We describe the use of this correlation function for tests of QCD, for studying underlying event and pile-up effects, and for tuning Monte Carlo event generators.
