## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0109180](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0109180) — Effects of jet algorithms from higher order QCD in $W^\pm$ mass determinations at LEP2 (E. Maina et al.)

_Watchlist hit (jet clustering, jet algorithms) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We analyse the impact of systematic effects due to the scale dependence of QCD corrections in combination with the use of different jet clustering algorithms in the measurement of the $W^\pm$ mass in the fully hadronic decay mode of $W^+W^-$ pairs produced at LEP2. We consider higher order contributions induced by both virtual and real gluon radiation onto the electroweak CC03 and CC11 channels through ${\cal O}(α_{s})$ at the parton level. We find that the associated uncertainties can be of order 100 MeV, thus competitive with those possibly arising in the non-perturbative regime and indeed above the current experimental estimates.
