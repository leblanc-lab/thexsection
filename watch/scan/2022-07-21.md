## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2207.10632](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.10632) — Open Heavy Flavor Studies for the ECCE Detector at the Electron Ion Collider (X. Li et al.)

_Watchlist hit (jet reconstruction) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The ECCE detector has been recommended as the selected reference detector for the future Electron-Ion Collider (EIC). A series of simulation studies have been carried out to validate the physics feasibility of the ECCE detector. In this paper, detailed studies of heavy flavor hadron and jet reconstruction and physics projections with the ECCE detector performance and different magnet options will be presented. The ECCE detector has enabled precise EIC heavy flavor hadron and jet measurements with a broad kinematic coverage. These proposed heavy flavor measurements will help systematically study the hadronization process in vacuum and nuclear medium especially in the underexplored kinematic region.
