## Muon collider

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9802368](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9802368) — Narrow Technihadron Production at the First Muon Collider (Estia Eichten et al.)

_Watchlist hit (muon collider) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

In modern technicolor models, there exist very narrow spin-zero and spin-one neutral technihadrons---$pi^0_T$, $rho^0_T$ and $omega_T$---with masses of a few 100 GeV. The large coupling of $π^0_T$ to $μ^+μ^-$, the direct coupling of $rho^0_T$ and $omega_T$ to the photon and $Z^0$, and the superb energy resolution of the First Muon Collider may make it possible to resolve these technihadrons and produce them at extraordinarily large rates.
