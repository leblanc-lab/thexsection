## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:1803.06347](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.06347) — Electroweak Gauge Boson Parton Distribution Functions (Bartosz Fornal et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Wouter Waalewijn) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Transverse and longitudinal electroweak gauge boson parton distribution functions (PDFs) are computed in terms of deep-inelastic scattering structure functions, following the recently developed method to determine the photon PDF. The calculation provides initial conditions at the electroweak scale for PDF evolution to higher energies. Numerical results for the $W^\pm$ and $Z$ transverse, longitudinal and polarized PDFs, as well as the $γZ$ transverse and polarized PDFs are presented.
