## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9402303](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9402303) — Quark Fragmentation Functions in Low-Energy Chiral Theory (Xiangdong Ji et al.)

_Watchlist hit (quark jet) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We examine the physics content of fragmentation functions for inclusive hadron production in a quark jet and argue that it can be calculated in low energy effective theories. As an example, we present a calculation of $u$-quark fragmentation to $π^+$ and $π^-$ mesons in the lowest order in the chiral quark model. The comparison between our result and experimental data is encouraging.
