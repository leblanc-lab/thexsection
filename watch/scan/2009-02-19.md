## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:0902.3322](https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.3322) — Kaluza-Klein gluon and b-jet forward-backward asymmetry (Gi-Chol Cho et al.)

_Watchlist hit (b-jet, quark jets) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The forward-backward asymmetry of $b$-quark jets on the $Z$-pole measured at LEP/SLD experiments shows us -2.8-$σ$ deviation from the Standard Model (SM) prediction. We examine a possibility of Kaluza-Klein (KK) gluon to explain the $\afbb$ data in a scenario based on the warped extra dimension model by Randall and Sundrum. In this scenario, the KK gluon strongly couples to $b$-quark by an appropriate choice of the bulk quark mass terms. We find that the $\afbb$ data could be explained if the KK gluon mass is few hundred GeV. Constraints on our scenario from the hadron collider experiments are discussed.
