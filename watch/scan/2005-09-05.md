## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0509034](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0509034) — Testing saturation with large-mass diffraction in DIS (C. Marquet et al.)

_Watchlist hit (gluon jet) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

In large-mass diffraction in deep inelastic scattering, we study the gluon jet close to the rapidity gap and show that its transverse momentum spectrum is peaked around a value which caracterizes the onset of S-matrix unitarity. We argue that such a measurement of diffractive jets could help understanding if at the present energies, unitarity comes as a consequence of saturation.
