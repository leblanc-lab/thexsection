## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0311247](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0311247) — Automated resummation of jet observables in QCD (Andrea Banfi)

_Watchlist hit (jet shape) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._
