## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0106018](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0106018) — Physics at Large Rapidities in $γ^*γ^* \to$ Hadrons at LEP2 (M. Cacciari et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Matteo Cacciari) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We compute the order-$\as$ corrections to the total cross section and to jet rates in $γ^* γ^* \to$ hadrons for the process $\epem\to\epem +$ hadrons. We use a NLO order general-purpose partonic Monte Carlo event generator that allows the computation of a rate differential in the produced leptons and hadrons. We compare our results with the experimental data for $\epem\to\epem +$ hadrons at LEP2.
