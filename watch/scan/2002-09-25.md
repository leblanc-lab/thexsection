## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ex/0209070](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0209070) — Measurements of $α_s$ from event shapes and the four-jet rate (G. Dissertori)

_Watchlist hit (event shapes) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

New results from measurements of the strong coupling constant $α_s(M_Z)$ at LEP are presented. In particular, a new LEP combination of results based on event-shape variables has become available, where a new method for the estimation of the theoretical uncertainty has been implemented. Furthermore, two other analyses are quoted, based on power corrections and the four-jet rate.
