## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9808430](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9808430) — Six-jet production at lepton colliders (Stefano Moretti)

_Watchlist hit (jet clustering) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We study electron-positron annihilations into six jets at the parton level in perturbative Quantum Chromo-Dynamics (QCD), via the elementary processes $e^+e^-\ar q\bar q gggg$, $e^+e^-\ar q\bar q q'\bar q' gg$ and $e^+e^-\ar q\bar q q'\bar q' q''\bar q''$, for massive quarks $q, q'$ and $q''$ and massless gluons $g$. Several numerical results of phenomenological relevance are given, at three different collider energies and for a representative selection of jet clustering algorithms. We also present helicity amplitudes and colour factors needed for the tree-level calculation
