## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9910412](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9910412) — Soft-Gluon Resummation in Heavy Quarkonium Physics (Matteo Cacciari)

_Watchlist hit (Matteo Cacciari) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Soft-gluon resummation within the framework of heavy quarkonium hadroproduction is considered. A few selected cases are studied in detail. A sizeable increase of the cross sections with respect to the next-to-leading order predictions with central factorization/renormalization scale choice can generally be observed. Improvements in the dependence of the cross sections on the two scales, especially when they are kept equal, are also found.

## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9910414](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9910414) — Interjet Rapidity Gaps in Perturbative QCD (Gianluca Oderda et al.)

_Watchlist hit (energy flow) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We discuss a formalism in which high-$p_T$ dijet rapidity gaps are identified by energy flow in the interjet region. When the gap energy, $Q_{\rm gap}$, is sufficiently large, the cross section may be computed from standard factorization theorems. For $p_T\gg Q_T\ggΛ_{\rm QCD}$, this is a two-scale perturbative problem, in which we may resum logarithms of $Q_{\rm gap}/p_T$. The cross section computed as a function of $Q_{\rm gap}$ reproduces many of the features of the Tevatron dijet gap data. The factorized cross section gives meaning to the color content of the hard scattering.
