## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0007338](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0007338) — Tests of large-Nc QCD from hadronic tau decay (S. Peris et al.)

_Watchlist hit (hadronic tau) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We use the Aleph data on vector and axial-vector spectral functions to test simple duality properties of QCD in the large Nc limit, which emerge in the approximation of a minimal hadronic ansatz of a spectrum of narrow states. These duality properties relate the short- and long-distance behaviours of specific correlation functions, which are order parameters of spontaneous chiral symmetry breaking, in a way that we find well supported by the data.
