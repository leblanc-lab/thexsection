## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2001.05309](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.05309) — Study of the semi-boosted topology in top anti-top quark pair spectra (Josef Pácalt)

_Keyword hit (boosted top) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The aim of this contribution is to present results of adding the semi-boosted transition region between the resolved and boosted top quark pair reconstruction in the $\ell$ + jets channel. This region is often omitted due to more complex reconstruction, which usually leads to a smaller efficiency. The reconstruction is performed for all three topologies for comparison and combination purposes. Hypothetical particle $Z'$ was used as a probe to study the increase in the number of events in the studied energy region.
