## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ex/9908038](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/9908038) — A Preliminary Direct Measurement of the s-Quark Coupling (The SLD Collaboration et al.)

_Watchlist hit (thrust axis) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present an updated direct measurement of the parity-violating coupling of the Z boson to strange quarks, A_s, derived from the full SLD data sample of approximately 550,000 hadronic decays of Z bosons produced with a polarized electron beam and recorded by the SLD experiment at SLAC between 1993 and 1998. Events with primary strange quarks are tagged by the presence in each event hemisphere of a high-momentum charged K meson, neutral K meson or Lambda hyperon identified using the Cherenkov Ring Imaging Detector and/or a mass tag. The CCD vertex detector is used to suppress the background from heavy flavor events. The strangeness of the tagged particle is used to sign the event thrust axis in the direction of the initial strange quark. The coupling A_s is obtained directly from a measurement of the left-right-forward-backward production asymmetry in polar angle of the tagged strange quark. The background from primary up and down events is measured from the data, as is the analyzing power of the method for primary strange events. We measure: A_s = 0.85 +- 0.06(stat.) +- 0.07(syst.) (preliminary).

## Muon collider

### [arXiv:hep-ex/9908041](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/9908041) — Muon Colliders: New Prospects for Precision Physics and the High Energy Frontier (Bruce J. King)

_Watchlist hit (muon collider) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

An overview is given of muon collider technology and of the current status of the muon collider research program. The exciting potential of muon colliders for both neutrino physics and collider physics studies is then described and illustrated using self-consistent collider parameter sets at 0.1 TeV to 100 TeV center-of-mass energies.

### [arXiv:hep-ex/9908042](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/9908042) — Muon Colliders: the Ultimate Neutrino Beamlines (Bruce J. King)

_Watchlist hit (muon collider) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

It is shown that muon decays in straight sections of muon collider rings will naturally produce highly collimated neutrino beams that can be several orders of magnitude stronger than the beams at existing accelerators. We discuss possible experimental setups and give a very brief overview of the physics potential from such beamlines. Formulae are given for the neutrino event rates at both short and long baseline neutrino experiments in these beams.
