## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1401.5930](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.5930) — Decays of polarized top quarks to lepton, neutrino and jets at NLO QCD (W. Bernreuther et al.)

_Watchlist hit (b-jet) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We compute the differential and total rate of the semileptonic decay of polarized top-quarks $t\to \ell ν_\ell + b{\rm jet} + {\rm jet}$ at next-to-leading order (NLO) in the QCD coupling with an off-shell intermediate $W$ boson. We present several normalized distributions, in particular those that reflect the $t$-spin analyzing powers of the lepton, the b-jet and the $W^+$ boson at LO and NLO QCD.
