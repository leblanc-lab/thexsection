## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9610307](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9610307) — Hadronic Final States in e+e- Interactions and their Relevance to Cosmic Rays (Claus Grupen)

_Watchlist hit (quark and gluon jets, gluon jets) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Elementary particle physics using beams from accelerators and cosmic rays are compared. In particular, recent results on the energy evolution of charged particle multiplicities, the differences between quark and gluon jets, and scaling violations observed in electron-positron annihilation are described.

## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:hep-ph/9610313](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9610313) — Stop decays in SUSY-QCD (W. Beenakker et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Tilman Plehn) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

The partial widths are determined for stop decays to top quarks and gluinos, and gluino decays to stop particles and top quarks (depending on the masses of the particles involved). The widths are calculated including one-loop SUSY-QCD corrections. The radiative corrections for these strong-interaction decays are compared with the SUSY-QCD corrections for electroweak stop decays to quarks and neutralinos/charginos and top-quark decays to stops and neutralinos.
