## Muon collider

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0405021](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0405021) — Higgs production at a muon collider in the Two Higgs Doublet Model of type II (Carlos A. Marín)

_Watchlist hit (muon collider) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We calculate Higgs production cross sections at a muon collider in the Two Higgs Doublet Model of type II. The most interesting productions channels are mu^- mu^+ --> h^0 Z^0, H^0 Z^0, H^- H^+, A^0 Z^0 and H^{-/+} W^{+/-}. The last channel is compared with the production processes p pbar --> H^{-/+} W^{+/-} X and p p --> H^{-/+} W^{+/-} X at the Tevatron and LHC energies, respectively, for large values of tan(beta).
