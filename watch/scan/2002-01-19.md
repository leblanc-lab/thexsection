## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0201180](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0201180) — Threshold Top Quark Production (Iain W. Stewart)

_Watchlist hit (Iain Stewart) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Predictions for the total cross section for e+e- -> t tbar near threshold are reviewed. The renormalization group improved results at NNLL order have improved convergence and reduced scale dependence relative to fixed order results at NNLO. Prospects for measurements of the top-quark mass, width, and Yukawa coupling are discussed.
