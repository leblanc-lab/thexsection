## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:0805.2267](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.2267) — Analytical studies for non-perturbative QCD of jets at hadron colliders (Mrinal Dasgupta et al.)

_Watchlist hit (event shape, jet algorithms) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Inspired by the success of analytical models for non-perturbative effects, used to investigate event shape variables at LEP and HERA, we apply them to a study of jets at hadron colliders such as the Tevatron and the LHC. We find that simple analytical estimates are able to shed considerable light on issues that could previously be tackled only through Monte-Carlo simulations, for example the role of different non-perturbative effects in various jet algorithms. In this context, we also provide testable numerical results for the commonly studied inclusive-jet $p_t$ distribution, and we introduce new observables that could be employed to verify our calculations.

### [arXiv:0805.2302](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.2302) — Bs Mixing, DeltaGamma_s and CP Violation (G. P. Di Giovanni)

_Watchlist hit (flavor tagging) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We discuss the results from the Tevatron experiments on mixing and {\sf CP} violation in the $B_s-\bar{B}_s^0$ system, with particular emphasis to the first measurements of the decay width-difference $ΔΓ_s$ and the {\sf CP} violating phase $β_s$ using flavor tagging information. We also briefly review the charge asymmetry measurements in semileptonic $B_s$ decays and in $B^\pm \to J/ψK^\pm$ decays.
