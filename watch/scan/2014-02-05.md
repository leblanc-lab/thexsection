## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1402.1043](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.1043) — NNLO low-energy constants from flavor-breaking chiral sum rules based on hadronic tau-decay data (M. Golterman et al.)

_Watchlist hit (hadronic tau) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

Using spectral data from non-strange and strange hadronic tau decays, flavor-breaking chiral sum rules involving the flavor ud and us current-current two-point functions are constructed and used to determine the SU(3) NNLO low-energy constant combinations C_{61}^r, C_{12}^r+C_{61}^r+C_{80}^r and C_{12}^r-C_{61}^r+C_{80}^r. The first of these determinations updates the results of an earlier analysis by Durr and Kambor, while the latter two are new. The error on the C_{12}^r+C_{61}^r+C_{80}^r is particularly small. Comparisons are made to model estimates for these quantities. The role of the third combination in significantly improving the determination of the NLO low-energy constant L_{10}^r from NNLO analyses of the flavor ud V-A correlator is also highlighted.

### [arXiv:1402.1189](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.1189) — Finding physics signals with event deconstruction (Davison E. Soper et al.)

_Watchlist hit (boosted top) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We develop a matrix element based reconstruction method called event deconstruction. The method uses information from the hard matrix element and a parton shower to assign probabilities to whether a final state was initiated by a signal or background process. We apply this method in the signal process of a Z' decaying to boosted top quarks in an all hadronic final state and discuss envisioned improvements of the method. We find that event deconstruction can considerably improve on existing reconstruction techniques.
