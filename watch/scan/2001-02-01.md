## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ex/0102001](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0102001) — BSAURUS- A Package For Inclusive B-Reconstruction in DELPHI (Z. Albrecht et al.)

_Watchlist hit (b-jets) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

BSAURUS is a software package for the inclusive reconstruction of B-hadrons in Z-decay events taken by the DELPHI detector at LEP. The BSAURUS goal is to reconstruct B-decays, by making use of as many properties of b-jets as possible, with high efficiency and good purity. This is achieved by exploiting the capabilities of the DELPHI detector to their extreme, applying wherever possible physics knowledge about B production and decays and combining different information sources with modern tools- mainly artificial neural networks. This note provides a reference of how BSAURUS outputs are formed, how to access them within the DELPHI framework, and the physics performance one can expect.
