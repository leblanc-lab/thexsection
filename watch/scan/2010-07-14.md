## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1007.2384](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.2384) — Determining $α_s$ at NNLO from Event Shape Data (Gionata Luisoni)

_Watchlist hit (event shape, event shapes) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We report on several methodologically different NNLO determinations of the strong coupling constant from event shapes and related observables in e+e- annihilation. The event shape distributions are analyzed within a combined framework of NNLO and NNLA resummation. We also investigate the role of hadronisation corrections, using both Monte Carlo generator predictions and analytic models to parametrise non-perturbative power corrections.
