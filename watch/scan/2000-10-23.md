## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0010267](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0010267) — Near-to-planar 3-jet events in and beyond QCD perturbation theory (A. Banfi et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Giulia Zanderighi, Andrea Banfi) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present the results of QCD analysis of out-of-event-plane momentum distribution in 3-jet e+e- annihilation events. We consider the all-order resummed perturbative prediction and the leading power suppressed non-perturbative corrections to the mean value <K_out> and the distribution and explain their non-trivial colour structure. The technique we develop aims at improving the accuracy of the theoretical description of multi-jet ensembles, in particular in hadron-hadron collisions.
