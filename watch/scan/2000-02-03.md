## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ex/0002012](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0002012) — QCD studies with e+e- annihilation data at 172-189 GeV (The OPAL collaboration et al.)

_Watchlist hit (event shape) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We have studied hadronic events from e+e- annihilation data at centre-of-mass energies of sqrt{s}=172, 183 and 189 GeV. The total integrated luminosity of the three samples, measured with the OPAL detector, corresponds to 250 pb^-1. We present distributions of event shape variables, charged particle multiplicity and momentum, measured separately in the three data samples. From these we extract measurements of the strong coupling alpha_s, the mean charged particle multiplicity <nch> and the peak position xi_0 in the xi_p=ln(1/x_p) distribution. In general the data are described well by analytic QCD calculations and Monte Carlo models. Our measured values of alpha_s, <nch> and xi_0 are consistent with previous determinations at sqrt{s}=MZ.

## Muon collider

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0002042](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0002042) — Higgs Boson Decays to tau-pairs in the s-channel at a Muon Collider (V. Barger et al.)

_Watchlist hit (muon collider) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We study the observability of the $\tautau$ decay mode of a Higgs boson produced in the $s$-channel at a muon collider. We find that the spin correlations of the $\tautau$ in $τ\to πν_τ, ρν_τ$ decays are discriminative between the Higgs boson signal and the Standard Model background. Observation of the predicted distinctive distribution can confirm the spin-0 nature of the Higgs resonance. The relative coupling strength of the Higgs boson to $b$ and $τ$ can also be experimentally determined.
