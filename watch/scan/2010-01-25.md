## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1001.4467](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.4467) — Electroweak corrections to hadronic event shapes (Ansgar Denner et al.)

_Watchlist hit (event shapes, thrust distribution) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We report on a recent calculation of the electroweak O(α^3α_s) corrections to 3-jet production and related event-shape observables at e+e- colliders. The calculation properly accounts for the experimental photon isolation criteria and for the corrections to the total hadronic cross section. Corrections to the normalised event-shape distributions, which are exemplarily discussed here for the thrust distribution at LEP and linear-collider energies, turn out to be at the few-per-cent level and show remnants of the radiative return to the Z pole even after inclusion of appropriate cuts.
