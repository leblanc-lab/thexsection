## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:1206.3039](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.3039) — A new approach to gluon-quark multiplicity ratio (Paolo Bolzoni)

_Watchlist hit (quark jets) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present a new approach in considering and including both the perturbative and the nonperturbative contributions to the multiplicity ratio $r$ of gluon and quark jets. The new method is motivated by recent developments in timelike small-x resummation obtained in the $\bar{\rm MS}$ factorization scheme. A global analysis to fit the available data is also presented.
