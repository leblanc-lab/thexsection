## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:hep-ph/0405232](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0405232) — Two-loop electroweak correction of O(G_F M_t^2) to the Higgs-boson decay into photons (Frank Fugel et al.)

_Watchlist hit (gluon jets) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We compute the dominant two-loop electroweak correction, of O(G_F M_t^2), to the partial width of the decay of an intermediate-mass Higgs boson into a pair of photons. We use the asymptotic-expansion technique in order to extract the leading dependence on the top-quark mass plus four expansion terms that describe the dependence on the W- and Higgs-boson masses. This correction reduces the Born result by approximately 2.5%. As a by-product of our analysis, we also recover the O(G_F M_t^2) correction to the partial width of the Higgs-boson decay to two gluon jets.
