## Authors to watch

### [arXiv:1201.5882](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.5882) — W+n-jet predictions at the Large Hadron Collider at next-to-leading order matched with a parton shower (Stefan Hoeche et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Stefan Höche) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

For the first time, differential cross sections for the production of W-bosons in conjunction with up to three jets, computed at next-to leading order in QCD and including parton shower corrections, are presented and compared to recent experimental data from the Large Hadron Collider.

### [arXiv:1201.5889](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.5889) — D+s production at central rapidity in pp collisions at 7 TeV with the ALICE experiment (Gian Michele Innocenti)

_Watchlist hit (Gian Michele Innocenti) — abstract only, not yet read for a briefing._

We present the preliminary pt differential cross section in pp collisions of the D+s meson measured in the mid-rapidity region of ALICE with an integrated luminosity of 4.8 nb^-1. The ratios between all the D meson preliminary pt differential cross sections measured in the ALICE experiment (D+s,D0,D+,D*+) are also presented and compared with the results of other experiments.
