## Muon collider

### [arXiv:2611.00349](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00349) — Top quark couplings at the muon collider (Nicol\'as Urcelay et al.)

**What they did.** Urcelay, Bouzas and Larios survey six flavor-diagonal top operators in the Warsaw basis: C_tW, C_tB, C_tφ, C_φQ^(−), C_φt and C_φtb. They drop three others: C_φQ^(+) is fixed by LEP/SLC, C_tG from ttg gives only ~0.8, and C_bW is better from bb̄/bb̄h. Setup:
- √s = 3, 6, 10 and 14 TeV, with ℒ = E²/10 ab⁻¹.
- Six production modes, with muon acceptance |y| < 2.5, 4 or 6.
- Limits from χ = S/√B = 2 on undecayed tops, with a flat efficiency K (hadronic tops, top-tag 0.81).

**Key results.**
- **Dipoles from tt̄h, not tt̄.** At ≥6 TeV, tt̄h beats tt̄ despite a 24–27% cross-section uncertainty. The tt̄ amplitude is chirally suppressed by m_t²/s, and the extra Yukawa chirality flip in tt̄h lifts it. C_tWr reaches [−0.004, 0.013] at 10 TeV and [−0.002, 0.007] at 14 TeV, consistent with Han–Liu–Wang.
- **tt̄νν̄ for the ttZ couplings and the Yukawa.** At 10 TeV: C_φQ^(−) = ±0.10, C_φt = [−0.050, 0.056] (about 40× narrower than the LHC), and C_tφ = [−1.07, 1.68], roughly 6.5% on y_t.
- **Forward-muon tagging matters.** If muons with |y| > 2.5 go untagged, tt̄μμ leaks into the missing-momentum sample. C_φt then degrades by ~3× and C_tφ by ~1.3×; C_φQ^(−) is unaffected.
- **Single top.** This gives the best C_φtb: [−0.35, 0.31] at 10 TeV with |y| < 6. That is about a third of the HL-LHC V_R projection (|C_φtb| < 1.22); it is ~1.1 with |y| < 2.5.

**Caveats.** The limits are statistical only, use parton-level undecayed tops, include no backgrounds, and are individual (marginalized only for the C_tW–C_tB plane). The appendix adds a 2% systematic. This mostly hurts high-rate channels: single top at 14 TeV goes from 0.6% to 4.0% total uncertainty. Treat this as a roadmap, not a projection.

### [arXiv:2611.01117](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01117) — LHμC Concept: Energy-Frontier Antimuon–Proton Collisions at CERN (Arda Goroglu et al.)

**Relevance.** These are workshop proceedings on a μ⁺p machine, not a μ⁺μ⁻ collider. They matter here because they push μTRISTAN-style ultra-cold μ⁺ technology, which needs no ionization cooling, as an earlier route to TeV muon beams.

**Concept.**
- 1 TeV μ⁺ on 7 TeV HL-LHC protons gives √s = 5.3 TeV.
- Peak ℒ is 8.8×10³³ cm⁻²s⁻¹ with a μTRISTAN-type booster, or 7.2×10³³ by reusing the LHeC ERL tunnel (50 turns, ~1.4 ms).
- The survival fractions are now 0.82 and 0.68. The original μLHC paper had 0.38 and 0.16 because of an incorrect muon mass, so its luminosities rise by about 2× and 4×.

**Physics claims.**
- DIS reach down to x = 3.6×10⁻⁷ at Q² = 10 GeV².
- Resonant colour-octet muon production gives a 5σ reach of 4.1 (4.4) TeV with 100 fb⁻¹ (1 ab⁻¹), versus 2.3 TeV at the HL-LHC.
- RPV λ′_2j1 can be probed to 0.01–0.02 for 1–3 TeV squarks.

**Caveats.**
- The 10⁵ enhancement in μ⁺ yield over J-PARC is undemonstrated.
- Fixed-field booster arcs are still needed.
- Neutrino radiation from μ⁺ decays is unaddressed.
- The tungsten nozzle costs 1 TeV muons about 19% of their energy and limits acceptance.

### [arXiv:2611.03218](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03218) — Probing aQGCs from dimension-eight operators via neutral triboson production at muon colliders (Parimah Hosseini et al.)

**What they did.** Hosseini et al. study the dim-8 transverse operators O_T0–O_T9, including T3 and T4, in γγγ, γγZ, γZZ and ZZZ.
- Energies: 3 TeV (1 ab⁻¹) and 10 TeV (10 ab⁻¹). ZZZ is 3 TeV only, 6e only, because 10 TeV generation "proved challenging".
- Chain: MG5 → Pythia → Delphes with the muon-collider card.
- Selection: single-variable cut-and-count on p_T^γ3, p_T^γ2 or m_Z2.
- Statistics: χ² with 5% systematic, one coefficient at a time, dim-6 set to zero.

**Key results.**
- Triphoton dominates. The best limits at 10 TeV are f_T8/Λ⁴ = ±2.64×10⁻⁶ TeV⁻⁴ and f_T9 = ±5.73×10⁻⁶.
- For comparison, CMS γγZ gives f_T8 ≈ [−1.06, 1.10] TeV⁻⁴, and FCC-hh with 5% systematic gives ~10⁻³.
- Their 10 TeV γγγ result is comparable to earlier 30 TeV muon-collider studies (~10⁻⁶). The paper doesn't explain why it matches the 30 TeV studies.
- Z channels are weaker because of leptonic branching ratios.

**Caveats.**
- The "first T3/T4 constraints" claim is soft. In these channels T2 and T3 have identical cross-section fits, as do T0/T1 (and T4/T7 in γγγ). So T3 is not separately constrained.
- The pure-BSM term grows as s³, yet there is no unitarity clipping or EFT-validity treatment.
- There is no beam-induced background.

### [arXiv:2611.01274](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01274) — Measurement of |V_cb| using tt̄ events at √s = 13 TeV (CMS) (CMS Collaboration)

**Result.** With 138 fb⁻¹ of lepton+jets data, CMS measures |V_cb| = [4.59 +0.44/−0.48 (stat) +0.64/−0.76 (syst)]×10⁻² from W→cb in tt̄. That is r = 1.27 +0.46/−0.44, against an expected 4.07×10⁻². It is more precise than ATLAS (5.0 +1.1/−1.4).

**Jet-relevant methods.**
- **Two-stage XGBoost jet–parton assignment.** The first BDT deliberately omits the DeepJet scores of the W-daughter candidates, so it doesn't pull g→bb̄ radiation into the W. The second BDT uses those scores only to resolve up/down ordering.
- **Assignment performance.** Correct-assignment efficiency is 67% for cb versus 86% for ud. Radiative heavy-flavor contamination is 6–10%.
- **Discriminant.** A TabNet discriminant is driven mainly by the W-daughter B scores. There is no dedicated c-tagger, and the c-jet scale factor is fixed to 1 with conservative uncertainties decorrelated in three p_T bins.
- **Gluon-splitting proxies.** min m_bb and min ΔR_bb rank fifth in importance and below.

**Systematics.** The leading term is μ_R/μ_F (±0.25 on r), mostly from the 4FS tt̄bb̄ prediction. The 4FS and 5FS fits differ by Δr = 0.19, which is 0.88σ in a bootstrap test. For a jets person, the interest is that g→bb̄ and heavy-flavor modelling limit a flavor-physics measurement.
