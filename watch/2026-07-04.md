## Muon collider

### [arXiv:2607.03775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03775) — Probing Fermion-Portal Scalar Dark Matter through Charged Vector-Like Fermions at Future Muon Colliders (Songshaptak De et al.)

This study investigates a dark matter (DM) model featuring a real singlet scalar ($s$) and vector-like singlet/doublet charged fermions ($E_S, F_D$), where the DM is stabilized by a $Z_2$ symmetry. By introducing Yukawa interactions ($Y_f$), the authors successfully move away from the minimal Higgs-portal scenario, which is currently under extreme pressure from LZ direct-detection limits. The model reproduces the observed relic density via $t$- and $u$-channel processes ($s s \to \nu \ell \bar{\nu} \ell$) and co-annihilations involving the new fermions.

The paper provides a detailed collider sensitivity analysis for the process $\mu^+ \mu^- \to E_1^+ E_1^- \to e^+ e^- + \cancel{E}_T$. Using detector-level simulation (Delphes), the authors demonstrate that a 3 TeV muon collider ($\mathcal{L}=1$ ab$^{-1}$) can reach a $5\sigma$ discovery for charged fermion masses up to $\sim 1.5$ TeV. Scaling to 10 TeV ($\mathcal{L}=10$ ab$^{-1}$) extends this reach to $\sim 2$ TeV, covering almost the entire parameter space favored by current relic density and direct-detection constraints.

The results are significant because they highlight the complementarity of a muon collider in probing "compressed" or weakly-coupled regions that the HL-LHC cannot reach, specifically for $Z_2$-odd states. A notable caveat is the reliance on the electron channel; the muon-associated Yukawa couplings are intentionally suppressed to evade $(g-2)_\mu$ and $\mu \to e \gamma$ constraints, which justifies the focus on electron-rich final states but constrains the model's flavor structure.
