## Muon collider

Two independent, cross-referencing papers appeared today (each cites the other as "note added"/companion) on the forward neutrino flux from muon decay at a 10 TeV MuC. They agree on the headline O(10⁹) interactions/year at a forward detector but differ substantially in emphasis, geometry, and — importantly — on whether an energy–angle "prism" correlation survives beam dynamics. Read together they define the emerging quantitative baseline for a MuC forward neutrino program.

### [arXiv:2608.02714](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02714) — Neutrino Fluxes at a Muon Collider (Burk, Han, Kilian, Kling, Kopp, Tabrizi) (Francis M. Burk et al.)

Comprehensive re-evaluation of all neutrino sources for the v0.8 IR design. Key number: beam-muon-decay flux is ~10¹⁹ ν/year in the forward direction, **~120× higher than the ISU/IMCC estimate**, driven by integrating over the full ~500 m straight section (not just 10 m) and increasing collimation away from the IP. They also quantify three previously-neglected subdominant sources: (i) primary μ⁺μ⁻ collisions, dominated by γγ→τ⁺τ⁻ (~0.4–1 nb, ~10¹⁰ ν_τ at 10 ab⁻¹, cross-checked between DGLAP-resummed PDFs and fixed-order WHIZARD to O(10%)); (ii) Michel-electron EM showers in the tungsten shielding, where Bethe–Heitler γN→τ⁺τ⁻/cc̄ and photo-nuclear γg→cc̄ give ~10¹² ν/year of all flavors — this is the leading source of ν_τ; (iii) ν-induced showers in rock (~10¹¹/year). For a 100 kg MuColν detector at z=500 m, r=25 cm (0.5 mrad acceptance, >80% acceptance for beam-decay ν), Table 1 gives 1.1·10¹⁰ ν_μ + 5.2·10⁹ ν̄_e per 10 yr, but only ~2.5 ν_τ+ν̄_τ events total. Physics case: 10¹⁰ DIS/ton/yr enables PDF/CKM (V_cd ×44, V_ub ×3), sin²θ_W to 0.03%, ν trident, NSI improved ~10× over their earlier projection, sterile Δm²~10³ eV².

Caveat, stated explicitly: absolute flux carries an O(1) machine-design uncertainty — v0.6 vs v0.8 differ by ~2–5× depending on chicane treatment. The tiny ν_τ yield is the weak point of the physics case at this detector size.

### [arXiv:2608.02718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02718) — The Forward Neutrino Flux and its Secondaries at a 10 TeV Muon Collider (Choi, Hostert, Li, Liu) (Ju-Yeol Choi et al.)

Introduces **MINT**, a public MC that propagates the central muon orbit through a MAD-X hybrid v0.6+v0.9 lattice including Twiss-based beam envelope and divergence (though not full particle tracking). Central result that qualitatively updates prior work: the beam divergence (O(0.1–1) mrad over the straight section) is ~50× larger than the intrinsic 1/γ≈0.02 mrad, so **the energy–angle "prism" correlation touted in preliminary studies is washed out** — recovering it would require β increasing ~100×. Benchmark: 3.2 t fiducial (gas Ar TPC + vertex tracker) at 5 km, ~60% acceptance, ~7·10¹⁸ ν/year crossing the face, O(10⁹) interactions/year.

Their distinctive contribution is the **secondary** flux from ν interactions in the upstream rock. Secondary muons: 6.5·10¹²/yr (μ⁺ beam) and 1.2·10¹³/yr (μ⁻) produced in the last 2.5 km, ~6·10¹¹/yr reaching the face at ⟨E_μ⟩≈1.2 TeV — **~2 muons per bunch crossing, ~40–45× the neutrino interaction rate**, a serious background requiring event-by-event (not per-bunch) vetoing, and >99.9% polarized (potential feature). Secondary ν_τ: charm→D_s→τ dominates but only ~0.2 events/year total (peaks near L=3 km) — consistent with 2714's conclusion that ν_τ are scarce, and they note photoproduction in the ring (2714's channel) likely dominates. Wrong-sign ν: O(4)/yr (μ⁻), O(0.7)/yr (μ⁺). BSM section estimates HNL reach (mixing |U_μN|²~2·10⁻¹¹ at m_N≈15 GeV; dipole d~4·10⁻¹⁰ GeV⁻¹ at m_N≈6 GeV) via ν upscattering, exploiting √s≈75 GeV(E_ν/3TeV)^½ to exceed D/B-meson kinematic ceilings.

Caveat, emphasized by the authors: HNL curves are **event-yield contours before backgrounds/efficiencies**, not sensitivity projections; the secondary-muon background and vertexing/timing requirements are the gating issues. MINT omits individual optics tracking.

**Net for the topic:** the ~120× flux upward revision (2714) and the demonstration that beam dynamics, not 1/γ, sets the beam profile and kills the prism effect (2718) together reset the design assumptions. Both agree ν_τ are rare (~0.2–2.5 events), tempering the "tau factory" framing; the real leverage is the ~10⁹–10¹⁰ ν_μ/ν_e CC sample and the secondary-muon background now quantified for the first time.
