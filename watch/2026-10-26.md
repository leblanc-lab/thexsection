## Jets and jet substructure

Both papers today sit in the transversity-via-final-state-spin corner of jet physics, and they fit together closely. The FEC paper computes its $p^\uparrow p$ Collins asymmetry with LO spin-transfer coefficients $T_{abc}$. FeynFacet supplies the NLO versions of those same hard functions.

### [arXiv:2610.31745](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31745) — FeynFacet: AI agents for analytic NLO QCD (Zhong-Bo Kang et al.)

This is primarily an automated NLO-hard-function paper. Its connection to jets comes through the final-state factor: the semi-inclusive jet function at leading power in small $R$, using the modified convolution $J_c(z, L_J + 2\ln z)$, or fragmenting jet functions for hadron-in-jet observables.

**Pipeline.** The LLM agents build process-specific programs; the finished programs run without further LLM calls.
- Amplitudes are generated with FeynArts.
- The algebra is computed in parallel with FeynCalc (BMHV mode), FORM/TFORM and Tracer.
- Real emission uses reverse unitarity.
- HVBM evanescent numerators $(\hat q^2)^n$ are mapped by dimensional-shift identities to scalar integrals in $D+2n$.
- Kira IBP reduction is performed at symbolic $d$, and SubTropica evaluates the Euler-integral masters.

**Validation.** The workflow reproduces known results:
- $pp$ UU and LL for all 16 tagged classes, at test points against the reference implementation; symbolically for $qg\to q$.
- Incoming TT for 5 channels.
- $\ell p$ UU and LL symbolically.
- One-loop TMD soft functions and beam matching coefficients, with $\nu$-cancellation checked.

**New results.**
- NLO transverse spin-transfer hard functions $\hat\sigma_T$ for $p^\uparrow p\to c^\uparrow X$, covering all seven tagged quark/antiquark classes.
- The analogous $\ell p^\uparrow\to q^\uparrow X$ result with the scattered lepton integrated over, including the photon-collinear subtraction.

These are the missing NLO production inputs for Collins and dihadron asymmetries in jets, $\Lambda$ polarization, and spin-dependent energy correlators.

**Caveats.**
- The available text truncates before the new-results section, so I can't quote the coefficients.
- The $pp$ transfer results are cross-checked against crossing (symbolic only at Born, test points at NLO) and an independent finite assembly. There is no external reference.
- The $\ell p$ transfer result rests on internal checks only: Born normalization, pole cancellation and RG consistency.
- Direct and resolved photoproduction enter only at Born accuracy.

Code and coefficients are on GitHub.

### [arXiv:2610.32715](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32715) — A universal probe of QCD spin physics from fragmentation energy correlator (Qing-Hong Cao et al.)

**Observable.** The fragmentation energy correlator (FEC) correlates a tagged hadron with the boost-invariant energy flow around it, measured in $(\Lambda_F = \sqrt2\,p_h^+\tan\theta/2,\ \phi)$. It is collinearly factorized like an FF, so it needs no jet algorithm and no recoiling heavy object. At leading twist an unpolarized hadron has two FECs:
- an unpolarized FEC $\mathcal D$;
- a chiral-odd "Collins FEC" $\mathcal H$, giving a $(-\sin\phi, \cos\phi)$ energy-flow asymmetry relative to the quark spin.

Because the asymmetry is collinear, it is not Sudakov-suppressed. Integrating the dihadron FF (DiFF) over one hadron, energy-weighted, gives the FEC; summing over $h$ gives the EEC jet function, in which $\mathcal H$ vanishes. This maps $\Lambda_F/z$ onto the EEC scaling variable.

**Fit to Pythia $e^+e^-$.** $\mathcal D$ is parametrized with a saturation-to-power-law form, fit at $\mu \in \{5, 25, 50, 250\}$ GeV and evolved with LO DGLAP.

| Quantity | Fitted value |
|---|---|
| Transition scale $\lambda_{\mathcal D}$, favored/unfavored | ≈3.24–3.39 GeV |
| $\lambda_{\mathcal D}$, $s$ | ≈2.7 GeV |
| $\lambda_{\mathcal D}$, $c$ | ≈5–6 GeV |
| $\lambda_{\mathcal D}$, $b$ | ≈6–7 GeV |
| $\lambda_{\mathcal D}$, $g$ | ≈4.4 GeV |
| Exponent $\kappa$ | > 1 (sharp transition) |

Pythia $ep$ and $pp$ (with $\Lambda_F/p_{hT} < 0.2$) agree with predictions built from the $e^+e^-$-extracted $\mathcal D$.

**Collins FEC and predictions.** $\mathcal H$ is modeled with a linear small-$\Lambda_F$ suppression and a $\Lambda_F^{-3}$ twist-3 tail. Its normalization is set from the JAM3D-22 Collins $k_T$-moment, and $\lambda_{\mathcal H}$ is scanned freely as a proxy for the chiral-symmetry-breaking scale. Predictions are given for:
- EIC $A_{UT}$;
- RHIC $A_{UT}$, which is $t$-channel enhanced at forward $y_h$ and comparable to STAR's energy-weighted hadron-in-jet measurement;
- a Belle $\cos(\phi_2 - \phi_1)$ asymmetry, of comparable magnitude to the measured Artru–Collins dihadron asymmetry.

**Caveats.**
- Everything is LO, and Pythia serves as pseudo-data.
- The shape parameters of $\mathcal H$ are fixed to averages from $\mathcal D$.
- The $\mathcal H$–Collins relation and the momentum sum rule hold only at bare level; the sum rule is imposed as an inequality.
- The $pp$ factorization is argued, not proven in detail.
