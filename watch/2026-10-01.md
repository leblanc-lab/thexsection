## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2609.39381](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39381) — New Observables for Resonant and Non-Resonant New Physics Searches at Colliders (Julien Touch\`eque)

This is a UCLouvain PhD thesis (Touchèque, supervised by Degrande). It is only tangential to substructure. The supplied text runs through the abstract, introduction and textbook SM material in Chapter 1, and is cut off before the analysis chapters. So the jet content below rests only on the abstract and chapter outline.

The relevant part is Chapter 4, which searches for top-philic colour-singlet and colour-octet scalars in four-top final states. It covers fully hadronic, leptonic and same-sign dileptonic channels, with signals generated at NLO QCD. The scalar decays yield boosted $t\bar t$ pairs:

- **Hadronic tops** are reconstructed with ML taggers for wide hadronic jets.
- **Leptonic tops** are reconstructed assuming on-shell $W$ decays.
- **Event selection** uses a new scheme built on the invariant masses of top pairs.

The abstract claims limits improved by up to an order of magnitude over a recast CMS analysis. The underlying work is published (JHEP 11 (2025) 091, plus PRD 111 (2025) 055037), so there is nothing new here for substructure beyond packaging. If boosted multi-top tagging matters to you, read the JHEP paper directly.

Chapter 3, on CP-odd SMEFT triple-product asymmetries in $WZ$/$W\gamma$, is unrelated to jets.

### [arXiv:2609.39515](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39515) — Probing jet quenching via the correlation of groomed jet substructure observables in Pb–Pb and pp collisions (ALICE Collaboration)

**Setup.** ALICE reports a fully corrected 2D $(\theta_g, z_g)$ measurement, unfolded with 3D iterative Bayesian unfolding in $(p_T, z_g, \theta_g)$. The sample is:

- Charged $R=0.2$ anti-$k_T$ jets with $60\le p_{T,\rm ch\,jet}<80$ GeV.
- pp (18 nb$^{-1}$) and 0–10% Pb–Pb (0.12 nb$^{-1}$) at 5.02 TeV.
- Soft Drop with $z_{\rm cut}=0.2$, $\beta=0$; tagging fractions are 89% (pp) and 87% (Pb–Pb).
- Projections in two bins: $z_g$ split at 0.35, $\theta_g$ split at 0.3.

**Results.**

- **Narrowing:** Pb–Pb jets are narrower in both $z_g$ bins. The appendix double ratio is flat, so the narrowing is effectively $z_g$-independent once the yield shift is accounted for.
- **Narrow jets ($\theta_g<0.3$):** $z_g$ shows no modification.
- **Wide jets ($\theta_g>0.3$):** the Pb/pp $z_g$ ratio has a slope (less balanced in Pb–Pb). Relative to a constant of ≈0.82, the significance is 2.7σ statistical only and 2.0σ with systematics. It is limited by pp statistics.
- **pp generators:** PYTHIA and POWHEG describe the data, while Herwig has too few wide jets.
- **Pb–Pb models:**
  - JEWEL overestimates the narrowing.
  - JETSCAPE describes it well but over-depletes large $\theta_g$ for unbalanced jets.
  - The Hybrid model is insensitive to the wake. At large $\theta_g$, unbalanced jets prefer the variant with elastic (Molière) scattering and balanced jets the variant without.

**Caveats.** Survival bias is not disentangled; the authors themselves point to the CMS γ-tagged $R_g$ result. The aggressive grooming at small $R$ kills sensitivity to medium response. Residual background mistags of 5–15% are handled via thermal non-closure.

The main advance is that this is the first correlated $(\theta_g, z_g)$ unfolding in AA. The physics signal, however, is a 2σ hint.

### [arXiv:2609.40224](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40224) — In medium evolution of the energy-energy correlators (Weiyao Ke)

These are Ke's proceedings, summarising Ke–Meçaj–Vitev (JHEP 04 (2026) 155).

**Framework.** In SCET$_G$ at first order in opacity, the regime is thin, dilute media with $p_T/L\gg\alpha_s\rho_G L\sim m_D^2$. The EEC jet function receives two kinds of medium correction:

1. **Non-contact fixed-order correction.** It changes character at $\theta_{\rm LPM}=\sqrt{8\pi/(p_T L)}$ and carries a Coulomb log. It depletes the EEC below $\theta_{\rm LPM}$ and enhances it above.
2. **Medium-induced anomalous dimension.** This is a matrix $\boldsymbol\Gamma^{\rm med}(N)$ acting in the window $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/p_T\ll\theta\ll\theta_{\rm LPM}$. Its strength is set by $w_{\rm med}=4\pi\alpha_s\rho_{\rm eff}L_{\rm eff}^2/(2p_T)$. The diagonal entries grow linearly in $N$, which motivates ratios of higher-point correlators to the EEC. Its effect is extra small-angle suppression, stronger for gluon jets.

The medium enters only through two path-integrated quantities, $\rho_{\rm eff}$ and $L_{\rm eff}$; coherence means the local density profile is not resolved at this order. The switch from vacuum to modified evolution occurs at $\mu_m\sim\sqrt{p_T/L_{\rm eff}}$.

**Phenomenology.**

- **p–Pb:** compared with preliminary ALICE minimum-bias data for $R=0.4$ jets in charged-jet $p_T$ bins of 20–80 GeV. The calculation gives small-angle suppression that weakens with $p_T$, consistent with the data.
- **O–O:** projections are given for O–O collisions at 5.36 TeV.

**Caveats.**

- Charged jets are approximated by $p_T^{\rm ch}\simeq\tfrac23 p_T^{\rm full}$, with no track functions.
- The uncertainty bands vary only the matching scale, not the medium parameters.
- Hadronization dominates at small $\theta$, and recoil and medium response matter near $\theta\sim R$.

**Connection to the ALICE paper.** Both are probing medium-modified angular structure, but in opposite regimes. ALICE uses dense central Pb–Pb with hard grooming, where survival bias dominates. Ke works in the thin-medium limit, where a modified anomalous dimension is a cleaner, calculable target. The $p_T$ and system-size scaling of $w_{\rm med}$ offers a falsifiable test that groomed $z_g$ currently does not.
