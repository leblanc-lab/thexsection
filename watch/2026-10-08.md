## Optimal transport

### [arXiv:2610.09320](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09320) — A Unified Geometric Framework for Understanding Collider Event Manifolds (Tianji Cai et al.)

The authors embed each jet as its vector of distances to $N_{\rm ref}$ randomly drawn reference jets (M3R). This puts the energy-flow W₂ metric, the spectral SW₂ metric (1D closed-form quantile matching), and the phase-space (PS) metric in a common Euclidean coordinate system. With all events as references the embedding is an exact $\ell_\infty$ isometry, and it inherits IRC safety from the underlying metric. Cost drops from $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$ to $\mathcal{O}(NN_{\rm ref})$ metric evaluations, which matters for 2D W₂ solves.

Results on 10⁴ JetClass jets per class:

- **Reconstruction fidelity at $N_{\rm ref}=100$** (JAUC / MADO) orders the metrics PS < SW₂ < W₂ in complexity:
  - PS: >0.92 / >0.97
  - SW₂: 0.82–0.88 / 0.86–0.92
  - W₂: 0.72–0.79 / 0.77–0.82
- **W₂ vs SW₂ on QCD jets:** the leading two principal components align closely (|cos| ≈ 0.95). SW₂'s PC1 explains 0.85 of W₂ variance, versus 0.92 for W₂'s own PC1. This is a manifold-level echo of their known soft-collinear equivalence.
- **Top jets break this.** W₂ decouples (|cos| ≲ 0.25 with both SW₂ and PS), while SW₂–PS stay aligned (≈0.85). This is consistent with spectral degeneracies for triangular three-prong configurations.
- **Asymmetric variance transfer on top jets.** W₂'s PC1 explains about 0.3 of SW₂/PS variance, but the reverse transfers explain ≲0.1 of W₂'s. The authors read this as W₂ containing the shared structure plus extra.
- **Tagging is more compressible than geometry.** SVM AUCs saturate by $N_{\rm ref}\sim10$. At $N_{\rm ref}=100$, the average relative gap to the full-metric SVM is ≈0% for W₂, 3% for SW₂ and 11% for PS, the reverse of the reconstruction ordering. This generalizes the linearized-W₂ idea.

OT is central here: two of the three metrics are OT-based, and the W₂/SW₂ comparison is the paper's sharpest result. The framework itself, however, is metric-agnostic.

Caveats:
- References are random and metric weights are set to 1.
- PS uses 10 $p_T$-ordered exclusive subjets as a stand-in for the permutation minimization.
- SVM hyperparameters are fixed.
- The provided text is truncated mid-§5.2, so the multi-metric/PID combination results (§6) are not covered here.
