## Muon collider

### [arXiv:2608.04399](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04399) — Production of the top partner $T$ of the Bestest Little Higgs Model at the future muon collider (E. Cruz-Albaro et al.)

Abstract only. A BLHM phenomenology study computing decay widths and branching ratios of the heavy top partner $T$ and its production at a $\mu^+\mu^-$ collider through both pair production, $\mu^+\mu^-\to(\gamma,Z,Z',h,H)\to T\bar T$, and single/associated production, $\mu^+\mu^-\to(\gamma,Z,Z',h,H)\to \bar t T + t\bar T$. Cross sections are scanned over several $\sqrt{s}$ values; the headline conclusion is that pair production is the most promising discovery channel. No numbers, mass points, or luminosity/reach figures are given in the abstract.

For the muon-collider track this is a standard added-physics-case exercise rather than a machine or analysis advance: the novelty is applying the $s$-channel neutral-current mediator set (including the BLHM-specific $Z'$, $h$, $H$) to top-partner production, but the abstract offers no sensitivity benchmark or comparison against hadron-collider limits. Judge relevance once the full text provides the actual $\sigma(\sqrt s)$ curves and the assumed $T$ mass range relative to the $Z'/H$ resonance thresholds — the associated $tT$ channel is the more collider-specific observable and worth checking whether it stays subdominant across all $\sqrt s$ or only near threshold.
