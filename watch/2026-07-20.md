## Muon collider

### [arXiv:2607.15356](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15356) — Multiboson Signatures of Doubly Charged Scalars at a Same-Sign Muon Collider (Mariana Frank et al.)

Frank, Fuks, Majumdar & Senapati study a same-sign μ⁺μ⁺ collider (the μTRISTAN 2 TeV / 1 ab⁻¹ configuration) as a probe of Type-II seesaw doubly charged scalars in the large-triplet-vev regime (v_t ≃ 1 GeV), where Δ^{±±}→W^±W^± dominates (BR >90%) rather than the dilepton mode. Production is VBF-like pair production μ⁺μ⁺→μ⁺μ⁺Δ⁺⁺Δ⁻⁻ with two spectator muons, and they target the fully hadronic 4W channel giving a μ⁺μ⁺+8j final state (~20% combined hadronic BR). Combinatorics are resolved via a global χ² minimization over the 105 ways of forming four non-overlapping dijets from the 8 leading jets, with σ_W=12 GeV. Backgrounds (μ⁺μ⁺+2j/4j, μ⁺μ⁺W⁺W⁻) are driven to the ~1–8 ab level by the N_j≥8 requirement. A cut-and-count analysis with three nested "tight-W" regions gives 3.7–4.7σ for m_{Δ}=200–300 GeV, dropping below 2σ near 400 GeV — the fall driven by the steeply falling production cross section, not reconstruction. An XGBoost BDT (per-mass classifiers, kinematic + reconstructed-W + spectator-muon + multi-W-mass observables) extends the 2σ reach to 425–430 GeV, a few tens of GeV beyond the cut-based result.

For this topic, the concrete takeaway is a benchmark reach number for a genuinely same-sign (μ⁺μ⁺) machine in a decay regime where LHC diboson searches only reach ~350 GeV (pair) / ~230 GeV (associated) — so the improvement over LHC is modest but comes from a qualitatively different production mode and clean initial state. Caveats stated explicitly: no ME-to-PS matching/merging (backgrounds treated conservatively/independently), no beam-induced-background or dedicated detector study, and the LO Delphes treatment with a generic muon-collider card. The extra "missing-energy" BDT variables are acknowledged as event-balance proxies, not genuine invisible-particle handles. Overall the paper is fully and centrally about the muon-collider capability, not a tangential keyword match.
