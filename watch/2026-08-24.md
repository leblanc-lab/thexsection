## Muon collider

### [arXiv:2608.21327](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21327) — On-Detector Machine Learning for Beam-Induced Background Rejection at a 10 TeV Muon Collider (Daniel Abadjiev et al.)

This is the smart-pixels concept ported from HL-LHC to the muon collider BIB problem, and it's squarely on-topic for detector R&D at √s = 10 TeV. The concrete driver: innermost vertex layer occupancy of O(10⁴) hits/cm²/BX at 30 kHz translates to >50 Gbps/chip (2×2 cm², 40-bit hits) — ~10× the HL-LHC pixel readout ceiling. The pitch is to reject BIB *in the pixel front-end* using cluster shape rather than relying solely on O(30) ps timing, which they argue is fragile at 25×25 µm² pitch (needs ~10× front-end power reduction) and vulnerable to calibration/radiation drift. Physics handle: collision particles hit at IP-consistent incidence angles (x_size grows with z_global), whereas BIB e± loop in at large angles regardless of position, giving distinct cluster sizes/profiles.

They train three quantized NN classes — engineered scalars (Model 1), 1D charge profiles (Model 2), full 21×13 CNN (Model 3) — via QKeras QAT, Pareto-scanned over architecture and 3–10 bit weights, with hls4ml→Vitis (FPGA) and Catapult/SAED32 (32 nm ASIC) resource estimates. Headline: 88–90% data reduction at 99% signal efficiency for Models 1–2 with ASIC area <1 mm² and 160–250 ns latency; Model 3 reaches 90.5% cluster rejection / 94.4% data reduction but at prohibitive area (~8 mm²). Crucially, cluster-shape rejection is shown orthogonal to timing — BIB is rejected across the full t_corr spectrum, and stacking a [3σ,5σ] window pushes data reduction to 98.5–98.9%.

Caveats are stated plainly and are significant: PIXELAV assumes a single isolated cluster in a clean ROI (no overlapping/warped BIB clusters, no edge effects, ROI-finding assumed upstream) — unrealistic at true muon-collider occupancy. Incoherent pair production is absent from the BIB sample (they argue IPP clusters look similar and should be rejectable if trained on). Signal is single muons, not physics events; geometry is MuColl_v1 / lattice v0.4; ASIC numbers are open-library first-pass, not manufacturable. So: a credible feasibility demonstration and benchmark (88–90% @ 99% eff, sub-mm² ASIC), not yet a validated occupancy-realistic result.

## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2608.21145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21145) — Unraveling QCD dynamics with heavy quark energy correlators (Ivan Vitev)

A proceedings-style theory writeup (Vitev, summarizing work with Ke and Meçaj) extending the energy-energy correlator jet-substructure program to massive quarks in vacuum and in QCD matter — genuinely central to jet substructure, specifically the EEC/dead-cone axis. Vacuum baseline: they give a compact closed-form one-loop heavy-quark EEC jet function at nonzero angle with δ = i m_Q/(p_T R_L), which reproduces massless twist-two scaling at R_L ≫ m_Q/p_T and turns over at R_L ~ m_Q/p_T. The heavy-to-light EEC ratio is thus the EEC realization of the dead cone, perturbatively controlled (bottom turnover at larger R_L than charm), with the argument that above the nonperturbative scale, deviations are medium effects rather than hadronization.

In matter, they add medium-induced jet functions in SCET_G via a first-order opacity expansion (RR+RV+VR+VV), with the heavy-quark mass entering three ways: channel-dependent eikonal regulator ν [(1−x)M, xM, M for Q→Qg, Q→gQ, g→QQ̄], a genuinely new helicity-flip amplitude A⁻ ∝ M/(Q²+ν²), and modified LPM formation-time phases. The key competition is θ_dc ~ M/E vs θ_LPM ~ √(8π/p_T L), parametrically comparable when M ~ √(P⁺/L⁺). The moment-space RGE anomalous dimension γ_Q = γ_vac + δγ_med interpolates across three regimes (ν²≪, ~, ≫ Q²_LPM), with the M²-proportional helicity-flip piece reshaping the dead-cone region most strongly. Phenomenologically the channels behave oppositely: Q→Qg gives extra small-angle suppression (deepened dead cone), while g→QQ̄ can *enhance* near θ_LPM — offering a handle to separate an identified heavy shower from gluon-initiated heavy flavor, complementary to the earlier soft-drop mass-hierarchy-inversion result.

Caveats per the text: results shown (Fig. 2, O+O 0–5% hydro medium) are preliminary and jet-function-only; opacity-one captures leading LPM but misses higher-order corrections to fully differential spectra; robust experimental window is Λ_QCD/p_T ≪ θ ≲ θ_LPM, with large angles contaminated by energy loss/medium response and small angles by hadronization/tracking thresholds. No full cross-section predictions or data comparison here — it's a framework and roadmap (pp–Pb, O–O, Pb–Pb, EIC).
