## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2611.13222](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13222) — AlphaHits: Full-Event Tracking and Reconstruction Towards a Hit-Level Foundation Model (Sascha Caron et al.)

This paper is mainly about tracking and a hit-level foundation model, so jets are only one downstream head. Nothing here concerns substructure.

**Setup.** A 12-layer Transformer (~9.7M parameters) is first pretrained on tracking. It is then fine-tuned with nine heads, one of which is a 12-query DETR-style jet decoder. The decoder predicts jet existence, (p_T, η, φ) and a 4-class flavour label. The training sample is 200k ttH(bb)/tt events at 14 TeV with no pileup, simulated with ACTS Fatras using the TrackML-layout silicon detector. Targets are GenJets: anti-k_t, R = 0.6, p_T > 20 GeV.

**Jet reconstruction results.**
- **Hits only:** 83.0% GenJet-finding efficiency at 2.0% fakes. The Delphes track-jet chain gets 61.7% at 0.9%. AlphaHits' p_T response is unbiased (median +0.003), whereas track jets sit 43% low, but the AlphaHits IQR is wide (0.33).
- **Hits plus Delphes calorimeter towers:** 86.4% at 1.5% fakes, against 86.7% at 2.3% for the full Delphes chain. Resolution is still worse than Delphes (IQR 0.19 vs 0.13).

**Flavour tagging.** At 77% b-efficiency, the hits-only model rejects light jets by about 38 and c-jets by about 5, with no explicit tracking or vertexing. It matches the Delphes track-jet reference only from 77% efficiency upward. At tighter working points it falls behind: light-jet rejection is 0.68× the reference at 70% and 0.41× at 60%. Adding towers raises all rejections by 10–35%.

**Event classification.** Higgs-vs-tt AUC is 0.932 from hits alone and 0.944 with towers. A ParT trained on Delphes objects reaches 0.877 (tracks only) and 0.924 (tracks plus calorimeter).

**Caveats.**
- The Delphes b-tag reference is a truth-seeded efficiency parametrisation, not a real tagger, so the "matches" claims are against an idealised baseline.
- There is no pileup in the multi-task model.
- Jets are set predictions rather than clustered objects, so IRC-safety and jet-definition questions go unaddressed.
- An auxiliary per-hit head regresses jet mass and secondary-vertex L_xy, but neither is evaluated as an output.

### [arXiv:2611.14318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14318) — Resolved Q₁^q–Q₇γ interference in B̄→X_sγ at NLO: Hard matching and radiative charm-loop jet function for m_c→0 (Riccardo Bartocci et al.)

This matched on "jet function" in the SCET sense; it is a B-physics paper with no collider jets. Its relevance is methodological: it is a complete example of NLO factorisation with RG improvement at subleading power for an inclusive observable.

**Two-loop jet function.** The authors compute the two-loop radiative charm-loop jet function J̄(u, ω₁) with full dependence on the collinear momentum fraction u, in the m_c → 0 limit.
- The calculation uses 31 two-loop diagrams, reduced with FIRE6 and LiteRed.
- The delta-function constraint on u is handled with reverse unitarity.
- This leaves 15 master integrals (12 in one topology, 3 in the other), solved via canonical differential equations in u (CANONICA) in terms of multiple polylogarithms.
- C_F terms from eikonal gluon attachments to the external b and s lines cancel, leaving only C_A pieces.
- Integrating over u reproduces their earlier integrated result. This cross-check is partly circular, since two boundary constants were fixed using that integrated result.

**Hard matching and evolution.** They also give the one-loop hard matching **H**₁₂(u) and its non-local evolution, which is ERBL-like plus a ln(u ū) term. They solve it analytically at leading and next-to-leading order in 1/N_c and compare with a 450-term Gegenbauer numerical solution:

| Approximation | Central region | Endpoints |
|---|---|---|
| Leading colour | ~2‰ deviation | up to 12% |
| Next-to-leading colour | ~3×10⁻⁵ | 4×10⁻³ at u = 0.01 |

**Caveats.**
- The m_c dependence of J̄ is still missing, and it is required for phenomenology.
- The provided text cuts off partway through the renormalisation-factor section, so I couldn't check the claimed pole-structure consistency check.
