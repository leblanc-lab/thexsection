## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2611.21763](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21763) — A new angle on coherence-violating logarithms in color-singlet processes (Mrinal Dasgupta et al.)

**How central it is.** This paper is tangential to substructure. It treats a Drell–Yan central rapidity gap with a $Q_0$ veto, so there are no jets in the final state. It matched because it is gaps-between-jets resummation technology: Coulomb phases, non-global logarithms and color evolution. The authors list dijet and other jet-production extensions as future work.

**What they did.** Dasgupta, Fraley, Monni and Nabeebaccus resum the leading coherence-violating logarithm (CVL) tower $\alpha_s^k L^{2k-m-2}Y^m$ for all $1\le m\le 2k-2$. This is the first time it has been done at finite gap width, and it is exact in $N_c$.

The main kinematic point is that out-of-gap emissions need $k_t<Qe^{-Y/2}$. The available logarithmic range is therefore $\Lambda=L-Y/2$, not $L$. The first term shows how much this matters:

$$\sigma^{(5)}=-\frac{\pi^2}{210}\,\frac{N_c^2-1}{N_c^4}\,\bar\alpha^5\,Y\,(L-Y/2)^7$$

Already at $Y=L/10$ this is about 30% below the narrow-gap value.

**Method.**
- Emissions and Coulomb exchanges are $k_t$-ordered with respect to the $ab$ dipole.
- The soft-collinear replacement $\omega_{ij}\to\Theta_{ij}$ collapses gap evolution to $R(k_t)\,\mathbf{T}_R^2$, which also removes the non-global logarithms.
- The result is expanded to two Coulomb exchanges. Between them, $\mathbf{T}_R^2$ is projected onto fund⊗adj $=f\oplus R_+\oplus R_-$.
- The color factor has a closed form, $(-1)^n 2^{n-j}(2^j-2)\dim R_\rho/N_c^5$, and the result reduces to a 2D integral (their Eq. 14).
- The linear-in-$Y$ limit reproduces Becher–Neubert–Shao–Stillger Eq. (7.7).

**Phenomenological result.** At their Fig. 11 kinematics ($Q=1$ TeV, $Y=2$, $Y/L=0.43$–$0.67$), the full result is only **4–7% of the $Y$-linear prediction**. This substantially deflates earlier CVL size estimates.

**Caveats.**
- Only the leading tower is resummed; they say subleading logarithms are essential for real phenomenology.
- The $\omega\to\Theta$ error is argued to be two logarithms down. This is checked numerically only through $\bar\alpha^8$, at $N_c=2,3$ and $Y/L=1/4,\,1/2$.
- The result serves as an analytic benchmark for amplitude-level showers.

## Muon collider

### [arXiv:2611.21813](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21813) — Probing τ lepton dipole moments at multi-TeV muon colliders (Dario Buttazzo et al.)

**Setup.** The study works with dimension-six $C_{\tau B}$ and $C_{\tau W}$, generated with MG5 and Whizard via the LEPTONdipole UFO. Channels are:
- direct $\tau\tau$ production with $m_{\tau\tau}>0.8\sqrt s$;
- an inclusive "VBS" sample in the lower-mass region;
- $\tau\tau H$, $\tau\tau Z$ and $W\tau\nu$;
- the radiative decay $H\to\tau\tau\gamma$.

**Why $\tau\tau H$ wins.** The SM–dipole interference is chirality-suppressed by $m_\tau v/s$, so quadratic terms dominate. The $(v+H)$ structure of the operators gives a $\tau\tau HV$ contact term with $\sigma_{\rm quad}\propto s/\Lambda^4$. As a result, the bound on $C/\Lambda^2$ scales roughly as $1/s$ in $\tau\tau H$, versus $1/\sqrt s$ in direct production. Because $g_V\simeq-0.02$, the $\gamma$–$Z$ cross term is small and the $(C_{\tau\gamma}, C_{\tau Z})$ contours are nearly diagonal.

**Projected 10 TeV reach ($\mathcal{L}\propto s$ benchmark):**

| Quantity | Direct $\tau\tau$ | VBS sample | $\tau\tau H$ | Combined |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $\lvert\Delta a_\tau\rvert$ | 8.8e-5 | 7.8e-5 | 1.2e-5 | 1.2e-5 |
| $\lvert d_\tau\rvert$ [e cm] | 4.8e-19 | 4.3e-19 | 6.8e-20 | 6.6e-20 |
| $\lvert\Delta a_\tau^Z\rvert$ | 1.4e-4 | 9.1e-5 | 2.0e-5 | 1.8e-5 |
| $\lvert d_\tau^Z\rvert$ [e cm] | 8.0e-19 | 5.2e-19 | 1.1e-19 | 1.0e-19 |

- The inclusive VBS sample overtakes direct production at around 10 TeV.
- At 14 TeV, $\tau\tau H$ alone reaches $6.3\times10^{-6}$ on $\Delta a_\tau$ and $3.5\times10^{-20}$ e cm on $d_\tau$.
- For scale, current limits are ATLAS/CMS $a_\tau\sim10^{-3}$ and Belle $d_\tau\sim10^{-17}$ e cm.
- Per the abstract, the reach is substantially stronger than FCC-ee on EDMs and complementary on magnetic moments.
