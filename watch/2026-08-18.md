## Muon collider

### [arXiv:2608.15656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15656) — Probing the ALP–Photon–Dark Photon Interaction through the Mono-Photon plus Missing-Energy Signature at a Muon Collider (Chong-Xing Yue et al.)

A detector-level (MadGraph5+Pythia8+Delphes generic MC card) study of the dark axion portal coupling $g_{a\gamma\gamma'}$ via $\mu^+\mu^-\to a\gamma'$, $a\to\gamma\gamma'$ at $\sqrt{s}=1.5$ TeV, $\mathcal{L}=500$ fb$^{-1}$, assuming $m_a\gg m_{\gamma'}$ so both dark photons escape and the signal is mono-photon + MET. Production runs through $s$-channel $\gamma$ and $Z$, with the EW-gauge-invariance relation $g_{aZ\gamma'}=-\tan\theta_W\,g_{a\gamma\gamma'}$. The dominant background is $\mu^+\mu^-\to\nu\bar\nu\gamma$, whose radiative-return peak ($E_\gamma\approx750$ GeV, $Z\to\nu\bar\nu$ on-shell) is killed by cuts on MET, $|\eta_\gamma|<1.2$, and recoil mass. Projected $2\sigma$ reach spans $g_{a\gamma\gamma'}\sim1.7\times10^{-5}$–$9.1\times10^{-5}$ GeV$^{-1}$ over $1\le m_a\le1200$ GeV for the fully polarized $P(\mu^+,\mu^-)=(-100\%,+100\%)$ beam, and $\sim1.0\times10^{-4}$–$4.5\times10^{-4}$ GeV$^{-1}$ unpolarized.

For the muon-collider topic this is a first look at the dark axion portal at a muon machine (previously only $e^+e^-$/hadron colliders), and its distinctive contribution is reach in ALP mass up to $\sim$1.2 TeV — well beyond the $\mathcal{O}(10\text{–}80)$ GeV windows of LEP/BaBar and the $m_a\lesssim50$ GeV coverage of the FCC-ee projection. That said, in raw coupling sensitivity it is outperformed everywhere by FCC-ee ($10^{-6}$–$10^{-7}$), STCF ($10^{-6}$), and Belle II/BESIII ($10^{-5}$); the value claimed is complementarity via high-mass coverage, not depth.

Two visible caveats blunt the headline. The strong ($10^{-5}$) numbers rely on ideal $\pm100\%$ beam polarization, which the authors concede is unrealistic; the unpolarized significances collapse to $SS=1.8\to0.35$ across $m_a=100\to1000$ GeV, i.e. below $2\sigma$ over much of the range. Prompt $a$ decay is assumed throughout — a long-lived/displaced treatment (left to future work) would change both signal efficiency and background.
