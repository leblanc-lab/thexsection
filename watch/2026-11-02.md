## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2610.41889](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41889) — Reconstruct everything everywhere all at once: An end-to-end model for future colliders (Max Hart et al.)

This is mainly an end-to-end reconstruction paper, not a substructure paper. Its jet relevance lies in two downstream outputs: jet energy resolution and learned particle-to-boson assignment. REM consists of two MaskFormer stages (17.2M parameters, about 30 ms/event on a GPU) that run on raw CLD hits from the full Geant4 simulation. The first stage does tracking, calorimeter clustering, PID and regression. The second assigns the resulting particle queries to W/Z/H candidates.

**Jet energy resolution.** In 240 GeV WW/ZZ/ZH events, jets are exclusive VLC jets (R=1.1, β=γ=1), compared against jets clustered from truth targets. Results:

| Method | JER | σ68 |
|---|---|---|
| REM | 2.2±0.1% | 4.9% |
| FlowFormer (learned PF on conformal tracks) | 2.9% | 6.1% |
| PandoraPFA | 4.6% | 7.0% |

Pandora's charged and neutral energy biases (medians 0.78 and 1.42) cancel in the total; REM recovers the correct partition.

**W/Z mass separation S.**

| Method | Semileptonic | Fully hadronic |
|---|---|---|
| Pandora + VLC | 2.02 | 1.46 |
| REM + VLC | 2.87 | 1.96 |
| REM resonance masks | 3.03±0.12 | 2.22 |
| Truth VLC (reference) | 3.78 | 2.57 |

In the fully hadronic channel, the learned assignment approaches the truth-level clustering-plus-pairing ceiling (2.57). This shows how much S is lost to the jet-pairing step itself (smallest dijet-mass difference).

**Caveats, several stated by the authors:**
- IRC safety of the learned assignment is not established, and generator dependence is untested.
- Masks are supervised on true parentage rather than a jet-algorithm-defined target. Part of the mass-peak recentering likely reflects this choice.
- The evaluation excludes, via truth matching, real particles outside the "tight" selection.
- REM applies an IoU/calorimeter-fraction filter before forming jets.
- The Pandora S values fall below the CLD design report's 2.1–2.6, which used a different geometry and software setup.
- Boson labeling is uneven: ZZ events are correctly labeled only 62%.
