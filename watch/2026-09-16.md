# Muon collider

### [arXiv:2609.16769](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16769) — Probing the Tau Neutrino Magnetic Moment via μ⁻γ → μ⁻νν̄ at Future Muon Colliders (D. Yilmaz et al.)

Model-independent EFT sensitivity study of the tau-neutrino diagonal magnetic moment μ_ττ through the photon-induced subprocess μ⁻γ → μ⁻ν_τν̄_τ, with the initial photon treated via the Weizsäcker–Williams EPA. Full MadGraph5+PYTHIA8+DELPHES3 (muon-collider card) chain, with cuts optimized per energy: at 3 TeV they require p_T(μ⁻)>600 GeV and MET>150 GeV; at 30 TeV p_T(μ⁻)>4500 GeV, MET>1500 GeV, plus a charged-lepton veto. Using Asimov significance with 5-year luminosities scaled as L=10 ab⁻¹(√s/10 TeV)², the 5σ reach is μ_ττ=7.52×10⁻⁷ μ_B (3 TeV, 0.9 ab⁻¹), 2.10×10⁻⁷ (10 TeV), and 5.21×10⁻⁸ μ_B (30 TeV, 90 ab⁻¹, 1% systematics) — up to an order of magnitude below the DONUT direct bound (3.9×10⁻⁷ μ_B) and beating LHC γp (~2×10⁻⁶) and CLIC (~9×10⁻⁷) projections.

For this topic, the paper is a straightforward extension of the authors' own earlier PLB study of dimension-7 ννγγ operators (ref [26]) to the dimension-5 dipole operator, reusing the identical process and simulation setup. The muon collider is essentially a high-energy photon-flux facility here; the physics case rests on the energy scaling of the anomalous cross section. Caveat: sensitivity degrades notably with systematics (5σ at 3 TeV moves from 7.5×10⁻⁷ to 1.15×10⁻⁶ at 10%), and only at 3 TeV/1.28σ does the reach merely match DONUT — the "order of magnitude" claim relies on the 10–30 TeV options with optimistic luminosity and 1% systematics.

### [arXiv:2609.17530](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17530) — Vanilla Scotogenic Model at the Future Muon Collider (Niloy Mondal et al.)

Central to the topic. The authors revive the vanilla scotogenic model (inert doublet + 3 RHNs, Z₂-odd) by exploiting a Casas-Ibarra choice (z₂₃=0+7i) that inflates 2nd/3rd-generation Yukawas to O(0.1), boosting the N₁ CP asymmetry and lowering successful leptogenesis to m_N₁≳1.4 TeV — set by the interplay of sphaleron decoupling (m_N₁/10≳131 GeV) and ΔL=2 washout (fails for λ″_ηH≲10⁻⁴). This brings the spectrum into muon-collider range while remaining consistent with LZ-2025, MEG II (BR(μ→eγ)<1.5×10⁻¹³), and relic density (DM mass pinned to ~550–650 GeV, or heavier with large Yukawas via t-channel ηη†→LL̄).

The muon-collider content is the paper's payoff. Compressed spectra make η⁺ long-lived, so pair production (μ⁺μ⁻→η⁺η⁻ and same-sign μ⁺μ⁺→η⁺η⁺) is analyzed at 3/10 TeV (opposite-sign) and 6/10 TeV (same-sign). A key observable: s–t interference in μ⁺μ⁻ produces a cross-section dip whose position tracks m_N₂ and |h_μ2|, while the same-sign channel (only t-channel via Majorana RHN) has no dip and is a direct LNV signature unique to the scotogenic mechanism. Prompt mono-γ + MET searches are found largely insensitive — for the benchmark, no exclusion at 1 ab⁻¹, with μ⁺μ⁺ runs outperforming μ⁺μ⁻ due to absence of gauge-mediated production. LLP/displaced-vertex searches are the promising avenue, subject to timing/vertex reconstruction. Caveat: the text cuts off mid-sentence in the prompt-search results, and quantitative LLP reach at the collider is not fully shown in the provided excerpt (HL-LHC DT projections reach ~700 GeV for δm≃0.02–0.2 GeV).

## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2609.16276](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16276) — PSICHE: Probabilistic, Structure-Intrinsic Clustering with Hierarchical Embedding for Jet Reconstruction (Margaret Lazarovits et al.)

A genuinely new clustering paradigm rather than a substructure measurement. PSICHE embeds a variational Gaussian mixture model (vGMM) inside Bayesian Hierarchical Clustering, so that jet multiplicity/scale (tree level) and internal substructure (mixture level) are inferred jointly and unsupervised, with no pre-set cone size or prong count. Key novelties for substructure: (i) dynamically learned, variable jet sizes; (ii) simultaneous multi-scale learning of subclusters; (iii) clustering in space *and time* (η,φ,t), with per-cell energy/time measurement precisions Λ*_n folded directly into the likelihood; (iv) energy-proportional data weights ω_n=ω·E_n that give jet-level IRC safety and preserve four-vector sums. Subcluster proliferation is controlled by hierarchical inheritance (child multiplicity seeded to sum of parents) plus explicit pairwise merge comparisons via L2 Gaussian overlap. Nearest-neighbor (Voronoi/Delaunay, FastJet 3.4-based) merge restriction brings scaling to ~N ln N.

Concrete substructure results: on boosted single-W samples PSICHE consistently resolves the two-prong parton structure as two subclusters, where a vGMM run post-hoc on AK-jet constituents fails to regulate multiplicity. In tt̄, it dynamically reconstructs both resolved and boosted top/W jets, tracking jet size ∝ m_jet/p_T across p̂_T^min=200→500 GeV, whereas AK jets impose hard cone cutoffs. A top-jet decomposes into three localized prongs (bb + W→qq) plus a dispersive soft-radiation component. The hyperparameter ω (=4 GeV here) softly sets the resolved scale — this replaces the cone parameter but is not eliminated. Validation is against anti-k_T (R=0.4/0.8/1.5) on Pythia 8.3 + idealized CMS-ECAL-like calo/PF simulation; there is no comparison yet to standard substructure taggers (N-subjettiness, soft drop) or performance under realistic detector/BIB effects. The time dimension enabling new time-based substructure observables (subcluster times, space-time covariance) is the most distinctive extension for the field.
