## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2610.21982](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21982) — First Observation of the Radiative Decay $D^0 \to \gamma\omega$ (BESIII Collaboration et al.)

**Relevance:** This is not a jet paper. It matched because BESIII uses Particle Transformer (ParT), the jet-tagging architecture, as an event-level classifier in a charm analysis. Its interest for this topic is as a data point on how well jet-tagging architectures transfer to other settings.

**What they did.** With 20.3 fb⁻¹ at √s = 3.773 GeV and the double-tag method, BESIII represents each recoil-side event as two "particle clouds":

- **Tracks:** angles, momentum, helix parameters, and dE/dx and TOF pulls.
- **Showers:** angles, energy, crystal multiplicity, E₃ₓ₃/E₅ₓ₅, lateral/secondary moments, and Zernike moments A20 and A42.

ParT was trained on about 0.84M balanced signal MC vs. inclusive-MC events (80/20 split). The dominant background, $D^0\to\pi^+\pi^-\pi^0\pi^0$, is essentially degenerate with signal in $M_{BC}$ and $\Delta E$. The yield is therefore extracted from an unbinned fit to the ParT score in [0.5, 1].

**Results.**
- $N_{DT} = 79.8\pm14.9$, which is 7.0σ statistical only and 6.0σ with fit-related systematics.
- $\mathcal{B} = (2.12\pm0.40\pm0.25)\times10^{-5}$, above all long-distance predictions. CLFQM comes closest.
- First determination of $\mathcal{D}^{\omega-\rho} = 0.15\pm0.24$.

**Notes for ML-tagger practice.**
- **Ensemble-based uncertainty.** BESIII trained 100 ensemble members with varied initialization, batch order and dropout. The spread of the resulting branching fractions, 10.9%, is taken as a "model uncertainty."
- **That uncertainty dominates.** It is the largest systematic of the 12.1% total.
- **Domain shift looks small, but is only partly tested.** It is estimated at just 0.4% from re-measuring $D^0\to\pi^+\pi^-\pi^0$; a $\pi^0\eta'$ control channel also agrees with earlier measurements. However, neither control channel has a lone high-energy radiative photon. The discrimination plausibly leans on shower-shape features, so photon-specific data/MC mismodelling is not directly probed.

**Other caveats.**
- Background score shapes come entirely from inclusive MC, with the main background yield varied by ±30%.
- Choosing the "nominal" network as the one nearest the ensemble mean is a convention.
- The cut-based cross-check is only in the supplement.
