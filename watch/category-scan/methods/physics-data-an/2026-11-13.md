## Data & computational methods

### [arXiv:2611.14567](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14567) — Learning to Calibrate Jet Energies (Bogdan Malaescu et al.)

_Watchlist hit (Large Hadron Collider, collider, Ben Nachman, Daniel Whiteson) — original abstract; no AI summary._

Hadronic jets are produced copiously at the Large Hadron Collider, and are a vital element of its physics program. Accurate reconstruction of their energies is therefore crucial, including correction for pile-up, detector response and data-simulation differences. Currently, each correction is performed by fitting parametric functional forms, an approach which is effective but can be fragile and challenging to extend. We present a prototype machine-learning pipeline which performs pile-up dependency removal, absolute corrections and data-mc corrections. No functional form is imposed on the correction, additional features can be easily added, and the pipeline matches or exceeds the performance of the functional approach at nearly every stage.
