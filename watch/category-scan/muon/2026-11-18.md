## Muon collider

### [arXiv:2611.22982](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22982) — Muon Collider: Towards the Energy Frontier (Mark A. Palmer et al.)

_Watchlist hit (muon collider) — original abstract; no AI summary._

A muon collider has emerged as a compelling candidate for the next energy-frontier particle accelerator, combining the discovery potential of multi-TeV lepton collisions with a footprint and power consumption far smaller than existing or proposed hadron and electron-positron colliders. Advances over the past two decades in high-power targets, muon production and capture, ionization cooling, rapid acceleration, high-field magnets, collider ring design, and machine-detector integration have transformed the concept from a long-term vision into a credible path toward a future flagship facility. This review summarizes the physics opportunities, the accelerator complex's design and status, the unique experimental environment, recent technical achievements, ongoing international R&D, and remaining steps toward realizing a high-energy muon collider by the middle of the century.
