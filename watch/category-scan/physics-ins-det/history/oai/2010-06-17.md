## physics.ins-det

### [arXiv:1006.3421](https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.3421) — Simulation Study of FPCCD Vertex Detector (Kohei Yoshida et al.)

_Watchlist hit (collider) — original abstract; no AI summary._

We are developing the vertex detector with a fine pixel CCD (FPCCD) for the international linear collider (ILC), whose pixel size is $5 \times 5$ $μ$m$^{2}$. To evaluate the performance of the FPCCD vertex detector and optimize its design, development of the software dedicated for the FPCCD is necessary. We, therefore, started to develop the software for FPCCD. In this article, the status of the study is reported.

### [arXiv:1006.3503](https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.3503) — The SiD Detector Concept (Andrew P. White)

_Watchlist hit (collider) — original abstract; no AI summary._

The SiD detector concept is one of two currently accepted approaches to providing a detector for the future International Linear Collider. The SiD design philosophy is described and an overview of the concept given. Each major component of SiD is described, with emphasis on the critical R&D issues in each area. The context of the paper is evolution of the SiD design and the associated R&D towards the Detailed Baseline Design in late 2012.
