### [arXiv:2101.05059](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.05059) — High Precision Digitizer for High Luminosity LHC (Miguel Bastos et al.)

_Watchlist hit (LHC) — original abstract; no AI summary._

One of the key elements of the HL-LHC project is the replacement of the magnets that focus the beams near the interaction points of ATLAS and CMS. The new magnets also call for higher precision powering, which is strongly dependent on the performance of the electric current measurement chain. We present a new metrology-grade digitizer developed at CERN, which is part of that high-precision measurement system.
