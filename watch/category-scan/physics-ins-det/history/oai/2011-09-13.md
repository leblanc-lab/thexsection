## physics.ins-det

### [arXiv:1109.2811](https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.2811) — Chronopixel Vertex Detectors for Future Linear Colliders (C. Baltay et al.)

_Watchlist hit (colliders) — original abstract; no AI summary._

Over the past few years we have developed, in collaboration with the SARNOFF Corporation, a design of a Monolithic CMOS Pixel Detector for ILC Vertex Detectors. The unique feature of this design is that each hit is accompanied by a time tag with sufficient precision to assign each hit to a particular bunch crossing of the ILC (thus the name Chronopixel). This reduces the occupancy even in the innermost Vertex Detector layer to negligible levels, allowing a robust Vertex Detector even in the case that the backgrounds in the ILC are higher than presently estimated.
