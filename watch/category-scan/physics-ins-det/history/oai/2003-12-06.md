### [arXiv:physics/0312039](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0312039) — The Vertex Tracker at Future e+e- Linear Colliders (Marco Battaglia)

_Watchlist hit (colliders) — original abstract; no AI summary._

The physics program of high energy e+e- linear colliders relies on the accurate identification of fermions to study in details the profile of the Higgs boson, search for new particles and later probe the multi-TeV mass region by direct searches and precision electro-weak measurements. This paper reviews the requirements, conceptual design and sensor R&D for the Vertex Tracker.
