## physics.ins-det

### [arXiv:2105.06245](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06245) — Integrated luminosity measurement at CEPC (Ivan Smiljanić et al.)

_Watchlist hit (collider) — original abstract; no AI summary._

The very forward region is one of the most challenging regions to instrument at a future $e^+e^-$ collider. At CEPC, machine-detector interface includes, among others, a calorimeter dedicated for precision measurement of the integrated luminosity at a permille level or better. Here we review a feasibility of such precision, from the point of view of luminometer mechanical precision and positioning, beam-related requirements and physics background.A method of the effective center-of-mass determination from $e^+e^- \to μ^+μ^-$, initially proposed for FCC, is also discussed for the CEPC beams.
