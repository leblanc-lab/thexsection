### [arXiv:0811.4759](https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.4759) — LCFI Vertex Package (Sonja Hillert)

_Watchlist hit (flavour tagging, collider) — original abstract; no AI summary._

The LCFIVertex software, developed by the Linear Collider Flavour Identification (LCFI) collaboration and providing tools for vertexing, flavour tagging and quark charge determination for low-mass vertex detectors of high point resolution is presented. Particular emphasis is given to code extensions since the first release in April 2007. A recently developed new vertex finder, ZVMST, and its performance at sqrt(s) = 91.2 GeV are described in more detail.
