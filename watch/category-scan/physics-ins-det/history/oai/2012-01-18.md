### [arXiv:1201.3836](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.3836) — Polarisation and Polarimetry at HERA (Blanka Sobloher)

_Watchlist hit (collider) — original abstract; no AI summary._

Longitudinal polarisation of the lepton beam is a key ingredient to the success of the world's unique e\pmp ring collider HERA. This article aims at providing a brief introduction to the physics motivation for deep-inelastic scattering of polarised electrons or positrons off protons, the basic mechanisms to establish lepton polarisation in the high-energy storage ring and to describe briefly the three different polarimeters, which measured both the transverse and the longitudinal polarisation.
