### [arXiv:physics/0306016](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0306016) — The Geant4 Hadronic Verification Suite for the Cascade Energy Range (V. Ivanchenko et al.)

_Watchlist hit (LHC) — original abstract; no AI summary._

A Geant4 hadronic process verification suite has been designed to test and optimize Geant4 hadronic models in the cascade energy range. It focuses on quantities relevant to the LHC radiation environment and spallation source targets. The general structure of the suite is presented, including the user interface, stages of verification, management of experimental data, event generation, and comparison of results to data. Verification results for the newly released Binary cascade and Bertini cascade models are presented.
