## physics.ins-det

### [arXiv:1206.6793](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.6793) — Searching for magnetic monopoles trapped in accelerator material at the Large Hadron Collider (M. Dam Joergensen et al.)

_Watchlist hit (LHC, Large Hadron Collider, collider) — original abstract; no AI summary._

If produced in high energy particle collisions at the LHC, magnetic monopoles could stop in material surrounding the interaction points. Obsolete parts of the beam pipe near the CMS interaction region, which were exposed to the products of pp and heavy ion collisions, were analysed using a SQUID-based magnetometer. The purpose of this work is to quantify the performance of the magnetometer in the context of a monopole search using a small set of samples of accelerator material ahead of the 2013 shutdown.

### [arXiv:1206.6800](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.6800) — Studies of muon-induced radioactivity at NuMI (David J. Boehnlein)

_Watchlist hit (colliders) — original abstract; no AI summary._

The JASMIN Collaboration has studied the production of radionuclides by muons in the muon alcoves of the NuMI beamline at Fermilab. Samples of aluminum and copper are exposed to the muon field and counted on HpGe detectors when removed to determine their content of radioactive isotopes. We compare the results to MARS simulations and discuss the radiological implications for neutrino factories and muon colliders.
