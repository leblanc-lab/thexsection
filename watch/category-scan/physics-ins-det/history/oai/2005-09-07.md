## physics.ins-det

### [arXiv:physics/0509051](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0509051) — TPC Performance in Magnetic Fields with GEM and Pad Readout (D. Karlen et al.)

_Watchlist hit (collider) — original abstract; no AI summary._

A novel charged particle tracking device, a high precision time projection chamber with gas electron multiplier and pad readout, is a leading candidate as the central tracker for an experiment at the International Linear Collider. To characterize the performance of such a system, a small TPC has been operated within a magnetic field, measuring cosmic-ray and laser tracks. Good tracking resolution and two particle separation, sufficient for a large scale ILC central tracker, are achieved.
