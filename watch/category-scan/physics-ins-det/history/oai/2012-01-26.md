## physics.ins-det

### [arXiv:1201.4982](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.4982) — Refined reconstruction and calibration of the missing transverse energy in the ATLAS detector (Marianna Testa)

_Watchlist hit (LHC) — original abstract; no AI summary._

The measurement of the missing transverse energy Etmiss is fundamental for many analyses at LHC. Good Etmiss resolution and calibration are essential for searches of new physics as well as precise measurements. We describe a refined reconstruction and calibration of Etmiss developed by ATLAS and its performances on events containing Z and W bosons. The data sample was collected in proton-proton collisions at a center-of-mass energy of 7 TeV, and corresponds to an integrated luminosity of about 36 pb-1. The determination of the absolute scale of the Etmiss, fundamental for determining systematic uncertainties in all analysis involving Etmiss measurements, is also presented.

### [arXiv:1201.5469](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.5469) — ATLAS Upgrade for the HL-LHC: meeting the challenges of a five-fold increase in collision rate (Peter Vankov)

_Watchlist hit (LHC) — original abstract; no AI summary._

With the LHC successfully collecting data at 7 TeV, plans are actively advancing for a series of upgrades leading eventually to about five times the LHC design-luminosity some 10-years from now in the High-Luminosity LHC (HL-LHC) project. Coping with the high instantaneous and integrated luminosity will require many changes to the ATLAS detector. The designs are developing rapidly for an all-new inner-tracker, significant changes in the calorimeter and muon systems, as well as improved triggers. This article summarizes the environment expected at the HL-LHC and the status of various improvements to the ATLAS detector.
