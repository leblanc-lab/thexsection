## physics.ins-det

### [arXiv:1107.2064](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.2064) — LHC High Beta* Runs: Transport and Unfolding Methods (Maciej Trzebinski et al.)

_Watchlist hit (LHC) — original abstract; no AI summary._

The paper describes the transport of the elastically and diffractively scattered protons in the proton-proton interactions at the LHC for the high beta* runs. A parametrisation of the attered proton transport through the LHC magnetic lattice is presented. The accuracy of the unfolding of the kinematic variables of the scattered protons is discussed.
