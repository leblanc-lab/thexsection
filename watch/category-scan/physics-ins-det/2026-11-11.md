## physics.ins-det

### [arXiv:2611.14040](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14040) — Characterization of BNLs 20 and 30 micrometer thick LGADs for the Electron Ion Collider (F. Zareef et al.)

_Watchlist hit (collider) — original abstract; no AI summary._

The 20 and 30 micrometer thick Low-Gain Avalanche Detectors (LGADs) produced at Brookhaven National Laboratory (BNL) were evaluated. Detailed electrical measurements indicate well-formed and stable gain layers with consistently low leakage currents. Transient Current Technique (TCT) was used to evaluate the gain. Fast-laser timing studies demonstrate time resolutions at the level of a few tens of picoseconds. To assess operational longevity under realistic conditions for Electron-Ion Collider sub-detector systems, devices were irradiated with 30 MeV and 60 MeV protons to fluences of 10^13 to 10^14 neq/cm^2, enabling a focused study of radiation-induced effects on gain stability and timing performance.
