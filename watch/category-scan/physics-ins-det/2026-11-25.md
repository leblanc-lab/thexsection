### [arXiv:2611.31311](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31311) — Machine learning for trigger, reconstruction and generation (Marco Giacalone)

_Watchlist hit (jet tagger, flavour tagging, LHC) — original abstract; no AI summary._

Machine learning (ML) is increasingly embedded in every stage of the LHC data-processing chain, from event generation to final reconstruction. This contribution reviews the latest ML developments from the ALICE, ATLAS, CMS and LHCb collaborations that were made public in the year preceding LHCP2026, following the workflow of the experiments: event generation and detector simulation, real-time trigger selection, and online/offline reconstruction. Highlights include generative models that accelerate the simulation of specific ALICE detector components by up to six orders of magnitude, the CMS AXOL1TL and ATLAS GELATO unsupervised anomaly- detection triggers now operating in Run 3, a quantised Deep Sets jet tagger for the CMS Phase-2 Level-1 trigger, ML-based jet energy correction and flavour tagging at LHCb, the first empirical scaling laws for transformer-based flavour taggers in ATLAS, and several transformer and graph- based reconstruction algorithms (ATLAS GN3 flavour tagging and MaskFormer tracking, LHCb GarNet, CMS MLPF) developed for HL-LHC operation.
