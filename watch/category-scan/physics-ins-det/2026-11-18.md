## physics.ins-det

### [arXiv:2611.22201](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22201) — Quality Control for the LGAD sensors of the ATLAS High Granularity Timing Detector (K. Ma et al.)

_Watchlist hit (LHC, Large Hadron Collider, collider) — original abstract; no AI summary._

The High Granularity Timing Detector (HGTD) currently in the production phase will mainly mitigate the pile-up effect in the ATLAS forward region and serve for bunch-by-bunch luminosity measurements in the high-luminosity (HL) phase of the Large Hadron Collider (LHC). The HGTD utilizes Low Gain Avalanche Detector (LGAD) technology, covering the pseudorapidity range between 2.4 and 4.0, and provides high-precision timing information to distinguish collisions occurring close in space but well-separated in time. A pre-production batch of 8-inch silicon wafers was fabricated and a systematic quality control test campaign was carried out. This paper describes the Quality Control (QC) required for full-size (15 $\times$ 15 array) sensors, including leakage-current and breakdown measurements performed on all sensors at the vendor sites, as well as more detailed electrical characterizations at the participating ATLAS-HGTD institutes. In addition, the electrical characterization of full-size sensors after irradiation is reported.
