## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2611.21491](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21491) — Unraveling QCD dynamics via charm-tagged jets with ALICE at the LHC (Emma Yeats (on behalf of the ALICE Collaboration))

This is a conference proceeding (Yeats, ALICE). It reviews three already-released D⁰-tagged charged-jet substructure measurements (anti-k_T, R=0.4, D⁰→K⁻π⁺) rather than presenting new data.

- **EEC (13 TeV, 10<p_T^ch jet<15 GeV/c; ALICE [arXiv:2504.03431](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.03431)).** The D⁰-tagged EEC is suppressed across the full R_L range and has a reduced integral, attributed to the large charm dead cone at low p_T. Its peak sits at roughly the same R_L as the inclusive EEC (~76% gluon jets). The author explains this as two effects cancelling: the gluon Casimir factor broadens inclusive jets, while the dead cone pushes the charm EEC to larger R_L.
- **Jet-axis differences (5.02 TeV; PRD 112, 092012).** The WTA axis and the D⁰ direction are aligned in (99±1)% of jets for 10–20 GeV/c and p_T^D⁰>5 GeV/c, so the D⁰ is effectively always in the leading prong. ΔR_STD−D⁰ shows a single-track-jet spike plus a broad soft-radiation peak near ΔR≈0.02 (the latter seen in PYTHIA). Soft Drop grooming (β=0, z_cut=0.1, 0.2) progressively depletes small ΔR, while all axis choices converge at the largest ΔR.
- **Angularities κ=1, α=1,3 ([arXiv:2606.20028](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20028)).** D⁰-tagged λ₁ peaks well below the inclusive distribution. It resembles inclusive jets with a 5.33 GeV/c leading-track cut, which enriches light quarks. The difference shrinks for α=3.

**What it adds to the topic:** a compact synthesis of how dead-cone and Casimir effects compete in IRC-safe observables. The degenerate EEC peak position is the key subtlety: it means peak location alone cannot isolate mass effects (cf. Craft et al.).

**Caveats:**
- PYTHIA 8 describes everything within uncertainties.
- The tension between the pQCD calculation and the EEC is stated but not quantified here.
- No numerical peak positions or goodness-of-fit values are given.
- Attributions to the dead cone versus Casimir factors are qualitative.
