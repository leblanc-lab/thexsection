# Private briefing: 2026-11-10

## Muon collider

### [arXiv:2611.12793](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12793) — Electroweak Parton Distribution Functions for Future Lepton Colliders (Tao Han et al.)

Han, Kilian, Ma, Mękała, Reuter and Xie have put the Han–Ma–Xie LL EW PDF grids into Whizard. The grids are accessed through a modified LHAPDF interface (`lhapdf_ew`) with a helicity-resolved three-digit PID scheme of 59 entries. Evolution runs QED-only below μ_QCD (0.5 or 0.7 GeV), then QED+QCD up to m_Z, then the unbroken SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1) above m_Z. Longitudinal W/Z are included only at leading-order EWA. The Higgs PDF and the mixed γ/Z and H/Z₀ states are placeholders, not yet used in the convolution.

Benchmarks at √s = 10 TeV:
- **Mini-jet background.** Inclusive QCD-induced dijets reach pb-level cross sections, with gg and gq dominant. They exceed γγ→qq̄ for p_T^j < 10 GeV. The result agrees with Han–Ma–Xie and agrees with an independent calculation to 5–10%.
- **HH, tt̄, XX (1 TeV scalar).** EW PDF and EWA agree with the full matrix element above roughly 1 TeV. EW PDF sits about 5–20% below EWA, which the authors attribute to resummation and momentum-sum-rule preservation.
- **Polarization separation.** HH at high subleading-Higgs p_T is dominated by W_T W_T, not W_0 W_0. This suggests an observable-based helicity separation.
- **Scale choice.** 2H_T is preferred over √ŝ/2. With it, ντν̄τ agrees within 20%.
- **WW→WW.** This case fails badly: the approximation overshoots by more than 10× because gauge cancellations are lost. It only recovers order-of-magnitude agreement with |η| < 2.44 (10°, detector-motivated), and the authors call that agreement "somewhat accidental".

Why it matters: this gives an out-of-the-box parton-level generator for mini-jet backgrounds and VBF signals at a muon collider. Caveats:
- Output is parton level only. Beam remnants and the shower interface are deferred.
- A single mass threshold at m_Z is used.
- Annihilation topologies (e.g. ZHH→ννHH) dominate the high-mass tail. The authors use an e⁻μ⁺ trick to remove them in comparisons.

### [arXiv:2611.10041](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10041) — Physics in Safety and Safety in Physics: Lessons from the LHC and its Underground Experiments (Alexander S. Sakharov)

This is tangential; the muon collider appears only as one future facility named in the abstract. The paper is a conceptual framework that treats LHC/ATLAS/CMS safety as a "safe operational phase space" Ω_safe plus a set of admissible trajectories between states. Its quantitative content is textbook scale-setting:
- 362 MJ per nominal beam
- about 10 GJ of magnet energy
- 1.6 GJ (ATLAS magnets) and 2.6 GJ (CMS solenoid)
- He II expansion of about 10³
- the 2008 sector 3–4 incident

The provided text truncates before the future-facilities section (Sec. VIII), so nothing muon-collider-specific can be reported. Skip unless you care about operations philosophy.

## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2611.11012](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11012) — Sensitivity of jet and dijet observables to medium effects in pp collisions at √s = 13 TeV using X-SCAPE/JETSCAPE (Lakshitha Fernando et al.)

The authors run two JETSCAPE/X-SCAPE studies with anti-k_T jets at R = 0.2 and 0.4.

**Bjorken brick (MATTER, 10⁷ events per sample).** The scan covers L = 0.5–2 fm and q̂₀ = 0.25 and 0.5 GeV²/fm. Dijet mass is the most responsive observable. Suppression is ordered in L and q̂, and is stronger for R = 0.2. The mean A_J shifts by only about +0.002, and no Δφ broadening is seen.

**Realistic sample (3D Glauber + MUSIC + MATTER, 10⁶ events each, about six weeks of grid time).** Vacuum and medium are compared in the top-20% event-activity class.
- Medium/vacuum ratios sit slightly below 1, with no monotonic trend.
- The mean A_J rises by 0.0056 ± 0.0018 (about 3σ) and 0.0057 ± 0.0020 (about 2.9σ) in the two leading-jet p_T bins.
- For Soft Drop (z_cut = 0.1, β = 0, 60–80 GeV, R = 0.2), θ_g and m_g show only bin-to-bin structure. There is no θ_g narrowing of the kind ALICE sees in Pb–Pb, and the tagging fraction is about 0.99 in both samples.

The useful conclusion is the size of the expected signal. It is smaller than the multiplicity-selection effects ALICE already measures in pp and PYTHIA reproduces without a medium. Caveats:
- The event-activity estimator includes hard-sector yield, so the vacuum and medium classes differ.
- Soft-background subtraction is idealized (perfect).
- Recoil is switched off in the realistic sample.

### [arXiv:2611.11600](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11600) — Threshold resummation for top-quark pair production in association with a jet at the LHC (Bakar Chargeishvili)

The main subject is tt̄+jet threshold resummation (NNLL in SCET for dσ/dM_tt̄j). Two sections matter for jet physics:

- **Jet ingredients.** The factorization uses an unmeasured jet function at μ_j = p_T^j·R and a coft function at μ_c ≈ ωR_eff/2. Non-global logarithms are included via the Dasgupta–Salam large-N_c parametrization. The factorization requires ω ≪ 2E_J, which fails for a fixed jet threshold of a few tens of GeV. There μ_c > μ_j and the evolution produces an unphysical enhancement. The author therefore sets μ_c = μ_j = μ_s, keeping ln R at fixed order and making the non-global factor trivially 1. They also use a scaling threshold p_T^j > 0.15M.
- **Shower and R dependence.** NLO+PS reduces the R = 0.4 cross section by 10–24% relative to NLO (shower uncertainty −6%/+14%). The reduction largely vanishes at R = 1.0, which points to ln R effects beyond what this resummation captures.

The headline physics result is negative:
- The leading-power kernel tracks exact real emission only for emitted energy below about 100–200 GeV.
- The correction beyond NLO ranges from 1–2% to 17–23% depending on how that region is treated, so it is not under perturbative control. NLO stays the central prediction.

Three errors are corrected in the published one-loop soft anomalous-dimension matrices for this process: s_tt̄ vs ŝ in L_β, the sign of an iπ term, and missing top–jet imaginary parts.

### [arXiv:2611.11957](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11957) — Measurements of CP violation with charm hadrons at Belle and Belle II (Jaeyoung Kim (on behalf of the Belle II Collaboration))

This is not a jet paper; the keyword match is spurious, at most via flavor tagging. It is a proceedings summary of charm CPV, and all results are consistent with zero:
- A_CP(D⁰→π⁰π⁰) = (0.30 ± 0.72 ± 0.20)%
- A_CP(D⁰→K_S K_S), combined = (−0.6 ± 1.1 ± 0.1)%
- Preliminary η h⁺ modes, and the first Λ_c/Ξ_c three-body baryon asymmetries.

The only loosely adjacent piece is the inclusive charm-flavor tagger. It uses charged-particle correlations in the rest of the event (effective efficiency 47.9%) and adds about 40% effective statistics in the K_S K_S mode.
