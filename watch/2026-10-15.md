## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2610.20164](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20164) — The dead-cone effect in charm, bottom and top quark jets in angular and momentum space (Redamy Perez-Ramos et al.)

This is a QCD 26 proceedings that consolidates the authors' earlier work (PRD 107, 094039 and [arXiv:2512.19874](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19874)). Most results are therefore not new.

- **LEP recap (c, b at the Z pole).** Heavy-quark fragmentation functions, corrected for primary heavy-hadron decays, fall to $\bar D_b/\bar D_{uds}\sim 10^{-1}$ at $\xi_p\simeq2$. The suppression is established at >5σ for both flavours. The MLLA subtraction $\bar D_Q(\xi,W)=\bar D_q(\xi,W)-\bar D_q(\xi-\xi_Q,\sqrt e M_Q)$, with $W_0^{(b,c)}=8.0,\,2.7$ GeV, describes the data for $1\lesssim\xi_p\lesssim3$ (b) and $1\lesssim\xi_p\lesssim2$ (c).
- **Top at √s = 1 TeV (Pythia only).** $\Theta_0\approx19.8^\circ$ and $W_0\approx286$ GeV, giving a predicted $\bar D_t/\bar D_{uds}\sim10^{-2}$ at $\xi_p\sim2$. MLLA agrees with the MC at the 10–15% level in the central region.

**Method.** The substantive technique is recovering the primary top radiation in the presence of the $\widehat{tb}$ decay-dipole emission:

1. Select production-stage radiation with $165\le M(b\ell\nu)\le175$ GeV.
2. Rotate each event so the b jet points along −X, and keep only partons with $X_p>0$, doubling the result.
3. Fit the $\xi_p$ spectra in bins of $X_b=\Theta_b/\Theta_0$ with a distorted Gaussian; the fit parameters are roughly linear in $X_b$.
4. Extrapolate to $X_b\to0$, where at leading order the decay-dipole contribution $\propto1-\cos\Theta_b$ vanishes.

Closure against a stable-top sample is about 5% at the peak and below 12% for $\xi_p\gtrsim3$.

**Angular observables fail.** The analogous extrapolation of $\Theta^2/\Theta_0^2$ distributions does not converge. Emissions from the b cascade fill the small-angle region, so the authors argue for fragmentation functions over angular/Lund-plane-style observables for the top dead cone. This is a useful counterpoint to the recent CMS and LHCb b-jet Lund-plane results they cite.

**Caveats.**
- Everything for the top is internal MC closure: Pythia, e⁺e⁻ at 1 TeV, leptonic decays only.
- Γ_t-dependent interference terms are neglected (≲10%).
- No detector effects or top-direction reconstruction are studied.
- The MLLA relation is not trusted near the kinematic endpoint.

**Centrality to the topic.** Moderately central. It is dead-cone physics with jet clustering (gen-$k_t$, R = 0.2), but the emphasis is on inclusive spectra rather than substructure observables.
