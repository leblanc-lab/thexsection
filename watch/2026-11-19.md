## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2611.23394](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23394) — Twist-4 QCD Factorization for One-Point Correlators at Next-to-Leading Order in Electron-Ion Collisions (Omar Elgedawy et al.)

Elgedawy, Kang and Xing define the observable ⟨sin²θ⟩ for the one-point energy correlator (OPEC, weight z_h) and the one-point charge correlator (OPCC, weight Q_h) in SIDIS. Here θ is the Breit-frame angle of the flux relative to the nucleus axis. The medium signal is Δ = eA − ep, which they compute at twist-4.

- **LO.** The result depends only on T_qg(x_B,0,0). Fragmentation functions enter only through the momentum sum rule (M=1) or the charge sum rule (M=Q_q).
- **NLO virtual.** The virtual correction is −2/ε² − 3/ε − 8, multiplying LO.
- **NLO real.** The 1/ε² from double scattering cancels against the virtual. The initial-state 1/ε is absorbed into DGLAP-like evolution of T_qg. The final-state poles organize into T_qg·P_qq and T_qg·P_gq and vanish via the sum rules ∫z(P_qq+P_gq)=0 and ∫P_qq=0.
- **Interference.** Single–triple scattering interference is finite.
- **Finite parts.** These also reduce to FF moments. This yields a fully perturbative twist-4 NLO formula; the coefficients are given in the supplement.

For jet substructure this is adjacent rather than central. The authors stress that this is a global flux angle, not the θ_ij of light-ray-OPE energy correlators. Its value is as a factorization template for medium-modified energy flow at NLO.

Caveats:
- **Dropped terms.** The O(α_s) real terms at x̂=1, ẑ<1 are omitted from the numerics because the k⊥ expansion fails for a soft measured parton. Their finite remainders also require a ẑ_min cut with ẑ_min-dependent terms discarded. This qualifies the "IRC-safe" claim in practice.
- **Partial numerics.** Only soft rescattering is evaluated (EPPS21, constant q̂₀=0.02 GeV²/fm, √s=89 GeV). Hard rescattering is dropped because the off-diagonal matrix elements are unknown.
- **Circular scaling claims.** The advertised linear A^{1/3} growth and 1/Q² suppression are put in through the model T_qg ∝ R_A q̂₀. They are therefore not independent confirmations.
- **No figure values in text.** Numerical values are not quoted in the text.

### [arXiv:2611.24241](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24241) — Search for Heavy Charged Higgs Bosons in Boosted Top and Bottom Quark Final States at a 1.4 TeV Future Lepton Collider (Majid Hashemi)

This is a CLIC 1.4 TeV, 1 ab⁻¹ study of e⁺e⁻→H⁺H⁻→tbtb for m_H± = 200–600 GeV (WHIZARD/Pythia/Delphes CLICdet).

**Relevance to substructure.** The paper is substructure-relevant only through a negative result. In the window 150 < m_jet < 200 GeV, the τ₃₂ distribution for H±-daughter tops and for prompt tt̄ tops overlap. Subjet multiplicity at fixed p_T is also identical. Any inclusive difference is a boost effect: signal tops reach p_T of 200–600 GeV.

**Results.** Discrimination comes from requiring ≥4 b-tags at the 90% working point plus a four-body rescaling that forces m_bbtt = √s.
- Total efficiency is 8.3% for the 600 GeV signal against 0.036% for tt̄.
- Cut-based significance peaks at Z ≈ 12.2 at 400 GeV, assuming a 10% background systematic.
- The BDT (AUC 0.86–0.90) reaches Z ≈ 18.7 at 400 GeV.

Caveats and internal inconsistencies:
- **Jet definition.** Large-R jets are C/A R=2.0 in Sec. IV but "R=1.0 (or exclusive k_t R=1.5–2.0)" in Sec. V.
- **Reach claim.** The conclusion claims 5σ up to 550–600 GeV cut-based, but Table 2 gives Z = 3.7 at 600 GeV.
- **ML status.** The conclusion lists ML as future work even though a BDT section is included.
- **Kinematic constraint.** The √s constraint ignores the beamstrahlung luminosity spectrum the paper itself plots.
