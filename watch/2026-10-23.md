## Muon collider

### [arXiv:2610.31312](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31312) — Lepton number and flavor violation mediated by doubly charged scalars at μTRISTAN μ⁺μ⁺ collider (Takeshi Fukuyama et al.)

The paper builds a parameter-extraction strategy for doubly charged scalars (SU(2) singlet k⁺⁺ coupling to l_R, or triplet Δ⁺⁺ coupling to ℓ_L) at the √s = 2 TeV μ⁺μ⁺ option. It assumes 12 fb⁻¹/yr, beam polarization P = 1/2 (surface μ⁺ halved by muonium formation), and a 10° nozzle acceptance, with 5° also shown. Z₂- or Z₃-symmetric coupling textures kill μ→eγ and μ→3e while leaving ΔL_μ = −ΔL_e = 2 (Mu→M̄u, μ⁺μ⁺→e⁺e⁺) and LNV channels.

The core idea is to combine a future Mu→M̄u measurement, which fixes |G_i| = |y_ee y_μμ|/(4√2 m_S²), with three collider observables:
- **μ⁺μ⁺→e⁺e⁺:** the ratio σ_Δ/σ_k is (1+P)²/(1−P)² = 9.
- **μ⁺μ⁺→e⁺e⁺γ:** for m_S < √s, the photon-energy peak sits at E_γ = E − m_S²/4E. This gives the mass, and the peak shape gives Γ_S independent of decay modes. The rate is θ_cut-sensitive through I_θ (2.9 vs 4.3).
- **μ⁺μ⁺→e⁺e⁺Z(→jj):** the Z coupling adds a factor (½−s_W²)²/s_W⁴ ≈ 1.35, so the singlet/triplet ratio is ≈ 12.2. O(100) events give >3σ representation discrimination.

Two side results:
- **LEP reinterpretation:** using the destructive-interference Bhabha bound gives |G₂|/G_F < 3.05×10⁻³·|g_μμ/g_ee|, comparable to the existing Mu→M̄u limit of 3.0×10⁻³. The authors flag that earlier work used the constructive case.
- **Background:** the e⁺e⁺4ν background is 1.9 fb, dropping to the zeptobarn level with a missing-energy veto.

Caveats:
- The provided text truncates in Sec. 5.3. The LNV tb̄tb̄ and μ⁺e⁻tb̄tb̄ analyses (Secs. 6–7) are not available here.
- Everything is generator-level with no detector simulation.
- The numerics assume S⁺⁺ decays only to ee/μμ with y_ee = y_μμ.
- Representation discrimination vanishes for an unpolarized beam, and the true P is uncertain.

## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2610.31316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31316) — Charmed baryon-to-meson ratios inside jets as a discriminator of hadronization mechanisms (Zi-Xuan Xu et al.)

This paper is about hadronization rather than substructure proper. Its relevance to this topic comes through in-jet z_∥ distributions and jet-axis radial profiles of Λc⁺/D⁰ in anti-kT R = 0.4 charged jets.

The setup is p+p in PYTHIA8 with CR-BLC Mode 2, and Pb+Pb in SHELL (Langevin plus higher-twist energy loss, CLVisc hydro, coalescence + Peterson fragmentation).

The headline result is a sign flip in E = R_jet/R_incl:
- **p+p:** E > 1 at low-to-intermediate p_T, growing with the jet-p_T cut. This is opposite to Λ/K⁰_S, and the fragmentation baseline gives E ≈ 1.
- **0–10% Pb+Pb:** 10–20% suppression for 4 < p_T < 8 GeV with p_T^jet > 10 GeV. The reading is that coalescence with uncorrelated thermal partons is filtered out by the jet requirement.

Both mechanisms soften Λc relative to D⁰ in z_∥, so a falling ratio with z_∥ signals recombination-type physics generically. The in-jet coalescence fraction falls with z_∥, from 0.97 to 0.82 for Λc and from 0.72 to 0.35 for D⁰. The PbPb/pp low-z_∥ enhancement reaches about 3 for Λc and about 2 for D⁰. With coalescence off, the z_∥ shape is insensitive to energy loss.

In both systems the radial profile rises with r. The bin displaced farthest outward is the lowest-p_T bin for color reconnection and the intermediate-p_T bin for coalescence.

Caveats:
- The rising CR-BLC radial profile is opposite to ALICE's indication that Λc sits closer to the jet axis than D⁰. The paper says these cannot yet be compared directly because kinematics and jet definitions differ.
- Profiles stop at r = 0.2 for statistical reasons.
- No Pb+Pb background subtraction is applied.
- The PbPb/pp ratio depends on the chosen p+p reference; the authors call it a decomposition tool, not R_AA.
- Only one color-reconnection tune is used.
- z_∥ shapes are compared to 13 TeV data only.
