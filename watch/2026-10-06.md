## Jets and jet substructure

### [arXiv:2610.05104](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05104) — Boosted top tagging via lepton-in-jet topology for vector-like quark searches in FCC-hh (Sanchari Bhattacharyya et al.)

This paper builds a lepton-seeded tagger for boosted t→bℓν at √s=100 TeV. Everything is defined in a ΔR<0.4 cone around a non-isolated lepton with p_T>100 GeV. The study uses PYTHIA plus Delphes with the FCC-hh card. The tagger is a four-class classifier (t_lep, t_had, b, c) trained separately in seven lepton-p_T bins from 0.1 TeV to above 3 TeV.

The two taggers take different inputs:
- **DNN:** engineered observables, including displaced-track ΔR and multiplicity, Q_κ jet charge, tower isolation, jet mass, and τ_N ratios.
- **ParT:** nine features per constituent (tracks, towers and the lepton) and pairwise angular attention biases.

**Main results**
- **Leptonic-top AUC:** ParT gives 0.994–0.997, flat in p_T. The DNN rises from 0.981 to 0.996.
- **Efficiency at score >0.4:** ParT reaches 94% to 97%, the DNN 89% to 96%.
- **High-p_T crossover:** above ~2 TeV the DNN has lower mistag rates for all three background classes. The authors read this as constituent information helping only while the decay products stay angularly resolved.
- **Track-only inputs (1.0–1.5 TeV bin):** ParT 0.981, GNN 0.964, DNN 0.942. The DNN drops from 0.992 with full inputs, so the high-level approach relies heavily on calorimeter information, which is the part most exposed to pile-up.
- **Cone size:** shrinking the cone to 0.2 leaves performance essentially unchanged.
- **Event level:** feeding the tagger into a B′→tW search gives 5σ discovery reach of ~7 TeV for Γ/m<25%, or ~10 TeV for Γ/m≲50%, at 30 ab⁻¹.

**Caveats**
- There is no pile-up overlay. The only proxy is raising the track threshold from 5 to 20 GeV, which leaves AUCs unchanged.
- Light-flavour jets are excluded from tagger training.
- Diboson events with boosted Z or W→cs produce a peak near tagger score 1.
- The event classifier is trained only at 5 TeV.
- The ParT+DeepSVDD anomaly result (AUC 0.894) treats the leptonic top itself as the nominal class, so its relevance to anomaly searches is limited.
