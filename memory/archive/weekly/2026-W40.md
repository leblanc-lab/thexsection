# The Cross Section: rolling synthesis, week ending 2026-09-29

This week the LZ 248 keV recoil stayed the field's most persistent topic. It changed its explanatory frame almost daily and ended the week on astrophysical inputs (halo velocities, excited-state fractions) rather than particle content. The most consequential movement came in flavor, where careful bookkeeping recalibrated two CKM tensions (inclusive $|V_{cb}|$, the angle $\alpha$). A new thread links supercooled phase transitions to baryogenesis timing through BBN deuterium and gravitational waves. LHCb's exotic beauty-strange state was the week's discovery but drew no follow-up. The digests covered 09-23, 09-24, 09-25, 09-28 and 09-29.

## Running themes

- **LZ recoil (steady, register shifting).** The frame moved through four stages:
  - *Elastic composite form factors (09-23):* Jung et al. [arXiv:2609.25723](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25723) and Khan et al. [arXiv:2609.25114](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25114).
  - *Engineering around solar capture (09-24):* Girmohanta et al. [arXiv:2609.28129](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28129) and Tang et al. [arXiv:2609.28415](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28415).
  - *Escaping capture by mass scale (09-25):* Murayama et al. push the inert doublet to 11–20 TeV [arXiv:2609.28819](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28819).
  - *Halo and excited-state inputs (09-29):* Oliveira [arXiv:2609.35650](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35650) and Das et al. [arXiv:2609.35737](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35737).

  One zero-paper day (09-28) was followed by seven papers. Model-building is increasingly paired with named tests:
  - a 271–800 keV LZ sideband search [arXiv:2609.26698](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26698);
  - a sub-MeV line at $E_\gamma\simeq\delta$ within COSI's reach [arXiv:2609.33952](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33952);
  - FCC-hh or a muon collider for thermal multiplets [arXiv:2609.26870](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26870).

- **CKM precision (heating).**
  - Fael et al. give an inclusive $|V_{cb}|=(42.32\pm0.30)\times10^{-3}$ [arXiv:2609.28752](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28752).
  - Žlebčík's convention fix lowers the $\alpha$ tension [arXiv:2609.31828](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31828).
  - The $V_{ud}$ thread continued with Gialidi et al. [arXiv:2609.31834](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31834) and Hayen [arXiv:2609.32873](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32873).
  - Feldmann et al.'s soft-collinear ChPT conflicts with Hard-Pion ChPT [arXiv:2609.34696](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34696).

- **Phase transitions, baryogenesis and GWs (new, heating).**
  - BBN deuterium disfavors NANOGrav-compatible transitions if the baryon asymmetry predates them [arXiv:2609.28348](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28348). Girmohanta et al. reach the same conclusion from darkogenesis [arXiv:2609.27978](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27978).
  - Theory infrastructure followed: Hybrid Partial Dressing [arXiv:2609.30366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30366) and $\mathcal O(g^6)$ thermal masses [arXiv:2609.33448](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33448).
  - On the detector side, MAGGIE proposes a kHz–MHz search [arXiv:2609.30318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30318).

- **ML and simulation as infrastructure (steady).**
  - Fast simulation: CaloClouds transfer [arXiv:2609.28661](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28661) and its review [arXiv:2609.30403](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30403). Parnassus went from ALEPH→SLD transfer [arXiv:2609.25061](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25061) to a public CLD surrogate [arXiv:2609.30775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30775).
  - Triggers: distilled trigger models [arXiv:2609.26899](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26899) and CMS anomaly triggers that selected more than four billion events in 2024 [arXiv:2609.31739](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31739).
  - Statistics tooling: SBI [arXiv:2609.30196](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30196) and optimal-transport negative-weight mitigation [arXiv:2609.30357](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30357), [arXiv:2609.30418](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30418).
  - Thaler's stocktake frames HEPML as infrastructure [arXiv:2609.32874](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32874).

- **Sterile neutrinos (steady).**
  - The short-baseline reanalysis is listed under Turning points.
  - The dark-matter side is contested: a bounded νMSM region [arXiv:2609.28457](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28457) versus a no-go for collisional freeze-in [arXiv:2609.29911](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29911) and saturation of resonant production [arXiv:2609.31340](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31340).

- **Collider QI (steady, turning practical).**
  - Nonlocal Advantage of Quantum Coherence (NAQC) in τ pairs, with a single witness [arXiv:2609.25214](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25214), set against the tomography positivity caveat [arXiv:2609.25217](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25217).
  - Spin correlation used as a DM-mediator search variable [arXiv:2609.31425](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31425).

- **Heavy-flavor spectroscopy (fading after 09-23).** No follow-up papers appeared after LHCb's discovery.

## Turning points

- **LHCb beauty-strange state** [arXiv:2609.25178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25178). Observed above 7σ, narrow and anomalously light, it is billed as the first nonconventional single-beauty hadron. BESIII added the η(2600) [arXiv:2609.25158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25158).
- **MiniBooNE+MicroBooNE joint 3+1 fit** [arXiv:2609.26965](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26965). The preference drops to 2.7σ, and the two experiments disagree at ≥2.5σ inside the model.
- **$\alpha$ tension recalibrated** [arXiv:2609.31828](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31828). A helicity-convention mismatch accounted for 0.8σ, taking the tension from 2.9σ to 2.1σ.
- **LZ intervals called incomplete** [arXiv:2609.35650](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35650). Published limits do not cover pseudo-Dirac halos with a surviving excited fraction.
- **Baryogenesis timing becomes a GW observable** [arXiv:2609.28348](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28348).
- **A new bound shape** [arXiv:2609.30335](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30335). Neutron-star arguments now exclude a finite *interval* of DM triggering rates.

## Open questions

- **$|V_{cb}|$ and $R(D^{(*)})$.** Fael et al. admit a poor fit and find predicted $B$ widths 1.1–1.2σ below measurement. They call for correlated moment measurements. The inclusive-vs-exclusive gap still bears on $R(D^{(*)})$ ([arXiv:2608.24332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24332)).
- **LZ: exothermic or endothermic?** Watch for:
  - the 271–800 keV sideband search;
  - a COSI line;
  - the halo tail (the LMC partly restores it);
  - Gemmell et al.'s link to the Galactic Center excess [arXiv:2609.26570](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26570), now shadowed by Abazajian et al.'s comment on the excess morphology [arXiv:2609.34155](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34155).
- **The LHCb state's mass deficit.** No interpretation has appeared yet.
- **MiniBooNE vs MicroBooNE.** Their disagreement inside 3+1 is unresolved.
- **Sterile-neutrino DM production.** It is unclear whether resonant or collisional production survives.
- **DIS entanglement-entropy picture.** Adelaja et al. raise a caution [arXiv:2609.31499](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31499).
- **Negative hadronic D-term.** Freese argues it now needs explaining [arXiv:2609.33151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33151).
- **Small systems.** IP-Glasma tuned to HERA underpredicts O+O and Pb+Pb suppression [arXiv:2609.29886](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29886), pointing to final-state energy loss.
