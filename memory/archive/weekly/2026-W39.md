# Rolling Synthesis — Week Ending 2026-09-23

The week belonged to the experiments, which spent it filling in — and then breaking out of — the hadron spectrum: BESIII's f0(980) triangulation early on, LHCb's doubly-charmed $\Omega_{cc}^+$ (Sunday) and a light, narrow beauty-strange state (Tuesday), plus BESIII's η(2600) and a sharper $\Omega_b^-$ lifetime. Beneath that spectroscopy surge, two long-running theory currents held steady — the multiloop precision-QCD/structure program and the shape-shifting LZ recoil interpretation saga — while a debunking of the 17 MeV boson and an eased R(D*) tension quietly cleared away two old storylines. Tooling matured from ML reconstruction into statistics and thermal-field-theory automation, and the collider quantum-information program reached its most ambitious claim yet.

## Running themes

- **Hadron spectroscopy (heating up, sharply).** The dominant thread by week's end. It began with three independent angles converging on f0(980) as scale-dependent $s\bar s$/molecular structure ([arXiv:2609.17974](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17974)), then turned experimental: $\Omega_{cc}^+$ at 8.7σ ([arXiv:2609.21921](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21921)), the >7σ beauty-strange state billed as the first nonconventional single-beauty hadron with a "mass deficit" ([arXiv:2609.25178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25178)), and BESIII's η(2600) ([arXiv:2609.25158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25158)). Molecular readings of exotics recur throughout.
- **Precision QCD / multiloop / structure (steady, the week's ballast).** A continuous center of gravity: b-jets at NNLO+PS ([arXiv:2609.17812](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17812)), NLO-EW on $gg\to\gamma\gamma$ ([arXiv:2609.18828](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18828)), the GPD review ([arXiv:2609.19544](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19544)), lattice GPDs into the $x\sim\pm\xi$ region ([arXiv:2609.20545](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20545)), and Blümlein et al.'s gluonic three-loop massive form factors ([arXiv:2609.22034](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22034), method paper [arXiv:2609.24279](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24279)), plus Kidonakis's four-loop massive cusp ([arXiv:2609.23247](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23247)).
- **The LZ 248 keV recoil (steady traffic, restless register).** The single most instructive trajectory — not the volume but the *framing*, which mutated daily: cosmological escape routes from IceCube/solar-capture ([arXiv:2609.18564](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18564)) → constraints and complementarity, ruling out weak-scale elastic operators ([arXiv:2609.21823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21823)) → confining/composite dark-QCD inelastic readings ([arXiv:2609.24988](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24988)) → elastic composite-form-factor readings ([arXiv:2609.25723](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25723), [arXiv:2609.25114](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25114)). Watch whether the register settles.
- **Tooling, from reconstruction to inference to automation (steady).** ML event-reconstruction (VyPER, PSICHE, self-supervised pretraining) gave way to the HEP statistics ecosystem migration — RooFit-AD/JAX/HS3 ([arXiv:2609.19992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19992)) and Dorigo's coverage-vs-ordering warning ([arXiv:2609.20007](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20007)) — and then to AutoTherm automating thermal production rates ([arXiv:2609.20936](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20936)).
- **Collider quantum information (heating up).** Reached its strongest claim: NAQC certifiable in τ pairs with a single witness ([arXiv:2609.25214](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25214)), tempered by the caution that H→4ℓ tomography can return non-PSD operators at NLO ([arXiv:2609.25217](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25217)).

## Turning points

- **Benmansour et al. ([arXiv:2609.18383](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18383))** — Geant4 cosmic-muon coincidences reproduce the Atomki opening-angle peaks at cosmic-to-signal ratio above unity, shifting the burden of the 17 MeV boson onto beam-off studies.
- **Belle II R(D*) ([arXiv:2609.21947](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21947))** — most precise hadronic-tag value, central value nudging the LFU tension *down*, not up; deflates a year-long "persistent anomaly" narrative.
- **ATLAS direct Higgs width ([arXiv:2609.19948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19948))** — coupling-independent $\Gamma_H<307$ MeV from $H\to\gamma\gamma$ interference, complementary to off-shell.
- **Lin et al. lattice axion-photon coupling ([arXiv:2609.22698](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22698))** — first-principles $\mathcal{C}_{\rm QCD}=1.965(35)$, resolving a standing ChPT discrepancy; a benchmark for every axion search.
- **ATLAS top-philic $Z'$ null ([arXiv:2609.24950](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24950))** — excludes $m_{Z'}<1150$ GeV, a direct constraint on the vector side of the toponium interpretation debate.

## Open questions

- **What is the LZ recoil?** No convergence — inelastic vs elastic, composite vs portal all live. Delepine et al. ([arXiv:2609.26698](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26698)) propose a decisive 271–800 keV sideband search testing inelastic $\delta\gtrsim330$ keV; the nuclear-structure form-factor caveat ([arXiv:2609.24764](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24764)) underlies all fits.
- **The exotic open-beauty sector.** LHCb's mass-deficit beauty-strange state ([arXiv:2609.25178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25178)) opens a charm-exotic counterpart that was long missing — expect theory rush.
- **Toponium vs scalar vs $Z'$.** The top-philic $Z'$ null narrows but doesn't close the interpretation question.
- **17 MeV boson** — now hinges on whether beam-off studies survive the cosmic-muon challenge.
- **$B\to K\pi\pi$ localized CP** — Guo et al. ([arXiv:2609.21464](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21464)) attribute the August flare to $\bar K_0^*(1430)$–$\rho(770)$ interference; watch for confirmation.
