The flavor front dominated the week, swinging from neutrino mixing numerology early on to a data-side eruption of $B$-physics anomalies and CP-violation discoveries by week's end. Two precision benchmarks anchored the collider side (ATLAS's full Run 2 Higgs combination, FCC-ee projections), while the collider-QI/tomography program matured from entanglement toward CP observables. Neutrino systematics visibly began tightening, and a provocative $4.8\sigma$ CMB-birefringence hint opened the week as the field's most argue-worthy unresolved number.

## Running themes

**Flavor and B anomalies — heating up sharply.** The week opened on neutrino/flavor model-building: Barger's $\pi/12$ model with a falsifiable $\delta=272^\circ$ ([arXiv:2608.08394](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08394)) and his broader commensurate quark-lepton mixing ([arXiv:2608.10312](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10312)), plus seesaw texture constructions. Then the weight swung decisively to *data*: LHCb's model-independent $C_9$ from transversity amplitudes at 4.3–4.8σ ([arXiv:2608.12215](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12215)) and Bouchard et al.'s $B\to PP$ fit demanding ~1000% SU(3)$_F$ breaking ([arXiv:2608.11298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11298)) — two independent anomalies in the same direction. This culminated 08-14 in LHCb's three-paper $B^+\to K^+\pi^+\pi^-$ harvest ([arXiv:2608.12612](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12612), [arXiv:2608.12613](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12613), [arXiv:2608.12614](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12614)), establishing six CP-violation phenomena at once, including first evidence in wave interference and a spin-3 resonance.

**Neutrino systematics and tooling — steady, consolidating.** The DUNE cross-section warning (Puhan et al., [arXiv:2608.08609](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08609): model choice shifts CP/octant sensitivity 25–30%) found a partial answer days later in NOvA's most-precise $\bar\nu_\mu$–H CCQE cross section ([arXiv:2608.12293](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12293)). Two differentiable oscillation engines appeared the same day — MANGO ([arXiv:2608.13429](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13429)) and Newtrinos.jl ([arXiv:2608.12940](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12940)) — betting on gradient-based inference as the new default.

**Collider-QI tomography — evolving from entanglement to CP.** The season-long quantum-information thread became a deployable toolkit: TauPolaris's normalizing-flow tau polarimetry with $4.3\sigma$ entanglement discrimination in $H\to\tau\tau$ ([arXiv:2608.10961](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10961)), then Perelstein et al. turning top-pair spin tomography into optimal CP-dipole observables reaching 40–200 TeV ([arXiv:2608.12463](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12463)).

**SMEFT/precision benchmarks — steady.** ATLAS's full Run 2 Higgs combination ([arXiv:2608.07332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07332), now signal-theory-limited) set a reference; Haisch et al.'s NLL SMEFT EWPO across all 210 coefficients ([arXiv:2608.10064](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10064)) and FCC-ee reaching 4 MeV Higgs-mass precision ([arXiv:2608.12071](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12071)) extended the thread toward future $e^+e^-$ global fits.

**Dark matter — model-building pile-up plus steady hardware.** Weak-scale DM revisited economically late in the week (singlet-doublet across all production mechanisms, [arXiv:2608.12480](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12480)); axion/haloscope hardware and detection-methods clusters ran throughout without a single decisive result.

## Turning points

- **ATLAS Higgs combination** ([arXiv:2608.07332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07332)) closed the incremental $HH$/$tH$ updates the memory tracked and shifted the leading uncertainty to theory.
- **The flavor axis flipped** from parametrization/numerology (Barger) to hard data-side tension (08-13 to 08-14). Nothing in recent memory anticipated an anomaly flare-up of this size.
- **NOvA's cross section** began pinning down the very inputs behind the DUNE systematics warning.
- **The quantum-GW no-go converged**: Miyauchi et al. ([arXiv:2608.08803](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08803)) extended Bao et al. ([arXiv:2607.27313](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27313)) from haloscopes to gravity — the second no-go in two weeks, closing on a wall from multiple directions.

## Open questions

- Will the $C_9$ ([arXiv:2608.12215](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12215)) and $B\to PP$ ([arXiv:2608.11298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11298)) anomalies survive scrutiny? Two independent sectors, same direction — the follow-up story to watch.
- **CMB birefringence** at $4.8\sigma$ (3.5σ post-dust, [arXiv:2608.06480](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06480)) — does it survive systematics? Points at an axion-like Chern-Simons coupling if real.
- Barger's falsifiable $\delta_{\rm CP}$ and $\varepsilon_2$ predictions await Hyper-K/DUNE resolution.
- Nowak et al.'s predicted narrow $1^+$ beauty-strange state at $5747\pm2$ MeV ([arXiv:2608.07268](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07268)) — a sharp spectroscopy follow-up.
- ALICE's ultra-central $v_3/v_2$ puzzle ([arXiv:2608.11199](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11199)) remains unresolved in the small-system/transport program.
- Muon EDM limit ($|d_\mu|<1.1\times10^{-19}\,e\,$cm, [arXiv:2608.11124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11124)) — a clean null, benchmark for future improvement.
