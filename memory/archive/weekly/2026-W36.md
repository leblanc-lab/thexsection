# The Cross Section — Rolling Synthesis, week ending 2026-09-04

One event dominated the week: LZ's single 248 keV nuclear recoil ([arXiv:2609.02823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02823), 2.6σ), which drove a theory storm before the collaboration paper even landed and has since become a case study in how astrophysical bounds, not model-building, settle a signal. Around it the durable threads continued at steadier pace — strong-CP/axions climbing the lattice ladder, quantum information at colliders maturing from tomography into a symmetry diagnostic, agentic-tooling coalescing into standards, and a run of clean experimental firsts (CMS LQ via muon–quark scattering, XENONnT's keV solar floor, the $b$-jet dead cone, a first Higgs-width bound). The week's mood: fewer anomalies, more consolidation.

## Running themes

**The LZ inelastic-DM event (heating, now inverting).** From Sept 2 onward a dozen groups converged on a ~1.1 TeV endothermic higgsino with ~300–370 keV splitting (Su [arXiv:2609.01475](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01475), Fan [arXiv:2609.01504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01504), Freese [arXiv:2609.01583](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01583), Wu [arXiv:2609.01590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01590), plus Yin/Nomura/Di Mauro/Visinelli/Yamashita). The story has since flipped to model-killing: Pospelov's solar-capture + IceCube bound demanding δ>566 keV ([arXiv:2609.02775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02775)), Rodd's empty high-energy sideband ([arXiv:2609.04175](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04175)), and McCabe's >50% annual-modulation prediction ([arXiv:2609.04181](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04181)). Escape routes now proliferate (Smirnov universal EW-multiplet [arXiv:2609.04144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04144); Jeesun [arXiv:2609.04185](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04185), Unwin [arXiv:2609.04186](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04186)). Recurring lesson: the kinematics fix mass/splitting so tightly that models barely constrain the physics.

**Strong CP & axions (steady-to-heating).** A thread carried since last week's lattice pair. Detection/cosmology angles early (fiber haloscopes [arXiv:2608.27551](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27551); domain-wall evasion + nEDM prediction, Gorghetto [arXiv:2608.27553](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27553)); reviews midweek (Ai [arXiv:2609.00620](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00620)); a second direct lattice determination of the θ-induced π-N coupling $\bar g_0$ (He [arXiv:2609.04129](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04129)), sibling to late-August's Li result. Also PQ-quality-from-proton-stability (Davighi [arXiv:2609.01712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01712)) and a multi-tesla REBCO haloscope cavity ([arXiv:2609.03879](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03879)).

**Quantum information at colliders (steady, maturing).** From NLO EW corrections to entanglement/magic (Chang [arXiv:2608.27559](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27559)) and top-spin steering ellipsoids ([arXiv:2608.30037](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30037)) toward entanglement *as a BSM/symmetry probe*: Kanemura's entropy-minimization conjecture [arXiv:2609.02691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02691), dark-monopole scattering [arXiv:2609.02467](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02467), tempered by Haddadi's skeptic comment [arXiv:2609.01686](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01686). MadSpin2 ([arXiv:2609.02794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02794)) is the entanglement-aware generator successor.

**Agentic/ML tooling (peaked, now consolidating).** HEPToolBench ([arXiv:2608.28232](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28232)) grew into a standards push: Menzo's versioned "scientific operations" [arXiv:2609.00107](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00107), Sun's knowledge-graph grounding lifting execution 58.5→76.0% [arXiv:2609.01095](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01095). Recurring finding: deterministic schemas beat native-syntax generation.

**Precision flavor & lattice/QCD firsts (steady).** The Misiak-group B→Xsγ upgrade — exact-$m_c$ NNLO ([arXiv:2608.29864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29864)) folded into $\mathcal{B}=(3.54\pm0.14)\times10^{-4}$ ([arXiv:2608.29863](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29863)), pushing $M_{H^\pm}>670$ GeV — retired a long-standing interpolation uncertainty. LHCb's most precise $B_s^0$ CPT limits [arXiv:2609.02475](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02475). Ortega-Gama's first two-hadron form factor from QCD [arXiv:2609.04142](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04142), plus real-time gauge-theory tensor-network work ([arXiv:2609.04114](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04114), [arXiv:2609.04117](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04117)).

## Turning points

- **LZ collaboration paper** ([arXiv:2609.02823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02823)) turned an interpretation scramble into a bounded problem; Pospelov ([arXiv:2609.02775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02775)) then excluded the consensus endothermic-higgsino reading outright.
- **B→Xsγ exact-$m_c$ NNLO** ([arXiv:2608.29863](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29863)) closed the interpolation gap; the inclusive-radiative sector is now fully SM-consistent.
- **Fiducial recoil** as a TMD/saturation projector (Lin [arXiv:2609.02280](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02280), Li [arXiv:2609.02356](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02356)) — a transferable method adopted across two observables.
- **XENONnT keV solar floor** ([arXiv:2608.29450](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29450)) — lowest neutrino threshold on record; already drew a critique ([arXiv:2609.02950](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02950)).

## Open questions

- **Is any LZ interpretation survivable?** Watch for the collaboration's sideband and annual-modulation checks (Rodd, McCabe); XLZD/PandaX could settle modulation. Axion-portal and atmospheric-ν escape routes remain untested.
- **R(D\*) tension** persists against the now-clean B→Xsγ / N³LO $|V_{ub}|$ picture — the radiative side agrees, the semileptonic doesn't.
- **Event-shape $\alpha_s$ spread** — new task force convened ([arXiv:2609.03002](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03002)) ahead of FCC.
- **Thin anomalies to revisit:** HAWC Earth-skimming ν candidates (2.1–2.5σ, [arXiv:2608.28951](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28951)); triangle-singularity claim in ψ(2S)→p p̄ η (3.8σ, [arXiv:2609.01006](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01006)); NA61 strangeness double-ratio step (3.7σ, [arXiv:2608.28189](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28189)).
