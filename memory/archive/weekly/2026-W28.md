This week ending July 9, 2026, was defined by a transition from speculative modeling toward foundational infrastructure and joint, high-precision frameworks. Prompted by sobering reminders of the ultra-long timelines of next-generation facilities ([arXiv:2607.08131](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08131)), the community focused on rebuilding its primary tools from first principles. This shift was evident across all scales: from raw calorimeter cell-level machine learning and joint PDF+SMEFT fits at the High-Luminosity LHC, to the rigorous dissection of degeneracies in the PMNS neutrino mixing matrix and the deployment of new three-dimensional cosmic backgrounds.

## Running themes

*   **Machine Learning Transitions from Calibration to Raw Reconstruction:** A major paradigm shift is underway. Within days of ATLAS presenting the performance of its transformer-based GN2 flavor-tagger ([arXiv:2607.05322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05322)), they deployed and calibrated it on Run 3 data ([arXiv:2607.06199](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06199)). This transformer wave immediately spread to electromagnetic calorimetry, where researchers bypassed high-level shower shapes to process raw cell-level signals ([arXiv:2607.08175](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08175)), achieving direct invariant mass regression for collimated diphotons. 
*   **The Breakdown of Isolated SMEFT and QCD Factorization:** It is no longer viable to treat standard inputs and new physics independently. The field is realizing that PDF profiling can accidentally "absorb" high-mass SMEFT signals, highlighting the urgent need for joint fits ([arXiv:2607.07791](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07791)). Similarly, sub-percent precision demands that standard QCD factorization be replaced by joint QCD+QED frameworks utilizing Lepton Distribution Functions ([arXiv:2607.07811](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07811)).
*   **Neutrino Degeneracies and CP Mimics:** As JUNO, DUNE, and Hyper-Kamiokande approach operation, the community is deeply concerned with "fake" signals. Non-unitary PMNS mixing ([arXiv:2607.06108](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06108)) or Lorentz-invariance violation ([arXiv:2607.06513](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06513)) can mimic or degrade CP asymmetries. Breaking these degeneracies requires exact analytical resummation schemes ([arXiv:2607.07213](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07213)) and combined multi-detector fits.
*   **Evasion Strategies for Cornered WIMPs and Axions:** Under pressure from LZ direct-detection limits, which have severely cornered traditional natural higgsino dark matter ([arXiv:2607.05648](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05648)), theorists are pivoting to unified discrete symmetries to naturally solve the MSSM $\mu$ problem while yielding viable DFSZ axions ([arXiv:2607.07921](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07921)).

## Turning points

*   **First Direct Bounds on Triple Higgs Coupling Modifier Space:** The CMS Collaboration's search for nonresonant $HHH \to 6b$ ([arXiv:2607.05145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05145)) directly excluded regions of the $(\kappa_3, \kappa_4)$ coupling space for the first time, replacing theoretical perturbative unitarity bounds with experimental data.
*   **Resolution of the $b$-Baryon Lifetime Tension:** Calculations of the NNLO QCD corrections to $b$-quark decay within the Heavy Quark Expansion ([arXiv:2607.06538](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06538)) dramatically reduced theoretical uncertainties, resolving a decades-long tension between predicted and measured $b$-baryon lifetime ratios.
*   **Neutron-Capture Calorimetry:** The demonstration that gadolinium-loaded liquid scintillators can capture delayed neutrons from hadronic showers ([arXiv:2607.08587](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08587)) cut energy resolution from 21.8% to 13.3%, offering a hardware solution to the problem of invisible hadronic energy fluctuations.

## Open questions

*   **The Fermi-LAT 20 GeV Galactic Halo Excess vs. Dwarfs:** A rigorous pixel-level likelihood analysis with full PSF forward folding ([arXiv:2607.08552](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08552)) has confirmed the 20 GeV excess in 15 years of Fermi-LAT data. However, its WIMP interpretation remains in $4\text{--}5\times$ tension with dwarf spheroidal limits. Will low-velocity-enhanced (Sommerfeld or Breit-Wigner) annihilation models be sufficient to reconcile this gap?
*   **The Relic Neutrino Wind Exposure Penalty:** Theoretical calculations have shown that observing the dipolar anisotropy of the Cosmic Neutrino Background via tritium $\beta$-decay requires a massive $10^5$ exposure penalty relative to observing the isotropic flux, with Majorana signals highly suppressed ([arXiv:2607.05221](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05221)). Can any planned tritium experiment scale to meet this threshold?
