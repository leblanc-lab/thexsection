# The Cross Section — rolling synthesis, week ending 2026-10-06

The week had two firm experimental anchors. JUNO's first indication of normal ordering was followed five days later by Super-K's first reactor-antineutrino observation, which already sits in mild tension with it on $\Delta m^2_{21}$. Interpretation of the LZ recoil narrowed: about five papers a day early in the week fell to a trickle, and the readings converged on heavy Higgsinos plus a Large Magellanic Cloud (LMC) halo tail as the main survivors of the IceCube solar-capture bound. Precision QCD had its strongest week, completing four-loop $P_{qq}$ and taking jet-vetoed Higgs to third logarithmic order. Two quieter threads kept growing: "corrections the size of errors" and LLM agents operating inside calculations.

## Running themes

- **LZ recoil: consolidating, volume fading.** The halo turn came first: Bozorgnia et al. showed the LMC tail pushes endothermic fits to larger splittings. Luengas et al. then generalized the solar-capture bound, requiring $\delta\gtrsim350$ keV up to 100 TeV, which rules out most thermal multiplets under the Standard Halo Model. After that, survivors went heavy:
  - Hor et al. embedded $\sim10^5$ GeV Higgsinos in SUSY SU(5), predicting $p\to K^+\bar\nu$ within Hyper-K/JUNO/DUNE reach.
  - Freese et al. found the thermal 1.1 TeV Higgsino only marginal, but 2 TeV to hundreds of TeV comfortable at δ≈350–525 keV.
  
  Elastic readings are now squeezed from the collider side by Anwar et al.'s ATLAS monojet recast. The non-Higgsino alternatives make distinct predictions: atomic-DM hyperfine (no Ar/Ge signal), a decaying parent (700–810 keV argon line), and charged gravitinos (microsecond-spaced multiple recoils).
- **Precision QCD: heating.** Gehrmann et al. completed four-loop $P_{qq}$. Jet-vetoed Higgs moved on two fronts: the first NLL non-global resummation (Becher et al.), then N³LO+N³LL at ~3% (Abreu et al.). Assi et al. delivered N³LO+approx. N⁴LL′ Drell-Yan with a lattice Collins-Soper kernel and positive weights.
- **Corrections the size of errors: steady, recurring.** Examples this week:
  - a −216 MeV mixed QCD-EW shift in $m_t$;
  - an 11–13% shift in gluon quasi-PDF moments from a $d-2$ contraction error;
  - Neyman-χ² biases that do not shrink with finer binning;
  - a >30% model dependence in NA62 dark-photon acceptance;
  - "keep the whole square" for exclusive QCD rates (Gong et al., Zhan et al.).
- **LLMs inside the calculation: heating.** The cluster began with agent-built $V$+jet helicity cross sections and LLM-designed IR subtraction. It then widened to model space (hAIthem), a retrieval pipeline over ~230K papers, and Schwartz's BootLoops, an LLM-operable multi-loop toolkit.
- **$b\to s\nu\bar\nu$ / Belle II enhancement: steady.** The thread ran from a WET reinterpretation preferring a vector enhancement, to SMEFT links with $R(D^{(*)})$ and $b\to s\tau\tau$, to the standard-cosmology ALP reading becoming marginal. Most recently, polarized $\Lambda_b\to\Lambda\nu\bar\nu$ at the FCC-ee Z-pole would probe chirality combinations the mesonic modes cannot.

## Turning points

- **JUNO** favors normal ordering at ≥2.1σ for any CP phase (Bayes factor 10.2) and measures $\Delta m^2_{31}$ to ~1%. It is an indication, not a determination.
- **LHCb beauty-strange state:** a lattice-fixed coupled-channel prediction made without LHCb input lands within 1.1–1.7σ, reading the state as a $\bar BK$ bound state. It also predicts the mass of a $1^+$ partner.
- **Solar capture became the reference test** for any heavy-multiplet LZ fit.
- **Dark Dimension graviton phenomenology took two independent hits:** $(q/m_n)^2$ suppression (Lee et al.) and $d$-wave near-threshold decays (Langhoff). Vafa's review still lists KK-graviton dark matter.
- **Red dwarfs exclude abrupt-$G$-step Hubble fixes** at 3.1–4.0σ.

## Open questions

- **Solar vs reactor $\Delta m^2_{21}$.** Super-K's reactor-only value is ~3σ above JUNO's, but $\sin^2\theta_{12}$ is barely constrained. Watch for SK-Gd updates and tests of Alikhanov's √2 relation.
- **The LZ solar bound's own systematics.** It depends on poorly known elastic Higgsino–nucleon scattering (Freese et al.), and the annihilation side can be evaded at the cost of a few-percent Higgsino fraction (Jeong et al.). Halo marginalization via neural ratio estimation may reframe the LMC argument.
- **$X(2370)$: glueball or molecule?** Giacosa et al.'s fractional-determinant glueball competes with Khemchandani et al.'s $\phi h_1(1380)$ reading; $\eta\eta\eta$ and $\eta\eta\eta'$ rates would discriminate.
- **Pisarski's fractional topological charge** predicts a generically second-order chiral transition, testable on 2+1 lattice data. It is expected to be contested.
- **Full-square exclusive rates** are a principled fix for negative NLO/NNLO rates, but expect pushback.
- **Smaller flags:**
  - MicroBooNE proton multiplicities match no generator;
  - COMET muon yields run ~30% above Geant4;
  - the $\Xi_c\to\Xi$ semileptonic lattice-versus-experiment puzzle is emerging.
