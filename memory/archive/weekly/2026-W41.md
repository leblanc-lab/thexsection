# The Cross Section: rolling synthesis, week ending 2026-10-07

The week's most persistent thread was the LZ high-energy recoil. The paper count kept falling, but the surviving explanations converged on heavy Higgsinos while the solar-capture bound was pressed from several sides. Alongside it, a "corrections the size of errors" motif kept recurring across lattice, statistics and exclusive QCD. Precision perturbative inputs advanced steadily, notably the third-order jet-vetoed Higgs prediction and $m_W$-relevant corrections. Hadron spectroscopy surged late in the week around the $B_{s0}^*(5700)$ and glueball radii. LLM-in-the-loop and ML tooling stayed a steady background, with the first explicit pushback against lossy generative surrogates. Several targeted results closed doors, most cleanly a stellar test against G-step Hubble fixes and two strikes against Dark Dimension graviton dark matter.

## Running themes

- **LZ recoil versus the Sun (volume fading, argument consolidating).**
  - [arXiv:2609.38316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38316) made the general-representation solar-capture bound ($\delta\gtrsim350$ keV) the reference test, excluding most thermal multiplets under the Standard Halo Model. An LMC-like halo tail is the escape route.
  - [arXiv:2610.01613](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01613) turned ~$10^5$ GeV Higgsinos into a SUSY SU(5) proton-decay prediction ($p\to K^+\bar\nu$).
  - [arXiv:2610.00226](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00226) conceded that its LZ-matching branch is disfavored.
  - [arXiv:2610.03692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03692) found the thermal 1.1 TeV Higgsino only marginal, with 2 TeV to hundreds of TeV comfortable. It also noted the solar bound depends on poorly known elastic scattering.
  - [arXiv:2610.05893](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05893) instead suppresses the solar signal on the annihilation side, at the cost of a sub-dominant Higgsino fraction.
  - [arXiv:2610.06756](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06756) used a monojet recast to exclude most elastic EFT readings below ~1 TeV.
  - Papers that skip the Sun, such as [arXiv:2610.00645](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00645) and [arXiv:2610.08648](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08648), are increasingly the outliers.
- **Corrections the size of errors (steady, now an explicit editorial file).**
  - [arXiv:2609.40229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40229) shifts the top mass relation by −216 MeV.
  - [arXiv:2609.40205](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40205) fixes the gluon quasi-PDF matching kernel, moving gluon moments by 11–13%.
  - [arXiv:2609.40019](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40019) shows Neyman-χ² biases in ratio fits that do not shrink with finer binning.
  - [arXiv:2609.40052](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40052) finds production-model dependence of NA62 dark-photon acceptance above 30%.
  - [arXiv:2610.04557](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04557) and [arXiv:2610.04293](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04293) square the full amplitude in exclusive QCD, keeping rates positive and raising them 11.6–18.4%.
  - [arXiv:2610.08606](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08606) weakens the $D^+$ bound by modelling the missing-mass distribution.
- **Precision inputs for $m_W$ and the Higgs (steady to heating).**
  - [arXiv:2610.02169](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02169) delivers N³LO+aN⁴LL′ Drell-Yan with a lattice Collins-Soper kernel and positive weights.
  - [arXiv:2610.03472](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03472) and [arXiv:2610.03687](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03687) take the jet veto to third logarithmic order.
  - [arXiv:2610.08282](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08282) gives a four-loop $\Delta r$ correction of a few hundred keV in $m_W$, relevant at FCC-ee.
  - [arXiv:2610.07233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07233) sets out CT's partial-N³LO fits, still using approximate four-loop splitting functions.
  - [arXiv:2609.39582](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39582) is a clean null: single-diffractive W/Z production biases $m_W$ by at most 1.58 MeV.
- **Hadron spectroscopy (heating at week's end).**
  - Three groups (Albaladejo et al. last week, [arXiv:2610.07106](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07106), [arXiv:2610.07171](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07171)) place the $B_{s1}$ partner at 5740–5755 MeV. Heavy-quark spin symmetry nearly forces that mass, so the agreement is not a strong test.
  - Glueballs recur. [arXiv:2610.00484](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00484) reads $X(2370)$ as a pseudoscalar glueball coupled to fractional determinants, contrasting with a molecular reading earlier in the week. Two scalar-glueball radius predictions agree closely: 0.28 fm from [arXiv:2610.01988](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01988) and 0.276 fm from [arXiv:2610.08545](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08545).
  - Model-dependent multiquark claims continue: [arXiv:2610.07818](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07818) and [arXiv:2610.06009](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06009).
- **LLMs and ML inside physics workflows (steady).**
  - LLM and agent tools: [arXiv:2609.38309](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38309) (hAIthem, model space), [arXiv:2610.00891](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00891) (open RAG over ~230K papers), [arXiv:2610.02286](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02286) (BootLoops, multi-loop machinery any agentic LLM can operate) and [arXiv:2610.07145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07145) (a human-in-the-loop agentic calculation).
  - ML scaling and fidelity: [arXiv:2610.06784](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06784) gives scaling laws on ~11 billion ATLAS jets, and [arXiv:2610.00569](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00569) finds token loss predicts physical fidelity.
  - Counterpoint: [arXiv:2610.08054](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08054) (HybridMC) argues generative flash simulations cannot reproduce the photon timing that tens-of-ps detectors need.

## Turning points

- **Solar capture as gatekeeper.** [arXiv:2609.38316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38316) became the standard test for heavy-multiplet LZ fits, and [arXiv:2610.03692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03692) consolidated the surviving parameter space.
- **G-step Hubble fixes excluded.** [arXiv:2610.04048](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04048) uses red dwarfs in eclipsing binaries to exclude a 4% step at 73 Myr at 3.1σ and an 11% step at 130 Myr at 4.0σ.
- **Dark Dimension graviton DM hit twice.** [arXiv:2610.01825](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01825) shows near-threshold KK decays are $d$-wave, following Lee et al. earlier in the week. Vafa's review [arXiv:2610.04909](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04909) still lists KK-graviton DM.
- **Jet-vetoed Higgs at ~3%.** [arXiv:2610.03687](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03687) is the likely new HL-LHC reference.
- **Super-K first reactor observation.** [arXiv:2610.03143](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03143) sees reactor $\bar\nu_e$ at ~6σ in SK-Gd.

## Open questions

- **Solar–reactor $\Delta m^2_{21}$.** Super-K's reactor-only value sits ~3σ above JUNO's, with weak $\theta_{12}$ constraint. [arXiv:2610.06738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06738)'s √2 relation fits JUNO and would be sharpened by Super-K's value.
- **Solar bound robustness.** Watch for work on the elastic Higgsino–nucleon input and on annihilation-side evasions.
- **Belle II $K\nu\bar\nu$ enhancement.** Several routes now probe it:
  - [arXiv:2609.40072](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40072): $b\to s\tau\tau$ breaks degeneracies $K\nu\bar\nu$ cannot.
  - [arXiv:2610.00460](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00460): the standard-cosmology ALP reading is now marginal.
  - [arXiv:2610.04309](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04309): polarized $\Lambda_b$ at the Z pole accesses chirality and CP combinations the mesonic modes miss.
- **$B_s^0\pi^0$ width of the $B_{s0}^*$.** This is the discriminating number: 10–20 keV with a bare core ([arXiv:2610.07171](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07171)) versus 34–82.5 keV (Albaladejo). The $B_{s1}$ radiative modes are flagged as a Run 3 test.
- **Chiral transition order.** [arXiv:2610.00472](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00472) proposes fractional topological charge below $T_\chi$, implying a generically second-order transition, with a check on 2+1 lattice data proposed.
- **Smaller items to watch:**
  - MicroBooNE proton multiplicities that no generator describes ([arXiv:2609.40237](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40237)).
  - COMET muon yields running ~30% above Geant4 ([arXiv:2610.02855](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02855)).
  - The lattice-versus-experiment $\Xi_c\to\Xi$ puzzle flagged in [arXiv:2610.03603](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03603).
  - A ±5% floor on the light-by-light pole term ([arXiv:2610.07493](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07493)).
