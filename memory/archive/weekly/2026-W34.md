# The Cross Section — Rolling Synthesis, week ending 2026-08-21

**Overview.** The week opened on flavor and heavy ions and closed on a collider bang. Its defining event is CMS's 6.1σ confirmation of a $t\bar t$-threshold excess in the single-lepton channel ([arXiv:2608.19546](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19546)), giving the toponium/near-threshold story two independent channels. Underneath the day-to-day, three methodological currents kept resurfacing: EFT hardening into the default collider language, a new family of jet-free precision observables built from conserved currents, and the collider quantum-information program pushing past entanglement. Flavor spent the week consolidating rather than breaking, and a season-long ML-methodology reckoning kept accreting community entries.

## Running themes

**Toponium / $t\bar t$-threshold excess — heating, now the marquee thread.** CMS's single-lepton confirmation ([arXiv:2608.19546](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19546)), using relative velocity plus spin/parity angular observables, cross-checks the earlier dilepton excess as a color-singlet pseudoscalar. Interpretation is already contested: Matsuoka ([arXiv:2608.19651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19651)) reads it as a top-philic Type-III 2HDM CP-odd Higgs and finds no viable baryogenesis branch. Two readings, one bump.

**EFT as default language — steady, hardening.** The 08-20 CMS aTGC merged-jet bounds ([arXiv:2608.18245](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18245)) and the 08-21 first global top-FCNC fit over all 24 SMEFT operators ([arXiv:2608.19547](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19547)) mark diboson and top searches converging on EFT combinations. Tooling followed: basis-independent two-loop four-fermion running ([arXiv:2608.19316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19316)).

**Jet-free observables from conserved currents — emerging, worth naming.** Zhang et al.'s one-point charge correlator, a jet-free reformulation of the EW forward-backward asymmetry from DELPHI LEP-1 open data ([arXiv:2608.14779](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14779), [arXiv:2608.15357](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15357)), set the template; projected energy correlators against $Z/\gamma$ ([arXiv:2608.14793](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14793)) and Ke et al.'s Gell-Mann–Nishijima jet-flow relation ([arXiv:2608.19708](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19708)) share the logic — precision from currents, not jet algorithms. The archival-data route is itself part of the point.

**Collider quantum information — steady, moving beyond entanglement.** Entanglement entries (gluon-pair Bell states [arXiv:2608.17685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17685); Bethe–Heitler GHZ/W states [arXiv:2608.18030](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18030)) gave way to "quantum magic" in EW scattering, with the weak mixing angle again near a magic-minimizing value ([arXiv:2608.19095](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19095)), and tripartite entanglement selecting gauge/diffeo-invariant theories ([arXiv:2608.18732](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18732)).

**Flavor — consolidating, not tensioning.** After a mid-week $C_9$ flare, LHCb's SM-consistent $\phi_s$ ([arXiv:2608.14119](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14119)), the completing leading-power factorization piece for $b\to s\ell\ell$ ([arXiv:2608.14341](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14341)), and a kaon global fit ([arXiv:2608.14474](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14474)) all pointed toward control. Separately, flavor-mixing numerology (Koide from topological condensation [arXiv:2608.19277](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19277); TBM-to-Cabibbo deformation [arXiv:2608.19691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19691)) returned — parametrization staking out falsifiable numbers.

## Turning points

- **CMS single-lepton toponium confirmation** ([arXiv:2608.19546](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19546)) — moved the threshold excess from single-channel curiosity to two-channel signal.
- **CMS baryon-to-meson enhancement in O–O** ([arXiv:2608.18256](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18256)) — the season's small-system-flow program paid off with a deconfinement signature in a genuinely small system.
- **Quartic Higgs self-coupling enters phenomenology** — first HL-LHC four-Higgs (8b) study bounding $c_3,d_4$ ([arXiv:2608.16998](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16998)), territory single/double-Higgs rates can't reach.
- **Third graviton-quantumness no-go** ([arXiv:2608.17015](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17015), after Bao et al. and Miyauchi et al.) — the wall against detectable graviton squeezing keeps holding.

## Open questions

- **Toponium vs elementary scalar**: is the $t\bar t$-threshold enhancement NRQCD pseudoscalar bound state or a 2HDM CP-odd Higgs? Watch further channels.
- **Two unclosed LHC excesses**: the $3.5\sigma$ CMS 13.6-GeV diphoton (ALP fit, [arXiv:2608.18506](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18506)) and the 146-GeV $e\mu$ excess ([arXiv:2608.18738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18738)) — both have concrete follow-up strategies proposed.
- **Gravitational parity violation** claimed from HERA Compton-polarimeter spectra ([arXiv:2608.14703](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14703)) — extraordinary; awaiting scrutiny.
- **Hadronic-asymmetry tensions**: does the LEP one-point charge correlator ([arXiv:2608.15357](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15357)) offer a new handle?
- **Absolute neutrino mass in oscillations** ([arXiv:2608.13736](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13736)) — surprising NLO claim; JUNO sensitivity to a novel mass combination.
- **Trials-factor accounting** for BSM resonance searches ([arXiv:2608.19764](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19764)) — a caution flag over how excesses get counted.
