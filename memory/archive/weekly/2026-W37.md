# The Cross Section — Rolling Synthesis, week ending 2026-09-10

The week belonged, again, to a single xenon recoil (LZ230616, ~248 keV). What began late August as a ~1.1 TeV endothermic higgsino has, over four weeks, run a full arc: from a model-building glut, through a wave of bounds that killed the thermal reading, into an exothermic pivot, and finally toward falsifiability — collider signatures, argon target-scaling tests, directional handles — and outright exile to 10⁵–10⁶ GeV. Around this dominant thread, real physics accumulated in three steadier channels: a live experimental axion wing now attached to the long-running strong-CP lattice program, QCD structure results from charmonium photoproduction, and a spreading ML/autodiff/quantum tooling front.

## Running themes

- **The LZ event (dominant, now maturing).** Traffic shifted decisively from fits to tests and exclusions. The endothermic higgsino is excluded from every direction (solar capture, empty high-energy sideband, runaway modulation). The field escaped into exothermic scattering (halo-robust, ~250 keV peak) and boosted-DM readings, then toward concrete tests. The register is now falsifiability, not fitting — heating but self-straining.
- **Strong CP: lattice program + detection wing (steady, broadening).** The θ-induced hadronic-coupling calculations ([arXiv:2609.04129](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04129), second group/method) now sit alongside a live axion-experiment wing — real limits, not projections.
- **Axion instrumentation (heating).** Two real limits ([arXiv:2609.08144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08144), KSVZ-level at 15.6 μeV) plus a higher-order-mode haloscope beating the volume penalty at high mass ([arXiv:2609.09717](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09717)). Astrophysical bounds (RGB-star axion-electron) reinforced.
- **QCD structure via charmonium (steady).** Near-threshold J/ψ photoproduction matured from CLAS12's neutron measurement into a quotable nucleon strong-interaction radius, 0.94±0.09 fm ([arXiv:2609.09318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09318)).
- **ML/quantum/autodiff tooling (steady, spreading).** Self-supervised transformers for LHCb tagging ([arXiv:2609.09275](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09275)), RL over SMEFT operator space, quantum Fisher info for JUNO, differentiable photon transport, quantum GNN jet tagging.

Lower-intensity but recurring: lattice methodology firsts (two-hadron form factor [arXiv:2609.04142](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04142), WZW-term formalism); the Higgs self-coupling thread ([arXiv:2609.04868](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04868), muon-collider case [arXiv:2609.08392](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08392)); LLP-in-borrowed-detectors; neutrino ledger-clearing (NOvA sterile null).

## Turning points

- **Solar-capture + empty-sideband bounds killed the thermal higgsino** (Pospelov's δ>566 keV; Rodd [arXiv:2609.04175](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04175); McCabe's >50% modulation [arXiv:2609.04181](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04181); re-executed by Bose [arXiv:2609.07807](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07807), Di Mauro [arXiv:2609.06760](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06760)). This forced the whole interpretation stream to react.
- **The exothermic pivot** (Dent [arXiv:2609.04673](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04673), de Lima, Gu) recovered halo-robustness — the exact vulnerability the endothermic case lost — resetting the conversation's default.
- **LZ-to-collider bridging** (Cheung disappearing tracks [arXiv:2609.08712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08712); Kotlarski Z′ at FCC-hh) marked the shift from spectroscopy to prediction.
- **Langhoff [arXiv:2609.09385](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09385)** took the thermal reading to its terminus: survives only at 10⁵–10⁶ GeV via early matter domination, tying LZ to strong CP.
- **Chattaraj [arXiv:2609.10504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10504)** closed off the mundane neutrino-scattering explanation entirely — the key null narrowing what's left.

## Open questions

- **Which LZ reading, if any, survives?** Distinguishing tests are now on the table and should be watched for follow-up: McCabe's annual-modulation signature (XLZD/PandaX), argon/germanium target-scaling (de Lima, Baer [arXiv:2609.06153](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06153), Khan [arXiv:2609.09230](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09230)), Asadi's coincident-photon directional handle ([arXiv:2609.09107](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09107)). Does a resolved nuclear cancellation fake the feature (Khan)?
- **Is the higgsino reading now too baroque to sustain?** Langhoff's 10⁵ TeV + early-matter-domination construction is the last standard exit; watch whether the field abandons the higgsino label.
- **Ξc⁰→Ξ⁻e⁺ν discrepancy** ([arXiv:2609.09802](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09802)): theory/lattice ~4% vs experiment — persistent, unresolved.
- **Event-shape α_s spread**: task force convened ([arXiv:2609.03002](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03002)), reconciliation pending ahead of FCC.
- **H→Zγ** at 1.9σ ([arXiv:2609.04402](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04402)) — SM-consistent but statistics-limited; awaits more data.
