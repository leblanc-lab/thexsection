# Rolling Synthesis — Week Ending 2026-09-07

**Overview.** The week belonged almost entirely to a single LZ nuclear-recoil event, whose interpretation traced a complete arc — from an interpretation glut, to consensus on a ~1.1 TeV endothermic higgsino, to a coordinated demolition of that reading by astrophysical and self-consistency bounds, to a sign-flip toward *exothermic* dark matter by week's end. Around this gravity well, three quieter but genuine trajectories advanced: a wave of long-lived-particle proposals that repurpose existing detectors; the steady maturation of quantum-information and quantum-simulation methods for QCD; and a run of precision-flavor and lattice-methodology results (B→Xsγ, strong-CP couplings, first two-hadron form factors). Experimental firsts from XENONnT and CMS bracketed the week as clean, anomaly-free milestones.

## Running Themes

**The LZ event (dominant, evolving).** LZ's official paper ([arXiv:2609.02823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02823)) reported one recoil at 248±23 keV, 2.6σ global. A dozen-plus model papers first converged on endothermic inelastic dark matter (~1.1 TeV higgsino, ~350 keV splitting). That consensus was then squeezed from every side (see Turning Points). By 09-07 the field pivoted to exothermic scattering (Dent et al. [arXiv:2609.04673](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04673), de Lima [arXiv:2609.05204](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05204), Gu et al. [arXiv:2609.05291](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05291)), which reproduces the ~250 keV peak while being "largely insensitive to halo uncertainties" — buying back exactly the robustness the endothermic case lost. The running lesson, repeated all week: the event's kinematics fix mass and splitting so tightly that models barely constrain the physics, while astrophysics does the real cutting.

**Long-lived particles in borrowed detectors (heating up).** Starting from GRENDEL (drainage-gallery scintillator above CMS, [arXiv:2609.00152](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00152)), the week accumulated proposals repurposing existing apparatus: HNLs at Belle II [arXiv:2609.04332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04332), LHC-produced LLPs decaying in KM3NeT/ORCA [arXiv:2609.04386](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04386), displaced Majorons inside 0νββ detectors [arXiv:2609.05352](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05352). Sharpest: Wang [arXiv:2609.04761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04761), fixing neutrino mass ordering from displaced-lepton flavor ratios alone, free of δ_CP.

**Quantum information & simulation for QCD (steady, broadening).** The collider-tomography strand extended into using entanglement *itself* as a probe — Kanemura et al.'s entropy-minimization/symmetry conjecture [arXiv:2609.02691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02691) — with Haddadi [arXiv:2609.01686](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01686) as skeptic. Quantum-simulation-for-QCD reached 3+1D (real-time string breaking [arXiv:2609.04114](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04114)) and jet tagging ran on real QPUs [arXiv:2609.04367](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04367).

**Precision flavor & lattice methodology (steady).** The B→Xsγ update anchored the flavor side. Strong-CP hadronic couplings from the θ-term got a second lattice determination (He et al. [arXiv:2609.04129](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04129)). Ortega-Gama et al. delivered the first QCD two-hadron form factor [arXiv:2609.04142](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04142); Romero-López et al. targeted the WZW term via lattice [arXiv:2609.05006](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05006).

**Agentic/ML tooling (steady).** Last week's HEPToolBench matured into a standards program — versioned "scientific operations" [arXiv:2609.00107](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00107), ROOT knowledge-graph grounding lifting code execution 58.5%→76.0% [arXiv:2609.01095](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01095).

## Turning Points

- **Endothermic higgsino killed, three ways.** Pospelov et al. [arXiv:2609.02775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02775) (solar capture + IceCube: δ>566 keV), Rodd et al. [arXiv:2609.04175](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04175) (empty high-energy sideband), and McCabe [arXiv:2609.04181](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04181) (>50% annual modulation) collectively forced the exothermic pivot.
- **B→Xsγ uncertainty retired.** Czaja et al.'s exact-$m_c$ NNLO ([arXiv:2608.29864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29864)) fed Misiak et al.'s $\mathcal{B}=(3.54\pm0.14)\times10^{-4}$ ([arXiv:2608.29863](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29863)), pushing Type-II $M_{H^\pm}>670$ GeV — the inclusive-radiative side now fully SM-consistent.
- **Experimental firsts.** XENONnT keV-scale solar $pp$ ([arXiv:2608.29450](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29450)); CMS dead-cone in $b$-jets ([arXiv:2608.29412](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29412)); CMS >5σ azimuthal asymmetry in coherent J/ψ photoproduction ([arXiv:2609.04376](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04376)).

## Open Questions

- **Can LZ/XLZD/PandaX discriminate exothermic vs. escape-route readings?** Dent predicts ~4.5 events in LZ's 1000-day run; McCabe's modulation and de Lima's argon/germanium signals offer near-term tests.
- **R(D\*) tension** persists against the now-consistent inclusive-radiative sector — watch the semileptonic side.
- **α_s from event shapes** — the Badea et al. task force [arXiv:2609.03002](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03002) is chartered to resolve the spread ahead of FCC.
- **Thin-statistics anomalies** to revisit: HAWC Earth-skimming candidates (2.1–2.5σ, [arXiv:2608.28951](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28951)); triangle-singularity claim (3.8σ, [arXiv:2609.00006](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29006)); Higgs H→Zγ at 1.9σ ([arXiv:2609.04402](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04402)).
- **Higgs width** on-shell bound (<92 MeV, [arXiv:2609.01385](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01385)) still far above the 4.1 MeV prediction — a new handle to tighten.
