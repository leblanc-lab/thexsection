# The Cross Section — Weekly Synthesis, week ending 2026-07-28

The week's center of gravity was **flavor precision anchored to NA62's golden-mode $K^+\to\pi^+\nu\bar\nu$ result**, which opened the week as phenomenology fodder and closed it propagating into $B$-physics — the clearest single trajectory. Around it, two experimental storylines matured: direct-detection experiments consolidating into a solar-neutrino observatory program, and AI agents graduating from checking calculations to orchestrating full pipelines. Cosmology ran defensively, with new "walls" hardening the Hubble tension rather than solving it, while collider polarization and lattice-precision structure work advanced steadily in the background.

## Running themes

- **Kaon → cross-mode flavor phenomenology** (*heating, the week's spine*). The NA62 $K^+$ measurement drove continuous interpretation: Blanke et al. [arXiv:2607.19055](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19055) mapped remaining KOTO II room for $K_L\to\pi^0\nu\bar\nu$; the lattice QCD+QED $R_{e/\mu}$ benchmark [arXiv:2607.22358](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22358) supplied the SM input PIONEER/NA62 need; and Allwicher et al. [arXiv:2607.24494](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24494) translated the kaon result into $B\to K\nu\bar\nu$ and $B_s\to\mu\mu$ constraints. BESIII's record $D\to\pi\ell\nu$ / $|V_{cd}|$ [arXiv:2607.23945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23945) tightened a second CKM rung the same day.

- **Direct detection as a solar-neutrino observatory** (*heating, newly coherent*). The neutrino floor became a program: SNuDD [arXiv:2607.22817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22817) set NSI limits from LZ/XENONnT/PandaX; Beccaria et al. [arXiv:2607.24584](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24584) bounded invisible $\nu_2$ decay from CEνNS at SNO-competitive level; Han et al. [arXiv:2607.24421](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24421) supplied the sub-percent flux framework. This grew out of the month's threshold-diversification mood (QUALIPHIDE [arXiv:2607.19319](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19319), RES-NOVA solar-axion repurposing).

- **AI agents orchestrating computation** (*heating*). Past LQCDMaster, agents now run pipelines: amplitude-as-program search with 805× speed-ups [arXiv:2607.21629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21629), SMEFT-Pheno-Agent [arXiv:2607.22331](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22331), agentic SFitter [arXiv:2607.22813](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22813) — reproducibility as the selling point. Adjacent: forecastable jet-foundation-model scaling laws [arXiv:2607.23377](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23377).

- **Hubble tension, defended not solved** (*steady, hardening*). Poulin et al. [arXiv:2607.20635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20635) and Sabogal et al. [arXiv:2607.21244](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21244) erected walls against early- and late-time solvers; Roy Choudhury's 1.1%-precision $H_0=73.3$ ($6.85\sigma$) [arXiv:2607.24443](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24443) refused to soften it.

- **Collider polarization & lattice structure** (*steady*). ATLAS's 4.0σ longitudinal $Z$ in $WZjj$-EW [arXiv:2607.20246](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20246) extended the $ZZ$ program; BESIII's secondary-scattering polarimetry [arXiv:2607.19948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19948) introduced a reusable method. Lattice closed proton momentum and spin sum rules [arXiv:2607.21230](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21230) and delivered a first gluon Collins-Soper kernel [arXiv:2607.24587](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24587).

## Turning points

- **Chattopadhyay et al. [arXiv:2607.19487](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19487)** put a number on the UHE-neutrino puzzle: 7.5σ tension for a diffuse flux, 5.9σ even for rare transients, given IceCube's null.
- **LHCb's 32 Tbps real-time processing [arXiv:2607.21492](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21492)** set a new field-wide throughput standard, the endpoint of the month's real-time-reconstruction wave.
- **Auger [arXiv:2607.22323](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22323)** converted its $X_{\max}$ deficit into a concrete demand: 2.8–4.6× hadronic-energy amplification — the muon puzzle quantified.
- **Bisal et al. [arXiv:2607.20588](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20588)** identified a Higgsino direct-detection blind spot below the neutrino floor via NLO cancellations.

## Open questions

- **UHE neutrino origin (KM3-230213A):** the 7.5σ tension stands; Earth-rotation timing [arXiv:2607.20608](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20608) disfavors ($p\simeq0.13$) but doesn't exclude a DM-decay origin. Watch for follow-ups.
- **BBN deuterium vs. EDE:** Poulin et al. read a growing $\sim3\sigma$ discrepancy as a hint of a *very* early dark-energy scalar — a new anomaly to track.
- **KOTO II room:** whether $K_L\to\pi^0\nu\bar\nu$ enhancement survives as more flavor assumptions get tested.
- **Neutrino mass floor:** Qu et al. [arXiv:2607.24742](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24742) relax $\sum m_\nu$ to $<0.41$ eV via late-Universe-free routes, near Project 8's target.
- **$X_{17}$:** refined E141 recast [arXiv:2607.22102](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22102) tightens tension with ATOMKI.
