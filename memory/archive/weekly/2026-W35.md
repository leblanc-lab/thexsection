# The Cross Section — Rolling Synthesis, week ending 2026-08-24

The week's spine was a single confirmed discovery — CMS's 6.1σ single-lepton observation of the $t\bar t$-threshold toponium excess — set against a field that otherwise spent its energy on *how to measure*, not *what was found*. Two long-running methodological threads dominated: a collider quantum-information program that pushed past entanglement into "magic," and a proliferation of jet-free, conserved-current precision observables. Dark-matter work stayed almost entirely on the detector-concept side, and EFT continued its quiet takeover as the default collider language. Flavor was consistent-to-null; a wild-card gravitational parity-violation claim hangs unadjudicated.

## Running themes

**Toponium, now real (turning point → heating).** CMS confirmed the $t\bar t$-threshold excess in a statistically independent single-lepton channel ([arXiv:2608.19546](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19546)), 5.1±0.9 pb, consistent with a color-singlet pseudoscalar. Two readings immediately compete: simplified NRQCD toponium vs. Matsuoka's top-philic 2HDM CP-odd Higgs ([arXiv:2608.19651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19651), which finds no viable baryogenesis branch). Watch for further channels to arbitrate.

**Jet-free / conserved-current observables (heating).** The week opened with Zhang et al.'s one-point charge correlator — a jet-free reformulation of the EW forward-backward asymmetry, measured from archival DELPHI LEP-1 data and pitched at the hadronic-asymmetry tensions ([arXiv:2608.14779](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14779), [arXiv:2608.15357](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15357)). The logic recurred all week: energy correlators as a jet-algorithm-free QCD benchmark ([arXiv:2608.14793](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14793)), gluon-pair entanglement read from azimuthal EEC modulation ([arXiv:2608.17685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17685)), Ke et al.'s color-triality jet-flow Gell-Mann–Nishijima relation ([arXiv:2608.19708](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19708)), and heavy-quark EEC dead-cone imprints ([arXiv:2608.21145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21145)).

**Collider quantum information (steady, broadening).** The season-long QI thread moved beyond entanglement: gluon Bell states ([arXiv:2608.17685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17685)), Bethe–Heitler GHZ/W states ([arXiv:2608.18030](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18030)), "quantum magic" in EW scattering with the weak mixing angle again near a magic-minimizing value ([arXiv:2608.19095](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19095)), and tripartite entanglement selecting gauge/diffeomorphism-invariant theories ([arXiv:2608.18732](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18732)). On the gravity side, Fang et al. ([arXiv:2608.17015](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17015)) added a *third* graviton-quantumness no-go — the wall keeps holding.

**Dark-matter detection methodology (heating).** Almost all DM activity was new hardware/handles rather than results: quantum-dot "barcode" arrays ([arXiv:2608.18204](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18204)), POLONAISE's levitated-ferromagnet ultraheavy search extending reach seven orders in mass ([arXiv:2608.20464](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20464)), a HFIR cold-neutron $n\to n'$ limit ([arXiv:2608.21120](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21120)), qubit-sensing reviews ([arXiv:2608.20745](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20745)), plus JUNO-Migdal and X-ray-binary axion probes. XENONnT's inelastic-DM null ([arXiv:2608.15149](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15149)) was the week's clean traditional entry.

**EFT as default collider language (steady).** CMS merged-jet aTGC bounds ([arXiv:2608.18245](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18245)) and the first global top-FCNC fit across all 24 SMEFT operators ([arXiv:2608.19547](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19547)), backed by basis-independent two-loop four-fermion running ([arXiv:2608.19316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19316)).

## Turning points

- **Toponium confirmed in a second channel** ([arXiv:2608.19546](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19546)) — the excess is now a two-channel result; the debate shifts to its nature (NRQCD vs. 2HDM).
- **CMS observes baryon-to-meson enhancement in O-O** ([arXiv:2608.18256](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18256)) — deconfinement signatures in a genuinely small system, the payoff of the season's OO/small-system program.
- **First quartic-Higgs phenomenology** ([arXiv:2608.16998](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16998)) — four-Higgs production opens territory single/double-Higgs rates can't reach.
- **First global top-FCNC EFT fit** ([arXiv:2608.19547](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19547)) — a reference framework for future combinations.

## Open questions

- **Gravitational parity violation** — Gharibyan et al.'s HERA-polarimetry claim ([arXiv:2608.14703](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14703)) is extraordinary and unscrutinized; watch for rebuttals.
- **Nature of the toponium excess** — NRQCD pseudoscalar vs. top-philic CP-odd Higgs remains open.
- **Charm CPV** — Pich et al. ([arXiv:2608.21212](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21212)) reaffirm the SM falls short even with tightened rescattering inputs; unexplained, not resolved.
- **Standing LHC excesses** — the 13.6-GeV diphoton (ALP fit, [arXiv:2608.18506](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18506)) and 146-GeV $e\mu$ excesses each have concrete follow-up strategies but no resolution.
- **$b\to s\ell\ell$** — LHCb's SM-consistent $\Lambda_b\to\Lambda(1520)\mu\mu$ ([arXiv:2608.21082](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21082)) continues consolidation after the mid-month $C_9$ flare; the tension is cooling, not gone.
- **Quarkonium ladder truncation** — where the radial $q\bar q$ ladder ends ($n\approx8$–10, [arXiv:2608.16875](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16875)) is a sharp, testable challenge.
