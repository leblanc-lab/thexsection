# The Cross Section: Rolling Synthesis, week ending 2026-10-21

**Overview.** The week's center of gravity was the field checking its inputs and backgrounds before arguing about new physics. The LZ 248 keV event moved through three stages. Model-building gave way to halo assumptions, then to the Galactic Center excess, and finally, on 10-21, to an audit of LZ's own electron-recoil background. SU(3)$_F$ breaking became a pressure point across $B$, $\Xi_b$ and charm physics. Several anomalies moved toward the SM once their inputs were sharpened, including the $t\bar t$ threshold, gallium and the $C_9$-via-$|V_{cb}|$ route. Lattice HVP results kept accumulating on one side of the $g-2$ gap. Precision theory had a strong week: two independent $g^6$ hot-QCD pressures matched the lattice, and a policy paper formalized error budgets beyond scale variation.

## Running themes

**LZ 248 keV event: heating up, and changing character.** The event drew one paper on 10-15 (Deka's exothermic freeze-in, [arXiv:2610.18974](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18974)) and none on 10-19. Over the week the argument shifted:
- **Halo tail.** Graham et al. ([arXiv:2610.21041](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21041)) showed that the thermal Higgsino survives the solar-neutrino bound only with a different high-velocity halo tail. They also proposed non-xenon tests.
- **Shared origin with the GCE, disputed.** Abazajian et al. ([arXiv:2610.23713](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23713)) found the 20 GeV halo excess is foreground-dominated and argued against a common origin with LZ. Kim et al. ([arXiv:2610.25423](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25423)) built exactly that common origin anyway.
- **Background audit.** Bandyopadhyay et al. ([arXiv:2610.27105](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27105)) put the event's significance itself in question. LZ's published calibration does not constrain the ER response tail. Tails consistent with that calibration give an ER contribution comparable to the entire quoted background in the comparison region.

**SU(3)$_F$ breaking: heating up.** Bhattacharya et al. ([arXiv:2610.20611](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20611)) found a >5σ failure of the SU(3)$_F$-limit SM in $\bar B\to DP$. Absorbing it requires about 60% breaking once CP observables are included, up from about 20% with branching ratios alone. This extends August's $B\to PP$ puzzle to the charm side. SU(3) breaking also dominates the normalization error in LHCb's first $\Xi_b^-\to\Xi^-\mu^+\mu^-$ observation ([arXiv:2610.22452](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22452)). The theory responses so far are a first-order U-spin sum-rule program ([arXiv:2610.24975](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24975)) and flavor-selective production to separate the two $D_0^*(2300)$ poles ([arXiv:2610.26175](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26175)).

**HVP lattice vs data-driven: steady, consolidating on the lattice side.**
- ETMC ([arXiv:2610.22224](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22224)) reached about 1% and widened the gap to pre-CMD3 data-driven values.
- A QCD sum-rule result ([arXiv:2610.20945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20945)) also lands on the lattice side.
- New tools target the gap: the $a_e$–$a_\mu$ correlation ([arXiv:2610.26130](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26130)) and the missing isospin-breaking $\rho\pi\pi$ correction for the τ route ([arXiv:2610.28506](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28506)).

**Anomalies dissolving into inputs: steady.**
- The gallium anomaly vanishes when the Sun is used as a calibrated source ([arXiv:2610.22219](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22219)).
- A neutrino-anomaly review ([arXiv:2610.23474](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23474)) finds the tensions weakened, with no uncontested explanation.
- The Galactic Center excess narrows to a τ-rich corridor once 30 dwarf spheroidals are imposed ([arXiv:2610.26648](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26648)).
- NA61/SHINE's kaon excess is the counter-current (see Open questions).

**Error budgets and precision QCD: steady.** Canonero et al. ([arXiv:2610.25091](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25091)) turned the cold-QED N³LO lesson into a prescription: supplement 7-point scale variations with known missing higher-order terms. N³LO building blocks kept arriving ([arXiv:2610.20820](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20820), [arXiv:2610.22533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22533)).

## Turning points

- **$t\bar t$ threshold.** Ball et al. ([arXiv:2610.21479](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21479)) propagated PDF, $m_t$ and $\alpha_s$ correlations through a global PDF analysis. They find nothing at 8 TeV and just over 2σ at 13 TeV, against CMS's 6.1σ, with a strong Δ–$m_t$ correlation. Any future threshold or toponium claim should be quoted jointly with $m_t$.
- **$C_9$ escape route closed.** Crivellin et al. ([arXiv:2610.22490](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22490)) gave a no-go for faking the lepton-universal $b\to s\ell\ell$ deficit through NP in $|V_{cb}|$ and hence $V_{ts}$.
- **$\mathcal O(g^6)$ hot Yang-Mills pressure completed.** Two separate calculations ([arXiv:2610.23675](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23675), [arXiv:2610.23676](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23676)) close a gap open for over two decades and agree with the lattice. This is a direct pass of dimensional-reduction EFT against data.
- **Weak magnetism in the free neutron.** Camarasa-Domene et al. ([arXiv:2610.26792](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26792)) separate $g_M$ from $\lambda$ and exclude zero at 5.1σ, consistent with CVC. The significance is robust to the neutron-lifetime tensions.
- **LZ significance on the table.** [arXiv:2610.27105](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27105) (above) changes the default frame for all 248 keV papers.

## Open questions

- **LZ ER tail.** Will LZ or others perform the calibrations listed in [arXiv:2610.27105](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27105)? Do model-builders begin propagating the tail uncertainty?
- **SU(3)$_F$.** Is ~60% breaking a failure of the amplitude parametrization or of the symmetry expansion itself? Watch for responses to [arXiv:2610.20611](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20611).
- **$|V_{cb}|$.** The inclusive/exclusive split remains unresolved. Marzocca's DIS route ([arXiv:2610.18961](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18961)) projects about 0.8% at LHeC and 0.7% at FCC-eh, with orthogonal systematics.
- **NA61/SHINE kaon excess.** The collaboration reports a charged-over-neutral kaon excess in Ar+Sc that known mechanisms do not explain ([arXiv:2610.23498](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23498)). Uncertainties are sizeable, so confirmation from other experiments is the thing to watch.
- **GCE corridor.** Only τ-rich channels and light-mediator $4b$ cascades survive [arXiv:2610.26648](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26648). Watch whether shared-origin models like [arXiv:2610.25423](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25423) move into that corridor.
- **Birefringence sources.** New non-axion and axion–monopole sources of the 3.5σ post-dust signal appeared ([arXiv:2610.27082](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27082), [arXiv:2610.27059](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27059)), joining Kasai's lepton-asymmetry route. The stiff-pseudoscalar window is closable by astrophysical axion searches.
- **Neutrino mass ordering.** Gluza et al. ([arXiv:2610.27270](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27270)) find most mixing schemes fit IO better. This sits awkwardly with the replacement, on 10-21, of Jin et al.'s 3.3σ normal-ordering result.
- **Smaller watch items:**
  - IceCube's 1.9σ linear Lorentz-invariance-violation preference ([arXiv:2610.25214](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25214)).
  - The compact $X_b$ partner predicted ~60 MeV below $B\bar B^*$ ([arXiv:2610.19066](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19066)).
  - The unexplained millikelvin SiPM double pulse ([arXiv:2610.20132](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20132)).
