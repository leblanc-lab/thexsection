# The Cross Section: rolling synthesis, week ending 2026-10-20

The week's dominant motion was **anomalies meeting their inputs**. Several long-running tensions were tested against better-controlled baselines and mostly lost ground: IceCube decoherence, gallium, the Galactic Center excess, and di-$J/\psi$ exotics. A few new pressure points went the other way. The largest is SU(3)$_F$ breaking in $B$ decays, now large enough to strain the flavor-symmetry expansion itself. LZ 248 keV model-building peaked early in the week (five papers on 10-14) and then thinned to one or zero a day. Its argument moved from the solar bound to the dark-matter halo. Precision QCD delivered a direct pass against the lattice (the hot Yang-Mills $g^6$ pressure). Its methodological lesson, that scale bands understate errors, is being formalized into policy.

## Running themes

- **LZ event: from veto to test to halo (cooling in volume, still evolving).** Di Mauro [arXiv:2610.14188](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14188) treats the solar-capture bound as a test to design around and proposes a dedicated IceCube analysis. Graham et al. [arXiv:2610.21041](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21041) show the thermal Higgsino survives only with a different high-velocity halo tail, which moves the fight to halo simulations. They also propose non-xenon tests: paleodetectors and large neutrino detectors. Some papers still omit solar capture entirely. By week's end, model-building had become speculative bridging:
  - a shared LZ–GCE origin [arXiv:2610.25423](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25423), proposed despite arguments against one;
  - a hexaquark reading [arXiv:2610.25925](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25925).
- **SU(3)$_F$ breaking as the flavor pressure point (heating).** In $B\to DP$, the breaking needed to fit the data reaches ~60% [arXiv:2610.20611](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20611). The $\Xi_b^-\to\Xi^-\mu\mu$ normalisation error is dominated by SU(3) breaking [arXiv:2610.22452](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22452). Responses have begun: first-order U-spin sum rules [arXiv:2610.24975](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24975). This connects to August's $B\to PP$ puzzle.
- **Anomaly nulls and SM-side inputs (steady, broad).**
  - Gallium anomaly gone when the Sun is used as the calibration source [arXiv:2610.22219](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22219).
  - Neutrino-anomaly review: tensions weakened, no uncontested explanation [arXiv:2610.23474](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23474).
  - GCE is foreground-dependent [arXiv:2610.23713](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23713), and dwarf limits exclude most annihilation channels [arXiv:2610.26648](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26648).
  - Lattice HVP strengthens: ETMC [arXiv:2610.22224](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22224) and a sum-rule result [arXiv:2610.20945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20945) both land on the lattice side.
- **Precision QCD and error budgets (heating).**
  - Two independent complete $g^6$ hot Yang-Mills pressures, both agreeing with lattice data.
  - N³LO building blocks: three-loop masters with one off-shell leg [arXiv:2610.20820](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20820) and three-loop rapidity beam-function kernels [arXiv:2610.22533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22533).
  - The Aspen writeup [arXiv:2610.25091](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25091) turns the 10-12 cold-QED lesson into a prescription beyond 7-point scale variation.
- **Exotics vs. threshold dynamics (steady, contested).**
  - Against exotics: Swanson's no-exotics di-$J/\psi$ fit [arXiv:2610.15248](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15248).
  - For exotics: Garcia Tecocoatzi et al. predict detectable $4P$ all-charm tetraquarks [arXiv:2610.22914](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22914).
  - Glueball readings keep weakening: the η′ fit [arXiv:2610.25216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25216) favors a radial excitation, following the X(2370) reassessment.

## Turning points

- **IceCube** [arXiv:2610.14144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14144) excludes natural Planck-suppressed decoherence for $0\le n\le3$, the first Planck-scale exclusion at $n=3$. IceCube then moved to precision, measuring the TeV $\nu_\mu$ CC cross section [arXiv:2610.24068](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24068).
- **$g^6$ pressure closed** after two decades: [arXiv:2610.23675](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23675) and [arXiv:2610.23676](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23676), a direct validation of the dimensional-reduction EFT against the lattice.
- **$|V_{cb}|$ escape from $C_9$ closed.** Crivellin et al. [arXiv:2610.22490](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22490) give a no-go for CKM-unitarity violation faking the lepton-universal $b\to s\ell\ell$ deficit.
- **MadNIS becomes MadGraph's LO default** [arXiv:2610.17177](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17177). Neural importance sampling is now infrastructure.
- **Weak magnetism observed in the free neutron** at 5.1σ, consistent with CVC [arXiv:2610.26792](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26792). This is a reanalysis of existing data, robust to the neutron-lifetime tensions.

## Open questions

- **$R(D^{(*)})$.** World averages remain in tension despite September's easing [arXiv:2610.16617](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16617). Parameter-free form factors sit 2.8σ from experiment [arXiv:2610.17289](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17289).
- **$|V_{cb}|$ inclusive/exclusive.** A high-energy route via charged-current DIS promises ~0.8% with orthogonal systematics [arXiv:2610.18961](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18961).
- **$C_9$.** New inputs: the first differential high-$m_{K\pi}$ $B^0\to K^+\pi^-\mu\mu$ measurement [arXiv:2610.14156](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14156), and the first time-dependent CPV in $b\to s\ell\ell$ [arXiv:2610.26188](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26188).
- **$t\bar t$ threshold.** A global PDF fit gives just over 2σ at 13 TeV, with a strong Δ–$m_t$ correlation [arXiv:2610.21479](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21479), compared with CMS's 6.1σ. Watch for joint-$m_t$ quoting and for the toponium-vs-CP-odd-scalar question.
- **HVP gap.** Lattice vs. data-driven widened. The $a_e$–$a_\mu$ correlation approach [arXiv:2610.26130](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26130) is a new handle.
- **Counter-currents to watch:**
  - NA61/SHINE's unexplained charged-over-neutral kaon excess [arXiv:2610.23498](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23498); the key question is independent confirmation.
  - 1.9σ linear Lorentz-invariance violation in HESE–GRB timing [arXiv:2610.25214](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25214).
  - The τ-rich GCE corridor left open by dwarf limits [arXiv:2610.26648](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26648).
- **NOvA–T2K tension.** Xenon solar-neutrino data already constrain the complex-NSI explanation [arXiv:2610.18971](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18971).
- **Search target.** A compact $X_b$ partner predicted ~60 MeV below $B\bar B^*$ [arXiv:2610.19066](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19066).
