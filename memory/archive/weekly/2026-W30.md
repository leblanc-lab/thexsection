# Rolling Synthesis — Week Ending 2026-07-21

A breadth-over-depth week with no single collaboration bombshell but several genuine firsts: ATLAS's observation of two simultaneously longitudinally polarized Z bosons ([arXiv:2607.12879](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12879)), NA62's 20%-precision golden-mode kaon branching ratio ([arXiv:2607.16413](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16413)), and COMPASS/JPAC's first high-energy-scattering observation of an exotic Reggeon ([arXiv:2607.17850](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17850)). Theory currents dominated: axions fanning out across every mass window, quantum information hardening into standard collider vocabulary, machine learning shifting from reconstruction to *discovery*, and a persistent, sharpening skepticism about the reliability of first-order-phase-transition GW predictions. Cosmology consolidated around an early-dark-energy consensus. The instrumentation and open-data communities each had a strong showing mid-week.

## Running themes

- **Axion diversification (steady, still loud).** Peaked 07-15 with five papers spanning merged-photon LHC bounds ([arXiv:2607.12004](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12004)), SN1987A gluon-only floors, ultralight coherence, and metrology. The charm-coupled GeV QCD axion ([arXiv:2607.12956](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12956)) tying isospin and quality problems together got a HMChPT follow-up 07-21 ([arXiv:2607.17277](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17277)) — no fatal constraint yet. Fields increasingly asked to double as dark energy/inflaton ([arXiv:2607.13128](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13128)).
- **Quantum information at colliders (heated, then quieted).** Emerged 07-15, consolidated 07-16/17 — spin states, entanglement, and quantum hardware treated as ordinary observables ([arXiv:2607.12015](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12015), cross-listed twice; [arXiv:2607.15153](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15153)). Went quiet in the last two digests; watch whether it resumes.
- **ML that discovers rather than reconstructs (heating).** From LQCDMaster ([arXiv:2607.15001](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15001)) to ShellFlow learning W/top/Z masses and the Weinberg angle from raw ATLAS open data with two priors ([arXiv:2607.16144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16144)) and cross-experiment tagger distillation ([arXiv:2607.15531](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15531)). A clear conceptual step beyond ML-as-trigger.
- **Phase-transition GW systematics (heating, skeptical).** A through-line from 07-17 (wall friction [arXiv:2607.14867](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14867); LISA cosmic-string reach degraded 10⁵× by foregrounds, [arXiv:2607.15219](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15219)) into a dense 07-21 cluster (fluid-vs-WallGo discrepancies [arXiv:2607.18207](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18207); daisy resummation disfavored for supercooling [arXiv:2607.18233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18233)). Recurring message: GW "signals" hinge on poorly controlled non-equilibrium inputs.
- **Dark matter, exclusionary + diversifying targets (steady).** A constraining mood (DM–neutrino scattering bound killing prior hints, [arXiv:2607.15020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15020)) alongside novel target proposals — carbon-nanotube plasmons ([arXiv:2607.15338](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15338)), Rydberg-atom hidden-photon detection, the Earth-as-transducer review ([arXiv:2607.16342](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16342)).
- **Flavor precision (steady).** CKM structure and QED systematics 07-16 (b-column blind spot [arXiv:2607.13136](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13136); QED-corrected B→τν [arXiv:2607.13144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13144)), capped by NA62's kaon measurement.

## Turning points

- **[arXiv:2607.13282](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13282) / [arXiv:2607.13283](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13283)** — the "H₀ World Cup" delivered a refereed verdict: early dark energy / early modified gravity win, cutting SH0ES discrepancy to ~2.5–3.6σ. Now the reference bake-off; Khoury's DESI phantom-crossing model ([arXiv:2607.16191](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16191)) is already framed as its sequel.
- **[arXiv:2607.14243](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14243)** — first experimental test of the N-naturalness tower excluded its GUT-scale (N=10⁴) Majorana benchmark, killing the framework's tidy no-fine-tuning selling point.
- **[arXiv:2607.14793](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14793)** — LHCb's parameterised Kalman filter reaching production (500 GPUs, 30 MHz), the real-time-reconstruction wave crossing into the trigger.
- **[arXiv:2607.16144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16144)** — ShellFlow reframed what ML can extract from raw data.

## Open questions

- **HVP saga still unsettled** — the Dyson-Schwinger determination ([arXiv:2607.12918](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12918)) lands on lattice consensus, but the picture remains open.
- **Belle II B⁺→K⁺νν excess** — LNV SMEFT can't explain it without fine-tuning unless a light right-handed neutrino is added ([arXiv:2607.13142](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13142)).
- **Charm axion viability** — [arXiv:2607.12956](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12956)/[arXiv:2607.17277](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17277) survive so far, with CMB-S4-testable ΔN_eff; watch for constraints.
- **CKM b-column blind spot** — [arXiv:2607.13136](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13136) promotes ε_K to leading probe of a direction B factories structurally can't see.
- **Extra GW polarizations as gravity-theory discriminant** — fresh 07-16 coalescence ([arXiv:2607.13146](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13146), [arXiv:2607.13483](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13483)); no follow-up yet.
- **Whether phase-transition GW forecasts can be trusted** — the systematics drumbeat suggests current LISA projections may be premature.
