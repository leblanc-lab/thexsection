# Rolling synthesis — week ending 2026-07-24

A theory-and-methods-heavy week bracketed by two experimental firsts (NA62's golden-mode kaon BR, COMPASS's exotic Reggeon) and closing on a cosmology beat that turned adversarial: after last week's "$H_0$ World Cup" crowned early-dark-energy scenarios, this week's papers spent their energy poking holes in the winners. Alongside, the field kept diversifying dark-matter searches, sharpening the microphysics inputs to phase-transition gravitational-wave predictions, consolidating exotic-hadron spectroscopy, and pushing ML from reconstruction toward discovery and real-time throughput.

## Running themes

- **Hubble/dark-energy tension — heating, and turning against solvers.** The week opened with Khoury et al.'s dark-axion/dark-baryon fit to DESI DR2 phantom crossing (Δχ²=−14.48, [arXiv:2607.16191](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16191)) and closed with two independent "walls": Poulin et al.'s BBN-deuterium discrepancy that *worsens* to ~3σ under EDE ([arXiv:2607.20635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20635)) and Sabogal et al.'s "shape wall" capping late-time $H_0$ gains at ~2% vs the needed ~8% ([arXiv:2607.21244](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21244)). Model-building continues (da Silva Pereira O(3) no-scale, [arXiv:2607.20320](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20320)) but the mood shifted from proposing fixes to constraining them.

- **First-order transitions & GW systematics — steady, now a live methods debate.** July 17's wall-friction thread persisted: Dorsch et al. ([arXiv:2607.18207](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18207)) benchmarked fluid ansatz vs WallGo; Kierkla et al. declared daisy resummation disfavored for supercooled models (theory error swamps LISA, [arXiv:2607.18233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18233)); Klose offered the constructive rebuttal with 2PI resummation ([arXiv:2607.21252](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21252)). Recurring message: GW "signals" hinge on poorly controlled non-equilibrium inputs.

- **Dark-matter detection diversification — heating, now with real limits.** New targets/thresholds all week: carbon-nanotube plasmons ([arXiv:2607.15338](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15338)), QUALIPHIDE's 13 meV MKID hidden-photon limits ([arXiv:2607.19319](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19319)), RES-NOVA archaeological-lead (inelastic DM, then solar axions [arXiv:2607.21363](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21363)), and Bisal et al.'s warning that NLO cancellations push Higgsino–nucleon scattering below the neutrino floor ([arXiv:2607.20588](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20588)).

- **Rare kaons & exotic/heavy-hadron spectroscopy — steady.** NA62's $K^+\to\pi^+\nu\bar\nu$ at 20% precision, SM-consistent ([arXiv:2607.16413](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16413)), immediately got its phenomenology sequel (Blanke et al. on remaining $K_L$ room, [arXiv:2607.19055](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19055)). The spin-exotic program ran from COMPASS's exotic Reggeon ([arXiv:2607.17850](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17850)) to the $K(1690)$-as-hybrid read ([arXiv:2607.20825](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20825)); glueball claims recurred (Nf=1 mixing [arXiv:2607.17294](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17294), BESIII's $X(2370)$ [arXiv:2607.20366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20366)); $T_{cc}^+$ lattice with pion exchange ([arXiv:2607.19009](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19009)).

- **ML discovery & real-time processing — heating.** ShellFlow learned SM masses/Weinberg angle from raw ATLAS open data ([arXiv:2607.16144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16144)); cross-experiment tagger distillation ([arXiv:2607.15531](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15531)); SymbolFit rediscovered fit functions ([arXiv:2607.19750](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19750)). The real-time-reconstruction wave reached production with LHCb's 32 Tbps architecture ([arXiv:2607.21492](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21492)).

## Turning points

- **The tension conversation inverted** (07-24): BBN-deuterium and shape-wall arguments reframed EDE and late-time fixes as increasingly constrained, not favored.
- **NA62 → KOTO II reframing** (07-21→22): the SM-consistent $K^+$ number set the reference and immediately redirected model space toward $K_L$/right-handed currents.
- **BESIII polarimetry gambit** (07-23): secondary-scattering polarimetry on a general spectrometer ([arXiv:2607.19948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19948)) — a reusable method beyond its first form-factor result.
- **UHE neutrino anomaly got a number** (07-23): joint ANITA/KM3NeT/IceCube fit quantified 7.5σ (diffuse) / 5.9σ (transient) tension ([arXiv:2607.19487](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19487)).

## Open questions to watch

- **What replaces EDE for $H_0$?** Poulin's "very early dark energy" hint ($\Delta H/H\simeq0.087$ at deuterium burning) — another light scalar? Follow-ups on whether any solver survives both walls.
- **KM3-230213A / UHE origin:** Cheek et al.'s Earth-rotation timing disfavors ($p\simeq0.13$) but doesn't exclude DM-decay origin ([arXiv:2607.20608](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20608)); the 7.5σ tension remains unexplained.
- **Daisy-resummation resolution:** does 2PI/Klose-style machinery reconcile the supercooled-transition GW predictions Kierkla flagged as unreliable?
- **Neutrino energy-reconstruction bias:** Lachner et al.'s material-response bias (7–9 MeV scint, 11–18 MeV argon, [arXiv:2607.20419](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20419)) adds to the Dolan/T2K generator-spread anxiety — watch for hybrid tracking+calorimetry adoption.
- **Doubly-longitudinal VBS:** ATLAS $WZjj$ longitudinal-$Z$ at 4.0σ ([arXiv:2607.20246](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20246)), but the doubly-longitudinal fraction still only bounded (<0.12).
