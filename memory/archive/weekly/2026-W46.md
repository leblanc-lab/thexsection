# The Cross Section: Weekly synthesis, week ending 2026-11-09

This week was mostly about calibration rather than discovery. The loudest thread was the $t\bar t$ threshold. Across three digests, the community built the tools needed to decide whether CMS's threshold excess is toponium or a CP-odd scalar. On the $g-2$ side, BaBar's new ISR ππ measurement opened an internal spread of ~8×10⁻¹⁰, and an independent generator cross-check arrived two days later. Light-ion flow kept turning into a probe of nuclear structure. Several long-running anomalies lost ground to nulls or no-gos: X17, DAMA, the gallium deficit and neutrino-sourced birefringence. A parallel "identifiability audit" habit spread through phenomenology and quantum-information claims. The cleanest flavor channels (LHCb Upgrade I) stayed on the SM.

## Running themes

- **Top threshold: toponium vs CP-odd Higgs (heating up).**
  - The NNLO pseudoscalar toponium cross section, 4.87 pb at 13 TeV, moved the dominant error to the Coulomb-scale dependence of the matrix elements ([arXiv:2611.03782](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03782)).
  - An NLO $t\bar t$ + 2HDM interference tool in POWHEG followed ([arXiv:2611.08388](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08388)).
  - Re's truncated-$\alpha_s/v$ reweighting reproduces threshold data ([arXiv:2611.09299](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09299)).
  - Interference shifts low-$\tan\beta$ $H/A$ rates by up to tens of percent ([arXiv:2611.09666](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09666)).
  - Open-Data $m_{t\bar t}$ reconstruction is limited by the $\nu\bar\nu$ approximation ([arXiv:2611.09797](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09797)).
  - Net drift: the bound-state reading may need less new machinery than assumed.
- **HVP via ππ (heating up).**
  - BaBar's new analysis gives $a_\mu^{\pi\pi}=525.9\pm3.5\pm3.8$. The combination with 2009 is 517.8 ± 2.4 ± 3.3 ([arXiv:2611.04596](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04596)).
  - KKMC and SHERPA agree to ≲0.04% on resummed QED for radiative return. Pions remain point-like scalars dressed with a form factor ([arXiv:2611.07067](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07067)).
  - A QED-PDF review flags slow convergence ([arXiv:2611.09408](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09408)).
  - The τ-route isospin budget tightened again with the ωπ term ([arXiv:2611.01683](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01683)).
- **Flow as nuclear-structure data (steady, broadening).**
  - O–O $\Lambda/K^0_S$ ordering follows transverse density, not multiplicity. This gives a falsifiable Ne–Ne prediction ([arXiv:2611.04865](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04865)).
  - Ne/O $v_2$ ratios are model-robust; $v_3$ ratios are not ([arXiv:2611.05239](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05239)).
  - Eccentricities respond linearly to the chiral LECs, and Ne–Ne mostly probes spin-orbit ([arXiv:2611.08122](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08122)).
  - Pb vibrations are claimed to resolve the ultracentral $v_2$/$v_3$ puzzle ([arXiv:2611.09404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09404)).
  - ALICE maps where partonic collectivity fades in pp/p–Pb ([arXiv:2611.08327](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08327)).
- **Identifiability and self-audit (heating up).**
  - Serao et al. find blind directions twice: Ge/Xe light mediators ([arXiv:2611.04600](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04600)) and unbounded X17 nucleon couplings ([arXiv:2611.06129](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06129)).
  - The quoted Adler $c_{5,1}=283$ is a linear extrapolation ([arXiv:2611.06824](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06824)).
  - "Minimal magic" conclusions depend on which measure is used ([arXiv:2611.08586](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08586)).
  - Mean-field neutrino oscillations undercut most quantum-advantage claims ([arXiv:2611.08595](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08595)).
- **FCC-ee as arbiter (steady).** This extends the 11-02 Tera-Z/SU(5) result.
  - Flavour-chain messengers are already bounded by LEP/SLD to $M_F/|y_F|\gtrsim3$ TeV, and FCC-ee would reach 100 TeV ([arXiv:2611.05464](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05464)).
  - Dvali's TeV color triplet is the opposite bet ([arXiv:2611.03783](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03783)).
- **Trigger-budget ML (fading this week).** It appeared only on 11-03, with SCRAMJet's "virtual channels" quantization pitfall ([arXiv:2611.00086](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00086)) and SpikePID ([arXiv:2611.00104](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00104)). The live question is now what survives quantization.

## Turning points

- **LHCb Upgrade I's first rare-decay result** ([arXiv:2611.00230](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00230)):
  - first single-experiment evidence for $B^0\to\mu^+\mu^-$;
  - world-best $B_s^0\to\mu^+\mu^-$ branching fraction and effective lifetime;
  - all SM-consistent, which reinforces that flavor tension lives in $C_9$ and $|V_{cb}|$, not the clean exclusives.
- **Birefringence no-go** ([arXiv:2611.07578](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07578)). Coupling photons to the neutrino-asymmetry current cannot explain the CMB rotation hint, because the electron coupling it forces is excluded. The axion Chern–Simons reading is left as the standing interpretation.
- **Gallium.** Realistic shell-model densities leave the cross section 20% too high. The non-factorization fix would need fine-tuned cancellations ([arXiv:2611.09788](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09788)).
- **Windows closed:**
  - FASER excludes $6\times10^{-5}<\epsilon_e<1.2\times10^{-4}$ for X17 ([arXiv:2611.03904](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03904));
  - DAMA cannot coexist with LZ, PICO-60 and CDMSlite even in full NREFT ([arXiv:2611.04890](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04890));
  - realistic squark limits move up to 2.6–3.0 TeV ([arXiv:2611.03794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03794)).
- **Born spreading in public MG5_aMC, including FxFx** ([arXiv:2611.06769](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06769)). $W$+jets samples generate 6× faster. This is a method adoption likely to show up downstream.

## Open questions

- **BaBar's internal ππ spread.** Watch how compatibility with the 2009 result is quantified, and the responses from KLOE, CMD-3 and the lattice.
- **Toponium vs scalar.** Does any combined fit adopt [arXiv:2611.03782](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03782) and [arXiv:2611.09299](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09299)? Can spin information separate top-philic scalar from fermion models ([arXiv:2611.08459](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08459))?
- **$|V_{cb}|$.** CMS's hadronic-input-free value from $W\to cb$ ([arXiv:2611.01274](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01274)) is still at ~19%. Watch for improvements.
- **LZ 248 keV event.** The first model is an exothermic Higgs-portal relic predicting a sub-MeV COSI continuum ([arXiv:2611.01158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01158)). Watch for competitors and for experimental follow-up.
- **X17 high-coupling window**, now compounded by unbounded nucleon directions.
- **Ne–Ne**: does the 3–5%-of-O–O prediction hold, and does Ne–Ne data feed back into chiral-EFT fits?
- **Neutrino mass.** Thermal Festina Lente's lower bound ([arXiv:2611.02559](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02559)) sits awkwardly against DESI's negative-$\sum m_\nu$ preference.
- **Super-K $\bar\nu_e$ excess** targeted by pion-DM semi-annihilation ([arXiv:2611.08440](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08440)). The link to the 10-27 DSNB indication is unconfirmed.
- **Low-$Q$ weak mixing.** The COHERENT $\sin^2\theta_W=0.231$ ([arXiv:2611.03751](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03751)) sits between CONUS+ and the global combination.
- **Large claims to verify:**
  - ≥7.2σ light-nucleus UHECR populations ([arXiv:2611.00205](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00205));
  - Pb-vibration resolution of the $v_2$/$v_3$ puzzle.
