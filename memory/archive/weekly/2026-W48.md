# The Cross Section: Rolling synthesis, week ending 2026-11-26

The week's main move was **auditing uncertainties people had taken for granted**. YR4 Higgs cross sections were retired, and the γ combination was tightened. GW forecasts were shown to swing by orders of magnitude depending on the resummation choice, and halo assumptions were shown to move LZ limits by up to 10×. A look-elsewhere review arrived just as several excesses lack such treatment. The AI-agent thread split along one line: agents produce verified new results where output can be checked exactly, but score only 31–60% of expert level when graded blind on analysis. The toponium debate changed shape: η_t and a pseudoscalar must be fitted *with interference*, not as separate templates. Smaller steady threads were LZ model-building, superradiance depletion, and neutrino interaction modeling.

## Running themes

- **AI agents: verifiability as the dividing line (heating up).** Agents did best where output could be checked exactly:
  - Petit Rosas et al. had Opus 5.5 produce new elliptic/K3 integral bases ([arXiv:2611.26688](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26688)), and CANON used the same deterministic-gate design ([arXiv:2611.33100](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33100)).
  - Novák et al.'s sealed-world benchmark scored analysis agents at 31–60% of the expert reference ([arXiv:2611.28194](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28194)).
  - Bonnet-Guerrini et al. found an IR-safety failure in a jet foundation model and repaired it, including by agent edits ([arXiv:2611.27079](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27079)).
  - On 11-26 came LLM-written NNLO subtraction checked against human code ([arXiv:2611.33279](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33279)) and NuSrcAgent's IceCube reproductions ([arXiv:2611.33324](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33324)).
  
  The recurring yardstick is Alimena's evidence-over-code standard (11-19).
- **GW forecasts: the uncertainty moves into the effective potential (heating up).** Zhao et al. found that resummation prescriptions differ by up to nine orders of magnitude in GW peak amplitude ([arXiv:2611.26417](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26417)). Two papers followed with fixes: Qiu et al. removed partial dressing's divergences ([arXiv:2611.26303](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26303)), and Münzenberg et al. extended dressing to gauge and ghost modes ([arXiv:2611.31359](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31359)). The latter erases the SM barrier, but its authors flag the treatment as unreliable there.
- **LZ event model-building (steady, templated).** Several models fit the event with endothermic or photon-mediated scattering ([arXiv:2611.25931](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25931), [arXiv:2611.26274](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26274), [arXiv:2611.34438](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34438)). Most never address solar capture, the bound that killed the higgsino reading. Li et al. ([arXiv:2611.33787](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33787)) are the exception and list it as a test. Shao et al. show that halo anisotropy can open kinematic access for the 248 keV event ([arXiv:2611.29197](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29197)), with explicit caveats.
- **Superradiance clouds rarely behave like free fields (consolidating).** Self-gravity is a third independent depletion mechanism ([arXiv:2611.26541](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26541)), after Foster (11-17) and Bottaro (11-19). Binary environments now enter the black-hole spin bounds on axions: a companion can partly offset depletion, and a companion-sourced Yukawa force can halt cloud growth ([arXiv:2611.29939](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29939)).
- **Neutrino mass ordering inputs (steady).** Two results feed the percent-level interaction-modeling target: SBND's high-statistics νe cross section prefers lower generator predictions ([arXiv:2611.25098](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25098)), and a MINERvA surrogate response model was released ([arXiv:2611.26271](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26271)). T2K's first Gd-loaded Super-K data still favour normal ordering, but no significances are quoted ([arXiv:2611.32106](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32106)). Barenboim et al. show that the T-odd term in νμ disappearance is real ([arXiv:2611.27985](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27985)).
- **Emerging: nanosecond L1 triggers.** Two papers ([arXiv:2611.28355](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28355), [arXiv:2611.30896](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30896)) fit signature-agnostic selection into the L1 latency budget. Separately, cosmological-correlator closed-form results recurred three times in one week.

## Turning points

- **Higgs cross sections: YR5 replaces YR4** ([arXiv:2611.26281](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26281)). PDFs now lead the uncertainty for the best-predicted modes, and the gluon-fusion PDF-order mismatch uncertainty is doubled.
- **CKM γ = 63.2°⁺²·³₋₂.₂** from LHCb's combination, with the best charm-mixing parameters to date ([arXiv:2611.26449](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26449)).
- **Toponium and a pseudoscalar must interfere** ([arXiv:2611.27704](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27704)). Additive template fits are wrong by construction, and the line shape is fixed by one complex κ with an amplitude zero just above M_A. Djouadi extended the same κ formalism to heavy MSSM Higgs searches ([arXiv:2611.09666](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09666)). Cesarotti et al. independently argued that amplitude zeros constrain UV completions ([arXiv:2611.27681](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27681)).
- **First blind benchmark for analysis agents** ([arXiv:2611.28194](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28194)). Its lasting contribution is the injection-test design, not the model rankings.
- **V_ud: QED is no longer the limiting piece** ([arXiv:2611.34804](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34804)). The neutron radiative correction is now 70× more precise, so the floor stays nuclear, at Novario's ~6×10⁻⁴ contact-term limit.
- **KPW rule under strain.** The KPW+SU(3)_F prediction for Ξc⁰→pK⁻ overshoots the measured rate by 17±6×, a 6.3σ discrepancy ([arXiv:2611.27716](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27716)).

## Open questions

- Will refits of the tt̄ threshold excess adopt the interference treatment of [arXiv:2611.27704](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27704)?
- Does any LZ model survive a solar-capture analysis?
- Can thermal resummation ambiguity be reduced enough to make LISA projections meaningful?
- Look-elsewhere treatment for ATLAS's 3.5σ instanton-like excess and for ML searches ([arXiv:2611.34888](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34888)).
- Watch for:
  - T2K significances and their pull on the 1.9σ ordering hint.
  - The eight Λc modes that would decide whether KPW survives.
  - LHCb's A_CP(KK) and A_CP(ππ) values.
  - Devoto et al.'s verdict on the LLM-written subtraction code.
- The YFS–KKMC difference of up to 2% for radiative return ([arXiv:2611.27883](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27883)), relevant to HVP.
- Time-weighted energy correlators are IR unsafe at two loops ([arXiv:2611.34886](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34886)). Will timing-based correlator measurements account for this?
