# The Cross Section: Rolling Synthesis, month ending 2026-09-30

September belonged to a single LZ nuclear recoil: one 248 keV event at 2.6σ ([arXiv:2609.02823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02823)). The theory response ran a full arc. It started with a consensus model, saw that model excluded, scattered into evasion strategies and then revived at high volume. Solar capture became the constraint every model is built around. Several other trajectories gained ground. Precision QCD became a center of gravity from mid-month. Flavor physics flipped from an LFU anomaly to a $|V_{cb}|$ puzzle. Spectroscopy had its strongest run of the season. The axion program widened from lattice work into detection and cosmology. ML tooling moved steadily up the stack, from agents to inference. The overall mood was consolidation: anomalies were mostly being settled by bounds rather than amplified.

## Running themes

**LZ inelastic dark matter (heating → fading → revived; always shaped by solar capture).**
- **W36, consensus:** Groups converged on a ~1.1 TeV endothermic higgsino with ~300–370 keV splitting ([arXiv:2609.01504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01504), [arXiv:2609.01583](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01583)).
- **Exclusion:** Pospelov's solar-capture + IceCube bound excluded that reading ([arXiv:2609.02775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02775)). Bose pushed the splitting bound to ≳557 keV ([arXiv:2609.07807](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07807)).
- **W37, evasion:** The thermal higgsino survives only at 10⁵–10⁶ GeV (Langhoff [arXiv:2609.09385](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09385)). The exothermic reframing gave the main escape route (Dent [arXiv:2609.04673](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04673)).
- **W38, cooling:** The thread thinned to cosmological survival strategies, including An's endo/exo selection ([arXiv:2609.17412](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17412)).
- **W39, revival:** Output rose to 5–7 papers a day, each built to dodge solar capture ([arXiv:2609.21823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21823)). Examples include confining sectors and Murayama's 11–20 TeV inert doublet ([arXiv:2609.28819](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28819)).
- **Lesson repeated all month:** The event's kinematics pin down mass and splitting, and astrophysics then decides which models survive.

**Precision QCD and nucleon structure (heating since W38).**
- Four-loop non-singlet splitting functions ([arXiv:2609.17267](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17267)).
- First GPD review in two decades ([arXiv:2609.19544](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19544)) and lattice GPDs near $x=\pm\xi$ ([arXiv:2609.20545](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20545)).
- W39 added three-loop massive form factors ([arXiv:2609.22034](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22034)) and a four-loop massive cusp anomalous dimension ([arXiv:2609.23247](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23247)).
- Much of this work is aimed at the EIC and FCC.

**Flavor: from LFU tension to $|V_{cb}|$ puzzle (pivoting).**
- The exact-$m_c$ NNLO calculation of B→Xsγ made the radiative sector fully consistent with the SM ([arXiv:2608.29863](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29863)).
- Belle II's $R(D^*)=0.242$ ([arXiv:2609.21947](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21947)) then eased the semileptonic side.
- The tension migrated to inclusive vs exclusive $|V_{cb}|$ ([arXiv:2609.28752](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28752) vs [arXiv:2608.24332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24332)). The inclusive fit quality is poor.

**Hadron spectroscopy (heating).**
- W38: f0(980) was triangulated as a scale-dependent state from three independent methods.
- W39: LHCb observed $\Omega_{cc}^+$ at 8.7σ ([arXiv:2609.21921](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21921)) and a light exotic beauty-strange state at >7σ ([arXiv:2609.25178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25178)). BESIII observed $\eta(2600)$ ([arXiv:2609.25158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25158)).

**Axions and strong CP (steady-to-heating, widening).**
- Lattice: a second $\bar g_0$ determination ([arXiv:2609.04129](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04129)).
- Detection: high-temperature-superconductor and higher-order-mode haloscopes ([arXiv:2609.08144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08144), [arXiv:2609.09717](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09717)).
- Cosmology: JWST limits exclude 0.5–2.5 eV QCD-axion dark matter ([arXiv:2609.10674](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10674)).
- Late-month benchmark: lattice $\mathcal{C}_{\rm QCD}=1.965(35)$ ([arXiv:2609.22698](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22698)).

**ML and statistics tooling (steady, climbing the stack).**
- Agentic standards came first: HEPToolBench ([arXiv:2608.28232](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28232)) and versioned scientific operations ([arXiv:2609.00107](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00107)).
- Autodiff reached the inference layer: IRIS-HEP blueprint ([arXiv:2609.19992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19992)).
- SBI was then applied to a live physics question ([arXiv:2609.26965](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26965)).

## Turning points

- **Pospelov's solar-capture exclusion** ([arXiv:2609.02775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02775)), **generalized** by Nguyen ([arXiv:2609.11833](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11833)). This is the constraint every LZ model must now beat.
- **Neutrino-scattering explanations of LZ ruled out** (Chattaraj [arXiv:2609.10504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10504)).
- **Belle II $R(D^{(*)})$** ([arXiv:2609.21947](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21947)): the first major result of the season to weaken the LFU tension.
- **Atomki reopened.** Cosmic muons reproduce the 17 MeV opening-angle peaks ([arXiv:2609.18383](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18383)).
- **Sterile-neutrino preference down to 2.7σ** in a joint MiniBooNE+MicroBooNE fit, with the two experiments in internal tension ([arXiv:2609.26965](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26965)).
- **Direct Higgs width $\Gamma_H<307$ MeV** from ATLAS ([arXiv:2609.19948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19948)).

## Open questions

- **Which LZ reading survives?** Two decisive tests are pending:
  - an LZ 271–800 keV sideband search ([arXiv:2609.26698](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26698));
  - argon's 336 keV line at DEAP-3600 ([arXiv:2609.15782](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15782)).

  Annual modulation (McCabe [arXiv:2609.04181](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04181)) is also untested. Heavy multiplets may require FCC-hh or a muon collider ([arXiv:2609.26870](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26870)).
- **$|V_{cb}|$:** Is missing theory behind the poor inclusive fit? Watch for new correlated moment measurements.
- **Beauty-strange mass deficit** ([arXiv:2609.25178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25178)): watch for interpretation papers and partner states.
- **17 MeV boson:** does the beam-off data survive the cosmic-muon reinterpretation ([arXiv:2609.18383](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18383))?
- **Collider quantum information:** entanglement is being pitched as a BSM probe ([arXiv:2609.02691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02691)). Against this, standard tomography may fail at NLO ([arXiv:2609.25217](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25217)).
- **Smaller threads to watch:**
  - toponium vs scalar ([arXiv:2609.11641](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11641));
  - $C_9$ via $Z'$ ([arXiv:2609.15026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15026));
  - the spread in event-shape $\alpha_s$ ([arXiv:2609.03002](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03002));
  - BBN vs supercooled transitions, new late in the month ([arXiv:2609.28348](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28348)).
