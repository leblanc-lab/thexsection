# The Cross Section — Rolling Synthesis, Month Ending 2026-08-31

**Overview.** August was bookended by theory and method, with data-driven flavor as its spine. The month opened flavor-heavy (CKM textures, baryogenesis, neutrino numerology), erupted mid-month into a genuine B-physics anomaly flare, then settled into consolidation — nulls firming up while R(D*) and charm CPV refused to fade. Two structural shifts defined the field's plumbing: the collider quantum-information program matured from entanglement into CP and "quantum magic," and an autodiff/GPU tooling wave that began as background hum became the dominant thread by month's end. A late-August CMS toponium confirmation and a lattice strong-CP arc were the sharpest single results. Underneath: EFT/SMEFT hardened into the default collider language, and small-system QGP paid off in Run 3 oxygen data.

## Running themes

**Flavor and CP — the month's spine, now bifurcated.** The arc ran numerology → data eruption → consolidation. Early CKM-texture and baryogenesis model-building (Bigaran et al.'s provocative CKM-alone claim, [arXiv:2608.05269](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05269)) gave way mid-month to a hard data flare: LHCb's model-independent $C_9$ at 4.3–4.8σ ([arXiv:2608.12215](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12215)), the $B\to PP$ SU(3)$_F$-breaking puzzle ([arXiv:2608.11298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11298)), and six CP phenomena in $B^+\to K^+\pi^+\pi^-$ ([arXiv:2608.12614](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12614)). Late month the exclusive channels *consolidated* (SM-consistent $\phi_s$ [arXiv:2608.14119](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14119); LHCb/Belle II nulls) — except that exclusive $|V_{cb}|$ ([arXiv:2608.24332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24332)) leaves $R(D^{(*)})$ with "little overlap" with data. Flavor ends split: nulls firm, LFU and charm CPV tensions live.

**Collider quantum information — heating, evolving beyond entanglement.** The season-long thread became a deployable toolkit and then a discovery program: complete $t\bar t$ density matrices ([arXiv:2607.28761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28761)), POVM tomography under missing neutrinos ([arXiv:2607.28346](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28346)), tau polarimetry ([arXiv:2608.10961](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10961)), CP-dipole observables to 200 TeV ([arXiv:2608.12463](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12463)), and finally "quantum magic" in EW scattering with the weak mixing angle near a magic-minimizing value ([arXiv:2608.19095](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19095)). It also reached low-energy nucleon structure via Compton scattering ([arXiv:2608.05330](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05330)).

**Autodiff/GPU tooling — from hum to dominant.** LLM/automation agents early; by late month the autodiff-everything pattern spanned event generation, likelihood fitting (RABBIT [arXiv:2608.26376](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26376)), amplitude analysis (CTPWA [arXiv:2608.25963](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25963)), photon transport, cosmology, and two differentiable oscillation engines (MANGO [arXiv:2608.13429](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13429), Newtrinos.jl). Gradient-based inference is now the default bet.

**EFT/SMEFT as default language — steady, hardening.** First global top-FCNC fit over all 24 operators ([arXiv:2608.19547](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19547)), NLL EWPO across 210 coefficients ([arXiv:2608.10064](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10064)), aTGC merged-jet bounds, FCC-ee projections. Diboson and top searches now report EFT combinations by default.

**Small-system QGP — heated then paid off.** Run 3 oxygen data landed ($\Upsilon$ suppression [arXiv:2608.00182](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00182), charm $v_2$ splitting, forward jet suppression), culminating in CMS's baryon-to-meson enhancement in O–O ([arXiv:2608.18256](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18256)) — a deconfinement signature in a genuinely small system.

## Turning points

- **CMS single-lepton toponium confirmation** ([arXiv:2608.19546](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19546)) — 6.1σ, moving the $t\bar t$-threshold excess to two independent channels; interpretation contested (NRQCD pseudoscalar vs 2HDM CP-odd Higgs).
- **The B-anomaly flare** (08-13/14) — nothing anticipated an eruption of this size; the month's most argue-worthy data.
- **Strong-CP made concrete** — Blum et al.'s nEDM ([arXiv:2608.22587](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22587)) plus Li et al.'s lattice $\tilde g_{\pi NN}$ ([arXiv:2608.23685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23685)) put strong CP on first-principles footing.
- **Higgs self-coupling matured** — quartic entered phenomenology ([arXiv:2608.16998](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16998)); Braathen et al. recalibrated the $\kappa_3$ bound 6.1→4.9 ([arXiv:2608.25705](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25705)).
- **ATLAS Run 2 Higgs combination** ([arXiv:2608.07332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07332)) — now theory-limited, closing the incremental-update era.

## Open questions

- **R(D*)/LFU tension** ([arXiv:2608.24332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24332)) — persists against a consolidating null landscape; the flavor follow-up to watch.
- **Do the $C_9$/$B\to PP$ anomalies survive?** Two sectors, same direction.
- **Toponium vs elementary scalar** — bound state or top-philic CP-odd Higgs?
- **CMB birefringence** at 4.8σ (3.5σ post-dust, [arXiv:2608.06480](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06480)) — axion Chern-Simons if real.
- **Charm CPV** — SM still short ([arXiv:2608.21212](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21212)).
- **Graviton quantumness** — three independent no-gos ([arXiv:2607.27313](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27313), [arXiv:2608.08803](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08803), [arXiv:2608.17015](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17015)); the wall holds.
- **Unclosed LHC excesses** — 13.6-GeV diphoton, 146-GeV $e\mu$; plus trials-factor/look-elsewhere reckoning ([arXiv:2608.19764](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19764), [arXiv:2608.24872](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24872)).
