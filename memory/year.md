# The Cross Section — Rolling Synthesis, Academic Year Ending 2026-08-31

**Overview.** The year's backbone was flavor and CP physics, which ran a full arc from model-building numerology through a mid-August B-physics anomaly flare into consolidation — nulls firmed, but lepton-flavor-universality and charm-CPV tensions refused to die. Two structural shifts changed how the field works rather than what it found: collider quantum information graduated from entanglement demonstrations into a CP and "quantum magic" discovery program, and an autodiff/GPU wave became the default inference paradigm across generation, fitting, and oscillation analysis. The marquee empirical results were CMS's toponium confirmation and a first-principles lattice attack on strong CP. EFT/SMEFT hardened into the standard collider language, and small-system QGP paid off in Run 3 oxygen data.

## Running themes

**Flavor and CP — the spine, now bifurcated (live).** Trajectory: numerology → data eruption → consolidation. CKM-texture/baryogenesis model-building (Bigaran et al., [arXiv:2608.05269](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05269)) gave way to a hard data flare — model-independent $C_9$ at 4.3–4.8σ ([arXiv:2608.12215](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12215)), the $B\to PP$ SU(3)$_F$-breaking puzzle ([arXiv:2608.11298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11298)), and six CP phenomena in $B^+\to K^+\pi^+\pi^-$ ([arXiv:2608.12614](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12614)). Exclusive channels then consolidated toward the SM ($\phi_s$, [arXiv:2608.14119](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14119)), *except* exclusive $|V_{cb}|$ ([arXiv:2608.24332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24332)), leaving $R(D^{(*)})$ with little data overlap. Net: nulls firm, LFU and charm-CPV tensions live.

**Collider quantum information — heating, evolving.** From deployable toolkit to discovery program: complete $t\bar t$ density matrices ([arXiv:2607.28761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28761)), POVM tomography under missing neutrinos ([arXiv:2607.28346](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28346)), tau polarimetry ([arXiv:2608.10961](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10961)), CP-dipole observables to 200 TeV ([arXiv:2608.12463](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12463)), and "quantum magic" in EW scattering ([arXiv:2608.19095](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19095)); reached nucleon structure via Compton scattering ([arXiv:2608.05330](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05330)).

**Autodiff/GPU tooling — now dominant.** Spans event generation, likelihood fitting (RABBIT, [arXiv:2608.26376](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26376)), amplitude analysis (CTPWA, [arXiv:2608.25963](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25963)), photon transport, cosmology, and two differentiable oscillation engines (MANGO [arXiv:2608.13429](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13429), Newtrinos.jl). Gradient-based inference is the default bet.

**EFT/SMEFT — steady, hardening.** First global top-FCNC fit over all 24 operators ([arXiv:2608.19547](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19547)), NLL EWPO across 210 coefficients ([arXiv:2608.10064](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10064)), FCC-ee projections. Diboson/top searches now report EFT combinations by default.

**Small-system QGP — paid off.** Run 3 oxygen data landed ($\Upsilon$ suppression [arXiv:2608.00182](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00182), charm $v_2$ splitting), culminating in CMS's baryon-to-meson enhancement in O–O ([arXiv:2608.18256](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18256)) — deconfinement in a genuinely small system.

## Turning points

- **CMS single-lepton toponium confirmation** ([arXiv:2608.19546](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19546)) — 6.1σ, moving the $t\bar t$-threshold excess to two channels; interpretation contested (NRQCD pseudoscalar vs 2HDM CP-odd Higgs).
- **The mid-August B-anomaly flare** — unanticipated, the term's most argued-over data.
- **Strong-CP made concrete** — nEDM ([arXiv:2608.22587](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22587)) plus lattice $\tilde g_{\pi NN}$ ([arXiv:2608.23685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23685)) put strong CP on first-principles footing.
- **Higgs self-coupling matured** — quartic entered phenomenology ([arXiv:2608.16998](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16998)); $\kappa_3$ bound recalibrated 6.1→4.9 ([arXiv:2608.25705](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25705)).
- **ATLAS Run 2 Higgs combination** ([arXiv:2608.07332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07332)) — now theory-limited, closing the incremental-update era.

## Open questions

- **R(D*)/LFU tension** ([arXiv:2608.24332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24332)) — persists against a consolidating null landscape; the flavor follow-up to watch.
- **Do the $C_9$/$B\to PP$ anomalies survive?** Two sectors, same direction.
- **Toponium vs elementary scalar** — bound state or top-philic CP-odd Higgs?
- **CMB birefringence** at 4.8σ (3.5σ post-dust, [arXiv:2608.06480](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06480)) — axion Chern-Simons if real.
- **Charm CPV** — SM still short ([arXiv:2608.21212](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21212)).
- **Graviton quantumness** — three independent no-gos ([arXiv:2607.27313](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27313), [arXiv:2608.08803](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08803), [arXiv:2608.17015](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17015)); the wall holds.
- **Unclosed LHC excesses** — 13.6-GeV diphoton, 146-GeV $e\mu$; plus a look-elsewhere reckoning ([arXiv:2608.19764](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19764), [arXiv:2608.24872](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24872)).
