# Rolling Synthesis — Week Ending 2026-07-16

**Overview.** A theory- and precision-dominated week with no single collaboration bombshell but several clean milestones (ATLAS longitudinal-$ZZ$, LHCb $\Sigma_c$ states, a high-precision lattice neutron EDM) punctuating a steady diffusion of model-building. The field's center of gravity moved daily — neutrinos-as-instruments → strong CP → axions/quantum information → flavor precision and $H_0$ — but the connective tissue is the same: extracting new-physics reach from well-understood channels rather than hunting bumps. Two long-running threads (axion diversification, precision flavor) stayed loud all week, while two genuinely new coalescences (quantum information at colliders; gravitational-wave polarimetry) appeared late.

## Running themes

- **Axions & strong CP — heating, fanning out.** The dominant thread. It spans foundational (Williams' argument that strong CP is *contingent* on optional global topology, [arXiv:2607.10272](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10272), against last week's Nelson-Barr/PQ flurry) through the new lattice neutron-EDM benchmark ([arXiv:2607.10606](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10606)) to a scattershot of phenomenology across every mass window: merged-photon LHC bounds ([arXiv:2607.12004](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12004)), gluon-only invisible axions ([arXiv:2607.12025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12025)), a charm-coupled GeV axion solving isospin+quality together ([arXiv:2607.12956](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12956)), and a dark-energy-scale "weak axion" tied to neutrino mixing ([arXiv:2607.13128](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13128)). Nobody is converging.

- **Flavor precision — steady, pivoting.** Focus migrated from the $b\to s\ell\ell$/$C_9$ debate (Bordone's dispersive $B\to K^*\mu\mu$ pulling $C_9$ back toward SM, $<2\sigma$, [arXiv:2607.09458](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09458), continuing Ciuchini July 3) toward CKM structure and QED systematics: a CKM $b$-column blind spot where $\varepsilon_K$ now leads ([arXiv:2607.13136](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13136)), and a factorization theorem for QED in $B\to\tau\nu$ ([arXiv:2607.13144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13144)).

- **Hadron spectroscopy — steady consolidation.** Molecular/compact exotics computed across many frameworks all week, anchored by experiment: LHCb's two new $\Sigma_c$ states ([arXiv:2607.10595](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10595)) and BESIII's $\eta_c\to p\bar p\eta$ ([arXiv:2607.11416](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11416)).

- **Neutrinos as instruments + degeneracy anxieties — fading from peak.** Peaked July 13 (hyperon FSI as neutron-star-EoS probe, [arXiv:2607.09273](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09273); Earth tomography). The recurring "fake-signal" worry resurfaced as Earth's matter profile faking a $3\sigma$ CPT signal at DUNE baselines ([arXiv:2607.10417](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10417)).

- **Cosmology / dark energy — culminating.** The scattered dark-energy work of prior weeks was refereed into a verdict: the "$H_0$ World Cup" ([arXiv:2607.13282](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13282), [arXiv:2607.13283](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13283)) ranks fourteen solutions, with early dark energy and early modified gravity winning.
