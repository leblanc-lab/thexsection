# The Cross Section — Weekly Synthesis, week ending 2026-07-31

**Overview.** The week's spine was flavor precision tightening from several directions at once — lattice, charm/kaon measurements, and a hadronic-amplitude anomaly that grew teeth — while a parallel automation wave saw LLM agents move from checking work to orchestrating full pipelines across pheno, fits, and now nuclear theory. Cosmology kept its month-long defect/phase-transition GW drumbeat going, direct-detection experiments consolidated into a genuine solar-neutrino program, and IceCube delivered a Galactic milestone. Two conversations turned sharply: a top-down attempt to explain CKM structure, and a fast theoretical rebuttal to the "quantized gravity from GWs" idea.

## Running themes

**Flavor precision — heating up, on two tracks.** The *measurement* track tightened the CKM ladder: the first lattice QCD+QED SD correction to $R_{e/\mu}$ for $\pi/K$ ([arXiv:2607.22358](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22358), the SM input PIONEER/NA62 will quote), BESIII's record $D\to\pi\ell\nu$ / $|V_{cd}|$ ([arXiv:2607.23945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23945)), the NA62 golden mode propagated into $b\to s$ new physics ([arXiv:2607.24494](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24494)), and a first $B^0\to K^0_S\tau^+\tau^-$ limit ([arXiv:2607.25607](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25607)). The *structure* track is newer and speculative: a geometric CKM reparameterization ([arXiv:2607.26681](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26681)) followed a day later by Arkani-Hamed et al.'s $\pi/8$ Yukawa textures ([arXiv:2607.27315](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27315)) — two swings at the same empirical pattern, the latter with falsifiable unitarity-triangle predictions.

**Agents at the console — heating up, spreading.** Building past LQCDMaster (July 17), the week added amplitude-as-program search (805× speedup, [arXiv:2607.21629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21629)), SMEFT-Pheno-Agent's full MadGraph→Delphes chain ([arXiv:2607.22331](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22331)), agentic SFitter recasting ([arXiv:2607.22813](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22813)), and CLVisc Agent for viscous-hydro heavy-ion runs ([arXiv:2607.27822](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27822)). Reproducibility/audit manifests are the explicit selling point.

**Defect & phase-transition GW — steady drumbeat.** Global $\mathbb{Z}_N$ strings ([arXiv:2607.21701](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21701)), global-monopole annihilation ([arXiv:2607.22481](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22481)), CosmoLattice 2.0 ([arXiv:2607.24978](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24978)), GUT-scale biased walls/monopoles ([arXiv:2607.27158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27158)), and repeated domain-wall spectrum work ([arXiv:2607.25073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25073), [arXiv:2607.28214](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28214)) — the tooling is maturing and IR artifacts are being cleaned up.

**Direct detection as neutrino observatory — new coalescence.** SNuDD ([arXiv:2607.22817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22817)), invisible-$\nu_2$-decay bounds from CEνNS rivaling SNO ([arXiv:2607.24584](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24584)), and a sub-percent solar-flux framework ([arXiv:2607.24421](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24421)). The neutrino floor is now a physics program.

**Collider EW/Higgs precision — steady.** EFT/aQGC is the default recast language (νSMEFT [arXiv:2607.21711](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21711), ALP pairs [arXiv:2607.21712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21712), ATLAS EW $\gamma\gamma jj$ at 6.2σ [arXiv:2607.25869](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25869)). Boosted di-$\tau$ reconstruction, debuting in the July 27 boosted $H\to\tau\tau$ evidence ([arXiv:2607.22193](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22193)), became standard kit across ATLAS's July 31 $\tau$ slate. CMS set a new mass benchmark, $m_H=125.13\pm0.15$ GeV ([arXiv:2607.28396](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28396)).

## Turning points

- **$B\to VV$ pushes charmless SU(3)$_F$ past $7\sigma$** ([arXiv:2607.27202](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27202)) — a hadronic-amplitude anomaly with deliberately minimal theory input, a distinct corner from the FCNC modes the week circled.
- **The GW-quantumness conversation flipped in one day.** Blas et al.'s coherent-GW$\to\gamma\gamma$ decay claim ([arXiv:2607.24930](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24930)) was answered by Bao et al.'s general no-go ([arXiv:2607.27313](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27313)), closing off "quantized gravity from GWs" proposals.
- **IceCube's 5.7σ Galactic-plane neutrino emission** ([arXiv:2607.25966](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25966)) — the constructive counterpart to the ongoing UHE ANITA/KM3NeT-vs-IceCube tension.

## Open questions

- Does ~30% SU(3)-breaking rescue the $B\to VV$ $7\sigma$ discrepancy? (flagged by the authors themselves.)
- The Hubble tension stayed hard: Roy Choudhury's $6.85\sigma$ ($H_0=73.3$) survived the corrected Pantheon+/DES reanalysis ([arXiv:2607.24443](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24443)).
- Auger quantified the muon puzzle into a demanded 2.8–4.6× hadronic-energy amplification ([arXiv:2607.22323](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22323)) — watch for hadronic-model responses.
- Will the $\pi/8$ / geometric CKM patterns yield testable deviations or fade as numerology?
- Small-scale-structure DM: a ~2.4σ dwarf central-density diversity tension with CDM ([arXiv:2607.27316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27316)).
