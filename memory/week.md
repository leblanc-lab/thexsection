# The Cross Section: rolling synthesis, week ending 2026-09-28

The LZ 248 keV recoil dominated the listings for most of the week, at five to seven papers a day. Each day the model-building organized itself differently, and by 09-28 there were no LZ papers at all. The firmest results came from elsewhere:

- LHCb reported a light, narrow beauty-strange exotic.
- A lattice value for the axion-photon coupling appeared.
- An inclusive $|V_{cb}|$ anchor was published.
- A careful SBI reanalysis cut the MiniBooNE/MicroBooNE sterile-neutrino preference to 2.7σ.

Infrastructure grew steadily underneath: fast generative simulation, SBI tooling and multiloop QCD. A new cosmology thread links supercooled phase transitions, BBN deuterium and gravitational-wave detection. No digests were provided for 09-26 and 09-27.

## Running themes

**LZ recoil: fading.** The interpretations moved through a clear sequence:
1. Confining-sector and composite inelastic readings (09-22): Sannino et al. [arXiv:2609.24988](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24988) and the hyperfine picture of [arXiv:2609.23477](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23477).
2. Elastic composite form factors (09-23): [arXiv:2609.25723](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25723).
3. Engineering around solar-capture bounds (09-24).
4. Escaping capture by mass scale, from 250 MeV to $10^{11}$ GeV (09-25). This included the 11–20 TeV inert doublet of Murayama et al. [arXiv:2609.28819](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28819).

After that, nothing appeared on 09-28. Two counterweights matter for future digests:
- Maier et al. [arXiv:2609.26870](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26870) argue that thermal electroweak multiplets fitting the event lie beyond the HL-LHC.
- Kalita [arXiv:2609.29462](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29462) turns the event into an $f_{\rm PBH}$ bound.

Watch whether the silence persists or new LZ data revives the thread.

**Fast simulation, SBI and trigger ML: heating up.** Several strands advanced together:
- **CaloClouds.** Cross-geometry transfer needs only about 100 target showers [arXiv:2609.28661](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28661). A review follows in [arXiv:2609.30403](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30403).
- **Parnassus.** The group moved from ALEPH→SLD transfer [arXiv:2609.25061](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25061) to a public CLD surrogate for FCC-ee [arXiv:2609.30775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30775).
- **SBI.** A second paper builds exact unbinned Asimov datasets [arXiv:2609.30196](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30196).
- **Trigger ML.** Work on FPGA-scale distillation [arXiv:2609.26899](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26899) and [arXiv:2609.28144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28144) sits alongside the CMS HL-LHC tracking cluster.
- **Caution.** Buss et al.'s Jevons'-paradox note [arXiv:2609.24306](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24306) warns that faster simulation may raise total energy use.

**Precision QCD and $t\bar tW$: steady.** Several multiloop results landed:
- Blümlein et al. extended the three-loop massive form factors [arXiv:2609.22034](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22034) with the method paper [arXiv:2609.24279](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24279).
- The four-loop massive cusp appeared [arXiv:2609.23247](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23247).
- Pozzoli's two-loop $t\bar tW$ amplitude [arXiv:2609.24910](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24910) was followed within a week by off-shell MiNLO $t\bar tW^+$ [arXiv:2609.31296](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31296).
- Gardi's Hidden Region Finder [arXiv:2609.29442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29442) is a likely infrastructure staple.

**Phase transitions and GWs: new and heating.** BBN deuterium disfavors NANOGrav-compatible supercooled transitions if baryogenesis came first ([arXiv:2609.28348](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28348), [arXiv:2609.27978](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27978)). This makes baryogenesis timing a GW observable. Work on 09-28 added two pieces:
- an all-temperature resummation scheme [arXiv:2609.30366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30366);
- a kHz–MHz detector proposal, MAGGIE [arXiv:2609.30318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30318).

**Collider quantum information: steady.** Three papers show the field maturing in both directions:
- **Ambition.** Afik et al. propose certifying Nonlocal Advantage of Quantum Coherence in τ pairs at Belle II and FCC-ee [arXiv:2609.25214](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25214).
- **Caution.** Naive $H\to4\ell$ tomography can return an unphysical (non-PSD) operator at NLO [arXiv:2609.25217](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25217).
- **Use as a search tool.** $t\bar t$ spin correlation adds reach on top-philic invisible mediators [arXiv:2609.31425](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31425).

## Turning points

- **LHCb beauty-strange state** [arXiv:2609.25178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25178). It is seen at >7σ, is light and narrow, and shows a mass deficit versus the quark model. LHCb bills it as the first nonconventional single-beauty hadron. BESIII's η(2600) [arXiv:2609.25158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25158) and the Ω_b⁻ lifetime [arXiv:2609.25370](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25370) landed the same day.
- **Axion-photon coupling from the lattice.** Lin et al. [arXiv:2609.22698](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22698) find $\mathcal{C}_{\rm QCD}=1.965(35)$, resolving the competing NLO ChPT results.
- **ATLAS top-philic $Z'$ null** [arXiv:2609.24950](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24950). It excludes $m_{Z'}<1150$ GeV for $c_t=1$, constraining the vector side of the toponium debate. Toponium itself is fading into feasibility footnotes ([arXiv:2609.23576](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23576), [arXiv:2609.28223](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28223)).
- **Sterile-neutrino preference weakened.** Woodward et al. [arXiv:2609.26965](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26965) find the 3+1 preference drops to 2.7σ, and the two experiments disagree at ≥2.5σ within the model.
- **Inclusive $|V_{cb}|$ anchor.** Fael et al. [arXiv:2609.28752](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28752) obtain $|V_{cb}|=(42.32\pm0.30)\times10^{-3}$.
- **New bound shape from neutron stars.** Yin et al. [arXiv:2609.30335](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30335) exclude a finite interval of DM triggering rates, where earlier survival arguments gave one-sided limits.

## Open questions

- **Decisive LZ test.** A 271–800 keV sideband search would test inelastic $\delta\gtrsim330$ keV [arXiv:2609.26698](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26698). Nuclear form-factor uncertainty [arXiv:2609.24764](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24764) sits under every exclusion.
- **$|V_{cb}|$ and $R(D^{(*)})$.** The inclusive value stands against the exclusive determination behind the $R(D^{(*)})$ tension ([arXiv:2608.24332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24332)). The global moment fit is poor, and the authors call for new correlated moment measurements.
- **Sterile-neutrino DM viability.** Three papers point in different directions:
  - Drewes et al. [arXiv:2609.28457](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28457) find a closed νMSM region within SHiP and LHC reach.
  - Li et al. [arXiv:2609.29911](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29911) claim plasma effects kill collisional freeze-in.
  - Gorbunov et al. [arXiv:2609.31340](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31340) show active oscillations can erase the lepton asymmetry needed for resonant production.
- **Interpreting the LHCb state.** Watch for molecular or tetraquark readings of [arXiv:2609.25178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25178).
- **Small systems.** A HERA-tuned IP-Glasma underpredicts suppression in O+O [arXiv:2609.29886](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29886), pointing to final-state energy loss. The oxygen runs are also becoming a short-range-correlation probe ([arXiv:2609.27566](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27566)).
