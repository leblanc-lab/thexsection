# Rolling Synthesis — Week Ending 2026-07-17

**Overview.** A theory- and tool-heavy week bracketed by hardware: it opened with phenomenologists reclaiming the floor (neutrinos-as-instruments, a widening dark-sector zoo, axions everywhere) and closed with the instrumentation people putting real-time reconstruction and silicon QC back on the table. The connective tissue was a steady swing from *model-building* toward *constraining and refereeing* — culminating in a Hubble-tension bake-off and several benchmark-killing exclusions. Two genuinely new coalescences emerged mid-week: quantum information as a routine collider observable, and gravitational-wave polarimetry as a gravity-theory discriminator. Precision flavor and precision QCD machinery hummed underneath throughout.

## Running themes

- **Dark matter: from zoo to gauntlet (turning).** Early week saw baroque candidate proliferation (heavy charged relics [arXiv:2607.09845](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09845), quark pellets, Migdal reactor DM). By week's end the mood was exclusionary: strongest-yet DM–neutrino scattering bound from Lyman-α ([arXiv:2607.15020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15020)), refined boosted-DM attenuation, and a killed GUT benchmark (see below). Watch whether model-building rebounds.
- **Axions across every altitude (heating, then easing).** Peaked July 13–16 — merged-photon LHC bounds ([arXiv:2607.12004](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12004)), charm-coupled GeV axion tying isospin + quality problems ([arXiv:2607.12956](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12956)), coherence probes, "weak axion" as dark energy ([arXiv:2607.13128](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13128)). Quieter by July 17. The quality-problem framing recurs as the connective thread.
- **Quantum information at colliders (heating).** Went from novelty to working vocabulary in three days: τ⁻τ⁺γ decoherence/entanglement ([arXiv:2607.12015](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12015), cross-listed twice), entanglement markers in Z′-mediated tt̄, spin-resolved ALP decay. Spin and entanglement now appear as ordinary observable columns.
- **Precision flavor (steady).** July 13's b→sℓℓ / C₉ debate handed off to July 16's CKM-structure and rare-kaon/B focus — a blind-spot in b-column rescaling ([arXiv:2607.13136](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13136)), QED-corrected B→τν ([arXiv:2607.13144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13144)), the Belle II B⁺→K⁺νν excess.
- **Real-time reconstruction & instrumentation (heating at week's end).** Belle II GNN calorimeter trigger commissioned ([arXiv:2607.09347](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09347)) early; LHCb's production Kalman filter on 500 GPUs ([arXiv:2607.14793](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14793)) late — the ML/real-time wave reaching the trigger, plus a dense HL-LHC silicon-QC cluster on July 17.
- **Neutrinos as instruments (steady→fading).** Strong July 13 (hyperon puzzle via FSI, Earth tomography, AGN spikes); later folded into constraint mode.

## Turning points

- **H₀ World Cup** ([arXiv:2607.13282](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13282), [arXiv:2607.13283](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13283)): fourteen tension solutions in one framework; early dark energy / early modified gravity win, shifting H₀ toward 70 and cutting the SH0ES gap to ~2.5–3.6σ. The reference bake-off going forward.
- **N-naturalness benchmark excluded** ([arXiv:2607.14243](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14243)): first experimental search for the neutrino tower kills the N=10⁴ GUT-unification (Majorana) benchmark — the framework's tidy selling point.
- **Strong CP, quantified and questioned same morning** (July 14): high-precision lattice neutron-EDM coefficient ([arXiv:2607.10606](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10606)) landing against Williams' argument that the problem is *contingent* on optional global topology ([arXiv:2607.10272](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10272)) — a foundational counterpoint to last week's "solving strong CP" flurry.
- **C₉ pulled back toward SM** ([arXiv:2607.09458](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09458)): data-driven dispersive B→K*μμ reading, tension ≤2σ, extending Ciuchini et al.'s July 3 hadronic-effects argument.
- **AGN-spike neutrino-DM bounds deflated** ([arXiv:2607.08904](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08904), Farzan): limits hinge on mediator mass — a cautionary result landing the same day as a paper deriving signals from the same physics.

- **Belle II B⁺→K⁺νν excess** — explicable only with a light right-handed neutrino under LNV SMEFT ([arXiv:2607.13142](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13142)); needs follow-up.
