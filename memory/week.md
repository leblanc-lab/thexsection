# The Cross Section — Week ending 2026-08-26

A collider-driven start (a confirmed toponium signal and a run of EFT fits) gave way to a methods- and precision-heavy back half. The clearest sustained arc is strong CP: a lattice nEDM extraction and, one day later, the θ-induced πNN coupling that sits directly above it — two rungs of the same calculation landing in sequence. Flavor split in two directions this week: the mesonic/baryonic $b\to s\ell\ell$ and LFV fronts kept returning SM-consistent nulls, while the $R(D^*)$/LFU tension refused to soften. Underneath everything, EFT-as-default-language and the autodiff/ML-tooling wave both kept spreading into new corners.

## Running themes

**Strong CP on the lattice — heating.** Blum et al.'s nEDM extraction from the topological-charge density ([arXiv:2608.22587](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22587), 08-25) confirmed strong CP is real ($|\bar\theta|\lesssim10^{-11}$); the very next day Li et al. gave the first direct lattice $\tilde g_{\pi NN}=0.0306(56)(124)\,\bar\theta$ ([arXiv:2608.23685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23685)), the hadronic coupling one rung up, consistent with the $n$–$p$-mass-splitting estimate. A coherent, method-demonstrating thread to watch for extension to other CP-violating operators.

**EFT as the default collider language — steady, now diagnostic.** aTGC merged-jet bounds ([arXiv:2608.18245](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18245)) opened the week, followed by CMS's first global top-FCNC fit across all 24 SMEFT operators ([arXiv:2608.19547](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19547)) and universal two-loop four-fermion running ([arXiv:2608.19316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19316)). By 08-25 Englert et al. used SMEFT diagnostically: single-top/$tH$ rate shifts form a weak-dipole+TGC pattern that no minimal UV multiplet reproduces ([arXiv:2608.21510](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21510)) — the framework moving from limit-setting to model-discrimination.

**Flavor: nulls consolidate, LFU tension persists — divergent.** LHCb's first $\Lambda_b\to\Lambda(1520)\mu\mu$ angular analysis ([arXiv:2608.21082](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21082)) and Belle II's $\tau\to\mu\gamma$ limit ([arXiv:2608.23009](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23009)) both landed on the SM. Against that quiet backdrop, Chen et al.'s exclusive $|V_{cb}|=(39.18\pm0.47)\times10^{-3}$ gave SM $R(D)=0.3069$, $R(D^*)=0.2548$ with "little overlap" with data ([arXiv:2608.24332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24332)) — the LFU anomaly still refusing to soften. Charm CPV likewise stayed unexplained (Pich et al., [arXiv:2608.21212](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21212)).

**Dark-matter detection methodology — steady, broadening.** A run of new hardware/handles: quantum-dot "barcodes" for DM-electron operators ([arXiv:2608.18204](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18204)), POLONAISE's levitated-ferromagnet ultraheavy search with leading composite-DM limits ([arXiv:2608.20464](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20464)), an order-of-magnitude HFIR $n\to n'$ improvement ([arXiv:2608.21120](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21120)), and Moroi's qubit-sensing review ([arXiv:2608.20745](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20745)). The emphasis stayed on instruments over results.

**ML/autodiff tooling wave — steady, spreading.** Differentiable methods reached GW cosmology ([arXiv:2608.18281](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18281)), fast-sim/Boltzmann cosmology, GPU-resident event generation ([arXiv:2608.21338](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21338)), and — newly — amplitude analysis, with S-matrix-informed neural networks doing a first dispersive pole extraction ([arXiv:2608.23750](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23750), [arXiv:2608.23738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23738)).

## Turning points

- **Toponium confirmed.** CMS's 6.1σ single-lepton $t\bar t$-threshold excess ([arXiv:2608.19546](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19546)) independently corroborates the dilepton result; a color-singlet pseudoscalar toponium reading. Matsuoka's elementary-scalar (top-philic 2HDM CP-odd Higgs) alternative ([arXiv:2608.19651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19651)) frames the open interpretive fork.
- **Strong CP confirmed and hadronically pinned** ([arXiv:2608.22587](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22587) → [arXiv:2608.23685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23685)), as above.
- **Significance accounting matured.** Reisch et al.'s search-budget/trials-factor framework ([arXiv:2608.19764](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19764)) and Zhang et al.'s procedural look-elsewhere reframing ([arXiv:2608.24872](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24872)) together sharpen what a "5σ" buys.

## Open questions

- **Toponium: bound state or elementary scalar?** Multiple channels now agree on the bump; the NRQCD-pseudoscalar vs 2HDM-CP-odd-Higgs (and baryogenesis) question is open ([arXiv:2608.19546](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19546), [arXiv:2608.19651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19651)).
- **$R(D^*)$/LFU** stays in tension even as other flavor observables null out ([arXiv:2608.24332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24332)). Watch for follow-up combinations.
- **Charm CPV** remains beyond the SM's data-driven reach, unexplained not resolved ([arXiv:2608.21212](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21212)).
- **Two unclosed LHC excesses**: the 13.6-GeV CMS diphoton (ALP interpretation, [arXiv:2608.18506](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18506)) and the 146-GeV $e\mu$ excess ([arXiv:2608.18738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18738)) — loose corners with concrete follow-up strategies.
- **$g_A$ radiative shift**: Tomalak et al.'s $\Delta(1232)$ contribution nulls it ([arXiv:2608.22845](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22845)), feeding the lattice-vs-experiment debate.
