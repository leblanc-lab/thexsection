# The Cross Section: Rolling synthesis, week ending 2026-10-19

This week the field leaned toward deflating anomalies. A run of clean nulls and input checks landed on the Standard Model side: IceCube decoherence, the solar-calibrated gallium null, lattice and sum-rule HVP, a PDF-based reading of the $t\bar t$ threshold, and a foreground scan of the 20 GeV halo excess. Flavor went the other way: the anomalies there persisted or sharpened rather than dissolving. LZ 248 keV model-building peaked at five papers on 10-14 and then fell to zero by 10-19, while the argument moved from particle models to astrophysical inputs. Precision QCD had its clearest success, with two independent $\mathcal O(g^6)$ hot Yang-Mills pressures matching the lattice. The single real counter-current is NA61/SHINE's unexplained charged-to-neutral kaon excess.

## Running themes

**LZ event: from model-building to astrophysics (fading in volume, shifting in content).** The daily paper count went 5 → 1 → 1 → 0. The solar-neutrino bound turned from a veto into a test. Di Mauro's $U(1)_B$ model ([arXiv:2610.14188](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14188)) proposes a dedicated IceCube solar-capture analysis. Graham et al. ([arXiv:2610.21041](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21041)) then put the burden on the high-velocity halo tail and suggested paleodetector tests that don't rely on xenon. Abazajian et al. ([arXiv:2610.23713](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23713)) undercut any shared origin with the 20 GeV halo excess. Some holdouts still ignore solar capture ([arXiv:2610.15557](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15557), [arXiv:2610.18234](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18234)).

**Flavor tensions: persisting, and SU(3)$_F$ is the pressure point (heating).**
- $R(D^{(*)})$ world averages remain in tension despite September's Belle II easing ([arXiv:2610.16617](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16617)). Parameter-free form factors put $R(D^*)$ 2.8σ from experiment ([arXiv:2610.17289](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17289)).
- The $B\to DP$ fit needs ~60% SU(3)$_F$ breaking at >5σ ([arXiv:2610.20611](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20611)). SU(3) breaking also dominates the normalization error in LHCb's first $\Xi_b^-\to\Xi^-\mu\mu$ ([arXiv:2610.22452](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22452)).
- $|V_{cb}|$ picked up an orthogonal route via charged-current DIS ([arXiv:2610.18961](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18961)). Crivellin et al. ruled out using it as an escape for $C_9$ ([arXiv:2610.22490](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22490)).

**Anomalies meeting their inputs (heating; a recurring digest frame).** Several anomaly claims were tested against their inputs this week, and most weakened:
- IceCube's decoherence null ([arXiv:2610.14144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14144)).
- The gallium null ([arXiv:2610.22219](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22219)).
- ETMC HVP at ~1% on the lattice side ([arXiv:2610.22224](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22224)), backed by a sum-rule estimate ([arXiv:2610.20945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20945)).
- A neutrino-anomaly review finding tensions weakened but none with an uncontested explanation ([arXiv:2610.23474](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23474)).
- Xenon solar-neutrino data constraining complex-NSI readings of NOvA–T2K ([arXiv:2610.18971](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18971)).

**Exotics vs. threshold dynamics (steady, contested).** Swanson's no-exotics di-$J/\psi$ fit ([arXiv:2610.15248](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15248)) set the null hypothesis and cut against the previous week's bound $cc\bar c\bar c$ claim. Garcia Tecocoatzi et al. still predict detectable $4P$ all-charm states ([arXiv:2610.22914](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22914)). Molecular readings continue for the 10.513 GeV $X_b$ ([arXiv:2610.19066](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19066)) and $\chi_{c1}(4010)$ ([arXiv:2610.23240](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23240)). The $t\bar t$ threshold falls under the same theme: Ball et al. ([arXiv:2610.21479](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21479)) give a quieter, $m_t$-correlated reading than CMS's toponium headline.

**N³LO and thermal precision (heating).** Two papers added N³LO building blocks: three-loop masters with one off-shell leg ([arXiv:2610.20820](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20820)) and three-loop rapidity beam kernels ([arXiv:2610.22533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22533)). Both $g^6$ pressures ([arXiv:2610.23675](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23675), [arXiv:2610.23676](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23676)) followed the 10-12 cold-QED N³LO result. Unlike earlier "next order" results, which mostly exposed error budgets, the $g^6$ pair validated the dimensional-reduction EFT directly.

## Turning points

- **Planck-suppressed decoherence excluded** for $E^n$ with $0\le n\le3$, the first Planck-scale exclusion at $n=3$ ([arXiv:2610.14144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14144)).
- **MadNIS becomes MadGraph's LO default** ([arXiv:2610.17177](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17177)), with speed-ups up to 250×. Neural importance sampling is now infrastructure. Agent-evaluation benchmarks matured in parallel ([arXiv:2610.18454](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18454), [arXiv:2610.14024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14024)).
- **Gallium anomaly removed as a sterile-neutrino argument** using the Sun as a calibrated source ([arXiv:2610.22219](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22219)).
- **Complete $\mathcal O(g^6)$ hot Yang-Mills pressure** from two groups, closing a gap of more than two decades. Both agree with the lattice ([arXiv:2610.23675](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23675), [arXiv:2610.23676](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23676)).
- **The $|V_{cb}|$/CKM-unitarity explanation of the $C_9$ deficit is closed** without fine-tuning ([arXiv:2610.22490](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22490)).
- **SU(3)$_F$ breaking in $B\to DP$ grows to ~60%** once CP observables enter ([arXiv:2610.20611](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20611)). The charm-side echo of August's $B\to PP$ puzzle has become a statement about the symmetry expansion itself.

## Open questions

- **$t\bar t$ threshold.** CMS reports 6.1σ, while the PDF-fit reading is just over 2σ at 13 TeV with a strong $\Delta$–$m_t$ correlation. The toponium vs. CP-odd-scalar question is unresolved. Watch for joint-$m_t$ treatments and toponium-as-CP-probe work ([arXiv:2610.15493](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15493)).
- **LZ event.** Does anyone run the IceCube solar-capture analysis, or produce halo simulations with the tail Graham et al. require?
- **SU(3)$_F$.** Is 60% breaking a parametrization artifact or a failure of the expansion? Watch the effect on SU(3)-normalized rare-decay results.
- **$R(D^{(*)})$ and inclusive/exclusive $|V_{cb}|$.** Both are still open. The Frascati workshop summary ([arXiv:2610.19917](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19917)) is the current status reference.
- **NA61/SHINE kaon isospin excess** in Ar+Sc ([arXiv:2610.23498](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23498)). The excess is consistent from 2.6 to 200 GeV, has no known mechanism, and carries sizeable uncertainties. It needs independent confirmation.
- **Hadronic inputs.** The lattice vs. pre-CMD3 data-driven HVP gap is widening. $\sigma_{\pi N}$ ([arXiv:2610.20362](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20362)) remains the hedge in last week's Bao et al. analysis. The pion gluon at small $x$ is disputed against Fantômas ([arXiv:2610.19415](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19415)).
- **Phase-transition GWs.** Expanding-universe amplitudes come out ~10× below Minkowski simulations ([arXiv:2610.14806](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14806)). Watch for the previous week's phase-transition cluster to respond.
