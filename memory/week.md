# Rolling synthesis — week ending 2026-07-30

The week's center of gravity was **flavor precision**, which built steadily from the NA62 kaon interpretation through record BESIII charm measurements and culminated in a >7σ SU(3)$_F$ anomaly in charmless $B\to VV$ — a hadronic puzzle distinct from the rare-FCNC modes the field usually watches. Cosmology's dominant story was destructive: two "walls" against Hubble-tension solvers early in the week, then a fresh 6.85σ distance-ladder result that refused to soften. Alongside these, three durable structural trends kept surfacing — LLM/agent automation moving from checking to orchestrating pipelines, direct-detection experiments reinventing themselves as neutrino observatories, and an all-month drumbeat of defect-network and phase-transition gravitational-wave modeling. Two clean observations (LHCb's 32 Tbps real-time processing; IceCube's Galactic-plane neutrinos) served as milestones.

## Running themes

- **Flavor precision (heating up, fast).** A ladder tightened all week: Allwicher et al. [arXiv:2607.24494](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24494) propagated the NA62 $K^+\to\pi^+\nu\bar\nu$ result into $B\to K\nu\bar\nu$/$B_s\to\mu\mu$; BESIII pinned $D\to\pi\ell\nu$ and $|V_{cd}|$ ([arXiv:2607.23945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23945)); lattice QCD+QED delivered the $R_{e/\mu}$ SM benchmark for PIONEER/NA62 ([arXiv:2607.22358](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22358)). Belle II added a first $B^0\to K^0_S\tau^+\tau^-$ limit ([arXiv:2607.25607](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25607)). The week closed on Bhattacharya et al.'s $B\to VV$ SU(3)$_F$ fit reaching >7σ ([arXiv:2607.27202](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27202)).

- **Hubble tension (steady, unresolved, tilting against solvers).** Poulin et al. [arXiv:2607.20635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20635) and Sabogal et al. [arXiv:2607.21244](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21244) erected BBN-deuterium and "shape" walls against EDE and late-time fixes; Roy Choudhury [arXiv:2607.24443](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24443) then pushed $H_0=73.3$ at 6.85σ, uncooled by the Pantheon+/DES reanalysis.

- **Automation & ML tooling (heating up).** The LQCDMaster lineage produced amplitude-search agents ([arXiv:2607.21629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21629)), a full SMEFT collider agent ([arXiv:2607.22331](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22331)), agentic SFitter ([arXiv:2607.22813](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22813)), plus jet-foundation-model scaling laws ([arXiv:2607.23377](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23377)) and symbolic-regression TMD extraction ([arXiv:2607.24710](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24710)). Reproducibility is now the explicit selling point.

- **Direct detection as a neutrino observatory (emerging).** SNuDD ([arXiv:2607.22817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22817)), a first invisible-$\nu_2$-decay bound from CEνNS rivaling SNO ([arXiv:2607.24584](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24584)), and the sub-percent flux framework ([arXiv:2607.24421](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24421)) turned the neutrino floor into a physics program — sharpened by Bisal et al.'s finding that Higgsino–nucleon scattering can drop below that floor ([arXiv:2607.20588](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20588)).

- **Defect networks & phase-transition GWs (steady drumbeat).** Global strings ([arXiv:2607.21701](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21701)), monopole annihilation ([arXiv:2607.22481](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22481)), CosmoLattice 2.0 ([arXiv:2607.24978](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24978)) with a domain-wall IR fix ([arXiv:2607.25073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25073)), and GUT-scale biased walls + monopoles ([arXiv:2607.27158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27158)) kept this thread alive, much of it NANOGrav-facing.

## Turning points

- **LHCb 32 Tbps real-time processing** ([arXiv:2607.21492](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21492)) — the production endpoint of the month's real-time-reconstruction wave; a new throughput standard.
- **IceCube Galactic-plane neutrinos at 5.7σ** ([arXiv:2607.25966](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25966)) — Galactic multi-messenger astronomy now robust; the constructive counterpart to the UHE anomaly.
- **Auger quantifies the muon puzzle** ([arXiv:2607.22323](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22323)) — an $X_{\max}$ deficit recast as a demanded 2.8–4.6× hadronic-energy-fraction amplification at ~100 TeV.
- **ATLAS EW $\gamma\gamma jj$ observation at 6.2σ** ([arXiv:2607.25869](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25869)) — next channel in the electroweak/aQGC dim-8 program.

## Open questions

- Does ~30% SU(3)$_F$-breaking rescue the >7σ $B\to VV$ discrepancy ([arXiv:2607.27202](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27202)), or is it real new physics in hadronic amplitudes?
- The UHE-neutrino tension (7.5σ ANITA/KM3NeT-vs-IceCube) persists; KM3-230213A's DM-decay origin is disfavored ($p\simeq0.13$) but not excluded ([arXiv:2607.20608](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20608)). Glashow-resonance flavor tagging ([arXiv:2607.26128](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26128)) is a new handle to watch.
- With the $H_0$ walls holding and 6.85σ standing, is very-early dark energy during BBN a genuine hint? Neutrino-mass bounds also loosen dramatically ($<0.056$→$<0.41$ eV) once late-Universe data is dropped ([arXiv:2607.24742](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24742)).
- Watch for follow-ups: ATLAS $HH\to bb\tau\tau$ upward pull ($\mu=2.6^{+1.4}_{-1.0}$, [arXiv:2607.26879](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26879)); the $X_{17}$/ATOMKI lifetime tension ([arXiv:2607.22102](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22102)).
