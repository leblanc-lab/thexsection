# The Cross Section: rolling synthesis, week ending 2026-11-10

The week's center of gravity was the $t\bar t$ threshold. An NNLO toponium cross section, a 2HDM interference generator and a perturbative $\alpha_s/v$ treatment all appeared within six days, each aimed at the toponium-vs-CP-odd-Higgs reading of the CMS excess. The HVP dispute gained a new BaBar ISR ππ measurement that is in tension with BaBar's own 2009 result, followed quickly by generator-level QED work on radiative return. Light-ion collisions moved from geometry inputs to nuclear-force data. A methodological habit spread from BSM identifiability into lattice and perturbative QCD: papers stating where their own fits fail. Several long-running anomalies (X17, DAMA, gallium, CMB birefringence via neutrinos) lost parameter space or proposed explanations. LEP's $N_\nu$ was re-examined, with a caveat attached.

## Running themes

**$t\bar t$ threshold, bound state vs scalar (heating).**
- NNLO pseudoscalar toponium gives 4.87 pb at 13 TeV, with the error now dominated by the Coulomb-scale dependence of the matrix elements ([arXiv:2611.03782](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03782)).
- An NLO 2HDM–QCD interference tool with spin correlations exists in POWHEG ([arXiv:2611.08388](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08388)).
- A coloured scalar and a vector-like fermion are EFT-degenerate below threshold ([arXiv:2611.08459](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08459)).
- Re's perturbative $\alpha_s/v$ reweighting agrees with data ([arXiv:2611.09299](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09299)).
- Signal–background interference raises low-$\tan\beta$ $H/A$ rates by up to tens of percent ([arXiv:2611.09666](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09666)).
- Open-data $m_{t\bar t}$ resolution reaches 30 GeV, limited by the $\nu\bar\nu$ approximation ([arXiv:2611.09797](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09797)).
- Drift: the bound-state reading may need less new machinery than assumed, while the scalar reading needs interference handled properly.

**HVP via ISR ππ (heating).** BaBar's full-sample re-measurement separates pions from muons by angular distribution and gives $a_\mu^{\pi\pi}=525.9\pm3.5\pm3.8$, about 8×10⁻¹⁰ from the combined value ([arXiv:2611.04596](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04596); also [arXiv:2611.04621](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04621)). KKMC and SHERPA agree to ≲0.04% at first order and add a new rule for radiative return, though pions remain point-like scalars ([arXiv:2611.07067](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07067)). A QED-PDF review stresses slow convergence ([arXiv:2611.09408](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09408)).

**Light ions as nuclear-structure probes (heating).**
- Density, not multiplicity, orders O–O strangeness, with a Ne–Ne prediction within 3–5% of O–O ([arXiv:2611.04865](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04865)).
- Ne/O $v_2$ ratios are model-robust; $v_3$ ratios are not ([arXiv:2611.05239](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05239)).
- Eccentricities respond linearly to the chiral LECs, and Ne–Ne mostly probes spin-orbit ([arXiv:2611.08122](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08122)).
- Pb vibrations are claimed to fix ultracentral $v_2$/$v_3$ ([arXiv:2611.09404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09404)).
- A single DREENA parameter set reproduces suppression from O to Pb ([arXiv:2611.12404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12404)).
- At the low end, ALICE sees partonic-flow signatures down to $N_{\rm ch}\geq25$, fading below ([arXiv:2611.08327](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08327)).

**Fits that audit themselves (spreading).**
- Serao finds Ge/Xe blind directions ([arXiv:2611.04600](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04600)) and unbounded X17 nucleon couplings ([arXiv:2611.06129](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06129)).
- Adler $c_{5,1}$ is known only to about ±24 ([arXiv:2611.06824](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06824)).
- "Minimal magic" depends on the choice of measure ([arXiv:2611.08586](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08586)).
- Mean-field neutrino oscillations suffice at large $N$ ([arXiv:2611.08595](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08595)).
- Covariance-optimized lattice estimators gain precision but lose robustness ([arXiv:2611.10773](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10773)).
- Glueball sum rules distinguish "a pole fits" from "this region is required" ([arXiv:2611.12073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12073)).
- $t\bar tj$ keeps NLO as central because the NNLL kernel fails ([arXiv:2611.11600](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11600)).

**FCC-ee as arbiter (steady, now tempered).** Flavour-chain messengers are reachable to 100 TeV ([arXiv:2611.05464](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05464)), and HNL dipole $Z$-pole reach matches HL-LHC at 1 TeV ([arXiv:2611.05501](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05501)). Madison's ~10 m dispersion requirement for s-channel Higgs is the first sobering number in this thread ([arXiv:2611.12265](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12265)).

**Haloscope readout (steady).** Readout gains came from electro-optic photon counting ([arXiv:2611.05444](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05444)) and a KI-TWPA ([arXiv:2611.06761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06761)). Broadband receivers gave the week's best new limits up to 159 μeV ([arXiv:2611.11328](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11328) is unrelated; the receiver paper is [arXiv:2611.12774](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12774)).

## Turning points

- **BaBar vs BaBar.** The ISR ππ dispute now has an internal-consistency dimension, not only BaBar vs KLOE/CMD-3 ([arXiv:2611.04596](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04596)).
- **Toponium error budget relocated.** The remaining uncertainty is a bound-state problem, not a scale problem ([arXiv:2611.03782](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03782)).
- **X17 low window closed.** FASER excludes $6\times10^{-5}<\epsilon_e<1.2\times10^{-4}$; only high coupling remains ([arXiv:2611.03904](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03904)).
- **Explanations killed:**
  - The neutrino-current route to CMB birefringence is excluded via its $SU(2)_L$-fixed electron coupling ([arXiv:2611.07578](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07578)).
  - The non-factorization fix for gallium still leaves the cross section 20% too high ([arXiv:2611.09788](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09788)).
  - DAMA remains incompatible after full NREFT plus extensions ([arXiv:2611.04890](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04890)).
- **Limits corrected upward.** Realistic squark limits are 2.6–3.0 TeV ([arXiv:2611.03794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03794)).
- **Method adopted.** Born spreading is public in MadGraph5_aMC@NLO, including FxFx ([arXiv:2611.06769](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06769)).

## Open questions

- How is BaBar 2009 vs 2026 compatibility quantified, and do the KKMC/SHERPA radiative-return rules move either result?
- Does the CMS threshold excess survive Re-style reweighting combined with NNLO toponium, or does interference favor $A$?
- X17's high-coupling window plus the unbounded nucleon directions: can any combination close them?
- The CMB rotation hint (4.8σ, 3.5σ after dust) has lost a mechanism but is itself unresolved.
- Madison's $N_\nu$ error of ±0.0040 depends on a common-rescaling assumption the author flags for scrutiny.
- Light-ion falsifiables to watch:
  - Ne–Ne within 3–5% of O–O.
  - A flat $v_2^{\rm SP}/(1-R_{AA})$.
  - Pb $\epsilon_2\{2\}/\epsilon_3\{2\}\approx0.68$–0.79.
  - Strange-quark $v_2$ exceeding NCQ scaling at the LHC ([arXiv:2611.11347](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11347)), relevant to the O–O φ result.
- Charm CPV: Belle/Belle II asymmetries are all null, including $K^0_SK^0_S$ ([arXiv:2611.11957](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11957)), against the predictions of [arXiv:2611.04014](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04014).
- Low-$Q$ $\sin^2\theta_W$: COHERENT gives 0.231, between CONUS+ (0.225) and the CEνNS+ν–e combination (0.233) ([arXiv:2611.03751](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03751)).
- Belle II $K^+$+invisible favors a ~2.1 GeV two-body state ([arXiv:2611.10218](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10218)).
- Xing's $\theta_{23}=43.46^\circ$ prediction is cheap to falsify ([arXiv:2611.09478](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09478)).
