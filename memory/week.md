# Rolling Synthesis — Week Ending 2026-09-01

A week where the field's weight sat firmly on **infrastructure and precision** rather than anomalies. The collaboration firsts that landed — ATLAS $tZq$, CMS leptoquarks-via-muon–quark scattering, LHCb $\Xi_c^0\to pK^-$, XENONnT's keV solar-$pp$ threshold, CMS dead-cone evidence — were clean measurements or nulls, not surprises. Underneath them ran three durable strands the memory has tracked all month: a **strong-CP/axion program** that migrated from lattice hadronic inputs to cosmology and detection; a still-accelerating **autodiff/ML/quantum-computing tooling wave**; and a **precision-flavor push** that resolved the inclusive-radiative side even as the $R(D^*)$ tension refused to soften.

## Running themes

- **Strong CP → axions (heating up).** Opened with the lattice pair — the nEDM extraction and Li et al.'s first direct $\tilde g_{\pi NN}$ [arXiv:2608.23685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23685) — then broadened on 08-31 into cosmology and detection: Gorghetto et al. [arXiv:2608.27553](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27553) evade the $N>1$ domain-wall problem and predict an nEDM within two orders of the limit; Dvali et al. [arXiv:2608.27551](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27551) (coiled-fiber ALP detection) and Di Vecchia et al. [arXiv:2608.28253](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28253) fill in. A coherent, widening thread.
- **The tooling wave (heating up).** Present every single day: S-matrix-informed neural nets [arXiv:2608.23750](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23750), CTPWA GPU PWA [arXiv:2608.25963](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25963), differentiable generators (ADoNIS), RABBIT differentiable likelihoods [arXiv:2608.26376](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26376), GPU photon transport, diffusion-learned TMDs, and by week's end quantum-simulation-for-QCD (fragmentation functions, open-system heavy-ion dynamics) plus LLM-driven collider software. Autodiff-as-default is now the field's connective tissue.
- **Precision flavor (steady, partly resolving).** $|V_{cb}|$/$R(D^*)$ tension persists [arXiv:2608.24332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24332); N³LO $|V_{ub}|$ progress [arXiv:2608.28172](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28172); then the Misiak-group $B\to X_s\gamma$ pair closed out the week — the inclusive-radiative sector is now fully SM-consistent.
- **Quantum observables at colliders (steady).** The entanglement/magic program went precision with NLO EW corrections [arXiv:2608.27559](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27559), extended to steering ellipsoids for top spin [arXiv:2608.30037](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30037) and Drell-Yan quantum circuits.
- **Amplitude analysis (steady).** An upgrade cycle: neural $\pi\pi$ + dispersive pole extraction [arXiv:2608.23738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23738), CTPWA, Flatté pentaquark fits, elastic-unitarity rules.

## Turning points

- **Misiak et al. $B\to X_s\gamma$** [arXiv:2608.29863](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29863), built on the exact-$m_c$ four-loop NNLO calculation [arXiv:2608.29864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29864), retires a long-standing interpolation uncertainty: $\mathcal{B}=(3.54\pm0.14)\times10^{-4}$, charged-Higgs bound to $M_{H^\pm}>670$ GeV.
- **Braathen et al.** [arXiv:2608.25705](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25705) identified a model-independent higher-order class shifting the $\kappa_3$ bound from 6.1 to ~4.9 — a genuine correction to how self-coupling limits should be read, with public tool.
- **Tait's BBN rate** [arXiv:2608.23699](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23699) pins $np\to d\gamma$ to sub-0.3%, effectively removing it from the primordial D/H budget.
- **CMS leptoquark search** [arXiv:2608.28377](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28377) used the proton's charged-lepton content as a new production mode — a fresh handle, not another pair-production limit.
- **Look-elsewhere reframing** [arXiv:2608.24872](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24872): a procedural-consistency reading of global-vs-local significance, companion to earlier search-budget accounting.

## Open questions

- **$R(D^*)$ LFU tension** stays alive — SM $R(D^{(*)})$ region "barely overlaps" data even as neighboring flavor observables consolidate on nulls. Watch for follow-ups.
- **$K^+/\pi^+$ horn** [arXiv:2608.28189](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28189): no single framework fits the NA61 system-size ladder; a $3.7\sigma$ double-ratio step claimed for genuine strangeness enhancement.
- **Soft-pion puzzle** eased but unresolved by finite-width $\rho/\omega$ spectral functions [arXiv:2608.28456](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28456).
- **Thin-statistics intrigue:** HAWC Earth-skimming neutrino candidates through a volcano (2.1–2.5σ) [arXiv:2608.28951](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28951); first claimed clean triangle singularity in $\psi(2S)\to p\bar p\eta$ at 3.8σ [arXiv:2608.29006](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29006).
- **Gorghetto nEDM prediction** within reach of upcoming experiments — a concrete near-term test of the domain-wall-evasion scenario.
