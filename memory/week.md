# Rolling Synthesis — Week Ending 2026-07-17

After a fortnight dominated by theory and precision-machinery, the week closed with experiment and hardware quietly reasserting themselves. No single collaboration bombshell landed, but several trajectories sharpened: the dark sector visibly pivoted from model-building to constraining; quantum information matured from novelty into a working collider vocabulary; real-time reconstruction crossed into production; and cosmology got its first refereed tournament of Hubble-tension fixes. The connective tissue all week was *instrumentalization* — neutrinos, spin states, gravitational-wave polarizations, and open data all treated as tools rather than objects.

## Running themes

- **Dark sector: from building to bounding (turning).** Early-week diversification (majorons, quark pellets, heavy charged relics, Dark Phoenix, reheating-set relics) gave way by week's end to a constraining mood — DM–neutrino scattering, boosted-DM attenuation, PBH minispikes. The candidate zoo is still widening, but the exclusionary papers now outnumber the inventive ones.
- **Axions, fanning out (heating).** The strongest sustained thread. Every mass window and philosophy appeared: merged-photon LHC bounds, gluon-only invisible QCD axions, a charm-coupled GeV axion tying isospin and quality problems together, ultralight coherence probes, and — mid-week — axions/inflatons asked to carry cosmological loads (dark energy, leptogenesis). Nobody is converging.
- **Quantum information at colliders (heating, consolidating).** Flagged as emerging July 15, consolidated by July 17. Spin states, entanglement, concurrence, CHSH now appear as ordinary observables in ττγ, Z′-mediated tt̄, and ALP decays. [arXiv:2607.12015](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12015) (Cheng et al., FSR decoheres *or* enhances entanglement) recurred across both days.
- **Flavor precision (steady, shifting focus).** Moved from the b→sℓℓ / C₉ long-distance debate (July 13) toward CKM structure and QED systematics (July 16): a b-column rescaling blind spot where ε_K leads, and QED-corrected B→τν.
- **Instrumentation / real-time reconstruction (heating).** Bookended the week: Belle II's GNN calorimeter trigger commissioned (July 13), LHCb's parameterised Kalman filter in production on 500 GPUs plus a dense ITk/irradiation hardware cluster (July 17). The ML- and firmware-trigger wave is reaching production.
- **AI as collaborator (steady undercurrent).** Progression from LLM-auditing and ML-verification (VERaiPHY) toward agentic systems handed the keyboard (LQCDMaster, [arXiv:2607.15001](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15001)).

## Turning points

- **H₀ World Cup** ([arXiv:2607.13282](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13282), [arXiv:2607.13283](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13283)): fourteen tension solutions, one framework — early dark energy and early modified gravity win, varying electron mass intermediate, late-time and enhanced-radiation scenarios fail. The reference bake-off going forward; Paper II's robustness gauntlet is what model-builders will cite.
- **N-naturalness benchmark killed** ([arXiv:2607.14243](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14243)): first experimental search for the N-naturalness neutrino tower excludes the N=10⁴ GUT-unification benchmark for Majorana neutrinos — the scenario's tidy selling point gone.
- **Strong CP, computed and questioned** (July 14): a high-precision lattice neutron-EDM coefficient ([arXiv:2607.10606](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10606)) landed the same morning as Williams' argument ([arXiv:2607.10272](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10272)) that the problem is contingent on optional global topology — sharpening tools and questioning premises at once.
- **C₉ pulled back toward SM** ([arXiv:2607.09458](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09458), Bordone et al.): data-driven dispersive B→K*μμ reading finds ≤2σ tension once long-distance effects are data-constrained — continuing Ciuchini et al.'s July 3 hadronic-effects argument.
- **AGN-spike bounds deflated** ([arXiv:2607.08904](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08904), Farzan): neutrino-DM attenuation limits shown to hinge on mediator mass, landing the same day as a paper deriving flavor signals from the same physics.
- **Strongest DM–neutrino scattering bound** ([arXiv:2607.15020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15020), Mosbech et al.): hydro-simulated Lyman-α data excludes earlier hints.
- **LISA cosmic-string realism check** ([arXiv:2607.15219](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15219)): astrophysical foregrounds degrade tension reach by ~10⁵ in Gμ.

## Open questions to watch

- **Belle II B⁺→K⁺νν excess**: LNV SMEFT can't explain it without fine-tuning unless a light right-handed neutrino is added ([arXiv:2607.13142](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13142)) — watch for follow-ups.
- **HVP / muon g−2**: still unsettled; a Dyson-Schwinger determination ([arXiv:2607.12918](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12918), 710.0×10⁻¹⁰) now agrees with lattice — is the continuum consensus firming?
- **b→sℓℓ**: is the residual C₉ pull genuinely hadronic, or does a small short-distance shift survive?
- **Strong CP**: is it even a problem? Williams' contingency claim awaits response.
- **Neutron-star hyperon puzzle**: whether neutrino-induced hyperon FSI at SBND/DUNE can survive YN systematics (Nowak trilogy).
- **T2K/Hyper-K systematics**: no current generator describes carbon–oxygen kinematic-imbalance data across phase space.
- **Extra GW polarizations**: a fresh modified-gravity coalescence (Weyl, GQFT, defect networks) betting on polarimetry — watch whether it persists.
- **KM3NeT 220 PeV event**: GUT-scale relic-decay interpretation ([arXiv:2607.10760](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10760)).
