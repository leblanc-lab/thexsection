# Rolling Synthesis — Week Ending 2026-08-13

The week opened theory-heavy (early-universe model building, then a long neutrino-mixing numerology run) and closed with two data-side flavor anomalies that nothing in the preceding days anticipated. The through-lines: a persistent baryogenesis/leptogenesis cluster giving way to flavor model-building, a steady SMEFT/FCC-ee precision thread, the collider quantum-information program maturing into deployable tools, and two precision benchmarks (ATLAS Higgs, cosmic birefringence) that will serve as reference points going forward. The neutrino-systematics conversation moved from warning to partial resolution inside the same week — a rare closed loop.

## Running themes

- **Flavor: from numerology to data (heating, and sharply).** Early-week baryogenesis (Bigaran's CKM/MFV leptoquark [arXiv:2608.05269](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05269), plus [arXiv:2608.05279](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05279), [arXiv:2608.05950](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05950)) and mid-week neutrino-mixing model-building (Barger's $\pi/12$ [arXiv:2608.08394](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08394) and commensurate-mixing [arXiv:2608.10312](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10312)) were the theory ballast. Then 08-13 flipped the thread to data: LHCb's model-independent $C_9$ from transversity amplitudes [arXiv:2608.12215](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12215) (4.3–4.8σ) and Bouchard et al.'s $B\to PP$ SU(3)$_F$ fit at ~5σ [arXiv:2608.11298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11298). Two independent $B$ anomalies in one day — this is the live front.
- **SMEFT / FCC-ee precision (steady).** A continuous thread: Celada's FCC-ee running-scenario accounting [arXiv:2608.05874](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05874), Haisch's full 210-coefficient NLL electroweak observables [arXiv:2608.10064](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10064), FCC-ee 4 MeV Higgs-mass recoil [arXiv:2608.12071](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12071), and Dittmaier's one-loop singlet-matching caveat [arXiv:2608.11306](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11306).
- **Collider quantum information (heating).** From $t\bar t$ tomography the program spread to a low-energy Compton-scattering entry (Hatta [arXiv:2608.05330](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05330)) and then to a practical tool — TauPolaris's normalizing-flow tau polarimetry, $4.3\sigma$ entanglement in $H\to\tau\tau$ [arXiv:2608.10961](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10961).
- **DM detection toolkit & hardware (steady drumbeat).** Barker's material-agnostic scattering ceiling [arXiv:2608.05282](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05282), phonon-scan wavelets [arXiv:2608.06452](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06452), haloscopes (ALPHA [arXiv:2608.10292](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10292), photonic-bandgap [arXiv:2608.07718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07718)), NaI(Tl) upgrades [arXiv:2608.11303](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11303)/[arXiv:2608.11678](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11678) — many results, no single pivot.
- **QCD matter & hadron structure (steady).** ALICE 3-proton femtoscopy [arXiv:2608.05708](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05708), lab-frame in-medium $T$-matrix [arXiv:2608.06731](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06731), first Bayesian baryon-susceptibility extraction [arXiv:2608.10238](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10238).

## Turning points

- **ATLAS full Run 2 Higgs combination** [arXiv:2608.07332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07332) — now the reference document (signal strength $0.990$, $\kappa_\lambda$, $\Gamma_H$, running $b$-mass); closes the incremental $HH$/$tH$ updates and is now theory-limited.
- **Cosmic birefringence at $4.8\sigma$** (Eskilt, ACT+Planck) [arXiv:2608.06480](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06480) — the week's most provocative hint; $3.5\sigma$ after dust.
- **Neutrino-systematics loop closes.** Puhan's DUNE cross-section warning (25–30% sensitivity shifts) [arXiv:2608.08609](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08609) was answered days later by NOvA's most-precise $\bar\nu_\mu$–H CCQE cross section [arXiv:2608.12293](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12293).
- **Quantum-GW program hits a wall from a second direction.** Miyauchi [arXiv:2608.08803](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08803) extends the Bao et al. no-go [arXiv:2607.27313](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27313) to squeezing; converging obstruction.
- **Muon EDM limit** [arXiv:2608.11124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11124) — clean new bound $|d_\mu|<1.1\times10^{-19}\,e\,$cm.

## Open questions

- Do the two $B$ anomalies ([arXiv:2608.12215](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12215), [arXiv:2608.11298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11298)) point to genuine NP or to underestimated hadronics? Watch for global-fit reactions and independent transversity analyses.
- Does cosmic birefringence [arXiv:2608.06480](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06480) survive foreground systematics — axion-like Chern-Simons coupling or dust?
- Is Bigaran's claim [arXiv:2608.05269](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05269) that CKM CP violation alone suffices for baryogenesis a genuine counterexample? Flagged for scrutiny.
- Barger's falsifiable $\delta_{\rm CP}$ / $\varepsilon_2$ predictions ([arXiv:2608.08394](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08394), [arXiv:2608.10312](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10312)) — Hyper-K/DUNE tests.
- Nowak et al.'s predicted narrow $1^+$ beauty-strange partner at $5747\pm2$ MeV [arXiv:2608.07268](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07268) — a sharp spectroscopy follow-up.
- ALICE's ultra-central $v_3/v_2$ puzzle [arXiv:2608.11199](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11199) — still open in the small-system/flow program.
