# The Cross Section: rolling synthesis, week ending 2026-11-04

The week's clearest signal came from small-scale structure. A 29-lens population study ([arXiv:2610.40766](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40766)) turned the SIDM-vs-CDM lensing thread from scattered per-perturber hints into a population-level preference. Precision theory produced a sharp verdict: three-loop running appears to exclude minimal SUSY SU(5). Flavor and $g-2$ kept their split. Clean exclusive channels landed on the SM, including Upgrade I's first rare-decay result, while the open tensions shifted to inputs: $|V_{cb}|$, the τ-route isospin budget, and a newly exposed internal BaBar spread in $\pi\pi$. Several long-running threads continued:
- Gravitational-wave forecasts dropping idealizations.
- Compact ML for triggers.
- Light-ion collisions moving past oxygen.
- Old anomalies such as X17 and DAMA being squeezed.

## Running themes

- **SIDM over CDM in strong lensing (heating up).** Gilman et al. ([arXiv:2610.40766](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40766)) is the third result in two weeks pointing away from CDM-density subhalos, after Zhang et al. (no sufficiently dense CDM subhalos) and Tekverk et al. (SIDM matches known perturbers).
  - The favored region is 20–80 GeV dark matter with an MeV mediator and $\sigma/m\sim10^2$–$10^4$ cm²/g at 10 km/s.
  - Mediator bounds push the model toward a neutrinophilic scalar that decays invisibly.
  - The authors still say "hints."
- **Loop order decides (heating up).** The higher-order threads this week:
  - Allwicher et al. ([arXiv:2610.40821](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40821)): at Tera-$Z$ precision, two-loop running points to PeV-scale SUSY in minimal SU(5), while three loops appear to exclude it. The colour-triplet mass is fixed from the infrared.
  - Dvali et al. ([arXiv:2611.03783](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03783)) make the opposite bet: a TeV-scale, long-lived triplet obtained by splitting Yukawas.
  - Groundwork: the full one-loop SM in BMHV ([arXiv:2611.00141](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00141)), a soft-gluon explanation of the non-planar $\zeta_2$ terms with five-loop predictions ([arXiv:2610.40823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40823)), and two-loop $t\bar t\gamma$ including elliptic integrals ([arXiv:2610.42062](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42062)).
  - NNLO pseudoscalar toponium ([arXiv:2611.03782](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03782)) gives 4.87 pb at 13 TeV. The dominant error is now the Coulomb scale, which is the piece the NRQCD-vs-CP-odd-Higgs reading of the CMS excess depends on.
- **Phase-transition GW forecasts, de-idealized (steady).**
  - Gravity's non-minimal couplings change which supercooled transitions complete ([arXiv:2610.40775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40775)).
  - Dark–visible sound-wave leakage suppresses the GW signal and reshapes its spectrum ([arXiv:2610.40781](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40781)).
  - Nucleated bubbles are non-spherical at the ~10% level ([arXiv:2610.41504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41504)).
  - In the type-II seesaw, transitions that pass $v_c/T_c>1$ may never complete. Those that do are weak, sphaleron-unsafe and below LISA ([arXiv:2611.01634](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01634)).
  - Two-field conformal models give genuine two-peak spectra ([arXiv:2610.40354](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40354)).
- **Flavor and HVP inputs (steady).**
  - LHCb Upgrade I ([arXiv:2611.00230](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00230)) gives the first single-experiment evidence for $B^0\to\mu\mu$ and the most precise $B_s\to\mu\mu$ branching fraction and effective lifetime. All agree with the SM.
  - CMS extracts $|V_{cb}|$ from $W\to cb$ in $t\bar t$ ([arXiv:2611.01274](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01274)), free of hadronic inputs but at ~19% uncertainty.
  - On the τ route, the $\omega\pi$ correction to $\tau\to\pi\pi\nu\gamma$ was tightened ([arXiv:2611.01683](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01683)).
  - BaBar's new $\pi\pi$ analysis ([arXiv:2611.04596](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04596)) gives $a_\mu^{\pi\pi}=525.9\pm3.5\pm3.8$. Combined with 2009 it gives $517.8\pm2.4\pm3.3$ (all ×10⁻¹⁰).
- **Structure over size in ML (steady, shifting focus).**
  - CoReNet ([arXiv:2610.40749](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40749)) uses 1508 parameters and matches a transformer more than 100× larger in $HH\to4b$. It is already used in CMS searches.
  - SCRAMJet ([arXiv:2611.00086](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00086)) runs on an FPGA in 147 ns. SpikePID ([arXiv:2611.00104](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00104)) does K/π cluster counting with 529 parameters.
  - The question has moved from "can models shrink" to "what survives quantization."
- **Light ions past oxygen (steady).**
  - Ne–Ne events can be geometry-tagged ([arXiv:2610.40724](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40724)).
  - The O–O $\Lambda/K^0_S$ shape follows transverse density, not multiplicity, and needs sub-nucleonic structure ([arXiv:2611.04865](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04865)).
  - Ne/O $v_2$ ratios are robust across models, but $v_3$ ratios are not ([arXiv:2611.05239](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05239)).

## Turning points

- **Minimal SUSY SU(5) flips** from viable to apparently excluded between two and three loops ([arXiv:2610.40821](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40821)). This is a model-killing result driven by perturbative order alone.
- **The lensing case became a population statement** ([arXiv:2610.40766](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40766)), not a set of individual perturbers.
- **$v_c/T_c>1$ is shown unreliable** as a completion criterion ([arXiv:2611.01634](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01634)).
- **FASER cuts directly into X17 parameter space** ([arXiv:2611.03904](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03904)), excluding $6\times10^{-5}<\epsilon_e<1.2\times10^{-4}$. This builds on September's finding that cosmic muons reproduce the Atomki opening-angle peaks.
- **DAMA stays incompatible** with LZ, PICO-60 and CDMSlite even across the full NREFT, including inelastic scattering, quenching and halo variations ([arXiv:2611.04890](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04890)).
- **Squark limits move up by ~1 TeV**, to 2.6–3.0 TeV, once realistic production is counted ([arXiv:2611.03794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03794)).
- **"Virtual channels" in quantization-aware training** ([arXiv:2611.00086](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00086)): channels carry signal in training but are dropped from the compiled circuit. Anyone using HGQ-style training is affected.

## Open questions

- **BaBar internal consistency.** The 2009 result must sit well below the new one, an internal spread of ~8×10⁻¹⁰. Watch how compatibility is quantified and how lattice and CMD-3 comparisons respond.
- **LHCb numbers.** How far does Run 3 tighten the $B^0/B_s$ ratio? The abstract gives no values.
- **$|V_{cb}|$ inclusive/exclusive split.** Can the top-decay route reach a precision that discriminates between the two?
- **LZ 248 keV event.** It got two blank days, then its first model: Roy et al. ([arXiv:2611.01158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01158)), an exothermic Higgs-portal relic that predicts a sub-MeV photon continuum for COSI. Watch for competing models.
- **SIDM.** Will a mediator or the neutrinophilic scalar be confirmed or excluded? Will lens-modeling systematics be challenged?
- **X17 high-coupling window** remains open.
- **Low-$Q$ $\sin^2\theta_W$ spread.** Values span 0.225 (CONUS+), 0.231 ([arXiv:2611.03751](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03751)) and 0.233 (CEνNS+ν–e combination). Separately, Ge and Xe targets share nearly parallel blind directions for light mediators ([arXiv:2611.04600](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04600)).
- **Ne–Ne prediction** to test: within 3–5% of O–O ([arXiv:2611.04865](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04865)).
- **Big claims to watch:**
  - UHECR light-nucleus populations at ≥7.2σ, read as evidence for neutron-star mergers ([arXiv:2611.00205](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00205)). The result is model-dependent.
  - Thermal Festina Lente's lightest-neutrino mass bound versus DESI DR2 ([arXiv:2611.02559](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02559)).
