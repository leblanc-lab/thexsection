# The Cross Section — Weekly synthesis, week ending 2026-11-16

This week's digests (11-10, 11-11, 11-13, 11-16) kept returning to the reliability of numbers rather than to new discoveries. Three habits strengthened. Fits now state where they stop being trustworthy, and that scrutiny has spread to exclusion limits. Gravitational-wave forecasts are increasingly treated as hostage to transition dynamics. Perturbative loop counts kept climbing, ending in a same-day N³LO push on $B\to\gamma\ell\nu$. On the neutrino side, the mass-ordering hint was shown to be fragile, with the remaining leverage sitting in percent-level interaction modeling. The LZ 248 keV event got its first elastic interpretation, which reopens a thread that had settled on inelastic models since September.

## Running themes

**1. Audits move from fits to limits (heating up).**
- **Fits stating their limits (11-10).** This carried last week's identifiability work (Serao, Shen) into lattice and perturbative QCD:
  - Covariance-optimized lattice estimators gain precision but fail built-in controls ([arXiv:2611.10773](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10773)).
  - Glueball sum rules separate "a pole fits" from "a region is required" ([arXiv:2611.12073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12073)).
  - A $t\bar tj$ resummation keeps NLO central because its own NNLL kernel fails ([arXiv:2611.11600](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11600)).
- **Bounds recalibrated (11-11).** Linear-coupling bounds break down for a pNGB near its source ([arXiv:2611.14473](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14473)).
- **DM limits moving both ways (11-16).**
  - Counting each DM particle once weakens the Fornax $\sigma_{\nu\chi}$ limit by ~50× ([arXiv:2611.17922](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17922)).
  - Resonant trapping evades supernova cooling bounds ([arXiv:2611.17604](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17604)).

**2. GW forecasts depend on transition dynamics (heating up).**
- **11-13:**
  - Near the completion boundary, homogeneous cosmology fails, which undercuts claims of strong monopole suppression ([arXiv:2611.14593](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14593)).
  - A supercooled electroweak step depends on the acoustic description still holding ([arXiv:2611.16845](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16845)).
  - The Higgs trilinear does not survive dimension-six operators ([arXiv:2611.15856](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15856)).
- **11-16:** Barni et al. show $(\xi_w,\alpha_N)$ does not fix a unique bubble flow when sound speeds differ ([arXiv:2611.17402](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17402), [arXiv:2611.17411](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17411)). This echoes a lesson first stated on 11-03.

**3. Loop counts climbing (heating up).**
- **11-11:** Local Unitarity reaches hadron colliders ([arXiv:2611.13184](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13184)).
- **11-13:**
  - Six-loop QED renormalization, pitched as a path to six-loop QCD ([arXiv:2611.15366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15366)).
  - Three-loop $V$+jet amplitudes ([arXiv:2611.15836](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15836)).
  - Two-loop electroweak work aimed at lepton colliders ([arXiv:2611.15807](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15807), [arXiv:2611.17171](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17171)).
- **11-16:** Three groups posted N³LO pieces for $B^-\to\gamma\ell\bar\nu$, aimed at a cleaner $\lambda_B$ ([arXiv:2611.18202](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18202), [arXiv:2611.18204](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18204), [arXiv:2611.18206](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18206)).

**4. Neutrino ordering is limited by interaction physics (steady).** Machado et al. show the 1.9σ inverted-ordering disfavor is a balance of conflicting pulls ([arXiv:2611.13629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13629)). Further progress past JUNO's ~3σ needs percent-level $\nu$–nucleus modeling. That gives the 11-02 generator-rebuilding thread a concrete target, and two 11-11 papers already work on it ([arXiv:2611.13618](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13618), [arXiv:2611.13602](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13602)).

**5. FCC-ee as arbiter, with costs attached (steady).**
- **The cost:** Madison finds that monochromatization for s-channel Higgs would need ~10 m of horizontal dispersion ([arXiv:2611.12265](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12265)).
- **The payoff:** $Z$ tomography in $ZH$ gives $\kappa_\lambda$ to ±2.9 independent of rate, and reports magic in three measures, partly answering Shen's 11-09 audit ([arXiv:2611.18136](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18136)).

## Turning points
- **Ordering hint decomposed.** Reactor+T2K and reactor+NOvA prefer opposite orderings. A 3.5% $\Delta m^2$ shift in one experiment flips the preference ([arXiv:2611.13629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13629)).
- **First elastic LZ fit.** An anapole-only Majorana fermion fits the 248 keV event with relic abundance at ~300 GeV ([arXiv:2611.15957](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15957)). On the same day, solar capture was recomputed with a transport equation: annihilation drops by orders of magnitude when nuclear scattering is rare, but the bound on the higgsino splitting tightens ([arXiv:2611.16689](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16689)).
- **LEP $N_\nu$ re-derived.** The result is $2.9965\pm0.0040$, but the halved error rests on an assumed common rescaling of uncertainties ([arXiv:2611.12265](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12265)).
- **Submerged sensors ruled out.** TESSERACT measures >99% phonon loss into superfluid ⁴He ([arXiv:2611.17365](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17365)).

## Open questions
- **LZ anapole model:** does it survive solar capture? Its abstract is silent on this. The model's predicted excess at lower recoil energies awaits the full LZ run.
- **$B\to\gamma\ell\nu$ trio:** watch for a cross-check of the overlapping three-loop kernel and jet-function results.
- **$|V_{cb}|$ and rare kaons:** Qu et al. ([arXiv:2611.17945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17945)) don't quote numbers, so their side of the inclusive/exclusive split is unknown. NA62's doubled $K^+\to\pi^+\nu\bar\nu$ sample ([arXiv:2611.16530](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16530)) has no measured value in the abstract.
- **Belle II $K^+$+invisible excess:**
  - Missing-mass fits favour a two-body state near 2.1 GeV ([arXiv:2611.10218](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10218)).
  - A leptoquark fit with $N_{\rm eff}$ bounds that can beat collider limits ([arXiv:2611.14616](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14616)).
- **Charm CPV:** still open. Belle/Belle II asymmetries are all null, including $K^0_SK^0_S$ ([arXiv:2611.11957](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11957)).
- **Light-ion QGP:**
  - Strange-quark $v_2$ exceeds NCQ scaling at LHC energies ([arXiv:2611.11347](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11347)), so be cautious reading ALICE O–O φ.
  - DREENA predicts a flat $v_2^{\rm SP}/(1-R_{AA})$ in light ions ([arXiv:2611.12404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12404)).
  - Hybrid hydro models miss ALICE's soft pions ([arXiv:2611.18290](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18290)).
- **Exotic spectroscopy:** the $T_{cc}$ pole points to mixing with a compact $cc\bar u\bar d$ state ([arXiv:2611.11472](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11472)). BESIII's 11σ $K_1^*(1870)$ is pitched as a hybrid-search input ([arXiv:2611.11328](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11328)).
- **Sign of $V(0)$:** the 5.8–7.9σ significance falls to 2.0–3.4σ after charm extrapolation ([arXiv:2611.17800](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17800)).
