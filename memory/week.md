# The Cross Section — Synthesis, week ending 2026-09-03

**Overview.** The week's gravity well was a single LZ nuclear-recoil event, which by Wednesday had swallowed roughly a dozen theory papers and then met its first hard astrophysical exclusion — a compressed case study in how a 2.6σ result drives a field. Around it, three slower trajectories kept building: collider quantum-information observables evolved from tomography toward genuine BSM diagnostics; the agentic/differentiable-tooling wave acquired standards and benchmarks rather than just demos; and precision flavor delivered a long-awaited B→Xsγ milestone. Experimental firsts (mostly clean or null) came almost daily. Strong-CP/axion work ran steadily underneath, extending last week's lattice pair into cosmology and detection.

## Running themes

**The LZ event (heating, now the centerpiece).** A single 248±23 keV recoil, 2.6σ global ([arXiv:2609.02823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02823)). Interpretations converged with suspicious tightness on ~1.1 TeV inelastic/endothermic dark matter split by ~300–370 keV — often a higgsino (Fan [arXiv:2609.01504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01504), Freese [arXiv:2609.01583](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01583), Su [arXiv:2609.01475](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01475), Wu [arXiv:2609.01590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01590), then Yin/Nomura/Di Mauro/Visinelli/Yamashita). The takeaway across two days: the kinematics fix mass and splitting so tightly that models barely constrain the physics — but astrophysics does.

**Collider quantum information (heating, shifting purpose).** From NLO EW corrections to entanglement/magic ([arXiv:2608.27559](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27559)) and steering-ellipsoid top-spin observables ([arXiv:2608.30037](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30037)), the program moved toward using entanglement *as* a symmetry probe: Kanemura et al. [arXiv:2609.02691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02691) conjecture entropy-minimization selects symmetry-preserving points; Jangid et al. [arXiv:2609.02467](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02467) read entropy as a portal constraint. Haddadi [arXiv:2609.01686](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01686) is the useful skeptic.

**Agentic & differentiable tooling (heating, formalizing).** RABBIT [arXiv:2608.26376](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26376), Opticks-for-DUNE [arXiv:2608.27306](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27306), and diffusion/phase-space generators fed the differentiable-pipeline tide. The LLM-agent thread went from HEPToolBench [arXiv:2608.28232](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28232) to a standards argument — Menzo et al. [arXiv:2609.00107](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00107) (versioned, citable "scientific operations") plus Sun et al.'s knowledge-graph grounding [arXiv:2609.01095](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01095) (58.5%→76.0% first-attempt execution).

**Strong-CP & axions (steady).** Domain-wall evasion with an in-reach nEDM prediction (Gorghetto [arXiv:2608.27553](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27553)), fiber and petawatt-beam detection schemes, Ai's review [arXiv:2609.00620](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00620), and PQ-quality-from-proton-stability [arXiv:2609.01712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01712).

**Experimental firsts (steady, mostly clean/null).** XENONnT's keV-scale solar $pp$ detection [arXiv:2608.29450](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29450), CMS dead-cone evidence at 4.3σ [arXiv:2608.29412](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29412), CMS's muon–quark leptoquark channel [arXiv:2608.28377](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28377) and first Higgs-width bound from diphoton interference [arXiv:2609.01385](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01385), LHCb $\Xi_c^0\to pK^-$ [arXiv:2608.27894](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27894).

## Turning points

- **Pospelov et al. [arXiv:2609.02775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02775)** — solar capture + IceCube demand δ>566 keV, excluding the endothermic-higgsino LZ reading the field had converged on 24 hours earlier. The conversation flipped from model-building to astrophysical elimination overnight.
- **Misiak group B→Xsγ [arXiv:2608.29863](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29863)** with exact-$m_c$ NNLO input [arXiv:2608.29864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29864) — retired a long-standing interpolation uncertainty, gave $\mathcal{B}=(3.54\pm0.14)\times10^{-4}$, and pushed Type-II charged-Higgs to >670 GeV. The inclusive-radiative sector is now fully SM-consistent (contrast the persistent R(D*) tension).
- **Lou et al. [arXiv:2609.01592](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01592)** — built an LZ absorption interpretation then excluded it themselves via KamLAND recast; a model for disciplined self-refutation.
- **Fiducial-recoil method** ([arXiv:2609.02280](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02280), [arXiv:2609.02356](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02356)) — a transferable geometric TMD projector adopted across two saturation observables in one day.

## Open questions

- **What is the LZ event?** With the endothermic-higgsino window shut, watch for surviving inelastic/dark-photon fits and any second event. The kinematics-vs-astrophysics tension is the thing to track.
- **Does entropy-minimization robustly flag symmetry-preserving points** ([arXiv:2609.02691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02691)), or are information-theoretic "resources" channel artifacts ([arXiv:2609.01686](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01686))? Unresolved.
- **Statistics-thin anomalies:** HAWC Earth-skimming candidates (2.1–2.5σ, [arXiv:2608.28951](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28951)), the $\psi(2S)\to p\bar p\eta$ triangle-singularity claim (3.8σ, [arXiv:2608.29006](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29006)), NA61 strangeness double-ratio step (3.7σ, [arXiv:2608.28189](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28189)).
- **Higgs width** still bounded only to <92 MeV vs 4.1 MeV SM [arXiv:2609.01385](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01385) — new handle, long road.
