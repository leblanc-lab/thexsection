# Rolling Synthesis — Week Ending 2026-07-14

A theory- and tool-heavy fortnight that ended with experiments quietly reasserting themselves. No single bombshell, but several coherent trajectories: the strong CP problem was worked from every altitude, the dark-matter search kept fragmenting into ever-stranger channels, and the field's chronic anxiety about *fake* new physics in long-baseline oscillations resurfaced repeatedly. Underneath, the ML-reconstruction wave crossed from prototype into production, and hadron spectroscopy kept consolidating. The mood alternated between sharpening tools and questioning premises — sometimes on the same morning.

## Running themes

- **Strong CP — heating and broadening.** The week opened with three simultaneous *solutions* (July 8): Baer et al.'s accidental-PQ/DFSZ axion from anomaly-free $Z_n^R$ ([arXiv:2607.07921](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07921)), Fileviez Perez et al.'s Nelson-Barr universal seesaw ([arXiv:2607.07794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07794)), and Figueroa et al.'s dark $\theta$-term as a *feature* ([arXiv:2607.07406](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07406)). By July 14 the conversation had pivoted from solving to measuring and doubting: a high-precision lattice neutron EDM ([arXiv:2607.10606](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10606)) versus Williams' argument that the problem is *contingent* on optional global topology ([arXiv:2607.10272](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10272)).

- **Dark-matter diversification — steady, almost baroque.** No WIMP renaissance, just widening scope: winos, massive gravitons, multipolar freeze-out (July 8); LISA-probed ultralight quadratic coupling ([arXiv:2607.08248](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08248)); majoron/reheating/"Dark Phoenix" model-building (July 13); and heavy charged relics trapped in terrestrial matter ([arXiv:2607.09845](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09845)), quark pellets, and reactor Migdal DM (July 14). Discrete symmetries and non-standard reheating remain the workhorses.

- **Neutrino degeneracies / fake CP — steady, recurring warning.** From Chaulagain et al.'s closed-form genuine-vs-fake CP separation ([arXiv:2607.07213](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07213)) to Koranga et al. showing a path-averaged Earth density can fabricate a $3\sigma$ CPT signal at DUNE baselines ([arXiv:2607.10417](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10417)). The lesson keeps migrating to new sources — now the matter profile itself.

- **Majorana/LNV — steady.** AMoRE's $0\nu2$EC repurposing ([arXiv:2607.08039](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08039)) anchored a four-angle July 9 assault on Dirac-vs-Majorana; July 14 added ab-initio $^{48}$Ca NMEs ([arXiv:2607.11733](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11733)) and DarkSide-50's $^{36}$Ar limit ([arXiv:2607.11476](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11476)).

- **ML reconstruction — reaching production.** GRAPES-3 GNN pipelines (July 8), transformer cell-level calorimetry ([arXiv:2607.08175](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08175)), then the Belle II GNN calorimeter trigger *commissioned* at 1.05 µs ([arXiv:2607.09347](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09347)), and antineutron reconstruction from ECAL alone ([arXiv:2607.11139](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11139)). The VERaiPHY review ([arXiv:2607.10039](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10039)) marks the governance counter
