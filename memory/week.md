# Rolling Synthesis — Week Ending 2026-07-15

The week opened cashing in on a fortnight of tool-building (AMFlow, ML reconstruction, next-gen facility costing) and closed in a fanning-out mood: no single collaboration bombshell, but two theory currents grew unmistakably loud — **axions/strong CP** and **quantum information as collider vocabulary** — while neutrinos dominated as both object and instrument. Experiments quietly reclaimed corners (ATLAS longitudinal $ZZ$, LHCb $\Sigma_c$ states, T2K cross sections), and the field's habitual anxieties (Majorana vs Dirac, oscillation degeneracies, HVP) all resurfaced without resolution.

## Running themes

- **Axions & strong CP — heating up sharply.** From a mid-week trickle to a full flood. July 14 paired a high-precision lattice neutron-EDM coefficient ([arXiv:2607.10606](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10606)) against Williams' foundational claim that the strong CP problem is *contingent* on optional global topology ([arXiv:2607.10272](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10272)). July 15 fanned out completely: LHC merged-photon bounds ([arXiv:2607.12004](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12004)), maximally-invisible gluon-only QCD axions ([arXiv:2607.12025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12025)), a GeV axion rescued by charm coupling that also solves the quality problem ([arXiv:2607.12956](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12956)), plus ultralight-coherence and quantum-sensing probes. Everybody diversifying, nobody converging.
- **Neutrinos as instruments — steady/heating.** The through-line of July 13: hyperon final-state interactions at DUNE/SBND as a handle on the neutron-star EoS ([arXiv:2607.09273](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09273)), Earth density tomography, and AGN-spike DM scattering. Complemented by the Majorana question (see below) and degeneracy worries.
- **Neutrino degeneracies & cross-section inputs — steady.** The "fake-CP" thread persists: July 14 showed a path-averaged (vs PREM) Earth density fabricates a spurious $3\sigma$ CPT signal at DUNE baselines ([arXiv:2607.10417](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10417)). T2K's first joint carbon–oxygen kinematic-imbalance cross sections found *no* generator describes the data ([arXiv:2607.10638](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10638), [arXiv:2607.10644](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10644)) — essential Hyper-K systematics.
- **Dark-matter candidate zoo — heating/baroque.** Ever-stranger candidates: heavy charged relics trapped in terrestrial matter ([arXiv:2607.09845](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09845)), primordial quark pellets, reactor-produced light DM via Migdal, plus a near-universal DM phase-space distribution ([arXiv:2607.12008](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12008)) and GW diffractive-lensing detection windows.
- **ML/quantum tooling — steady, with a new emergent branch.** ML reconstruction reached new detector types (antineutrons in ECAL, [arXiv:2607.11139](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11139)); the Belle II GNN calorimeter trigger went from radiation-tested to commissioned ([arXiv:2607.09347](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09347)). Newly loud on July 15: **quantum information meeting colliders** — $\tau$-pair decoherence in $Z$ decays, entangling-power diagnostics, QUBO track reconstruction — worth watching whether it matures.
- **Hadron spectroscopy — steady undercurrent.** Molecular-picture consolidation (unified $DD^*$ frameworks, $\psi(4660)$ tests) plus real new states: LHCb's $\Sigma_c(2900)^0$/$\Sigma_c(3200)^0$ ([arXiv:2607.10595](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10595)), BESIII $\eta_c\to p\bar p\eta$.
- **Precision QCD/EW — steady.** Threshold-enhanced $t\bar t$ generators for the toponium-like enhancement ([arXiv:2607.09539](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09539)), three-loop Higgs+3-parton amplitudes with surprisingly large sub-leading color ([arXiv:2607.12033](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12033)), NNLO antenna channels, Gfitter with the new $W$-mass average.
