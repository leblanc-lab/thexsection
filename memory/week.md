# The Cross Section — Rolling Synthesis, week ending 2026-07-13

The week opened as a coordinated stress-test of the neutrino CP-discovery program and closed with neutrinos recast as instruments rather than objects of study — a subtle but real shift in framing. Underneath, two multi-week continuities matured into concrete tooling: the joint QCD+QED/QCD+EW factorization program, and the ML-reconstruction wave moving from calibration papers into commissioned hardware. Hadron spectroscopy and dark-sector model-building both stayed in consolidating, framework-comparing modes. No single collaboration bombshell; the week's energy was in infrastructure, reinterpretation, and the deep flavor questions that never resolve.

## Running themes

**Neutrino degeneracies → neutrinos as probes (heating, evolving).** The "fake CP" thread crested July 7: non-unitarity ([arXiv:2607.06108](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06108)), Lorentz violation ([arXiv:2607.06513](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06513)), and quasi-Dirac splittings ([arXiv:2607.06862](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06862)) each showed the three-flavor picture is fragile exactly where DUNE hopes to measure, with the recurring answer "combine detectors." July 8 added algebraic separation of genuine vs fake CP ([arXiv:2607.07213](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07213)). By July 13 the framing had rotated: neutrinos as terrestrial gauges of neutron-star hyperon potentials (Nowak trilogy, [arXiv:2607.09273](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09273)), as Earth-tomography scanners ([arXiv:2607.09591](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09591)), and as AGN-dark-matter messengers ([arXiv:2607.08833](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08833), [arXiv:2607.08904](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08904)).

**Dirac vs Majorana (steady, central).** July 9 attacked it from four angles at once — AMoRE's $0\nu2$EC limit ([arXiv:2607.08039](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08039)), a cosmological $\Delta N_{\rm eff}$ handle ([arXiv:2607.08082](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08082)), ML-assisted mass textures ([arXiv:2607.08384](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08384)), and an algebraic pseudospin reformulation ([arXiv:2607.08739](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08739)). Unresolved; a good gauge of where the field's anxiety sits.

**Precision QCD+EW / QCD+QED factorization (heating).** From July 7's first automated resonance-aware NLO QCD+EW MC@NLO matching ([arXiv:2607.06059](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06059)) and the `ggxy` library ([arXiv:2607.06099](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06099)), through July 8's first inclusive-hadron/jet calculation in joint QCD+QED factorization ([arXiv:2607.07664](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07664)) and the PDF-profiling-degrades-SMEFT warning ([arXiv:2607.07791](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07791)), to July 13's mixed QCD×EW charged-current Drell-Yan ([arXiv:2607.09124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09124)) and NLO+PS threshold-$t\bar t$ generators ([arXiv:2607.09539](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09539)). The lepton-distribution-function push is now a mature, EIC-aimed program.

**Hadron spectroscopy / molecular states (steady, consolidating).** Correlation functions emerged as the tool of choice ([arXiv:2607.06317](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06317), [arXiv:2607.06715](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06715)). Unified $DD^*$ frameworks ([arXiv:2607.08040](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08040)), $\psi(4660)$ as molecular-nature test ([arXiv:2607.09093](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09093)), and a broad exotics sweep July 13 ([arXiv:2607.08817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08817), [arXiv:2607.09354](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09354), [arXiv:2607.09430](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09430)) kept the molecular-vs-compact debate ticking without resolution.

**Dark sector: discrete symmetries + reheating (steady, diversifying).** Axions doing double duty ([arXiv:2607.06774](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06774), [arXiv:2607.06375](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06375)), then majoron DM from $Z_N\subset U(1)_{B-L}$ ([arXiv:2607.09131](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09131)), "seesaw reheating" ([arXiv:2607.08828](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08828)), sub-sphaleron leptogenesis ([arXiv:2607.09282](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09282), sequel to [arXiv:2607.08663](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08663)), and the inventive "Dark Phoenix" mechanism ([arXiv:2607.08838](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08838)).

**ML reconstruction reaching production (heating).** Two ATLAS GN2 calibrations in one week ([arXiv:2607.06199](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06199)), transformer cell-level calorimetry ([arXiv:2607.08175](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08175)), and — the milestone — the Belle II GNN calorimeter trigger, radiation-tested July 3, now commissioned at 1.05 µs latency ([arXiv:2607.09347](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09347)).

## Turning points

- **[arXiv:2607.06108](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06108)** quantified when fake CP *dominates* the genuine signal (~130%), establishing the week's degeneracy benchmark and the "combine DUNE+Hyper-K" prescription.
- **[arXiv:2607.08552](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08552)** independently confirmed the 20 GeV Fermi-LAT halo excess at pixel level, narrowing viable WIMPs to fine-tuned low-velocity resonant annihilation.
