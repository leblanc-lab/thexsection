# Rolling Synthesis — Week Ending 2026-07-20

A breadth-over-depth week: four digests, no single collaboration bombshell, but a clear tidal pattern — theorists and phenomenologists held the floor early, then the experimentalists (July 17 instrumentation) and the flavor/cosmology referees (July 16) reclaimed corners of it. The dominant intellectual current remained strong CP and axions, approached from every altitude; alongside it, quantum information consolidated from novelty into a working collider vocabulary, dark matter pivoted from model-building to constraining, and cosmology got its first refereed tournament of Hubble-tension fixes. The recurring meta-thread — AI/ML in the discovery pipeline — advanced from *auditing* to *handing the agent the keyboard*.

## Running themes

- **Strong CP & axions (heating, the week's spine).** Opened July 14 with a sharp juxtaposition: a high-precision lattice neutron-EDM coefficient ([arXiv:2607.10606](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10606)) against Williams' argument that the strong CP problem is *contingent* on optional global topology ([arXiv:2607.10272](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10272)). July 15 fanned axion searches across five disjoint mass windows/methods (LHC merged-photon bound [arXiv:2607.12004](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12004); charm-coupled GeV QCD axion [arXiv:2607.12956](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12956)). July 16 asked axions to carry cosmological loads — the standout "weak axion" tying dark energy to neutrino mixing, testable at Hyper-K/DUNE ([arXiv:2607.13128](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13128)). Nobody is converging; everybody is diversifying.
- **Quantum information at colliders (emerging → consolidated).** Flagged as coalescence July 15, consolidated by July 17. Spin states, entanglement, and quantum hardware now treated as ordinary observables: τ-pair decoherence/entanglement under FSR ([arXiv:2607.12015](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12015), recurring), entanglement markers in $Z'$-mediated $t\bar t$ ([arXiv:2607.15153](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15153)), QUBO track reconstruction ([arXiv:2607.12821](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12821)).
- **Dark matter (mood shift: zoo → constraints).** July 14's baroque candidate proliferation (heavy charged relics [arXiv:2607.09845](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09845), quark pellets, reactor-Migdal) gave way by July 17 to an exclusionary posture — strongest-yet DM–neutrino scattering bound killing prior hints ([arXiv:2607.15020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15020)), and the first test of the $N$-naturalness tower excluding its GUT benchmark ([arXiv:2607.14243](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14243)).
- **Flavor precision (steady, densest July 16).** Focus moved from the $C_9$/$b\to s\ell\ell$ debate toward CKM structure and QED systematics: a model-independent $b$-column rescaling blind spot with $\varepsilon_K$ as leading probe ([arXiv:2607.13136](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13136)), the Belle II $B^+\to K^+\nu\nu$ excess ([arXiv:2607.13142](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13142)), QED-corrected $B\to\tau\nu$ ([arXiv:2607.13144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13144)).
- **Precision QCD & EW machinery (steady).** ATLAS's first observation of two simultaneously longitudinally polarized Z bosons (6.5σ, [arXiv:2607.12879](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12879)); three-loop Higgs+3-parton amplitudes where sub-leading color is as large as leading ([arXiv:2607.12033](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12033)); EIC-facing nucleon-structure work (proton gluon-cloud shape, [arXiv:2607.14955](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14955)).
- **Instrumentation & AI-in-pipeline (resurgent).** July 17's hardware cluster led by LHCb's parameterised Kalman filter in production at 30 MHz ([arXiv:2607.14793](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14793)). The ML-governance thread progressed: VERaiPHY verification review ([arXiv:2607.10039](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10039)) → the LQCDMaster agentic lattice system ([arXiv:2607.15001](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15001)).

## Turning points

- **H0 World Cup** ([arXiv:2607.13282](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13282), [arXiv:2607.13283](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13283)): first common-framework bake-off of fourteen tension solutions. Verdict — early dark energy / early modified gravity win, cutting residual SH0ES discrepancy to ~2.5–3.6σ; late-time and enhanced-radiation scenarios fail. Now the reference tournament.
- **$N$-naturalness GUT benchmark excluded** ([arXiv:2607.14243](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14243)) — removed the framework's tidy no-fine-tuning selling point.
- **DM–neutrino scattering hints ruled out** by hydro-simulated Lyman-α ([arXiv:2607.15020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15020)).
- **Real-time reconstruction crosses into production** ([arXiv:2607.14793](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14793)) — the hardware counterpart to this month's ML-trigger work.
- **Emergence of GW polarimetry as a gravity-theory discriminator** (July 16 cluster: [arXiv:2607.13146](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13146), [arXiv:2607.13483](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13483)) — a fresh coalescence, not previously tracked.

## Open questions

- **Is strong CP even a problem?** Williams' contingency claim ([arXiv:2607.10272](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10272)) sits unanswered against the field's simultaneous efforts to *solve* it (Nelson-Barr, PQ) and *quantify* it ([arXiv:2607.10606](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10606)). Watch for rebuttals.
- **HVP still unsettled.** Dyson-Schwinger/BSE $a_\mu$ ([arXiv:2607.12918](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12918)) lands on lattice consensus at $710.0\times10^{-10}$, but the saga isn't closed.
- **Belle II $B^+\to K^+\nu\nu$ excess** — explicable only with a light right-handed neutrino ([arXiv:2607.13142](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13142)); needs follow-up.
- **Neutrino matter-profile degeneracy** — path-averaged Earth density can fake ±3σ CPT violation at DUNE baselines ([arXiv:2607.10417](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10417)); a systematics caution to track.
- **KM3NeT 220 PeV event** — speculative GUT-scale $X\to\nu\bar\nu$ reading ([arXiv:2607.10760](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10760)); watch for competing interpretations.
- **Anomalies to eye:** T2K kinematic-imbalance data fit by *no* current generator ([arXiv:2607.10638](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10638), [arXiv:2607.10644](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10644)) — critical for Hyper-K; predicted per-mille CP asymmetry in $D^0\to K_SK_S$ ([arXiv:2607.11845](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11845)).
