# The Cross Section — Weekly Synthesis, week ending 2026-07-29

**Overview.** A week bracketed by neutrino astronomy at both ends: it opened with the UHE-neutrino anomaly hardening into a number and closed with IceCube's Galactic-plane detection — the destructive and constructive faces of the same multi-messenger program. In between, the electroweak-boson polarization/VBS campaign kept adding channels, the Hubble-tension debate shifted from "who wins" to "what walls constrain the winners" (with the distance ladder refusing to blink), lattice precision fed directly into flavor benchmarks, and LLM agents graduated from checking work to orchestrating full pipelines. Direct-detection experiments quietly reinvented themselves as solar-neutrino observatories.

## Running themes

- **Electroweak bosons, polarization, and aQGC (heating up).** A sustained ATLAS-led march: doubly-longitudinal $ZZ$ (prior week) → longitudinal $Z$ in $WZjj$ at 4.0σ ([arXiv:2607.20246](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20246)) → EW $\gamma\gamma jj$ at 6.2σ ([arXiv:2607.25869](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25869), slightly below SM). Dimension-8 EFT/aQGC has become the default interpretive language; FCC-hh $ZZ\gamma$ projections ([arXiv:2607.25935](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25935)) and large-$R$ jet calibration ([arXiv:2607.25893](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25893)) extend the runway.

- **Hubble tension: walls vs. the ladder (steady, contentious).** Poulin et al. ([arXiv:2607.20635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20635)) and Sabogal et al.'s "shape wall" ([arXiv:2607.21244](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21244)) closed room for both early- and late-time solvers; then Roy Choudhury ([arXiv:2607.24443](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24443)) reasserted a $6.85\sigma$ ladder tension that reanalysis doesn't soften. The tension is sharpening, not resolving.

- **Neutrino astronomy, two directions (heating up).** Chattopadhyay et al. ([arXiv:2607.19487](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19487)) put the UHE ANITA/KM3NeT-vs-IceCube tension at 7.5σ (5.9σ for rare transients); Cheek et al. ([arXiv:2607.20608](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20608)) used Earth-rotation timing to disfavor ($p\simeq0.13$) a DM-decay origin for KM3-230213A. IceCube's 5.7σ Galactic-plane detection ([arXiv:2607.25966](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25966)) is the constructive counterpart — the diffuse component those joint fits assume as background.

- **Direct detection as neutrino/solar-axion observatory (emerging).** A genuine coalescence: SNuDD solar-neutrino code ([arXiv:2607.22817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22817)), invisible-$\nu_2$-decay bounds from CEνNS rivaling SNO ([arXiv:2607.24584](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24584)), sub-percent flux framework ([arXiv:2607.24421](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24421)), plus solar-ALP repurposing of LZ/PandaX ([arXiv:2607.21168](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21168)). Meanwhile Higgsino-nucleon scattering was shown to sink below the neutrino floor ([arXiv:2607.20588](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20588)).

- **Lattice precision feeding flavor (steady).** First lattice QCD+QED SD correction to $R_{e/\mu}$ for PIONEER/NA62 ([arXiv:2607.22358](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22358)); proton momentum and spin sum rules both close at physical masses ([arXiv:2607.21230](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21230)); first gluon Collins-Soper kernel constraint ([arXiv:2607.24587](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24587)).

- **Flavor CKM ladder + agents (steady/heating).** BESIII $D\to\pi\ell\nu$ record $|V_{cd}|$ ([arXiv:2607.23945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23945)); NA62's $K^+\to\pi^+\nu\bar\nu$ propagated into $B\to K\nu\bar\nu$/$B_s\to\mu\mu$ ([arXiv:2607.24494](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24494)); Belle II's first $B^0\to K^0_S\tau^+\tau^-$ ([arXiv:2607.25607](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25607)). Automation thread past LQCDMaster: amplitude-as-program search ([arXiv:2607.21629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21629)), SMEFT-Pheno-Agent ([arXiv:2607.22331](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22331)), agentic SFitter ([arXiv:2607.22813](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22813)), with reproducibility as the selling point.

## Turning points

- **UHE tension quantified** ([arXiv:2607.19487](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19487)) — turned a hand-wave into 7.5σ, reframing all KM3-230213A follow-ups.
- **IceCube Galactic plane** ([arXiv:2607.25966](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25966)) — moves Galactic neutrino astronomy from search to measurement.
- **LHCb 32 Tbps real-time** ([arXiv:2607.21492](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21492)) — production endpoint of the month's real-time-reconstruction wave; a new throughput standard.
- **Hubble walls** ([arXiv:2607.20635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20635), [arXiv:2607.21244](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21244)) — shifted the conversation from solutions to constraints on solutions.
- **BESIII secondary-scattering polarimetry** ([arXiv:2607.19948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19948)) — a reusable method turning any spectrometer into a polarimeter.

## Open questions

- **UHE-neutrino origin** — 7.5σ tension unresolved; DM-decay disfavored but not excluded. Watch for new transient/diffuse fits.
- **Muon puzzle, quantified** — Auger's $X_{\max}$ deficit demands 2.8–4.6× hadronic-energy amplification ([arXiv:2607.22323](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22323)). Awaiting a hadronic-model response.
- **BBN deuterium discrepancy** ($\sim3\sigma$ under EDE) — hint of a very-early light scalar; needs independent D/H confirmation.
- **$b\to s$ new physics** — kaon→$B$ propagation and $B\to K\tau\tau$ limits circling; no anomaly yet.
- **$X_{17}$** — refined E141 recast ([arXiv:2607.22102](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22102)) tightens tension with ATOMKI's preliminary lifetime.
- **Direct-detection blind spot** — pure Higgsinos below the neutrino floor; how to probe?
