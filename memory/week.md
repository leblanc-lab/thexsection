# The Cross Section: Rolling Synthesis, week ending 2026-10-02

The LZ recoil remained the week's loudest thread, but its character changed. The early-week volume came back, then gave way to an elimination phase. Halo modelling and solar capture now decide which readings survive, and the survivors are drifting to very high masses. Two other threads gathered momentum. First, a run of "small" corrections turned out to be as large as quoted uncertainties, from a ρπ helicity convention to a −216 MeV top-mass shift. Second, precision QCD closed a milestone with the complete four-loop $P_{qq}$. ML has settled into infrastructure, and LLMs are now appearing *inside* calculations, which is new as a cluster. JUNO's indication of normal ordering was the week's firmest experimental result.

## Running themes

**LZ recoil: cooling in volume, sharpening in constraints.** Daily paper counts ran 0, 7, 5, 3, 3.
- *Halo turn (09-29/30):* Das et al. [arXiv:2609.35737](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35737) and Bozorgnia et al. [arXiv:2609.36184](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36184) show that Gaia/LMC velocity tails barely move exothermic fits. Endothermic fits are pushed to larger splittings. Oliveira [arXiv:2609.35650](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35650) shows LZ's intervals do not cover pseudo-Dirac halos.
- *Solar capture (10-01):* Luengas et al. [arXiv:2609.38316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38316) close most thermal multiplets under the Standard Halo Model. The LMC tail is the main escape.
- *Heavy survivors (10-02):* Hor et al. [arXiv:2610.01613](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01613) put ~$10^5$ GeV Higgsinos in SUSY SU(5), with $p\to K^+\bar\nu$ in Hyper-K/JUNO/DUNE reach. Khan et al. [arXiv:2610.00226](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00226) concede their LZ-matching branch is disfavoured.
- *Testable predictions accumulating:*
  - a COSI sub-MeV line [arXiv:2609.33952](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33952);
  - a 700–810 keV argon line [arXiv:2609.37561](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37561);
  - atomic-DM target pattern: Xe yes, Ar/Ge no [arXiv:2609.36814](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36814);
  - microsecond-spaced track recoils from charged gravitinos [arXiv:2609.39529](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39529).

**Precision QCD: heating up.**
- Moch completed the non-singlet four-loop splitting functions [arXiv:2609.34837](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34837). Gehrmann et al. completed the pure-singlet piece [arXiv:2609.37563](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37563), so $P_{qq}$ is done.
- Resummation-scale uncertainty entered PDF fits [arXiv:2609.35344](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35344), with a follow-up in [arXiv:2609.37556](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37556).
- Logarithm structure advanced on two fronts: super-leading logs got a Monte Carlo treatment [arXiv:2609.34984](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34984), and the first NLL non-global resummation for jet-vetoed Higgs appeared [arXiv:2609.35972](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35972).
- The week closed with N³LO Drell-Yan [arXiv:2610.02169](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02169). It uses a lattice Collins-Soper kernel and maximum-entropy positive weights, a different answer to the negative-weight problem than the optimal-transport papers [arXiv:2609.30357](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30357) and [arXiv:2609.30418](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30418).

## Turning points

- **JUNO** [arXiv:2609.37895](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37895) gives ~1% precision on $\Delta m^2_{31}$. Combined with long-baseline data, it favours normal ordering at ≥2.1σ for any CP phase (Bayes factor 10.2). This is an indication, not a determination.
- **α tension drops from 2.9σ to 2.1σ** after the helicity-convention correction [arXiv:2609.31828](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31828).
- **Top mass relation shifts by −216 MeV** [arXiv:2609.40229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40229), comparable to the experimental error.
- **The solar-capture bound** [arXiv:2609.38316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38316) is now the reference test for heavy-multiplet LZ fits.
- **LHCb beauty-strange state answered:** a lattice-fixed prediction [arXiv:2609.38022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38022) describes it as a $\bar BK$ bound state, effectively resolving its mass-deficit question. It also predicts the $1^+$ partner.
- **Dark Dimension graviton phenomenology** took two independent hits: KK-graviton decay suppression [arXiv:2609.36234](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36234), and $d$-wave threshold KK decays excluding flat-dimension graviton DM [arXiv:2610.01825](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01825).

## Open questions

- **LZ origin.** The live readings are exothermic/pseudo-Dirac, LMC-assisted endothermic, ~$10^5$ GeV Higgsino, and elastic routes [arXiv:2609.40234](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40234). Watch for argon, COSI and proton-decay checks, and for papers that ignore the Sun, such as [arXiv:2610.00645](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00645).
- **$X(2370)$.** Is it a $\phi h_1(1380)$ dynamical state [arXiv:2609.37653](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37653) or a fractional-determinant glueball [arXiv:2610.00484](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00484)? $\eta\eta\eta$ and $\eta\eta\eta'$ rates would discriminate.
- **Order of the chiral transition.** Pisarski [arXiv:2610.00472](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00472) claims it is generically second order, a "beyond Landau" argument. Watch for responses and 2+1 lattice checks.
- **Enhanced $K\nu\bar\nu$ rate.** It persists, but its interpretation is contested.
- **MicroBooNE proton multiplicities.** No generator describes them [arXiv:2609.40237](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40237), which bears on low-energy-excess modelling.
