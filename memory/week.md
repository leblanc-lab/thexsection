# The Cross Section: rolling synthesis, week ending 2026-11-25

This week the field audited its own error bars. Statistical coverage, GW-forecast priors and resummation, dark-matter halo assumptions, and superradiance cloud models were all shown to carry more hidden freedom than their quoted uncertainties imply. Two default references were replaced: the Higgs cross sections and the CKM angle γ. The toponium debate changed shape once interference was shown to be unavoidable. The AI-agent thread split cleanly into tasks whose output can be verified exactly and tasks whose output cannot. Integral technology and energy correlators were steady background activity.

## Running themes

- **GW phase-transition forecasts are fragile on both sides (heating up).**
  - *Data side:* a realistic SMBHB population cuts the PTA phase-transition preference from 2.8σ to 1.5σ ([arXiv:2611.23371](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23371)). It climbs back above 3σ if binaries take more than about 6.5 Gyr to merge. LVK constraints also depend strongly on the prior ([arXiv:2611.24625](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24625)).
  - *Theory side:* the choice of thermal-resummation prescription moves 2HDM GW peak amplitudes by up to nine orders of magnitude ([arXiv:2611.26417](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26417)). Proposed fixes are an improved partial-dressing scheme ([arXiv:2611.26303](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26303)) and an extension of dressing to gauge and ghost modes ([arXiv:2611.31359](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31359)). The latter removes the SM barrier entirely but warns it is not quantitatively reliable there.
  - *Trajectory:* the identified weak link keeps moving upstream, from hydrodynamics to the effective potential itself.

- **Superradiance clouds keep shrinking (consolidating).** Three independent mechanisms now cap or deplete the clouds:
  - Foster's gravitational Auger effect (11-17).
  - Self-interactions ([arXiv:2611.23384](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23384)).
  - Self-gravity, which dominates above $f_a\gtrsim6\times10^{16}$ GeV ([arXiv:2611.26541](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26541)).

  The consensus is that free-field cloud models are never realized, so black-hole spin bounds on axions need redoing. A binary companion partly offsets the depletion, and a companion-sourced Yukawa force can halt cloud growth ([arXiv:2611.29939](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29939)). Environment is now part of the bound.

- **AI agents: verifiability is the dividing line (heating up).**
  - Alimena's evidence-over-code standard ([arXiv:2611.23330](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23330)) became the week's reference point.
  - Claude Opus 5.5 produced new Feynman-integral bases that could be checked exactly ([arXiv:2611.26688](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26688)). CANON repeats the agent-plus-deterministic-verifier design ([arXiv:2611.33100](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33100)).
  - On analysis tasks, the same model scored 31% of the expert reference on the sealed HEP-Ex-Bench ([arXiv:2611.28194](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28194)), against Gemini 4 Argon's 60%. The lasting contribution there is the injection-test design.
  - Separately, sparse-autoencoder and agent edits were used to repair an infrared-safety failure in a jet foundation model ([arXiv:2611.27079](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27079)).

- **Toponium versus pseudoscalar becomes an interference problem (turning).** See the Turning points section. The broader idea is that amplitude zeros and dips act as UV diagnostics ([arXiv:2611.27681](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27681)). Steerability at low $M_{t\bar t}$ isolates a singlet-dominated threshold state ([arXiv:2611.23368](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23368)).

- **Dark-matter assumptions audited (new this week).**
  - Halo anisotropy moves LZ limits by up to 10× below 15 GeV ([arXiv:2611.29197](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29197)).
  - Dropping kinetic equilibrium shifts the couplings needed at the Higgs resonance by up to 10× ([arXiv:2611.28230](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28230)).
  - Two near-identical photon-mediated LZ model templates ([arXiv:2611.25931](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25931), [arXiv:2611.26274](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26274)) skip solar capture entirely.

- **Steady threads:**
  - Nanosecond-scale, signature-agnostic L1 triggers ([arXiv:2611.28355](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28355), [arXiv:2611.30896](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30896)), now confirmed running in Run 3 via AXOL1TL/GELATO.
  - EFT fitting infrastructure ([arXiv:2611.28216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28216), [arXiv:2611.30272](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30272)).
  - Lepton-collider and HVP generators: MiNNLO ([arXiv:2611.26718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26718)), YFS+NLO, which disagrees with KKMC by up to 2% ([arXiv:2611.27883](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27883)), and McMule $K\bar K$ ([arXiv:2611.31080](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31080)).

## Turning points

- **Higgs Report 5 supersedes YR4** ([arXiv:2611.26281](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26281)). PDFs now lead the uncertainty, and the gluon-fusion PDF-order mismatch uncertainty is doubled.
- **LHCb γ = 63.2°⁺²·³₋₂.₂** ([arXiv:2611.26449](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26449)), with world-leading charm mixing.
- **Toponium and $A$ must interfere** ([arXiv:2611.27704](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27704)). Adding the two templates separately is wrong by construction. The same complex $\kappa$ now governs heavy MSSM $H/A\to VV, hh, Zh$ searches ([arXiv:2611.09666](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09666)).
- **ATLAS limits over-cover** ([arXiv:2611.24065](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24065)): 99.2% of observed limits fall inside the 2σ band, while CMS matches nominal coverage. Expected limits computed after a fit to data are a leading suspect. The authors' cheap fix is for publications to state how expected limits were computed.
- **The KPW rule is under strain**: a 6.3σ overshoot in $\Xi_c^0\to pK^-$ ([arXiv:2611.27716](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27716)). The paper lists eight $\Lambda_c$ modes that would decide whether KPW survives.

## Open questions

- Which of the three explanations drives ATLAS over-coverage, and what does the answer mean for the open-excess list, including the new 3.5σ instanton-like entry?
- Keshet's WIMP line triads ([arXiv:2611.21595](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21595)) give 2.8σ from the blind scan alone. The quoted 6.1σ requires combining with the author's earlier samples. Watch for independent checks.
- The $V_{ud}$ value from ¹⁰C with three-body δ_NS ([arXiv:2611.24825](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24825)) is not in the abstract. Compare it with Novario's coupled-cluster result (11-17), which came in 30% below QMC.
- For the 248 keV LZ event, anisotropic halos make splittings kinematically accessible, so solar-neutrino bounds on the higgsino reading may weaken. The authors stress that access does not establish viability.
- Mass ordering:
  - T2K with Gd still favours normal ordering, near-maximal CP violation and, slightly, the upper octant, but quotes no significances ([arXiv:2611.32106](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32106)).
  - The global hint stands at 1.9σ.
  - $\nu_\mu$ disappearance carries a genuine T-odd term in asymmetric matter ([arXiv:2611.27985](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27985)).
  - SBND's νe cross section prefers generators that predict lower values ([arXiv:2611.25098](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25098)).
- Spectroscopy:
  - Interference among known pseudoscalars undercuts BESIII's extra X(2800) and the glueball reading of X(2370) ([arXiv:2611.24372](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24372)).
  - The $D_{s1}(2460)$ radiative ratio is predicted at 0.57, below Belle's 0.74±0.08 ([arXiv:2611.27363](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27363)).
- Should an extended $t\bar t$ threshold fit using $\kappa$ be expected from the experiments?
