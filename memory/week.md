# The Cross Section: rolling synthesis, week ending 2026-09-30

The LZ recoil was the week's dominant thread, with five to seven papers on most days. By the week's end the conversation had moved from the particle content to the halo. The firmest new measurement is JUNO's indication of normal ordering. Around it, the week was a series of careful re-analyses that lowered tensions rather than raising them: 3+1 steriles fell to 2.7σ and the α tension fell to 2.1σ, while inclusive |V_cb| got a 0.7% anchor. Precision QCD completed the four-loop quark splitting functions within two days. Meanwhile ML and LLM work increasingly reads as infrastructure, with LLMs appearing *inside* calculations for the first time as a cluster.

## Turning points

- **JUNO ([arXiv:2609.37895](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37895)):** Δm²₃₁ is now known to about 1%. Combined with long-baseline data it favors normal ordering at ≥2.1σ for any CP phase (Bayes factor 10.2). This is an indication, not a determination.
- **Woodward et al. ([arXiv:2609.26965](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26965)):** The 3+1 preference drops to 2.7σ, and MiniBooNE and MicroBooNE disagree with each other at ≥2.5σ inside the model.
- **Oliveira ([arXiv:2609.35650](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35650)):** LZ's published intervals do not cover pseudo-Dirac halos. This moved the LZ discussion toward halo composition.
- **Žlebčík ([arXiv:2609.31828](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31828)):** Fixing a convention mismatch takes the direct-versus-indirect α tension from 2.9σ to 2.1σ, giving α = (87.0^{+11.8}_{−4.7})°.
- **Albaladejo et al. ([arXiv:2609.38022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38022)):** A lattice-fixed prediction made without LHCb input lands within 1.1–1.7σ of LHCb's beauty-strange state. It reads the state as a B̄K bound state and predicts a 1⁺ partner.
- **Yin et al. ([arXiv:2609.30335](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30335)):** Neutron-star survival arguments now exclude a finite *window* of DM triggering rates, a new bound shape.

## Open questions

- **LZ: halo effect or particle?** Watch for the testable predictions:
  - A sub-MeV line at E_γ ≈ δ within COSI's reach ([arXiv:2609.33952](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33952)).
  - A 700–810 keV argon line ([arXiv:2609.37561](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37561)).
  - Atomic DM, which predicts nothing on Ar or Ge ([arXiv:2609.36814](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36814)).
  - The fine-tuning and near-Landau-pole couplings in the heavy scenarios.
- **The MiniBooNE/MicroBooNE internal inconsistency** is left unexplained.
- **|V_cb|:** The inclusive fit to the full moment set is poor, and predicted B widths sit 1.1–1.2σ below measurement. The authors call for new correlated moment measurements.
- **Can sterile-neutrino DM be produced?** Resonant νMSM ([arXiv:2609.28457](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28457)) versus the suppression claims ([arXiv:2609.29911](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29911), [arXiv:2609.31340](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31340)).
- **Mass ordering:** Watch for combined fits that push past ~2σ.
- **Smaller threads worth follow-ups:**
  - Final-state energy loss in small systems ([arXiv:2609.29886](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29886)).
  - The D-term sign ([arXiv:2609.33151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33151)).
  - A caution against the entanglement-entropy picture of DIS ([arXiv:2609.31499](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31499)).
  - ALICE Dπ femtoscopy fit-window dependence ([arXiv:2609.35133](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35133)).
  - Dark Dimension cascades, argued ruled out ([arXiv:2609.36234](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36234)).
