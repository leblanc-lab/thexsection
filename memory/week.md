# The Cross Section: rolling synthesis, week ending 2026-10-01

The LZ recoil was again the week's main thread, but the argument changed. Early in the week papers escaped solar capture by moving the dark-matter mass. By midweek the debate had turned to the halo velocity distribution, ending with a general solar-capture bound that the Large Magellanic Cloud (LMC) tail partly reopens. JUNO delivered the firmest experimental result: a first indication of normal ordering. Precision QCD closed a milestone with complete four-loop quark splitting functions. A late-week pattern emerged of "small" corrections as large as the quoted errors. ML moved further toward infrastructure, and LLM agents started appearing inside the calculations themselves.

## Running themes

**LZ recoil: from particle content to halo inputs (heating, shifting ground).** The week's arc runs as follows:
- Mass-scale escapes from solar capture came first, via a heavy inert doublet ([arXiv:2609.28819](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28819)) and PeV Higgsinos ([arXiv:2609.28740](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28740)).
- A one-day lull on 09-28 followed.
- Halo-driven readings came next. A surviving excited-state fraction cuts the required cross sections by up to $10^4$ ([arXiv:2609.35650](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35650)). Gaia halos penalize endothermic fits ([arXiv:2609.35737](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35737)), and the LMC tail pushes them to larger splittings ([arXiv:2609.36184](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36184)).
- Under the Standard Halo Model, the general-representation solar bound ([arXiv:2609.38316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38316)) excludes most thermal multiplets. An LMC-like tail revives many of them.
- The thread is generating falsifiable side predictions:
  - a sub-MeV line within COSI's reach ([arXiv:2609.33952](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33952));
  - a 700–810 keV argon line ([arXiv:2609.37561](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37561));
  - an atomic-DM target pattern of xenon near threshold, tungsten open, and no signal on Ar/Ge ([arXiv:2609.36814](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36814));
  - microsecond-spaced track recoils ([arXiv:2609.39529](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39529)).

**Flavor and CKM tensions (steady, increasingly bookkeeping-driven).**
- An inclusive $|V_{cb}|=(42.32\pm0.30)\times10^{-3}$ ([arXiv:2609.28752](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28752)) anchors the inclusive/exclusive puzzle behind $R(D^{(*)})$ ([arXiv:2608.24332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24332)).
- The $V_{ud}$/first-row thread continued ([arXiv:2609.31834](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31834), [arXiv:2609.32873](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32873)).
- $R(D^{(*)})$ is now being linked to enhanced $B^+\to K^+\nu\bar\nu$ through $b\to s\tau\tau$ ([arXiv:2609.37146](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37146) → [arXiv:2609.40072](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40072)).

**Precision QCD (heating).**
- The four-loop non-singlet splitting functions are complete ([arXiv:2609.34837](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34837)), and the pure-singlet $P_{qq}$ followed a day later ([arXiv:2609.37563](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37563)).
- Resummation-scale uncertainty now enters PDF fits ([arXiv:2609.35344](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35344), [arXiv:2609.37556](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37556)).
- The MARZILI line runs from super-leading logs ([arXiv:2609.34984](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34984)) to NLL non-global jet vetoes ([arXiv:2609.35972](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35972)).

**Corrections the size of errors (emerging, 09-29 onward).** Several convention, matching or statistics choices turned out to move results by about their quoted uncertainty:
- the ρπ helicity mismatch ([arXiv:2609.31828](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31828));
- a −216 MeV mixed QCD-EW shift in the top mass relation ([arXiv:2609.40229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40229));
- an 11–13% shift in lattice gluon moments ([arXiv:2609.40205](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40205));
- Neyman-χ² bias in ratio fits ([arXiv:2609.40019](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40019));
- a >30% shift in NA62 dark-photon acceptance ([arXiv:2609.40052](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40052)).

Watch whether this becomes a recurring editorial lens.

**ML as infrastructure, LLMs as collaborators (heating).**
- Fast-simulation surrogates are maturing: a public FCC-ee CLD surrogate ([arXiv:2609.30775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30775)) and CaloClouds transfer with few showers ([arXiv:2609.28661](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28661), [arXiv:2609.30403](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30403)).
- Thaler's largely agent-written stocktake ([arXiv:2609.32874](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32874)) frames HEPML as infrastructure.
- A new cluster puts LLMs inside calculations: Claude-built NLO $V$+jet ([arXiv:2609.37238](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37238)) and LLM-designed IR subtraction ([arXiv:2609.36007](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36007)). It then extended to model space with hAIthem ([arXiv:2609.38309](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38309)).

## Turning points

- **JUNO** ([arXiv:2609.37895](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37895)) measures $\Delta m^2_{31}$ to about 1%. Combined with long-baseline constraints it favors normal ordering at ≥2.1σ for any CP phase, with a Bayes factor of 10.2. This is an indication, not a determination.
- **The α tension drops from 2.9σ to 2.1σ** after a helicity-convention fix ([arXiv:2609.31828](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31828)).
- **The general solar-capture bound** ([arXiv:2609.38316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38316)) is now the reference test for heavy-multiplet LZ fits.
- **The LHCb beauty-strange state was predicted from lattice-constrained coupled channels** with no LHCb input. It lands within 1.1–1.7σ of the observed peak, as a narrow $\bar BK$ bound state ([arXiv:2609.38022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38022)).
- **The four-loop $P_{qq}$ is complete** ([arXiv:2609.37563](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37563)), direct input to N³LO evolution.

## Open questions

- **LZ origin.** Is the event particle physics or halo physics? Much now hinges on how robust the LMC tail is. Watch for COSI, argon and tungsten follow-ups on the predicted signatures.
- **Inclusive $|V_{cb}|$ fit quality.** The fit to the full moment set is poor, and predicted $B$ widths sit 1.1–1.2σ below measurement. The authors call for new correlated moment measurements ([arXiv:2609.28752](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28752)).
- **The $R(D^{(*)})$ + $K\nu\bar\nu$ picture.** Will $b\to s\tau\tau$ searches test the favored SMEFT regions ([arXiv:2609.40072](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40072))?
- **The residual 2.1σ α tension** after the convention fix ([arXiv:2609.31828](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31828)).
- **Top mass.** How does the −216 MeV shift ([arXiv:2609.40229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40229)) propagate into $m_t$ interpretations?
- **keV–MeV sterile-neutrino DM production is squeezed from two sides:**
  - plasma effects suppress freeze-in ([arXiv:2609.29911](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29911));
  - active oscillations can erase the lepton asymmetry needed for resonant production ([arXiv:2609.31340](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31340)).
- **Generator modelling.** No generator describes MicroBooNE's proton multiplicities ([arXiv:2609.40237](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40237)), which bears on the low-energy excess.
