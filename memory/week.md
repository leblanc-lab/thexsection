# The Cross Section — Rolling Synthesis, week ending 2026-09-11

The week belonged, again, to one event: the LZ 248 keV recoil (LZ230616), which occupied roughly a quarter of daily output and drove a visible arc from model-fitting to falsifiability to outright evasion. The endothermic higgsino died definitively; the thermal higgsino was exiled to 10⁵–10⁶ GeV; and by week's end the survivors are light-mediator, boosted, and two-component readings scrambling to dodge a solar-neutrino bound that keeps being reconfirmed and generalized. Around this spine, three quieter threads gained real weight: a widening axion program (now spanning lab haloscopes, high-mass cavities, and cosmology), a maturing collider quantum-tomography effort, and steady heavy-ion jet-quenching methodology.

## Running themes

- **The LZ event — HEATING, but transforming.** The register shifted twice this week. First from fits to *tests* (09-09): the model zoo saturated, so the useful papers became collider bridges (Cheung disappearing tracks [arXiv:2609.08712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08712), Kotlarski FCC-hh Z′ [arXiv:2609.06750](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06750)) and argon/directional discriminants. Then from tests to *evasion* (09-11): Qi's $U(1)_{B-3L_\tau}$ light mediator suppressing solar iron capture [arXiv:2609.10636](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10636), Heikinheimo's cosmic-ray-boosted momentum-dependent operators [arXiv:2609.11600](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11600), Fan's two-component exothermic [arXiv:2609.10491](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10491). The exothermic pivot (Dent [arXiv:2609.04673](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04673), de Lima [arXiv:2609.05204](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05204), Gu [arXiv:2609.05291](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05291)) remains the most-cited escape from halo-tail fragility.
- **Axions — HEATING and widening.** Started as a lattice/strong-CP thread; this week grew a detection wing (Lee HTS cavity 1.3×KSVZ [arXiv:2609.08144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08144), Kim TM₀₂₀ higher-order-mode haloscope [arXiv:2609.09717](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09717)) and a cosmology cluster (Luu relativistic-oscillation EFT [arXiv:2609.11055](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11055)/[arXiv:2609.11056](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11056), Gemmell resolving JWST limits to exclude QCD axion DM at 0.5–2.5 eV [arXiv:2609.10674](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10674), Hook Kalb–Ramond ultralight DM [arXiv:2609.10671](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10671)).
- **Collider quantum information — STEADY/building.** CMS's >5σ J/ψ photon-polarization asymmetry [arXiv:2609.04376](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04376) opened the week; Kanachova extended entanglement certification to qubit-qutrit ($\Lambda\phi$, $\Lambda K^{*0}$) hadronic states [arXiv:2609.11151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11151) by week's end.
- **Jet quenching — STEADY.** Bossi+Mehtar-Tani established color-decoherence EEC evidence [arXiv:2609.05609](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05609)/[arXiv:2609.05614](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05614); Casalderrey-Solana's "Efficient Wake" [arXiv:2609.10679](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10679) supplies the fast jet-medium-response tool to exploit it in MC.
- **ML/autodiff & precision lattice — STEADY background.** Recurring tooling: self-supervised transformer tagging at LHCb [arXiv:2609.09275](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09275), differentiable photon transport (candela [arXiv:2609.04695](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04695)). Lattice/EFT precision kept producing quotable numbers — deuteron polarizability [arXiv:2609.04615](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04615), nucleon strong-interaction radius 0.94 fm [arXiv:2609.09318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09318), heavy-strange decay constants [arXiv:2609.11026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11026).
- **LLP borrowed-detector thread — FADING.** Strong 09-07 cluster (Belle II [arXiv:2609.04332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04332), KM3NeT [arXiv:2609.04386](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04386), Majoron in 0νββ [arXiv:2609.05352](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05352), Wang's flavor tomography [arXiv:2609.04761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04761)); no follow-up since.

## Turning points

- **Endothermic higgsino excluded from every direction** — Bose pushing the splitting bound to ≳557 keV [arXiv:2609.07807](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07807), Di Mauro confirming the thermal higgsino dies [arXiv:2609.06760](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06760).
- **Langhoff's exile** [arXiv:2609.09385](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09385): the thermal-higgsino reading survives only at 10⁵–10⁶ GeV via early-matter-domination production (Kim–Nilles), tying LZ to strong CP — the moment the "ordinary" higgsino ran out of room.
- **Chattaraj** [arXiv:2609.10504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10504) ruled out all neutrino-scattering explanations of the recoil, closing the mundane alternative.
- **Nguyen** [arXiv:2609.11833](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11833) reconfirmed and *generalized* the Pospelov–Ramani solar-neutrino bound across the whole inelastic-DM class — the constraint every escape route now has to beat.
- **Gemmell** [arXiv:2609.10674](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10674) resolved the JWST decaying-axion disagreement, converting a muddle into a clean exclusion.

## Open questions

- **Which LZ escape survives?** Exothermic, cosmic-ray-boosted, and light-mediator readings all now claim halo/solar robustness. The proposed discriminant is target scaling — argon and germanium predictions (de Lima, Baer's clean argon [arXiv:2609.06153](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06153), Khan's argon target-scaling test [arXiv:2609.09230](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09230)). Watch for the first non-xenon data or a decisive kinematic split.
- **Directional/coincidence signatures**: Asadi's coincident photon at energy δ [arXiv:2609.09107](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09107) remains a distinctive, untested handle.
- **Toponium vs scalar in $t\bar t$**: Bellagroudi's top-spin discriminant [arXiv:2609.11641](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11641) made the debate quantitative but unresolved.
- **$\Xi_c^0\to\Xi^- e^+\nu$**: persistent ~4% theory/lattice-vs-experiment discrepancy flagged by Ni [arXiv:2609.09802](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09802).
