# The Cross Section — Weekly Synthesis, week ending 2026-09-09

One event dominated the week: the LZ collaboration's single 248 keV nuclear recoil (2.6σ, [arXiv:2609.02823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02823)) drove roughly a quarter of daily output through three distinct phases. It opened as an endothermic-higgsino interpretation glut, was systematically shot down by astrophysical and kinematic bounds, pivoted to exothermic scattering to buy back robustness, and by week's end had matured into a search for *falsifiable* signatures — colliders, argon, directional photons. Around this gravity well, genuinely new threads surfaced (jet-quenching color decoherence, a live axion-detection wing), while the season's quantum-information and gluon-tomography programs held steady.

## Running themes

**The LZ event — heating, but transformed.** The arc is the story of the week. Endothermic higgsino (~1.1 TeV, ~300–370 keV splitting) converged early, then died from every direction: Pospelov's solar-capture δ>566 keV ([arXiv:2609.02775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02775)), Rodd's empty high-energy sideband ([arXiv:2609.04175](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04175)), McCabe's runaway annual modulation ([arXiv:2609.04181](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04181)), and re-executed by Bose et al. ([arXiv:2609.07807](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07807), δ≳557 keV) and Di Mauro et al. ([arXiv:2609.06760](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06760)). The field escaped via exothermic scattering ([arXiv:2609.04673](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04673), Dent et al.) and boosted DM, then toward tests: Cheung et al.'s disappearing tracks ([arXiv:2609.08712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08712)), Kotlarski's FCC-hh Z′ link ([arXiv:2609.06750](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06750)), Asadi et al.'s coincident-photon directional handle ([arXiv:2609.09107](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09107)). Model-building has saturated; watch for the collaboration's response to the sideband/modulation checks.

**Strong CP + axions — heating, now with a detection wing.** The lattice hadronic-coupling thread continued (He et al.'s second direct θ-term ḡ₀, [arXiv:2609.04129](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04129)). By week's end it gained real experimental limits: Lee et al.'s HTS haloscope at 1.3×KSVZ, 15.6 μeV ([arXiv:2609.08144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08144)), Winter et al.'s axion-EDM NMR bound ([arXiv:2609.07908](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07908)), and Levasseur et al.'s RGB-star axion-electron bound ([arXiv:2609.08504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08504)). REBCO/HTS cavity tech ([arXiv:2609.03879](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03879)) is enabling this.

**Gluon tomography & QCD structure — steady.** The fiducial-recoil pair ([arXiv:2609.02280](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02280), [arXiv:2609.02356](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02356)) offered a transferable TMD-projector trick. CMS's >5σ spin-dependent azimuthal asymmetry in coherent J/ψ photoproduction ([arXiv:2609.04376](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04376)) added a new polarization handle.

**Jet quenching — new, promising.** A late data-plus-theory pairing: Bossi et al. ([arXiv:2609.05609](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05609)) show EEC+R_AA demands color decoherence to restore Casimir scaling, with Mehtar-Tani's generating-functional machinery ([arXiv:2609.05614](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05614)). A clean new thread to watch.

**Quantum information as a probe — steady/uncertain.** Entanglement recruited as a symmetry diagnostic (Kanemura et al. [arXiv:2609.02691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02691); Jangid et al. [arXiv:2609.02467](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02467)), with Haddadi's skeptical comment ([arXiv:2609.01686](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01686)) as healthy caution. MadSpin2's spin-density rebuild ([arXiv:2609.02794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02794)) is the tooling counterpart. Went quiet after Sep 3.

## Turning points

- **Pospelov's solar-capture bound ([arXiv:2609.02775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02775))** flipped the LZ conversation from model-building to model-killing overnight.
- **Dent et al. ([arXiv:2609.04673](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04673))** switched the interpretation's sign to exothermic, recovering halo robustness.
- **Ortega-Gama et al. ([arXiv:2609.04142](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04142))** — first two-hadron form factor from QCD, a finite-volume milestone.
- **NOvA's joint ν/ν̄ sterile search ([arXiv:2609.08900](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08900))** excludes most of IceCube's allowed region — a decisive null.

## Open questions

- Will LZ's own follow-up (annual modulation, sideband population) confirm or kill the exothermic reading? McCabe predicts >50% modulation testable at XLZD/PandaX.
- Can any surviving iDM scenario produce a *coincident* collider or directional signature (Cheung disappearing tracks; Asadi photon-at-δ) before the model zoo collapses under its own degeneracy?
- The persistent event-shape α_s spread — the Badea et al. task force ([arXiv:2609.03002](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03002)) is now convened ahead of FCC.
- Does color decoherence in jet quenching hold as more R_AA+EEC data arrives?
