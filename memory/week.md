# The Cross Section: rolling synthesis, week ending 2026-10-22

This week the 248 keV LZ debate stopped being about which model survives the solar bound. It became a question of whether the inputs hold: first the halo tail (10-16, 10-22), then LZ's own electron-recoil background (10-21). Precision results kept siding with the SM:

- the gallium anomaly dissolved against a solar calibration;
- lattice HVP firmed up at ~1%;
- two independent $\mathcal O(g^6)$ hot-Yang-Mills pressures matched the lattice.

The error-budget thread moved from lesson to policy. Two smaller trends held steady: SU(3)$_F$ breaking as the flavor pressure point, and jet taggers shrinking toward ParT-level performance at a fraction of the size. Notable one-offs were a 5.1σ observation of neutron weak magnetism and a frontal refutation of observable RG running of neutrino mixing.

## Running themes

**LZ 248 keV event: heating up, with the focus moving from model-building to the inputs.**
- *Halo-tail rescue.* Graham et al. [arXiv:2610.21041](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21041) showed the thermal Higgsino survives solar capture only with a high-velocity tail (~1100 km/s). X. Zhang et al. [arXiv:2610.28731](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28731) then used Gaia plus a FIRE-2 LMC analog and found no local DM above ~900 km/s. They also found the inferred splitting shifts by up to ~130 keV with the tail.
- *Background audit.* Bandyopadhyay et al. [arXiv:2610.27105](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27105) found LZ's calibration does not constrain the ER tail at the relevant probabilities. Plausible tails make the ER contribution comparable to the whole quoted background. This is the first paper to question the event's significance itself.
- *Models still appearing.* Model papers continued at a lower rate: the heavy pseudo-Dirac doublet [arXiv:2610.29335](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29335) and the hexaquark reading [arXiv:2610.25925](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25925). No model papers appeared on 10-19.
- *Galactic Center link.* A shared GCE–LZ origin was argued against by Abazajian et al. [arXiv:2610.23713](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23713), on foreground dependence. One day later it was built anyway in a dark-Higgs model [arXiv:2610.25423](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25423). Kim et al.'s dSph-vs-GCE comparison [arXiv:2610.26648](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26648) leaves only τ-rich and light-mediator $4b$ corridors.

**Anomalies meeting their inputs: steady, and mostly resolving toward the SM.**
- Gallium null: [arXiv:2610.22219](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22219).
- Neutrino-anomaly review: [arXiv:2610.23474](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23474) concludes several tensions weakened, with no uncontested explanation.
- HVP: ETMC [arXiv:2610.22224](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22224) and a QCD sum rule [arXiv:2610.20945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20945) both sit on the lattice side. New attacks on the lattice-vs-data-driven gap came via $a_e$–$a_\mu$ correlations [arXiv:2610.26130](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26130) and an isospin-breaking $\rho\pi\pi$ correction for the τ route [arXiv:2610.28506](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28506).

**Error budgets and precision QCD: heating up.**
- The Aspen writeup [arXiv:2610.25091](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25091) turns the 10-12 cold-QED N³LO lesson into a prescription: go beyond 7-point scale variation.
- The same thread reached $0\nu\beta\beta$. Radiative corrections raise the rate by ~5% and bias $\xi_{31}$ [arXiv:2610.28742](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28742).
- N³LO building blocks arrived: three-loop masters [arXiv:2610.20820](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20820) and rapidity beam functions [arXiv:2610.22533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22533).

**SU(3)$_F$ breaking and $b\to s\ell\ell$: steady.**
- The 10-15 $B\to DP$ strain (~60% breaking) recurred in the $\Xi_b^-\to\Xi^-\mu\mu$ normalisation [arXiv:2610.22452](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22452).
- It also motivated first-order U-spin sum rules [arXiv:2610.24975](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24975).
- Crivellin et al. [arXiv:2610.22490](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22490) closed the $|V_{cb}|$/CKM-unitarity escape route for $C_9$.

**Tagger compression: heating up.**
- SParTA [arXiv:2610.27106](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27106) matches ParT with one-seventh the parameters.
- A distilled 2k-parameter L-GATr-slim [arXiv:2610.29439](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29439) also matches ParT.
- Trigger-level LEAF [arXiv:2610.27078](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27078) runs at 35 ns.

## Turning points

- **Hot Yang-Mills $g^6$ pressure closed.** Two calculations, [arXiv:2610.23675](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23675) and [arXiv:2610.23676](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23676), agree with each other and with the lattice. This is a direct pass for the dimensional-reduction EFT, unlike recent "next order" results that mainly exposed error budgets.
- **Top threshold excess deflated in a global fit.** Ball et al. [arXiv:2610.21479](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21479) find nothing at 8 TeV and just over 2σ at 13 TeV, against CMS's 6.1σ toponium claim. The strong $\Delta$–$m_t$ correlation means any threshold significance must be quoted jointly with $m_t$.
- **Weak magnetism observed in the neutron.** The reanalysis [arXiv:2610.26792](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26792) gives $g_M=4.47(87)$, excluding zero at 5.1σ. The result is robust to the neutron-dataset tensions.
- **Observable neutrino-mixing RG running refuted.** Barenboim et al. [arXiv:2610.28744](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28744) target a whole class of signatures and add a short-baseline bound of 0.7°–2.4° on angle change between production and detection.
- **Halo tail measured.** [arXiv:2610.28731](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28731) is now the halo input every 248 keV paper needs.

## Open questions

- **LZ.** Will LZ or others perform the ER-tail calibrations proposed in [arXiv:2610.27105](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27105)? Does Graham's Higgsino rescue survive the Gaia-anchored tail, or do rebuttals contest the reconstruction?
- **HVP gap.** The gap between lattice and pre-CMD3 data-driven values widened. Watch whether τ-based inputs close it.
- **NA61/SHINE kaon isospin excess.** The charged-over-neutral excess in Ar+Sc [arXiv:2610.23498](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23498) is unexplained by known mechanisms and consistent from 2.6 to 200 GeV. Uncertainties are large, so confirmation from other experiments is the test.
- **Neutrino mass ordering.** Gluza et al. [arXiv:2610.27270](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27270) find most mixing schemes fit IO better. This came the same day Jin et al.'s 3.3σ normal-ordering result was replaced. Track the revised result.
- **Mild IceCube LIV hint.** HESE–GRB timing gives a 1.9σ preference [arXiv:2610.25214](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25214). It is weak, but worth tracking against the earlier decoherence exclusion.
- **Expected replies.** Watch for responses to [arXiv:2610.28744](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28744), and for whether the CMS toponium framing adapts to [arXiv:2610.21479](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21479).
- **Exotics.** Glueball readings have weakened twice this month: the X(2370) reassessment, then the η′ fit [arXiv:2610.25216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25216). The all-charm $4P$ tetraquark prediction [arXiv:2610.22914](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22914) stands against the 10-14 no-exotics di-$J/\psi$ fit.
