# Rolling synthesis — week ending 2026-09-22

The week's gravitational center stayed in **precision QCD** — anchored Monday by the four-loop non-singlet splitting functions and reinforced daily by multiloop form factors and DIS moments — while the field's most-watched anomaly, **the LZ 248 keV recoil**, completed a full register-shift: from halo-kinematic fits, through dark-sector cosmology, to bounds and complementarity, and finally to confining/composite-sector readings by Friday. Flavor experiments provided the sharpest single data points (Belle II's R(D*), LHCb's Ωcc⁺), and the season's tooling/automation ethos kept spreading — into statistics infrastructure, thermal field theory, and even LLM-generated FORM code.

## Running themes

- **Precision QCD, multiloop & DIS machinery (heating, dominant).** The through-line of the week. Gehrmann et al.'s complete four-loop non-singlet splitting functions [arXiv:2609.17267](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17267) opened it; the momentum carried through b-jets at NNLO+PS [arXiv:2609.17812](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17812), NLO EW in gg→γγ [arXiv:2609.18828](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18828), and closed the week on Blümlein et al.'s gluonic three-loop massive form factors [arXiv:2609.22034](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22034) with their large-moment method paper [arXiv:2609.24279](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24279), Kidonakis's four-loop massive cusp [arXiv:2609.23247](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23247), and the first two-loop t̄tW amplitude [arXiv:2609.24910](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24910).

- **The LZ recoil, a moving interpretation target (steady volume, shifting register).** The clearest trajectory of the week. Early: dynamically-selected endo/exo via dark-sector cosmology (An et al. [arXiv:2609.17412](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17412)); mid-week: asymmetric-DM escape from IceCube bounds (Nagata et al. [arXiv:2609.18564](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18564)); Monday: firm constraints via solar capture (Bose et al. [arXiv:2609.21823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21823)) and halo-velocity limits (O'Hare [arXiv:2609.21444](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21444)); Friday: composite/confining-sector readings (Sannino et al. [arXiv:2609.24988](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24988), Sheng et al. [arXiv:2609.23477](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23477)). Nuclear-response caveats recurred throughout (Ge et al. [arXiv:2609.16529](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16529); Zurowski et al. [arXiv:2609.24764](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24764)).

- **Heavy-flavor at the experiments (heating).** Charm-CPV SM bound narrowed (Mohamed et al. [arXiv:2609.16219](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16219)); the August B→Kπ⁺π⁻ CP flare made mechanistic via resonance interference (Guo et al. [arXiv:2609.21464](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21464)); then Belle II's most precise hadronic-tag R(D*) [arXiv:2609.21947](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21947) and LHCb's Ωcc⁺ discovery [arXiv:2609.21921](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21921).

- **Nucleon structure / GPDs / EIC (steady).** A dense, persistent stack: Guo et al.'s first GPD review in two decades [arXiv:2609.19544](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19544), and Su et al. extending lattice computability into the x∼±ξ region with a first-seen threshold kink [arXiv:2609.20545](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20545).

- **Tooling & automation (steady, broadening).** The autodiff/automation wave matured into the statistics layer (RooFit-AD/HS3, IRIS-HEP blueprint [arXiv:2609.19992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19992); Dorigo's covariance-ordering warning [arXiv:2609.20007](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20007)), reached thermal field theory (AutoTherm [arXiv:2609.20936](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20936)), and surfaced in LLM FORM code generation [arXiv:2609.23367](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23367).

## Turning points

- **Benmansour et al. [arXiv:2609.18383](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18383)** — cosmic-ray muon coincidences reproduce the Atomki opening-angle peaks at cosmic-to-signal ratio above unity; the strongest challenge yet to the 17 MeV boson, shifting the burden to beam-off studies.
- **ATLAS [arXiv:2609.19948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19948)** — first direct, coupling-independent Higgs width bound Γ_H<307 MeV from on-shell H→γγ interference.
- **Belle II R(D*) [arXiv:2609.21947](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21947)** — central value nudges the LFU tension *down*, not up; neither sharpens nor kills it.
- **Lin et al. [arXiv:2609.22698](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22698)** — first-principles lattice axion-photon coupling C_QCD=1.965(35), resolving a standing NLO ChPT discrepancy; a benchmark for axion searches.
- **ATLAS top-philic Z′ null [arXiv:2609.24950](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24950)** — excludes m_Z′<1150 GeV, the vector-resonance handle the toponium-vs-scalar debate needed.

## Open questions

- **LFU / R(D*):** left unresolved — the anomaly survives but is not reinforced. *The* flavor thread to watch for follow-up combinations.
- **Nature of the LZ recoil:** endo vs exo, elastic vs inelastic, composite vs portal — now compounded by nuclear form-factor/shell-model uncertainties [arXiv:2609.24764](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24764). Watch whether the confining-sector framing consolidates.
- **Toponium vs top-philic scalar/vector:** the Z′ null narrows but doesn't settle it.
- **Strangeness tension:** dimuon-vs-LHC strange PDF, possibly eased by NNLO small-x suppression [arXiv:2609.17120](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17120).
- **Cabibbo unitarity / |V_ud|:** Seng's δ_C basis-independence critique [arXiv:2609.20448](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20448).
- **Hadronization puzzles:** BESIII double-s̄s at 40% of inclusive φ [arXiv:2609.20154](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20154), echoing the double-charm surprise.
