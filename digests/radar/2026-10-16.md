### [arXiv:2609.06760](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06760) — Solar Capture Tests of Inelastic Dark Matter after the LZ High-Recoil Event (Mattia Di Mauro et al.)

Di Mauro and Shaikh propagate three LZ-fitting interpretations through Solar capture, post-capture orbital evolution, and IceCube annihilation limits.

- **Thermal Higgsino.** The authors independently confirm the Pospelov–Ramani exclusion, matching the thermalized endpoint to within 1–2 keV. IceCube requires δ ≳ 0.51–0.56 MeV, while the LZ-preferred splitting is about 377 keV. The gap survives the uncertainty in the loop-induced elastic cooling cross sections and a deliberately pessimistic WW/ZZ treatment.
- **Pseudo-Dirac vector model.** The model captures plenty of dark matter, C⊙ = 1.53×10²⁰ s⁻¹, roughly at the naive equilibrium scale for the IceCube b-bbar limit. However, annihilation requires one χ₁ and one χ₂, and this two-state kinetics caps Γ_A at about 4.4×10¹⁴ s⁻¹, roughly 1.7×10⁵ below the limit.
- **Neutron-philic 𝒪₄ model.** Capture is starved because it can proceed only on trace odd-neutron isotopes, leaving the equilibrium rate a factor of about 154 below the limit.

The paper flags real caveats: b-bbar is used only as a proxy for the annihilation spectrum, and re-excitation cycles need a full phase-space treatment.

It closes off the most predictive interpretation while stating plainly that endothermic explanations are not generically excluded. That gives both skeptics and model-builders something to cite.
