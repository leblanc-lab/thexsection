## Entanglement from gluons to gravitons

No single collaboration result anchors the day — the weight is spread across theory and phenomenology. The one thread that runs through it is the collider quantum-information program the memory has tracked all season, which surfaces three times: gluon-pair polarization correlations, three-qubit states in Bethe–Heitler scattering, and — from the gravity side — a third entry in the run of graviton-squeezing no-go arguments. The other cluster worth opening is a first phenomenological look at the *quartic* Higgs self-coupling via four-Higgs production. LHCb supplies the day's clean null with a multimuon search.

## Themes

### Entanglement, from partons to gravitons
Yang et al. [arXiv:2608.17685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17685) compute the polarization density matrix of back-to-back gluon pairs, finding a maximally entangled Bell state in $q\bar q\to gg$ at perpendicular emission (and a separable-but-nonzero $gg\to gg$ channel), extractable via the $\cos2\Delta\Phi$ energy-correlator modulation. Cao et al. [arXiv:2608.18030](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18030) recast the familiar $e+p\to e+p+\gamma$ Bethe–Heitler process as a source of GHZ and W states. Meanwhile Fang et al. [arXiv:2608.17015](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17015) show astrophysical graviton squeezing gets washed out at far-field detectors — global squeezing looks classically thermal locally. That is the third graviton-quantumness no-go in the memory, after Bao et al. and Miyauchi et al.; the wall keeps holding from new directions.

### Higgs self-coupling and its CP structure
Papaefstathiou et al. [arXiv:2608.16998](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16998) give the first HL-LHC study of quadruple-Higgs production in the eight-$b$-jet channel, setting simultaneous bounds on $c_3$ and $d_4$ — the quartic coupling, previously untouched by phenomenology. Two companion papers from Hernández-Juárez et al. probe CP structure in Higgs vertices: the SM one-loop CP-odd $HWW$ form factor [arXiv:2608.17106](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17106) (tiny, $\sim10^{-5}$) and a forward-backward asymmetry in $H\to Z\gamma\to\bar ff\gamma$ [arXiv:2608.17126](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17126) reaching $10^{-1}$ at HL-LHC. Read alongside the ATLAS Higgs combination from 08-10, this is the coupling program pushing into the corners loose data can't yet constrain.

### Dark baryons and dense matter
A neutron-star cluster. Espriu [arXiv:2608.17395](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17395) proposes Peccei-Quinn domain walls sweeping up quarks to seed primordial quark pellets as dark matter; Kalita et al. [arXiv:2608.17409](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17409) track density-induced neutron→dark-baryon conversion in hyperon/$\Delta$-admixed stars, with the pulsar-mass constraint disfavoring lighter $\chi$. Panteleeva et al. [arXiv:2608.17457](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17457) reconstruct the EoS with a denoising diffusion model that keeps prior, physics, and data separate — a clean template for ill-posed inverse problems. Blaschke et al. [arXiv:2608.18038](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18038) add thermal twin stars as a supernova-explodability criterion.

### Quantum simulation of gauge theory
Draper [arXiv:2608.17115](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17115) gives an efficient block encoding of the plaquette operator in the irrep basis, removing a longstanding matrix-element scaling wall — a concrete advance for lattice-QCD quantum simulation. Hayashi et al. [arXiv:2510.15493](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15493) argue for deconfinement-Higgs continuity in the SU(2) adjoint Higgs model.

## Deep dives

### [arXiv:2608.16998](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16998) — Quadruple-Higgs boson production at the high-luminosity LHC (Andreas Papaefstathiou et al.)

First phenomenological study of $gg\to hhhh$ as a probe of the Higgs self-couplings, targeting the eight-$b$-jet final state at $\sqrt{s}=14$ TeV, $3000\,\mathrm{fb}^{-1}$. The SM LO rate is tiny — $\sigma(gg\to hhhh)\simeq1.24\times10^{-4}$ fb, a factor $\sim350$ below triple-Higgs — but the four-Higgs amplitude carries more quartic-coupling structure. An XGBoost classifier trained on 52 reconstructed observables, combined with a five-bin Poisson likelihood over the $(c_3,d_4)$ plane (where $c_3=\kappa_3-1$, $d_4=\kappa_4-1$), yields simultaneous 95% bounds $-10.3<c_3<11.2$ and $-222<d_4<234$. The signal hypothesis actually combines $hhhh$, $hhh+b\bar b$, and $hh+4b$; at the SM point it expects only 0.39 signal vs 21.9 background events. They also show $hhhh\to8b$ events with $\geq6$ tags feed the ATLAS $hhh\to6b$ region — with $\sim89.5\%$ tag probability the four-Higgs contribution can reach 10–100% of the $hhh$ rate in parts of the plane.

Why it matters: the quartic coupling $\kappa_4$ is essentially unconstrained by phenomenology; this opens a channel and argues for jointly treating $hhh$ and $hhhh$ in inclusive multi-$b$ analyses, complementing the ATLAS Higgs-combination push into loose corners. Caveats are heavy and honest: statistics-only limits, no detector/PDF/scale systematics, the $hh+4b$ piece computed in $m_t\to\infty$ with only $c_3$ dependence, forced-splitting approximations, and sub-unit expected counts making asymptotic contours approximate. On a like-for-like basis the reach is comparable to ATLAS $hhh$ in $c_3$ but weaker in $d_4$.

### [arXiv:2608.17685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17685) — Entanglement of back-to-back gluon pair (Lei Yang et al.)

Full polarization density matrices for back-to-back gluon pairs in unpolarized $pp$ collisions, computed at tree level for the $q\bar q\to gg$ and $gg\to gg$ channels. For $q\bar q\to gg$, chirality conservation plus the MHV structure restrict the state to the $|\Psi^\pm\rangle$ Bell sector; at perpendicular emission ($\theta=\pi/2$) the pair becomes the pure Bell state $|\Psi^-\rangle$ — maximally entangled, concurrence $\mathcal{C}=-c_q(\theta)$ reaching unity and $S_{\max}>2$ (CHSH-violating) across $0<\theta<\pi$. In contrast, $gg\to gg$ is separable at all angles ($\mathcal{C}=0$, $S_{\max}\leq10\sqrt2/9<2$) but retains an appreciable linear-polarization correlation, $c_g(\pi/2)=1/9\approx11\%$. The observable link is the four-point energy correlator (E4C): contracting the pair density matrix with collinear splitting kernels maps the correlation onto a $\cos2\Delta\Phi$ azimuthal modulation between the two branching planes, with asymmetry $\mathscr{A}^{\bm\kappa}=\tfrac12(C_{xx'}+C_{yy'})a^{\bm\kappa}(n)$.

Why it matters: it extends the collider quantum-information program into the gluon sector, showing entanglement is generated purely by QCD helicity structure rather than a color-singlet vertex as in $H\to gg$. The distinction between an entangled $q\bar q$-initiated state and a merely correlated $gg$-initiated one is physically sharp. Caveat: the inclusive E4C signal suffers strong cancellations among $q\bar q q\bar q$, $q\bar q gg$, $gggg$ splitting channels; heavy-flavor tagging in both jets is needed to isolate $Q\bar Q Q\bar Q$ and recover sensitivity. Extraction relies on the one-step collinear-splitting approximation, and the tagged sample is expected to be $gg\to gg$-dominated with $q\bar q\to gg$ as irreducible Born contamination.

### [arXiv:2608.17015](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17015) — Astrophysical Graviton Squeezing Can Be Hidden in the Far-Field (Cheng-Jun Fang et al.)

Using spatial quantum-optics methods extended to gravity, the authors compute the second-order coherence $g^{(2)}$ accessible to a single far-field detector for gravitons emitted in a macroscopically squeezed state from a localized astrophysical source. An EFT description of squeezed-graviton generation yields Bogoliubov coefficients that inherit the source's momentum envelope; the far-field projection (retarded pole extraction, angular stationary phase) shows a local observer effectively measures the asymptotic out-state along a single line of sight. The key result is a "same-cone" kinematic conflict: for a non-relativistic source the squeezing matrix peaks on the anti-diagonal ($\mathbf{p}+\mathbf{q}\approx0$, back-to-back pairs), so the anomalous coherence $M_D$ requires one internal momentum simultaneously near $\mathbf{p}$ and $-\mathbf{p}'$ — incompatible for same-direction detection. The result is $g^{(2)}-2\sim\mathcal{O}[\exp(-2d\,\mathcal{E}/k_*)]+\mathcal{O}(L_D/r)$, so a globally squeezed state appears classically thermal ($g^{(2)}=2$) locally.

Why it matters: this is the third graviton-quantumness no-go the memory has tracked (after Bao et al. and Miyauchi et al.), attacking macroscopic squeezing from the far-field-observability angle rather than production. Global squeezing does not guarantee local detectability. The authors frame it as a conditional no-go requiring three conditions: non-relativistic source, single localized detector, near-flat propagation. Each suggests an escape: relativistic/collinear sources (BH binaries, jets) could restore $\mathcal{E}/k_*\sim1$; multi-detector networks probing both partner cones recover cross-correlations (feasible for a nearby solar axion halo); and strong-curvature lensing near compact objects can redirect back-to-back pairs into one cone. A concurrent independent preprint (ref. 51) reached consistent conclusions via a different framework.
