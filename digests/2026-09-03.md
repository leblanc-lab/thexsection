## The LZ event gets its paper, and its refutations

The actual LZ collaboration paper landed today ([arXiv:2609.02823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02823)) — one nuclear recoil at $248\pm23$ keV, 2.6σ global — turning the interpretation storm of the last two days into a scramble around a now-official source. Alongside it came four more model-builders and, more usefully, two hard exclusions: Pospelov et al. [arXiv:2609.02775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02775) use solar capture and IceCube to demand $\delta>566$ keV, cutting straight through the ~350 keV higgsino window that three papers converged on yesterday. The rest of the day is a diffuse spread — an entanglement-as-BSM-probe cluster, a matched pair on fiducial-recoil gluon tomography, and a precise LHCb $CPT$ test — but the center of gravity is unambiguously xenon.

## Themes

### The LZ event, now bounded from both sides
The interpretation glut continues: Yin [arXiv:2609.01892](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01892), Nomura [arXiv:2609.02505](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02505), Di Mauro [arXiv:2609.02608](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02608), Visinelli [arXiv:2609.02807](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02807), and Yamashita [arXiv:2609.02868](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02868) all fit ~380 GeV–1.1 TeV inelastic dark matter with a ~300–370 keV splitting — endothermic higgsino, EW doublet, or dark photon. What changed today is the counter-pressure. Pospelov et al. [arXiv:2609.02775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02775) show the quasi-Dirac higgsino would be captured and annihilate in the Sun, and IceCube's non-observation pushes $\delta>566$ keV — flatly excluding the endothermic-higgsino reading that yesterday's cluster (Fan, Freese, Su) landed on. The lesson from two days: the event's kinematics fix the mass and splitting so tightly that the models barely constrain the physics, but the astrophysical bounds do.

### Entanglement recruited as a symmetry probe
Three papers push quantum information past the collider-tomography program the memory has tracked all season, toward using entanglement itself as a diagnostic. Kanemura et al. [arXiv:2609.02691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02691) conjecture — and check across LNV, BNV, LFV, and FCNC channels — that minimizing entanglement entropy selects the symmetry-preserving point when a BSM interaction opens a violating channel. Jangid et al. [arXiv:2609.02467](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02467) read von Neumann entropy in dark-monopole scattering as a portal-parameter constraint. Haddadi's comment [arXiv:2609.01686](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01686) is the skeptic: he argues the "memory-assisted" quantum resources claimed in hyperon–antihyperon systems are artifacts of a phenomenological channel, not established dynamics — a healthy caution as the field reaches for information-theoretic observables.

### Fiducial recoil unmasks saturation
A clean methodological pair. Lin et al. [arXiv:2609.02280](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02280) and Wanchen Li et al. [arXiv:2609.02356](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02356) both use "fiducial recoil" — subtracting the hadronic transverse momentum in a chosen rapidity interval — to turn detector acceptance into a transverse-momentum projector, suppressing Sudakov broadening that otherwise washes out gluon-TMD and small-$x$ signals. Lin targets $h_1^{\perp g}$ in DIS (4.6× enhancement); Li applies the same trick to forward $Z^0$ in $pA$, lowering the effective Sudakov scale from $M_Z$ to ~7.5 GeV. The same geometric handle, two saturation observables.

### Precision flavor and decay plumbing
LHCb [arXiv:2609.02475](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02475) sets the most precise $CPT$-violation constraints in the $B_s^0$ system via $D_s^-\pi^+$ interference, reaching $\Delta a_\mu$ at $10^{-14}$ GeV within the SME. On the tooling side, Argyropoulos et al. release MadSpin2 [arXiv:2609.02794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02794), rebuilt on spin-density matrices to handle loop-induced processes, $N$-body decays, and amplitude-level interference — the entanglement-aware successor to a workhorse generator.

## Deep dives

### [arXiv:2609.02823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02823) — Search for dark matter particle interactions in an extended nuclear recoil energy window with LZ (D. S. Akerib et al.)

The now-official source of the week's theory storm: in a 2.84 tonne-year exposure (220 live days, 4.71 t fiducial), LZ extended its nuclear-recoil window to ~270 keV to probe NREFT and inelastic WIMP models whose spectra populate higher recoils. After selections and salt removal, **one event** survives in the NR band, consistent with a **248±23(stat)±23(sys) keV elastic recoil** (S1$_c$=540.1 phd, S2$_c$=9268 phd), sitting 1.5σ below the NR median and 6.7σ below the ER median. A profile-likelihood scan over 293 distinguishable models finds a **maximum local significance of 3.4σ** (typically for masses >200 GeV/c²) and a **global 2.6σ** after look-elsewhere correction. The upper limits are world-leading on all models; the lower limits lift off zero because of the event. Compatible interpretations include inelastic $\mathcal{O}_1^s$ (Higgsino-like) with ~300–360 keV splitting and the magnetic-moment $\mathcal{L}_{10}^s$.

Why it matters: this fixes the kinematics the interpretation papers have been chasing — mass and splitting are tightly constrained, which is exactly why Pospelov et al.'s solar bound can bite. Honest caveats abound in the text: the salt (bias-mitigation) injection failed to cover the high-energy signal region, so this is explicitly a **non-blind analysis** above 55 keV; the dominant local backgrounds (accidentals, atmospheric-ν CEνNS, MSSI) each carry a 100% MSSI rate uncertainty and rely on absence of a larger low-energy population; and no background could be positively identified as the culprit. One event at 2.6σ remains one event.

### [arXiv:2609.02775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02775) — Strong Constraints on Higgsino Dark Matter from Solar Capture (Maxim Pospelov et al.)

Pospelov and Ramani turn the Sun into an inelastic-DM accelerator. Infalling quasi-Dirac Higgsinos ($m_\chi\simeq1.08$ TeV, the thermal-relic mass) reach ~1400 km/s in the solar core — far above the terrestrial ceiling — enabling endothermic $Z$-exchange up-scattering off heavy elements (iron through uranium) and capture. Captured DM annihilates to $W^+W^-$, and IceCube's 10-year non-observation of solar neutrinos caps the annihilation rate at $\Gamma_{\rm ann}^{\rm lim}=1.5\times10^{19}$ s⁻¹. Tree-level $Z$ exchange alone, with up-scattering stalling near $0.2R_\odot$ on uranium, already excludes **$\delta\lesssim506$ keV**; including the robustly predicted loop-level spin-dependent elastic channel on hydrogen thermalizes the captured population and strengthens the bound to **$\delta>566$ keV**, moving only ~60 keV across decades of loop cross-section.

Why it matters: the LZ event requires $\delta\simeq340$–360 keV, where the annihilation rate would exceed the IceCube limit by $O(10^3)$. This flatly excludes the endothermic-higgsino reading that yesterday's cluster (Fan–Reece, Freese, Su et al.) converged on — the exact counter-pressure the digest flagged. The authors note a factor-4 correction to the tree-level cross-section that has propagated (wrongly, for a vector-like Higgsino) in the literature. Caveat: the escape hatch is explicit — dark-photon-mediated inelastic models with sub-GeV $A'$ produce no prompt solar neutrinos and evade the bound entirely, so the LZ event survives, just not as a Higgsino.

### [arXiv:2609.02475](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02475) — Stringent limits on $CPT$- and Lorentz-invariance violation from $B_s^0$ decays (LHCb collaboration et al.)

LHCb exploits the interference between $B_s^0$–$\bar B_s^0$ mixing and flavor-specific $B_s^0\to D_s^-\pi^+$ decay in the full Run 2 dataset (6 fb⁻¹, 13 TeV, 378,500 signal candidates, ~84% purity) to search for $CPT$ violation. The large $\Delta\Gamma_s$ gives access to $\mathcal{R}e(z)$ — complementary to $B^0$ systems, which are sensitive mainly to $\mathcal{I}m(z)$ — while the ~20× mean boost of $B_s^0$ mesons in the forward acceptance amplifies any sidereal Lorentz-violating modulation. Results: $\mathcal{R}e(z)=0.018\pm0.016\pm0.003$ and $\mathcal{I}m(z)=0.003\pm0.004\pm0.002$, both consistent with $CPT$ symmetry and the **most precise in the $B_s^0$ system**. Within the SME, the Lorentz-violating four-vector reaches $\Delta a_\parallel=(+0.62\pm0.71\pm0.32)\times10^{-14}$ GeV, with $\Delta a_X,\Delta a_Y$ at the same $10^{-14}$ GeV level.

Why it matters: a quiet precision milestone superseding the earlier $J/\psi\phi$ (3 fb⁻¹) and D0 constraints, probing quark-content dependence of $CPT$/Lorentz coefficients through the $b\bar s$ system. A binned sidereal-phase cross-check gives a 1.8σ preference for oscillation over the constant hypothesis — compatible with a statistical fluctuation, so no evidence for Lorentz violation. Systematics (dominated by fixed $\Gamma_s$, $\Delta\Gamma_s$, and decay-time acceptance) are subdominant to the statistical uncertainties throughout.
