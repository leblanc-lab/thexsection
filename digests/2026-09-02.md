## Four papers, one LZ event

LZ's single 248 keV nuclear recoil picked up four independent interpretations today, three of which land on nearly the same point: a ~1.1 TeV higgsino split by about 350 keV. Su et al. get there from generic endothermic dark matter ([arXiv:2609.01475](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01475)), while Fan et al. ([arXiv:2609.01504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01504)) and Freese et al. ([arXiv:2609.01583](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01583)) argue the higgsino case specifically, where the off-diagonal $Z$ coupling fixes the inelastic cross section rather than leaving it free. Agreement that tight suggests the event's kinematics are doing most of the work, not the models. Around it: last week's agentic-software wave acquires standards, and CMS posts a first on the Higgs width.

## Themes

### One event, four models
The cluster splits by mechanism, not conclusion. Wu et al. ([arXiv:2609.01590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01590)) add that the same 1.1 TeV thermal point fits the mild Fermi-LAT Galactic-centre preference and predicts a line near H.E.S.S. sensitivity. Lou et al. ([arXiv:2609.01592](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01592)) take the other road — fermionic dark-matter absorption on xenon, a 247 MeV particle giving a monoenergetic 248 keV recoil — then recast KamLAND's neutron-emission channel and exclude their own benchmark. The self-inflicted null is the most useful paper of the five.

### Agentic tooling gets standards
Last week's HEPToolBench pair has turned into a program. Menzo et al. ([arXiv:2609.00107](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00107)) argue the stable parts of agentic workflows should become versioned, citable "scientific operations" behind common protocols, with machine-readable contracts to stop independently built tools composing badly. Sun et al. ([arXiv:2609.01095](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01095)) supply the empirical half: grounding generation in a ROOT software knowledge graph lifts first-attempt execution from 58.5% to 76.0% across 275 tasks, at ~1% extra cost. Young et al. ([arXiv:2609.00611](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00611)) carry the same instinct into pre-training, one point-cloud recipe transferring across LArTPC, collider TPC, and water Cherenkov.

### A Higgs width, and things to build
CMS constrains $\Gamma_\mathrm{H}$ through H→γγ–continuum interference for the first time ([arXiv:2609.01385](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01385)), reaching $<92$ MeV observed — the tightest on-shell bound to date, still far above the 4.1 MeV prediction. Citron et al. propose GRENDEL ([arXiv:2609.00152](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00152)), 110 m of scintillator bars in the PX56 drainage gallery above CMS, reaching 0.2–15 GeV long-lived particles with no civil engineering.

### Strong CP, reviewed and probed
Ai takes stock of the strong-$CP$ problem ([arXiv:2609.00620](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00620)), a convenient anchor for the thread running since last week's lattice pair. Paul et al. ([arXiv:2609.00123](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00123)) propose axion-induced third-harmonic generation from colliding petawatt beams, tuned by beam angle rather than apparatus volume; Li et al. ([arXiv:2609.00128](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00128)) find accreting axion clouds emit discrete lines at odd multiples of the bound-state energy.
