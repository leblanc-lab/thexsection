## The kaon program regroups after NA62

Yesterday's NA62 golden-mode measurement already has its phenomenology sequel: Blanke et al. [arXiv:2607.19055](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19055) work out what the SM-consistent $K^+\to\pi^+\nu\bar\nu$ result leaves open for KOTO II, showing $K_L\to\pi^0\nu\bar\nu$ can still be enhanced tenfold with new complex phases and a right-handed $\bar sd$ current while keeping $K^+$ and $\varepsilon_K$ SM-like. That's the day's clearest thread of continuity. The other real weight is experimental and sits at the opposite end of the mass scale — QUALIPHIDE's 13 meV superconducting sensor array [arXiv:2607.19319](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19319) setting the strongest hidden-photon limits over 13–90 meV. Around them, a dense cluster of domain-wall/phase-transition GW model-building and a genuinely nice conceptual paper reframing astrophysical neutrino sources as colliders.

## Themes

### Dark matter detection at new thresholds
The diversifying-detection mood the memory has tracked all month keeps going, now with real limits attached. QUALIPHIDE [arXiv:2607.19319](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19319) runs a 41-pixel MKID array at a 13 meV threshold with a data-driven on/off-focus background model, setting the strongest hidden-photon mixing limits in its window plus among the first terrestrial light-DM scattering bounds down to 5 MeV. Complementing it at the heavy end, RES-NOVA [arXiv:2607.18378](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18378) uses archaeological-lead PbWO$_4$ to push inelastic-DM mass splittings past 500 keV, and Zhang et al. [arXiv:2607.18647](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18647) turn magnetic white dwarfs into a time-domain axion probe — with an honest warning that higher-harmonic stellar variability fakes a signal.

### Domain walls, transitions, and gravitational waves
A coherent early-universe cluster, mostly model-building this time rather than the systematics drumbeat of July 17/21. Missoni et al. [arXiv:2607.18800](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18800) study confinement at finite $\theta$ in holographic $SU(N_c)$ Yang-Mills, producing domain walls from bubble coalescence; Behera et al. [arXiv:2607.18803](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18803) tie a modular-$A_4$ resonant-leptogenesis model to a double-peaked GW spectrum from DW annihilation plus a first-order transition. Barenboim et al. [arXiv:2607.18449](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18449) map cosmological stasis onto BBO/DECIGO bands, and Inada et al. [arXiv:2607.18381](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18381) probe quantum-corrected inflaton potentials via preheating GWs.

### Sources and photons as colliders
Argüelles et al. [arXiv:2607.19254](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19254) extract two-sided bounds on $pp$ and $p\gamma$ inelastic cross sections from IceCube/KM3NeT point sources by fixing every astrophysical input independently — reaching $\sqrt{s}$ well beyond LHC and, for $p\gamma$, beyond HERA. It pairs naturally with the day's ultra-peripheral work: Contreras et al.'s review [arXiv:2607.18535](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18535) and Krauss et al.'s automated Sherpa framework for photon-induced processes with full impact-parameter dependence [arXiv:2607.18834](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18834).

### Heavy-flavor and lattice spectroscopy

## Deep dives

### [arXiv:2607.19319](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19319) — Dark matter searches with a 13 meV threshold superconducting sensor array (Christopher Albert et al.)

QUALIPHIDE-FIR ran a 41-pixel array of aluminum microwave kinetic inductance detectors (MKIDs) — FIR astronomy photon-counters, peak-tuned at 25 µm/50 meV — as a dual-mode dark matter search with a 13 meV threshold, the lowest yet in a direct-detection experiment. The array couples to a gold-plated spherical dish (5163 mm²) that converts hidden-photon DM into real photons collected at the focus; four on-focus pixels see any signal while 37 off-focus pixels build a data-driven background model, giving genuine discovery potential rather than upper limits only. A blind analysis of 22 hours found no excess (peak local significance 1.33σ at 82 meV), setting the strongest hidden-photon kinetic-mixing limits over 13–90 meV, reaching χ = 1.5×10⁻¹² at 50 meV and improving on prior terrestrial bounds above 90 meV by >20×. The same Al inductors serve as scattering targets: with 1.33 ng·day exposure and the optimal-interval method, the search reaches DM masses of 5 MeV (nuclear) and 20 keV (electronic), roughly an order of magnitude below QROCODILE's 0.11 eV threshold, carving out new sub-overburden parameter space.

Why it matters: this is the diversifying-detection theme with real limits, opening the meV "THz gap" where cavity and TPC methods fail. The 13 meV threshold also probes the low-energy-excess background at single-phonon energies (below Al's ~41 meV maximum) for the first time. Caveats are honest: the dominant efficiency systematic η₀ = 0.46±0.06 lumps uncharacterized losses; resolution above 70 meV is uncalibrated (treated as a single counting bin); and the projected scattering sensitivity assumes an aggressive 1000× background reduction, versus the ~10× already demonstrated.

### [arXiv:2607.19055](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19055) — Prospects for $K_L\to\pi^0\nu\bar\nu$, $K_S\to\mu^+\mu^-$, $K_L\to\pi^0\ell^+\ell^-$ and $\varepsilon'/\varepsilon$ after the new $K^+\to\pi^+\nu\bar\nu$ result from NA62 (Monika Blanke et al.)

Abstract only. Blanke et al. take yesterday's NA62 result — a $K^+\to\pi^+\nu\bar\nu$ branching ratio (2016–2024 data) fully consistent with the precise SM prediction — and ask what room remains for its neutral counterpart. The answer: with suitably large new complex phases and a small right-handed $\bar sd$ coupling added to the left-handed structure, $K_L\to\pi^0\nu\bar\nu$ can still be enhanced by an order of magnitude relative to the SM while keeping both $K^+\to\pi^+\nu\bar\nu$ and $\varepsilon_K$ SM-like. In the same scenario $K_S\to\mu^+\mu^-$ (LHCb) and $K_L\to\pi^0\ell^+\ell^-$ (KOTO II) are strongly enhanced and the Dual-QCD $\varepsilon'/\varepsilon$ anomaly is removed, illustrated with a specific $Z'$ model.

Why it matters: this is the direct phenomenology sequel to the NA62 golden-mode measurement, showing that an SM-like charged channel does not close the door on KOTO II's discovery target — the golden $K_L$ mode retains up to tenfold enhancement. The construction is explicitly a proof-of-existence within a chosen $Z'$ framework, and hinges on tuning new phases plus right-handed currents; the authors note the final NA62 result (2016–2026) could still shift.

### [arXiv:2607.19254](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19254) — Astrophysical Neutrino Sources as Colliders (Carlos A. Arg\"uelles et al.)

Argüelles et al. present a recipe to extract two-sided bounds on the inelastic $pp$ and $p\gamma$ cross sections from neutrino point sources by independently pinning every astrophysical input — cosmic-ray luminosity (bounded above by Eddington/relativistic-beaming limits, below by the power needed to sustain observed neutrino and X-ray fluxes) and target column density (from X-ray spectroscopy, Thomson depth, or variability) — leaving $\sigma$ as the only free parameter. Applied to IceCube's TXS 0506+056, NGC 1068, the Galactic plane, the stacked 11-source Seyfert population, and KM3NeT's 220-PeV KM3-230213A, the bounds span $\sqrt{s}\sim1$ GeV to $\sim10^5$ GeV. Concrete bands: TXS 10-year data gives $\sigma_{pp}\simeq0.5$–70 mb at $\sqrt{s}\simeq0.9$–2.8 TeV; the Seyfert stack gives $\sigma_{pp}\in[2.3,70]$ mb and $\sigma_{p\gamma}\in[0.08,9]$ mb with the smallest astrophysical uncertainties. Dedicated Pythia 8 simulations revise the per-flavor neutrino energy fraction upward to $\xi_\nu^{pp}\simeq0.086$–0.099, ~1.8× the canonical leading-pion estimate, relaxing lower bounds by nearly 2×. Several bounds are more stringent than the Froissart–Martin unitarity limit, and $p\gamma$ constraints reach beyond HERA's $\sqrt{s}\simeq209$ GeV.

Why it matters: it reframes neutrino telescopes as flux-based hadronic colliders scanning continuously in $\sqrt{s}$, complementary to the shape-based (flux-independent) Auger air-shower method and to collider fixed points. The SM cross section sits inside every band — a nontrivial consistency check. Caveats are prominent: band widths (factors of ~30) are set by astrophysical systematics, not neutrino statistics; TXS relies on an upper-bound-only column density and requires super-Eddington CR power even after beaming correction; and KM3/UHE bounds are projections under a "standard-candle" idealization awaiting source association.
