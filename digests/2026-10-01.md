## Small corrections, real consequences

Gaggero et al. [arXiv:2609.40229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40229) compute the three-loop $\mathcal{O}(\alpha\alpha_s^2)$ mixed QCD-EW piece of the top pole-to-$\overline{\rm MS}$ relation. They find a −216 MeV shift in $m_t(M_t)$, roughly the size of the quoted experimental error. That paper sets the day's pattern. Several papers find effects that look like footnotes but are as large as the uncertainties people quote. This is the same lesson as Monday's ρπ helicity-convention fix, now spread across lattice PDFs and fit statistics. On the LZ side, Luengas et al. close most of the thermal electroweak-multiplet space under the Standard Halo Model. The LMC tail that Bozorgnia et al. raised yesterday is again the escape route.

## Themes

### Corrections the size of errors
- **Gluon quasi-PDFs.** Monahan et al. [arXiv:2609.40205](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40205) trace a finite polynomial in the Balitsky–Morris–Radyushkin gluon matching kernel to a transverse-index contraction over 2 rather than $d-2$. Fixing it moves the extracted normalized second and third gluon moments by 11–13%.
- **Fit statistics.** Csanád et al. [arXiv:2609.40019](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40019) show Neyman-χ² biases of about $-3/\lambda$ in histogram ratios. These biases do not shrink with finer binning, which matters for femtoscopy.
- **Dark-photon acceptance.** Zhong et al. [arXiv:2609.40052](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40052) find that the choice of production model shifts NA62 dark-photon acceptance by more than 30% at fixed yield, so it cannot be treated as a simple rate rescaling.
- **A clean null.** Harris et al. [arXiv:2609.39582](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39582) find that single-diffractive W/Z production biases $m_W$ by at most 1.58 MeV.

### Solar capture squeezes the multiplets
Luengas et al. [arXiv:2609.38316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38316) generalize the Higgsino solar-capture analysis to higher representations. IceCube requires $\delta\gtrsim350$ keV up to 100 TeV, which rules out most thermal multiplets as the source of the LZ event under the SHM. An LMC-like tail lifts the preferred splitting back above the bound for many of them. Roy et al. [arXiv:2609.40234](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40234) offer an elastic bino/singlino route through the Higgsino asymmetry $X$, which escapes the inelastic-splitting problem. Meissner et al. [arXiv:2609.39529](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39529) return with supermassive charged gravitinos. Their reading predicts microsecond-spaced multiple recoils along a straight track, which is at least a sharp test.

### Tauonic penguins and R(D*)
Baltà et al. [arXiv:2609.40072](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40072) map the SMEFT regions favored by $R(D^{(*)})$ and the enhanced $B^+\to K^+\nu\bar\nu$ rate. In those regions, $b\to s\tau\tau$ rates can rise by orders of magnitude, and they break degeneracies that $K\nu\bar\nu$ alone cannot. This follows yesterday's Belle II WET reinterpretation. On the experimental-method side, Xiao et al. [arXiv:2609.39998](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39998) show that the sPlot-based amplitude extraction for $B\to K\pi\mu\mu$ still works with non-factorizing efficiency and background.

### Faster sums, fewer categories
- **Colour sums.** Frederix et al. [arXiv:2609.39574](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39574) apply symmetric-group FFTs to colour sums, cutting the 11-gluon case from about 1700 s to 0.8 s.
- **Event weighting.** Yang et al. [arXiv:2609.38189](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38189) replace sensitivity categories with continuous S/B weights, demonstrated on CMS Open Data diphotons.
- **LLM-guided searches.** Elsharkawy et al.'s hAIthem [arXiv:2609.38309](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38309) pairs an RL agent pretrained on Lagrangians with LLM agents that propose signatures. It extends yesterday's LLM-in-the-loop cluster from calculation to model space.

## Deep dives

### [arXiv:2609.40229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40229) — $\mathcal{O}(\alpha\alpha_s^2)$ corrections to the Top Quark mass (Daniele Gaggero et al.)

The first three-loop mixed QCD–EW term in the top pole-to-$\overline{\rm MS}$ relation shifts $m_t(M_t)$ by **−216 MeV** at $M_t=172.3$ GeV. The pure $\mathcal{O}(\alpha_s^3)$ term shifts it by −554 MeV, so the mixed term is about 0.5% of the "NLO" result, comparable to the QCD piece. The total NLO→NNLO shift is −770 MeV, leaving $M_t-m_t^{(n=2)}(M_t)=106$ MeV. Across the full $M_t$ range the shift is a steady ~200 MeV, against the ATLAS+CMS uncertainty of ±0.33 GeV. The calculation uses the Jegerlehner–Kalmykov scheme with tadpoles kept, so the $\overline{\rm MS}$ mass is gauge invariant. Master integrals are built in $\epsilon$-factorized form. The $W$ topologies contain the two-loop-sunrise elliptic period, and the $Z/H$ topologies contain the three-loop equal-mass banana. Several required forms do not survive into the physical result: the weight-four modular form and $\mathcal{F}_1$. $\gamma_5$ traces in the $\chi$-Goldstone diagrams are handled with local counterterms. The authors check the RGE-predicted poles, the Yukawa consistency of the single pole, and the NNLO QCD top width extracted from the imaginary part.

**Why it matters:** the term is the same size as the experimental error. In the effective series that groups the one-loop QCD and EW pieces together, the scale band shrinks from $175.2^{+9.2}_{-7.9}$ GeV to $172.1^{+2.4}_{-5.0}$ GeV.

**Caveats:** the residual band is still several GeV wide and asymmetric. The authors also warn that the known, sizable $\mathcal{O}(\alpha^2)$ corrections are left out of their mass-scan plot.

### [arXiv:2609.40205](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40205) — Unpolarized quasi- and pseudo-distributions at one loop: gluon correlator decomposition, matching, and the region $|x|>1$ (Christopher Monahan et al.)

The Balitsky–Morris–Radyushkin gluon-in-gluon kernel misses a finite polynomial. The polynomial is the same in all three of their index combinations. The authors trace it to one appendix step, where a loop-generated index between the two field strengths is summed over 2 transverse directions instead of $d-2$. That drops an evanescent operator, which produces exactly the missing polynomial when it multiplies the collinear pole. Their $\overline{\rm MS}$ result agrees with Yao–Ji–Zhang, and the same polynomial separates Yao–Ji–Zhang from BMR. In ratio-scheme pseudo-PDF extractions, $\langle x\rangle_g$ is protected. The normalized second and third moments, however, come out roughly **11% and 13% too low** at $\alpha_s=0.3$, so the true $\overline{\rm MS}$ gluon is harder than reported.

The framework splits the correlator into six covariant form factors with a $6\times6$ mixing matrix. That matrix has three eigenvalue sectors ($Z_{\perp\perp}^2$, $Z_{\perp\perp}Z_{\parallel\perp}$, $Z_{\parallel\perp}^2$), so any lattice index choice follows without new loops. The result is cross-checked in $R_\xi$ and axial gauge.

A second conceptual result uses Paley–Wiener. The $|x|>1$ exterior comes entirely from the branch point of $\log(\tilde z^2\mu^2)$ at $\nu=0$, and at leading power it is fixed pointwise by the renormalization group. At one loop it is $n_f$-independent, with tail coefficients $5C_A/6$ for $\mathcal{M}_{pp}$ and $11C_A/6$ for $\mathcal{G}^{ziiz}$. Fitting the exterior with independent parameters therefore risks absorbing power corrections into the leading-twist PDF.

**Caveats:** the analysis is one-loop only, the moment shifts are an estimate at fixed $\alpha_s$, and gluon target-mass corrections are not known.

### [arXiv:2609.38316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38316) — Constraints on Complex Electroweak WIMPs from Solar Capture (Juan P. Luengas et al.)

Endothermic capture depends only on hypercharge: the $Y=1$ rate is 4× the $Y=1/2$ rate. For $n\ge4$, the loop-induced SI elastic scattering ($\propto n^4$) and the Sommerfeld-enhanced annihilation keep the Sun in equilibrium. The IceCube bound is therefore a single universal curve per $Y$ for fermions and scalars alike.

The excluded splitting is largest at TeV masses, ~600 keV for $Y=1$ and ~570 keV for $Y=1/2$, and it falls to 350–400 keV by 100 TeV. Under the SHM this excludes the $3_1$, $5_1$, $4_{1/2}$ and $6_{1/2}$ as sources of the LZ 248 keV event. The $8_{1/2}$ is marginal. Only the $10_{1/2}$ (158 TeV) and $12_{1/2}$ (253 TeV) survive, because they sit in the heavy-mass LZ island.

The solar curve barely depends on the halo, since capture is dominated by slow particles. The LMC tail instead moves the LZ-preferred $\delta$ above 500 keV. Under that tail, $6_{1/2}$ and above become allowed, $4_{1/2}$ is marginal, and $3_1$, $5_1$ escape IceCube but match LZ only at ~3σ.

For the doublets:
- The fermion doublet reproduces the earlier Higgsino bound, $\delta\lesssim566$ keV.
- The scalar doublet has no spin-dependent sinking channel and stalls at 0.1–0.2 $R_\odot$. Its bound peaks near 510 keV and is gone by 10 TeV.

**Why it matters:** it makes the velocity-tail question decisive for any heavy-multiplet LZ fit.

**Caveats:**
- The fit rests on a single event, and its two mass islands are separated by only 1–2σ.
- Sommerfeld factors are parametric, good to ~30% off resonance.
- The analysis restricts to $Y\le1$ and assumes no tree-level Higgs portal for scalars. A portal with $\lambda_{\chi H}\gtrsim0.01$ would strengthen the scalar-doublet bound.
