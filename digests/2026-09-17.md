## The 17 MeV boson meets cosmic muons

The day's sharpest single paper is a debunking: Benmansour et al. [arXiv:2609.18383](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18383) show that Geant4 simulations of cosmic-ray muon coincidences in the Atomki five- and six-arm spectrometers reproduce the very opening-angle peaks — near 140° in the ⁸Be window, 120° in the ⁴He window — long read as evidence for a 17 MeV boson, with a cosmic-to-signal ratio above unity. It doesn't close the case, but it puts the burden squarely back on the beam-off studies. Around it the weight sits where it did yesterday, in precision QCD and nucleon structure, plus a genuine f0(980) convergence and a quiet continuation of the LZ story — now down to three papers, all cosmological escape routes rather than fits.

## Themes

### Precision QCD and structure
A dense fixed-order/structure day. Gauld et al. [arXiv:2609.17812](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17812) deliver bottom-flavoured jets through $b\bar b$ production at NNLO+PS with full mass effects, confronting ATLAS/CMS/LHCb and pinning the jet-algorithm differences to $g\to b\bar b$ splittings. Chen et al. [arXiv:2609.18828](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18828) complete the NLO electroweak corrections to $gg\to\gamma\gamma$ — sub-percent inclusive but −10% in the tails, with a dip-bump at the Higgs threshold. On the formal side, Skands [arXiv:2609.18102](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18102) proposes "Cat's Cradle" diagrams as an intermediate power counting for resolved jet substructure, Rainaldi [arXiv:2609.18627](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18627) recasts DGLAP scheme transformations as a gauge group, and Prygarin et al. [arXiv:2609.18269](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18269) give a Lévy-process reading of the forward BFKL kernel. This extends yesterday's four-loop/splitting-function center of gravity.

### The f0(980) under a microscope
Three independent angles on the same scalar, a day after BESIII's $D_s\to f_0(980)$ favored an $s\bar s$-dominant picture. Kumano [arXiv:2609.17974](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17974) argues from Belle fragmentation functions that f0(980) looks like $q\bar q$ ($s\bar s$) at high energy even if it is $K\bar K$-molecular at low energy — structure that depends on the probing scale. Lacey [arXiv:2609.17765](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17765) reaches a compatible conclusion via anisotropy-response scaling in p+Pb, finding a single-meson-like response distinct from the molecular benchmark. Zheng et al. [arXiv:2609.18890](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18890) work the heavier f0(1500) in rare $B_s$ decays. A rare case of low- and high-energy methods triangulating one state.

### LZ evasion goes cosmological
The recoil traffic is a trickle, and the surviving papers all target the IceCube/solar-capture bound. Nagata et al. [arXiv:2609.18564](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18564) set the relic abundance by a primordial asymmetry rather than freeze-out, so there is no antiparticle population to annihilate in the Sun — the cleanest structural escape yet. Xing [arXiv:2609.17935](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17935) produces the excited state by galactic self-scattering and detects it exothermically; Ge et al. [arXiv:2609.16529](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16529) supply relativistic inelastic Xe response functions that could shift any of these fits. The falsifiability pivot the memory tracked has fully given way to model cosmology.

### ML in event reconstruction
The tooling wave keeps colonizing reconstruction. Mao et al. [arXiv:2609.18928](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18928) introduce VyPER, a hypergraph-plus-diffusion framework doing jet assignment and neutrino kinematics jointly; Lazarovits et al. [arXiv:2609.16276](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16276) offer PSICHE, probabilistic clustering with learned variable jet sizes in space and time. Two Tsoi et al. papers push self-supervised pre-training — VICReg-based mass regression [arXiv:2609.17726](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17726) and augmentation-free EMD pairing [arXiv:2609.17738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17738) — and Benne et al. [arXiv:2609.17659](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17659) use domain adaptation to kill background sculpting in weakly-supervised anomaly searches.
