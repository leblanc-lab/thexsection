## $B\to\gamma\ell\nu$ goes to three loops

Three groups posted pieces of an N³LO prediction for $B^-\to\gamma\ell^-\bar\nu$ on the same day. Two compute the photonic radiative jet function at three loops. Two obtain the three-loop Lange–Neubert kernel for the $B$-meson LCDA, one by direct calculation and one by a conformal bootstrap that needs no new three-loop diagrams. The practical payoff is a cleaner extraction of $\lambda_B$. This carries 11-13's loop-count run from QED and $V$+jet into heavy-flavor SCET. Elsewhere: FCC-ee tomography joins the $\kappa_\lambda$ question, bubble-wall hydrodynamics turns out to be non-unique, and dark-matter limits move in both directions.

## Themes

### Exclusive B at N³LO
Huang et al. [arXiv:2611.18202](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18202) find that the largest three-loop effect comes from the jet function, not the recently computed hard correction. Liu et al. [arXiv:2611.18206](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18206) compute both the kernel and the jet function using a regulator-free IR subtraction, which enables N³LL resummation. Ji et al. [arXiv:2611.18204](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18204) bootstrap the kernel and verify RG invariance of the factorization formula. None of the abstracts reports a comparison of the overlapping results, so that is the first thing to look for.

### Form factors: a sign and a $|V_{cb}|$ input
Zhang et al. [arXiv:2611.17800](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17800) find that the $K^+\to\pi^+\ell^+\ell^-$ vector form factor is negative, $V(0)<0$. The significance is 5.8–7.9σ at a heavy charm mass, falling to 2.0–3.4σ after extrapolation to physical charm. Qu et al. [arXiv:2611.17945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17945) combine LCSR at large recoil with FNAL/MILC and HPQCD lattice data in BGL to extract $|V_{cb}|$ and $R(D)$. The abstract quotes neither number, so it's unclear which side of September's inclusive-vs-exclusive $|V_{cb}|$ split it lands on.

### Bubble walls without a unique profile
Barni et al. [arXiv:2611.17402](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17402) [arXiv:2611.17411](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17411) show that when the sound speeds on either side of the wall differ, $(\xi_w,\alpha_N)$ no longer determines a single stationary flow. A new "mixed hybrid" class appears, and its GW spectrum is mimicked by ordinary hybrids. Specifying the microphysics removes the ambiguity, and the public code HydróDex implements the full classification. This sharpens 11-13's warning that a GW forecast is only as good as its transition dynamics. Formaggio et al. [arXiv:2611.17401](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17401) add that a first-order QCD transition needs $b\gtrsim0.028$, which then has to be diluted by ~6.5 e-folds of little inflation.

### Dark-matter bounds, in both directions
- **Supernova core heating.** Suezaki et al. [arXiv:2611.17922](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17922) count each DM particle once, which weakens the Fornax $\sigma_{\nu\chi}$ limit by ~50×. Applied to low-surface-brightness galaxies, the same method gives $8.5\times10^{-25}$ cm² at 1 GeV.
- **Supernova cooling.** Cappiello et al. [arXiv:2611.17604](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17604) show that resonant $s$-channel scattering on neutrinos can trap neutrinophilic DM and weaken or evade cooling bounds.
- **Superfluid helium.** TESSERACT Collaboration et al. [arXiv:2611.17365](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17365) measure >99% loss of the phonon signal into superfluid ⁴He, which rules out submerged sensors.

This is the week's audit habit, now applied to limits.

## Deep dives

### [arXiv:2611.18202](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18202) — Next-to-Next-to-Next-to-Leading-Order Prediction for the Radiative $B_u^-\to\gamma\ell^-\bar\nu_\ell$ Decay (Yong-Kang Huang et al.)

**What they did.** The photonic radiative jet function $J_\gamma$ is now known at three loops, which completes the leading-power N³LO ingredients for $B^-\to\gamma\ell\bar\nu$. Two independent Feynman-gauge calculations agree: one from the SCET operator definition (1372 diagrams, 68 masters) and one from the hard-collinear region of full QCD. The masters were obtained both analytically and with AMFlow to 80 digits. A new photon-singlet colour structure, $d^{abc}d^{abc}$, appears with weight $Q_\Sigma/Q_{\rm ext}=1$ for $u,d,s,c$. It is finite and independent of $L$.

At $\mu=1.5$ GeV, the one-, two- and three-loop terms shift $J_\gamma$ by −9.8%, −5.0% and −3.4%. The form factor at $E_\gamma=2.2$ GeV moves 0.340 → 0.318 → 0.300 from NLO+LL to N³LO+NNLL. Using Belle's $R_\pi$, the extracted $\lambda_B(1\,\text{GeV})$ goes 501 → 462 → 430 MeV. The new jet coefficient alone accounts for 449 → 430 MeV.

**Why it matters.** This supports the digest's headline: the three-loop jet term, not the hard term, drives the latest shift in $\lambda_B$. All 27 logarithmic coefficients match the RG prediction built from the companion three-loop LN kernel (overlapping authors). No comparison with Liu et al.'s independent jet function appears in the text.

**Caveats.**
- The perturbative series decreases slowly.
- The three-loop jet effect depends on the LCDA shape at fixed $\lambda_B$: −4.2%, +2.5% and −5.8% for $\alpha=2.0$, 1.7 and 2.5.
- $R_\pi$ and $R_\tau$ give $\lambda_B$ values about 57 MeV apart.
- The experimental input is weak: $R_\pi=(1.7\pm1.4)\times10^{-2}$.

### [arXiv:2611.18136](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18136) — Quantum tomography of Higgsstrahlung: Higgs boson self-coupling and $Z$ boson anomalous couplings (Clelia Altomonte et al.)

**What they did.** The three $\lambda_{hhh}$ diagrams in $e^+e^-\to ZH$ are UV-finite as a set. They project onto only two structures:
- a tree-level rescaling $c(s)$, which sets the rate;
- an effective CP-even coupling $b_Z=\kappa_\lambda\beta(s)$, with $\beta\approx5.6\times10^{-4}$ at 240 GeV, which changes the $Z$ polarization.

Since the normalized $Z$ density matrix is blind to $c(s)$, tomography constrains $\kappa_\lambda$ independently of the rate:
- **$\kappa_\lambda$:** ±3.9, or ±2.9 with full angular information. The quantum-Fisher-information limit is about ±1, while the rate alone gives 28%.
- **Anomalous couplings (no assumptions):** bounds at the $10^{-3}$ level, e.g. $\delta\,\mathrm{Re}\,b_Z=1.1\times10^{-3}$.
- **Rate-blind couplings:** $\mathrm{Im}\,b_Z$ and $\mathrm{Re}\,\tilde b_Z$ are accessible only through the $\hat T$-odd density-matrix entries.

**Quantum observables.** At 365 GeV and $\theta=\pi/2$, the KCBS contextuality inequality is violated: 2.06 against the bound of 2. The inclusive state is neither contextual nor magic. The three magic measures disagree on where magic appears, and all are basis-dependent.

**Caveats.**
- Signal versus SM background: the $\kappa_\lambda$-independent SM loops generate $\bar b\approx-5.5\times10^{-3}$, ten times the $\kappa_\lambda$ piece. The per-unit-$\kappa_\lambda$ Fano shifts ($\sim10^{-4}$) are comparable to the assumed 10% theory uncertainty on that background.
- Hard ISR is deferred to reconstruction.
- The quantum properties are insensitive to the anomalous couplings, shifting by at most a few $\times10^{-3}$.

### [arXiv:2611.17402](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17402) — Microscopic Realizability of Non-unique Bubble Hydrodynamics (Giulio Barni et al.)

**What they did.** The setup is a constant-sound-speed equation of state with $c_+>c_-$, i.e. a stiff phase ahead of the wall and a softer one behind it.
- **New flows:** "mixed hybrids" appear for $c_-<\xi_w<c_+$, with rarefactions on both sides of the wall.
- **Non-uniqueness:** for larger hierarchies, the map from wall state to $(\xi_w,\alpha_N)$ folds. This happens only on the direct-hybrid, inverse-detonation and MH⁻ branches, and the direct-hybrid onset threshold is given analytically.
- **Benchmark $(0.9,0.16)$:** up to three profiles coexist at fixed $(\xi_w,\alpha_N)$, and inverse solutions reach $\alpha_N\simeq0.35$.
- **Resolution:** fixing the phase normalization $\Psi_N$ plus an entropy-production ratio $\mathcal R$ selects at most one profile.

**Where the hierarchy can occur.**
- **Thermal plasmas:** $c_+/c_-\simeq[\tfrac43\ln(m/T_+)]^{1/2}$. Fold onset needs $m/T_+\approx70$, and a ratio of 5 needs $m/T_+\sim10^9$.
- **SU(3) Yang–Mills:** only superheated transitions place the softer phase behind the wall, giving $c_+/c_-\lesssim2.8$, i.e. at most onset.
- **Finite density:** $\mu_+\gamma_+=\mu_-\gamma_-$, so any hierarchy needs only $\mu_-$ near the mass threshold. Free nucleons at saturation give $c_-\approx0.16$, the benchmark value.

**Why it matters.** It sharpens the digest's point that GW forecasts inherit transition microphysics. For thermal early-universe plasmas the ambiguity is mostly academic; in neutron-star matter it may be real.

**Caveats.**
- Sound speeds are taken as constant.
- The finite-density case is treated only in the barotropic limit.
- That $\Psi_N$ separates competing profiles is verified only for the benchmark.
- No lattice data exist for metastable phases.
